## Plateau: exact blockers (decided autonomously, founder asleep)
- **P5 (KX >= 1.25 vs noroles)**: noroles@1 (same ring-up and gate, species roles, no bait) kills as fast as the candidate, and the Bait draws damage without dealing any (kaiju loss 71 vs 55 per 100 hunts). The candidate needs a damage mechanic where drawing the lock makes the strike cheaper: in the harness the mechs fire at the nearest kaiju only and switch (3 s re-lock) as soon as a striker is nearer than the Bait, so the saving is a few seconds of 6 dps. Next step: either let the Bait stay nearest during the whole strike approach (strikers approach on a line that keeps them behind the Bait), or measure KX on engagements with 3-4 mechs where the lock mechanic matters (mixed guards), or make `noroles@1` a literal ablation without the gate (needs a founder decision on what the control is).
- **P6 (participation >= 0.90)**: the Strider members (D_eng 300 m = the mech weapon range 300 m) are inside D_eng only while holding behind or at the Bait; the heavy Anchor is inside its 800 m from far out and needs 40-55 s to close to melee (standoff 450-560 m, charge 11.6 m/s), so the engagement interval opens with the heavy and the L members are engaged for 30-50 % of it. Next step: move the heavy's staging to its engagement slot at pack arrival (pace so the heavy arrives first and holds), or per-species gate timing (the heavy leaves the ring earlier by its travel time).
- **P4 ratio**: strict 0.60 is fine; the role-agnostic rate (0.39) includes the long post-strike aim episodes on strikers (about 200 per 108 hunts, 24 % with another Charge entry). Raising it needs fewer or shorter post-commit aim episodes (faster kills, same as P5).
- **P1 LL:L (mixed)**: raiding LL packs have a forced Raider with Strider fit 0.3 < 0.6 (R-103 4.2 *Assumed* table, mirrored in `Tools/m007_bay.py FIT`); structural unless the Strider Raider fit or the n=2 raid slot set changes (founder or T-040 data).
- **C1 composition CI guard (LLMH)** and **C2 FE (needs FF >= 0.9515)**: small residuals (one composition, 5 % of ticks with a second claim around a kill).
- **C3**: the churn run needs >= 200 voluntary retarget switches; with a pack focus there are 33. Either C3 stays NOT_RUN for the pack arm or the churn scenario needs more fleeing mechs (spec change, not a threshold).
- **Formation logic belongs in the module**: the ring-up/gate/slot geometry now lives in `PackCoordinator`/`PackMemberBrain` (module, not harness); what is still harness-side is the mech model (nearest-target lock, patrol), the 0.5 rad/s turn limit, and kaiju speeds, which the measure depends on. Proposal: a `Game.AI.Pack` route planner on the Bay cost field (Track 1) so transit is not a straight line through water, then re-baseline.
