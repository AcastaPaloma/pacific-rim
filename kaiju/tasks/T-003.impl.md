## Decisions (Assumed, recorded; founders asleep)
1. **Baked data location**: `Assets/StreamingAssets/Geo/` so the importer reads it by path in the editor and players
   ship it; a nested `.gitattributes` puts `*.r16` in LFS (root attributes cover .tif).
2. **Terrain render = RTIN** (crack-free right-triangle network), split threshold 0.1 m: measured max 0.196 m, RMS
   0.033 m, 589k triangles. RTIN split error is not a strict bound, so the measured error is what is stated. Physics =
   full-resolution TerrainCollider (no Terrain renderer).
3. **Buildings**: chunked render meshes (256 m, draw calls 118) + one GameObject and MeshCollider per building, so
   removal by bid is per building and only touches one chunk. Not one mega-mesh.
4. **Outline remainder rule**: a structure with `building:part`s renders its parts plus the outline area its parts
   do not cover, at the outline's own height (26 structures, 53,280 m^2; e.g. One Maritime Plaza's 121 m body whose
   only part is a lobby). Without it, area recall was 0.961.
5. **Pier rule**: OSM piers raised to a flat deck (3DEP deck median, else the 75th percentile of dry land at the root).
   Bare-earth DEMs and CUDEM have seabed or hydro-flattened -1.07 m under most piers.
6. **Water mask** cuts out every cell touched by a footprint or pier (W2 tower, pier sheds), so no building stands in
   drawn water.
7. **Roads** drawn as a terrain decal (no geometry, no z-fighting). Bridges and tunnels flagged, not drawn (no deck
   heights in the data). Bay Bridge waits for F-041.
8. **V5 skyline** excludes Coit Tower itself (the camera at 146 m NAVD88 is inside its OSM ring part). Same exclusion
   in the Unity capture, R-src and R-ind.
9. **R-004 merge threshold** "MHW (+1.80 m)" read as +1.798 m (the value; MHHW by name).
10. **Viewpoint eye heights** taken as NAVD88 (V4 10 m, V5 146 m).
11. **Landmark anchor** = footprint area centroid; R-004 reference positions are Overpass `out center` (bbox centre):
    p95 1.12 m difference, well inside 5 m.
12. **Pipeline land check** excludes the 3DEP/CUDEM feather band exactly (weight 1 on every node within 2 m) and
    slopes > 45 deg using the 3x3 maximum (one retaining wall otherwise gave 1.43 m).
13. **NGS**: 0 of 29 in-tile marks qualify under R-004 filters, so M-005.2 gates on the pipeline check (per C3).
14. **Core config asset**: adding `WorldGeo.json` made Core's own editor hook regenerate
    `Assets/Game/Core/Unity/Resources/GameCoreConfig.asset` (new flag-file reference). Committed because Core's test
    `Adapter_ConfigAsset_PointsAtCommittedFiles` requires it; no Core code was edited.

## Not met / not run, and why
- M-005.4 default share 13.3 % (> 10 %): R-004's DataSF IoU > 0.6 rule rejects row houses OSM splits from one DataSF
  block. A containment rule (>= 80 % of the OSM footprint inside one DataSF footprint, relief > 3 m) gives 6.4 %; not
  applied (heights only by the documented rule). Proposed Q below.
- M-005.7 vs R-ind (Assumed thresholds) fails: V4 IoU 0.845 / MAE 11.9 px; V5 0.635 / 12.0 px. Causes visible in the
  overlays: trees (Telegraph Hill), cranes, rooftop structures, towers whose OSM footprints differ from lidar (54
  candidates in `known_gaps.md`). No horizontal misalignment (cross-correlation best shift 2 m, IoU change 0.002).
  A buildings-only lidar variant was tried: CA_SanFrancisco_B23 has no class 6 (building) points, so it is not usable.
- C11 seam median |3DEP - CUDEM| in the feather band 0.457 m > 0.1 m; the blend adds <= 0.489 m step; all-land
  median 0.061 m (R-004 measured 0.02 signed). The band sits on the +1.8 m contour, seawalls and 3DEP gaps.
- Out-of-tile M-005 sub-measures (9): not_applicable_in_tile, due M1 (GG preview on L0_26_37 kept as information).
- Machine queue rule: the final evidence run started one Unity batch launch while 2 other batch processes were
  counted (the scripted run did not wait). Earlier launches checked and were within the limit. Process noted.
- Blind "which city" review is for eval-bay, not produced here.
