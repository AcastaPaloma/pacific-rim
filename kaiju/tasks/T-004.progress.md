## Design chosen for "dither only in gradients" (all on the wip branch)
1. **Gradient mask.** `Runtime/Shaders/PixelDither.shader` now writes R = threshold, G = mask (target `R8G8_UNorm`, `PixelDitherFeature.cs`). `SurfaceGradient()` = largest step of sRGB scene value to a 4-neighbour on the same surface (relative depth within `gradientDepthTolerance`, `PixelPipeline.json`, 0.03); mask = 1 where it exceeds `PaletteProfile.ditherGradientMin` (0.001) and in the sky when Haze is on. `PixelQuantise.shader` mixes the two LUT entries only where `PixelGradientMask()` (in `PixelCommon.hlsl`) is 1; elsewhere it takes the nearer entry. Flat faces are one colour.
2. **Haze seams of constant texel width.** `PixelHaze.shader`: seam half width in bands = min(`hazeSeamTexels` × change of level to the next texel of the same surface, `hazeSeamWidth` / 2). A face at one distance gets no seam. Mirrored in `Maths/HazeMath.cs` `Band(level, bands, levelGradient, seamTexels, seamWidthMax, threshold)` with a dotnet test.
3. **Value ladder.** `Maths/PaletteLut.cs` `Ladder()`: on-hue palette entries (a*, b* within `ladderHueTolerance` of the ramp colour of the same L*), thinned to `ladderMinStepL` (4.5), ends forced to the ramp's dark end and `ladderTopL` (0 = ramp light end; slate_crimson 50). Neutral LUT cells use rungs only and mix only neighbouring rungs; the grade maps scene value linearly over the rungs. `PaletteProfile.HazeBandCount` = one haze band per rung when `hazeBands` is 0. Also `lutChromaWeight` (2.0).
4. Pipeline colour buffer set to 64-bit (`Editor/PixelPipelineAssets.cs`, `m_HDRColorBufferPrecision`), because the mask compares neighbouring texels.
Tuning lives in `Assets/Game/Config/Rendering/PaletteProfileTuning.json` (defaults and per-profile `hazeStartM`, `hazeFullM`, `ladderTopL`).
