## Findings, routed (nothing relaxed)
1. **Streaming holds under real I/O latency (FAIL, Track 2 owner).** R-101 defines a hold as "ring not fully resident"; the 512 m ring's edge chunks enter the ring at 512 m and take ~0.2-0.4 s (read + mesh + bake) to become resident, so every new chunk freezes the body (mechs, kaiju and the debris anchor, 7-12 % of frames show one). T-111's M-015.e got 0 holds only because its harness drains 10 zero-time ticks per simulated second. Options for the owner: prefetch ring larger than the hold ring, or hold only on the chunk under the body. M-008.f stays FAIL meanwhile.
2. **Hitches 30-90 ms**, max_work_item 29-72 ms (a host build slice: tile mesh attach or collider attach), once or twice per 10 min; advisory M-008.c. Candidates: GeoL0TileReader model build handoff, `UnityTileBuilder.Build` for a full L0 render tile. Needs a profile (Profiler markers) on the Streaming path; not done here.
3. **Rendering not measured.** Batch `-nographics`; M-008.b NOT_RUN. A rendering run (Metal, pixel pipeline, Camera.Render timing) is the follow-up; the Mac verdict also needs it before M-008.a means "frame".
