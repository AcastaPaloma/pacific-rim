## Step 3 of 4: debris, mass and energy account (done)
- `DebrisAccounting` (Runtime, pure): `f_d` per state (0, .01, .05, .25, phi + (1-phi) .25; phi W1 .03, C2.L .13, C2.M .10, C2.H .05 [S9]); debris = `f_d(k) M0` is cumulative, so any path to a state gives the same mass and energy account (two-step = direct by construction). Pieces: ceil(M_d / 2000 kg) capped at 64, last piece takes the remainder.
- Energy (R-021 §3): `dPE = M_d g (H/2 - h_p/2)`, `h_p = V_d / A_spread`, rubble 1,200 kg/m3, spread = (sqrt(A) + 2 * 0.5 H)^2. Sinks: `E_frac = min(500 J/kg M_d, 25 % dPE)`, `KE_ej = 5 % dPE` (cap 10 %), `E_ground = 98 %` of the rest, `E_res` = remainder (about 2 %). W_ext is reported (`ExternalWorkJ`) but not in the balance (it is already in the state energy; at 0 -> 1 for a house it is about 10 % of dPE, which would break the 5 % bound).
- Contracts: new `DebrisAccount` struct, `IDamageModel.TryGetDebrisAccount(id, out DebrisAccount)` (read-only, for the M-052 tool). `DamageModel` records now keep MassKg and FootprintM2.
- `Apply` and `ApplyShaking` fill `BuildingDamaged.DebrisKg` (increment, kg, float) and `BuildingDamageState.DebrisKg` (total, double). `Register` re-derives DebrisKg from the kept state and the new mass.
- New constants in `DamageParameters`, tagged. ModelRevision still 1 (nothing saved yet).
- Tests: `dotnet test DotnetTests/WorldDestruction.Tests` 28/28 pass (new `DebrisAccountTests`: mass conserved over 13 state paths x 5 archetypes, two-step = direct, monotone and increments sum to total, event increment for impact and shaking, energy bounds for 5 archetypes x 4 states, R-021 mid-rise example order). Unity not run; new metas hand-written.
- Assumed choice to flag: `E_frac` cap 25 % dPE (a house's fall at 34 J/kg cannot pay 500 J/kg). The tool should treat residual in [0, 5 %] of dPE as the M-052 check.
