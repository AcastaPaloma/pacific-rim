# T-007 implementation notes · Phases B and C (asset-mech-greybox, 2026-11-14)

Branch `task/T-007` (worktree `.wt/T-007`). Phase A (concept layers) was done earlier by the concept agent; this file covers
the Blender greybox (B) and the Unity import (C). Criterion-by-criterion evidence: `evidence/T-007/EVIDENCE.md`.

## What was built
- **Greybox by script** (`Pipelines/blender/mech_frame_balanced/build_greybox.py`, headless Blender 5.3: the Blender MCP
  servers were not reachable, port 9876 refused, so no founder session was touched). 15 rigid parts named
  `MechBalanced_<segment id>`, each one closed manifold shell (exact boolean union of convex hulls, minus clearance cuts),
  origin at its proximal joint from `research/R-005/mech_frame_balanced.json`, parented by the JSON `parent` field.
  R parts are exact mirrors of L. 8 socket empties (shoulder, hip, elbow, hardpoint × L/R). 2,952 triangles, 4 material
  slots by palette role (`palette_roles.json`, entries of `research/R-002/palettes.json`), no textures.
- **Forms** follow the chosen concept (layer 2, job 2fa84a4e image 2) and `DESIGN_NOTE.md`: bow-wedge chest with a keel,
  small flat sensor cap (one horizontal slit, no face, no crest) sunk between two off-white shoulder blocks on posts,
  box forearms with an off-white hardpoint plate on the outer face, closed fists with a flat knuckle face, column legs,
  wide flat 15 × 10 m feet with the toe corners chamfered, paired rams across knees (front) and elbows (back), balls at
  spherical joints, drums at hinge joints. One orange accent panel on the left chest.
- **Measurement and evidence** scripts: `measure.py` (dimensions.json), `render_views.py`, `rom_check.py` (boolean
  intersection volumes for the AC-21 poses and every R-005 DOF at its range ends), `compose_evidence.py` (markers,
  180/48/24 px silhouettes, blind decoys, human and concept IoU), `compose_unity.py`.
- **Unity** (`Assets/Game/Units/Mech/Art/`): assemblies `Game.Units.Mech.Art` (MechPartMap, MechArtRoot, MechFrameConfig)
  and `Game.Units.Mech.Art.Editor` (MechFrameImport, MechFrameCapture); prefab variant of the FBX with socket
  orientations and the map reference; the map asset; four URP Lit materials. `MechFrameImport.Run` is idempotent
  (second run: empty `git status`). EditMode suite `MechFrameBalancedArtTests` (8 tests) passes through the CLI.
  The config copy `Assets/Game/Config/Mech/mech_frame_balanced.json` is the integration branch's file (merged, not edited).

## Decisions (Assumed, recorded)
- **Axes.** Blender = (Fx, Fz, Fy) of the Unity frame (a reflection, Unity is left-handed); FBX written Z-up
  (`axis_forward Y, axis_up Z`), Unity `bakeAxisConversion` on. The first two attempts (`bake_space_transform`, and
  −Z/Y export) gave alternating 90° rotations or a root rotation and mirrored L/R; the final set gives identity rotations,
  scale 1 and positions equal to the JSON (unity_hierarchy.txt, Axes test).
- **Joint housings belong to the child** (shoulder/hip balls on the arm/thigh, knee drum on the shank, ankle drum on the
  foot) so that a housing rotates in place in its own clearance cup. Departure from the design note, which put the hip
  drums on the pelvis: with drums on the pelvis the thighs cannot flex 120°.
- **Pelvis** is a narrow keel core between the thighs plus a back plate behind the hip balls; no front skirt (it would
  block hip flexion).
- **Shoulder blocks** sit 3 m behind the shoulder line and their outer-bottom edge is bevelled, so the arm can rise to
  160° flexion and 150° abduction. Their tops are at 77 m, 3 m below the head top (C7 needs the head as a bump).
- **Legs** are 8.0 m (thigh) and 6.8 × 6.4 m (shank) columns in both views; a hamstring and a calf channel recessed
  up the back keep the segment volumes inside ±30 % of the R-005 mass fractions while the silhouettes stay full width.
- **Head height** is measured from the neck collar top (73.8 m) to the head top: 6.2 m = 0.078 H.
- **Volume / mass**: 9,762 m³, 12.0 kt at the config mean hull density (1,232 kg/m³); volume centroid 42.46 m vs config
  CoM 42.54 m.

## Not met / limits (measured, not relaxed)
- **C27** concept overlay IoU: front 0.393, side 0.288 (≥ 0.6 expected). The concept arms hang far out (shoulder joints
  ≈ 0.45 H apart) with forearms wider than the shoulders; the JSON puts the shoulder joints 27 m apart (0.34 H), C11
  caps the shoulder width at 0.40 H and C7 wants the shoulders to be the widest mass, so the forearms cannot follow the
  concept. The concept middle view is a perspective 3/4, not a true side, which depresses the side IoU further.
- **C21 (B7 part)**, R-005 DOFs beyond B7's own list: neck flexion −30°/+40° gives 2.5–2.7 % head/collar overlap (neck is
  locked at M0; fix when it is unlocked: open the collar into a cone). Shoulder adduction −10° and external rotation
  −60° put the hanging forearm/hand into the thigh (arm hangs 0.15 m beside the thigh: a stance consequence of the JSON
  joint spacing and the C12 thigh width). Hip adduction/rotation hit the other leg in the isolated test (feet 3 m
  apart by the JSON stance) and pass in the cleared test. The B7 core DOFs (hip/knee/ankle/shoulder/elbow flexion, neck
  yaw) and all four AC-21 poses pass.
- **C3, C5, C7 (blind looks), C16** are panel judgements for eval-style; the artifacts are in place.
- **C28** mood-frame cluster histogram: no histogram artifact from Phase A was found in `evidence/T-007/`.

## Proposed breakdown entries
- Q-NEW-a (joint limits vs geometry): R-005 hip adduction −25°, hip internal rotation ±40°, shoulder adduction −10° and
  external rotation −60° are not reachable from the rest stance without limb-on-limb contact (feet 3 m apart, arms
