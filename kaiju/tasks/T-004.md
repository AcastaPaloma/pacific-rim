  - Colour: "Two or three dominant hues per scene plus **one accent**. … Limited, posterised value bands. **Atmospheric perspective dominates**: distant layers are lighter and lower in contrast, near layers are darkest."
  - Pixel treatment: "A visible pixel grid at a low internal resolution: target 1/4 to 1/6 of the output resolution, tuned by the evaluation agents. **Ordered dithering** in gradients, smoke and sky. Third-person camera: snap the camera position to the texel grid and compensate for sub-pixel offset to reduce shimmer during movement. Evaluate shimmer during rotation (M-004)."

## Scope
1. `Rendering.Pixel.Contracts` + `Rendering.Pixel`: each stage a **separate** URP renderer feature (low-res target with point upscale, depth haze bands, ordered dithering, palette quantisation, optional outlines), reorderable and individually toggled by feature flag (default off; the M0 style-frame configuration switches them on through config, not code).
2. `PaletteProfile` assets generated from `palette_set@v1` by an Editor script: at least the four district families named in §9 (warm rust/sepia, slate teal, lavender-blue with peach, slate with crimson accent), each with its haze ramp. LUT baking from a profile.
3. `Camera.Contracts` + `Camera`: `ICameraRig`, `ThirdPersonRig` following a target at mech scale, exposing stabilisation hints (texel-snapped position, sub-pixel offset) that the pipeline consumes; a camera-impulse entry point for F-009 footfall coupling (subscribes to the Core bus by event contract; no reference to mech code).
4. A headless **capture tool** (`Tools/` Editor code, runnable by Unity CLI `-executeMethod`) that writes `style_capture@v1`: colour at internal and output resolution, linear depth, sidecar JSON; stills and the M-004 motion sequences (static, translation, yaw).
5. A neutral **style test scene** built by Editor script (never text-edited): large simple forms at 10 m to 30 km depth, a ground plane, sky, a gradient-lit large surface, a 1.8 m figure marker and an 80 m block, so every sub-measure of M-003/M-004 can be exercised before the real tile and mech exist.
6. EditMode tests (LUT baking, palette nearest-colour maths in engine-free code, Bayer matrix), a PlayMode or capture test, `MODULE.md` per module.

## Constraints
- `MODULARITY.md` §5 recipe in order. Rendering.Pixel is L1: it may not reference Camera, Units or World; it receives stabilisation hints through its own contract which the rig implements or feeds.
- Renderer assets and settings are changed by Editor script, never by hand-editing asset text. Do not break the existing `PC_RPAsset` for other users: add a dedicated renderer/pipeline asset for the pixel pipeline.
- Verify every URP API against the installed package source before use (R-003 lists the files). Must work in batch-mode capture on macOS Metal and must not use APIs unavailable on Windows D3D11/12.
- Output must be exactly on-palette where the spec demands it; do not post-process captures outside the engine.
