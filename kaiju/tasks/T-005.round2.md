## What round 2 builds (R-008's recommended option, in its order)
1. **Stance-leg joint impedance around a corrected reference.** Replace the pure force-loop stance with joint-space impedance (stiffness and damping per joint and per gait phase, from config) tracking the reference's stance trajectory, so the trailing foot can roll from heel to toe and push off; keep virtual-force terms only as corrections. Correct the reference per R-008 §1: heel strike near 25°, toe-off 20–40°, about 10° of forward pelvic tilt, and the ankle demand checked over the whole stance, not one instant.
2. **Trunk and arm angular-momentum compensation** sized from R-008's balance (cancel at least 80% of the legs' yaw angular momentum at Froude 0.10; waist rotation about 21–23° within its torque limit), with the yaw moment on the stance foot logged against the friction budget.
3. **Feed-forward from the body's own inverse dynamics** where the engine exposes it (verify the current ArticulationBody API against the Unity documentation before use), not from differenced targets.
4. Capture-point foot placement and the existing balance feedback remain as corrections around the reference.
5. Raise speed by continuation from the stable gait, in small Froude increments, with the progress table (speed, Froude, slip, touchdown peak, power peak, yaw moment against budget) after each.
6. Close the evaluator defects that remain open, and implement the round-2 readings of C20 and `M-006@v1.1` from the spec (both v1 and v1.1 values reported).

## Target and stop rule
Round 2's aim is a stable unassisted walk inside the registered band (Froude ≥ 0.10) with all sub-measures reported whole-capture; the nominal 0.20 remains the registered cruise target and is not expected from this approach alone. If three consecutive increments fail to hold, stop at the best stable speed, regenerate the complete evidence for it and report. No assistance (anything added is flag-gated, logged, reported both ways; the gate is judged with it off), no threshold edits, no change to `mech_frame_balanced.json`.
