## Scope
**Part 1 · The style frame.**
1. A scene built by Editor script (never text-edited) that loads tile `L0_30_36` through `World.Geo`, places the T-007 mech prefab standing in the Bay off the downtown waterfront at true scale in its rest pose (static: no physics body, no animation), with the water at the tile's sea level and the mech wading at the real seabed depth there, and enables the pixel pipeline with one palette profile through config and feature flags only.
2. The hero shot through the `ThirdPersonRig`: low horizon, large sky, the mech as the dominant near mass, the real downtown skyline behind it in haze, and true-scale tiny foreground objects for scale (1.8 m figures, cars, a small boat: simple original placeholder props, clearly listed as placeholders; no invented buildings and nothing from the references' subject matter).
3. The same shot in all five palette profiles, and three alternative framings in the default profile (closer, wider, and from the waterfront looking out), so the founders can choose.
4. Captures in `style_capture@v1` (internal and output resolution at 1920×1080 and 2560×1440, depth, sidecar), `M-003@v1` reports for every capture, a short slow orbit and a slow push-in around the hero framing for `M-004@v1` in each anchoring mode, the in-frame skyline check against the tile's own data, and frame cost and draw calls for the composed scene.
5. A founder review board: the hero frame beside the reference panels of the same palette family, the five-profile strip, the alternatives, with file names only (no scores) so it can be judged cold.

**Part 2 · The M0 checkpoint packet** in `checkpoints/M0/` (for orchestrator §4.3 and §8; it reports, it does not judge):
6. About 10 captures per metric that has captures, each set spanning clearly good to clearly bad on that metric (use the pipeline's own controls and stage toggles for the bad end): M-003 (style stills), M-004 (short sequences as lossless frame folders plus an animated preview at integer scale), M-005 (tile views and skylines), and, from the unmerged walking-mech branch evidence as it stands, M-001/M-002 walk strips labelled "not at target pace" (read-only use of `evidence/T-005/` from branch `task/T-005`; do not merge that branch).
7. A rating sheet per metric (capture ID, a 1–10 score column per founder, a notes column) in the ratings-file format the `Tools/Metrics` `calibrate` command reads, plus a one-page guide: what to look at, how to rate, how to run `calibrate` afterwards.
8. An index page (`checkpoints/M0/README.md`) listing the decisions asked of the founders at this checkpoint with links: style-frame approval, palette choice, dither look and anchoring, internal resolution, and the open walk-gate question.

## Constraints
- Compose only through module contracts and config; no edits to other modules' code. If a module lacks something this needs, record the request in `tasks/T-006.impl.md` and work around it in the tool assembly.
- Nothing is post-processed outside the engine. No hand-painted pixels. Placeholder props are primitives or simple meshes built by script at true scale.
- 1:1 scale everywhere; the mech is 80 m; do not move, scale or restyle buildings or terrain for the shot.
- Captures deterministic and reproducible from one CLI command; committed capture volume under about 120 MB (bulk sequences git-ignored and regenerable).
