# T-006 implementation notes

## Step 1
Style-frame scene builder, `Assets/Game/Tools/StyleFrame/Editor/` (asmdef `Game.Tools.StyleFrame`, Editor only; references Core, World.Geo (+Contracts, Unity), Rendering.Pixel (+Contracts, Editor), Camera, Tools.StyleCapture, URP). No other module edited.

Command (from `kaiju/`):
`unity run . --no-tail --log-file <log> --timeout 900 -- -executeMethod Game.Tools.StyleFrame.StyleFrameScene.BuildCli [-styleFrameProfile slate_teal]`
Exit 0 on success, 1 on any exception (logged with `[StyleFrame] FAILED`). Output: `Assets/Game/Tools/StyleFrame/Scenes/StyleFrame.unity`, `evidence/T-006/build_log.json`.
Prerequisite: pixel pipeline assets exist (`PixelPipelineInstaller.InstallAll` already run; the builder refuses otherwise, it does not install).

What `Build` does:
- Tile `L0_30_36` through `GeoTileBuilder.Build` (floating origin = tile corner, so scene metres = tile-local; hashes verified by the loader), then SHA-256 of terrain, building-chunk, water and seabed (terrain vertices below sea level) mesh vertex data, of the heightfield, and of the baked files (checked equal to the manifest). Hashes are taken before anything else is added.
- Site: tallest surface building is the tower anchor (expected Salesforce Tower); Ferry Building is placed from the lon/lat offset to it (cancels projection bias). Search window 100-1000 m east, -500..+300 m north-south of that point. Candidates on a 4 m grid: water cell, flat (8 points at 10 m within 0.75 m of the centre depth, all water), distance to nearest land (chamfer transform of the water mask) 300-800 m, depth 0.5-6 m, terrain-only line of sight to the tower top. Shallowest wins (tie-break nearer 500 m offshore). Fallback passes B-D relax line of sight, depth, distance; the pass used is recorded in the log and as a warning. Depth = sea level (`WaterLevelNavd88`, +0.969 m) minus `SampleData` (baked CUDEM-merged terrain.tif) at the foot centre.
- Mech: `MechFrameBalanced.prefab` instantiated (prefab link kept), scale 1, yaw 270 (faces west, toward the city). Colliders, bodies, joints, animators are stripped if present (the prefab has none; stripping unpacks the instance). Soles rest on the highest rendered-surface height under either foot rectangle. Fails if renderer-bounds height is not 80 +/- 0.5 m.
- Water: the tile's own water meshes; the builder checks their y equals sea level (fails otherwise), never moves them.
- Pixel: light, world camera with `PixelCameraBinder` (catalog + the profile), present camera, `ThirdPersonRig` (same set-up as the StyleCapture test scenes), flags from `Config/FeatureFlags` plus `M0StyleFrame.json` (checked to enable `rendering.pixel.enabled`; names logged). Camera pose is provisional (420 m SE of the mech); later steps drive the shot pose.
- Fails if any transform in the scene has a non-unit local or lossy scale.

Known limits: line of sight ignores other buildings. The saved .unity embeds the tile meshes and TerrainData (large file, tens of MB). Ferry Building is only an approximate anchor (search window, not a survey point).

## Module requests

- World.Geo: an "unmodified tile" check (mesh hashes against baked-file-derived expected values) would make C8 a module test. Today only the baked files are hashed against the manifest.
- StyleCapture: `StyleTestScenes.AddCameras` is private; the camera pair is duplicated here.

## Step 2: props, hero camera, framings, capture CLI
Files (all under `Assets/Game/Tools/StyleFrame/Editor/`): `StyleFrameShots.cs` (planning maths, props, JSON), `StyleFrameCapture.cs` (CLI), edits to `StyleFrameScene.cs` (calls the planner, one fixed low sun, `-styleFrameMechYaw`). Not compiled or run by the implementer; the orchestrator compiles and runs it.

Commands (from `kaiju/`):
- Rebuild scene + stills: `unity run . --no-tail --log-file <log> --timeout 1800 -- -executeMethod Game.Tools.StyleFrame.StyleFrameCapture.CaptureAllCli`
- Motion: same with `-styleFrameSet motion` (`-styleFrameSet all` = both). Options: `-styleFrameOut <dir>` (default `evidence/T-006/captures_local`, git-ignored by `evidence/T-006/.gitignore`), `-styleFrameProfile`, `-captureCommit`. Exit 0/1; a per-set log `capture_log_<set>.txt` is written next to the sets and any logged error fails the run.
- The scene build alone (`StyleFrameScene.BuildCli`) now also writes `evidence/T-006/shot_params.json` and `placeholder_props.json`.

Sets written (style_capture@v1, through `StyleCaptureSession`, poses through `ThirdPersonRig.SetScriptedPose`, on the texel grid so the remainder is zero):
- `hero_1080p/<profile>`, `hero_1440p/<profile>`: five profiles (slate_teal, slate_crimson, lavender_peach, warm_amber_haze, warm_rust_sepia).
- `alt_1080p/`, `alt_1440p/`: `slate_teal_closer`, `slate_teal_wider`, `slate_teal_waterfront`.
- `controls_hero_1080p/`: `dither_off`, `unquantised`, `haze_off`, `bilinear` (flags / present control, same pose and profile).
- `motion_<screen|direction|hybrid>/`: `hero_static` (30 frames), `hero_orbit` (120 frames, 10 degrees, constant distance/height), `hero_pushin` (120 frames, 0.2 m/frame toward the mech), 1080p with output frames. The anchoring mode is `Settings.ditherAnchor`.
- Sidecars carry an extra `style_frame` member (framing, profile, purpose, pointers to shot_params.json and placeholder_props.json).
Expected run size: stills 20 frames (small, well under 100 MB); motion 810 frames (roughly 0.2-0.5 GB local, not committed); plus the scene build (a few minutes: tile load and hashing).

Shot method (analytic, pinhole 60 deg horizontal / 36.0 deg vertical, 16:9, 480x270 at 1080p, 512x288 at 1440p): the mech's waterline (y = sea level) and top set a distance D and pitch so the visible mech spans the wanted rows; the camera sits over water east of the mech at the azimuth where the tallest tower (Salesforce) falls left of the mech. Hero: visible fraction 0.47 (0.50 counting the 5.1 m below the waterline), camera 12 m above the water, base row 27.5% from the bottom. Closer 0.54 / 8 m; wider 0.26 / 22 m; waterfront: a shore cell on the Embarcadero side, eye 3 m above ground, looking out at the mech (about 0.32). Logged per framing in shot_params.json: pose in BayTM, pitch, distance, mech fractions, horizon row, tower projection. The mech fraction there is analytic; C1 is measured on the rendered mask.
Light: one fixed directional sun, compass azimuth 195 deg (SSW), elevation 15 deg, no shadows (was 45 deg provisional). Side light for the hero (looking west) and for the waterfront (looking east).
Props: box meshes at scale 1 (size in the mesh, so the all-scales-1 check holds), unlit flat value through `Game/StyleTest/Surface`: three figures 0.5x1.8x0.4 m (value 0.04, on the shoreline), one car 1.8x1.5x4.5 m (value 0.92, shoreline, bow toward the camera), one boat 2.4x1.5x8 m hull (value 0.92, on open water 360-560 m from the hero camera). Placement is by ray search from the hero camera so the 1080p footprint is 1-3 px (figure height 1.8 m at 300-520 m is 1.4-2.5 px; width is below 1 px by geometry); footprints at 1080p and 1440p, and in the other framings, are in placeholder_props.json and warnings are logged if a prop misses 1-3 px, is out of frame or could not be placed. Contrast of 10 L* to the surround is plausible by value choice but not verified here.

Known limits / things to check on first run:
- Mech yaw stays 270 (faces the city), so the hero sees its back; `-styleFrameMechYaw <deg>` turns it (the build recomputes feet and depth).
- The waterfront camera and shore props are placed on the heightfield only; they are not checked against building footprints or piers.
- Pixel footprints are analytic and pre-snap; the measured ones come from the `scale_cues_plate` step.
- Tile edge: the hero camera is inside the tile; the tile has no data west of x = 0 or beyond 2048, so the far skyline ends at the tile edge.

## Step 5 timing
`Assets/Game/Tools/StyleFrame/Editor/StyleFrameTiming.cs` (C15). Not compiled or run by the implementer. Run with the Unity binary directly under a watchdog (`unity run` adds -quit, which kills a self-ticking job):
`/Applications/Unity/Hub/Editor/6000.6.9f1/Unity.app/Contents/MacOS/Unity -batchmode -projectPath <kaiju> -logFile <log> -executeMethod Game.Tools.StyleFrame.StyleFrameTiming.TimingCli` (needs a graphics device: no -nographics).
Method: builds the scene, hero pose from `StyleFrameShots.LastPlan` (slow 0.01 deg/frame pan), self-ticks on `EditorApplication.update`; per row 120 warm-up + 600 measured frames; rows: pipeline_full at 1080p and 1440p, baseline_pixel_off (dither/haze/quantise off, no present) at both. Per frame: render-call Stopwatch (CPU), render + one-pixel readback (GPU finished), ProfilerRecorder Draw Calls / Batches / SetPass Calls / GC Allocated In Frame (previous editor frame), GC.Alloc samples inside the render call, GC collections and Mono heap delta over the window. Hard cap 12000 ticks / 1500 s wall (exit 1). Options: `-styleFrameTimingOut`, `-styleFrameTimingFrames`, `-styleFrameTimingWarmup`, `-styleFrameProfile`.
Writes `evidence/T-006/timing/timing.json` and `timing.md` (hardware named, "M-008 unverified (not the target PC)"). Exit 0 / 1.
Assumptions to check on first compile: `StyleCaptureCli.Arg` and `Capture.OutputTarget` / `Binder.InternalColour` / `Binder.Layout` are accessible (copied from StyleTiming); the three counter names exist on this Unity (rows show n/a if not). The timing was not part of `-styleFrameSet` (the earlier `timing` stub in StyleFrameCapture still throws; it is unrelated).
