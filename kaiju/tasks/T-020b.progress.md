# T-020b progress: fill default building heights (done, not run at district scale)

## Design
- Only buildings whose R-004 chain ends at `default` are re-resolved (`bake.py` build_record, `if hsrc == "default"`); every other feature is untouched.
- Fill order (`vector.fill_default_height`): (a) `lidar_3dep` = median over 1 m cells (centre inside footprint eroded 1 m) of first-return p95 minus terrain (`h` grid, NAVD88); needs >= 70 % of those cells (inside the grid) with returns, mean multi-return share <= 0.30 (vegetation test, same NumberOfReturns>=2 share as lidar_ref_v11), result >= 2 m; (b) `assessor_stories` = stories x metres_per_level (4.22); (c) stays `default`. Parts use their own footprint for lidar and their structure's stories. The DEF-T003-bay-2 parent cap still applies afterwards. Thresholds are in `config/bake.json` buildings.lidar_fill (0.30 and 2 m are Assumed).
- Lidar input: `Pipelines/geo/cache/lidar/rind_v11__<tile>.npz` (same file lidar_ref_v11 writes; git-ignored). `fetch_lidar_dsm.py --tile <key> [--offline]` wraps lidar_ref_v11's EPT read (3DEP CA_SanFrancisco_1_B23; its EPT extent in EPSG:3857 covers the Golden Gate strait and the Marin Headlands side, L0_26_38 returned 7.1 M first returns) and prints 1 m cell coverage; a tile outside the project gives an empty grid and a "lidar unavailable" note. The bake never fetches; missing cache -> stderr note, step (a) skipped.
- Fingerprint includes the lidar npz sha; manifest buildings layer gets `height_fill_rule` text; `build_info.json` gets `height_source_counts` and `lidar_fill` (applied / skipped).
- Existing test allow-list of height_source extended; the slow byte-identical rebake test now tolerates buildings differing only on formerly-default features until the committed tile is re-baked.
