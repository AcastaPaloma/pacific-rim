# T-004 fix cycle 2: C6 depth layers ordered to 30 km, C7 scale marker

## Cause
The haze is logarithmic in distance, t = ln(d/hazeStartM) / ln(hazeFullM/hazeStartM), saturating at hazeFullM (PixelHazeFeature). The
c1 fix had set hazeFullM to 5200-7200 m, so the 10 km and 30 km upright layers both sat at the airlight rung (mean L* identical, e.g. teal
67.1 / 67.1; lighter in 1-5 of 12 frames). The slate_teal C7 scale marker (595 m) failed (12 px, dL* 4.2) from the same short ramp, where the marker
was dithered between rungs 5 and 7. There is no shape/gamma key for the curve; only hazeStartM, hazeFullM and band count/rung step move it.

## Change (PaletteProfileTuning.json only)
hazeFullM 20000 for all five profiles (10 km is then at t = 0.89, below saturation at 30 km). hazeStartM moved up where it kept the 1 km slope:
rust 44 -> 130, amber 48 -> 110 (+ ladderMinStepL 5.0), crimson 42 -> 60 with hazeBands 5 -> 7 (5 bands left the c2 haze_cal ramp flat over 5 samples)
and ladderMinStepL 6.5 -> 8.0; teal 6500 -> 20000 (start 44), lavender 7200 -> 20000 (start 48).

## Open trade-off (not fixed)
C7 1 km far block (dL* >= 4 in 9/9) now fails for warm_rust_sepia (6/9), warm_amber_haze (6/9) and slate_crimson (1/9); teal and lavender still 9/9.
With a logarithmic ramp the ordering of 10 vs 30 km needs hazeFullM >= ~20 km, and a long ramp lowers the L* change between a block at ~900 m and the surfaces
1.2x behind it (ln 1.2 / ln(F/s) of the haze range), so it only exceeds 4 L* when a rung boundary falls between them. The previous passes were such boundary
coincidences at F 5200-7200. Fixing it for rust, amber and crimson needs either a shader-side curve shape key (a gamma on t, not allowed here) or a per-profile
search of start/step/bands for a rung boundary near 900-1000 m (iterations 3-5 did not find one within the budget). Crimson also needs >= 7 bands at
F 20000 for c2, which removes its previous 5-band boundary alignment.
