# T-106c: M-005@v2 Bay-scale fixes (impl notes)

Thresholds are the R-100 values and were not touched. All decisions below were taken autonomously (founder offline, watchdog stands in for the M2 build lead).

## Root causes
1. **terrain_seam_all_edges (29 edges, max 8.0 m).** Neighbouring tiles did not see the same 3DEP projects. `config/dep3_fill.json` (T-103) lists the tiles that had *placeholder* nodes; only they read the older projects that fill holes in the newest project. A tile just outside that list (e.g. L0_57_47: its newest project B22 holds data for 0.6 % of the window) fell back to CUDEM, its filled neighbour (L0_58_47) used 3DEP, up to 8 m apart. 25 of the 29 edges sit on the border of the fill region (x_60|x_61, 57|58 columns), 3 more were a tile with no 3DEP (`dep3: false` in the plan) next to one with 3DEP.
2. **collider_overlap (16 pairs, 1659 m2).** Source data: OSM has overlapping footprints, and the bake extruded every one. Kinds: a relation plus its own outer ways, both tagged building (r15693615 / w1160467319-21); one 150 m untagged shed under three houses (w349649806); a podium building:part inside a 98 m tower part (SF); small partial overlaps. Only edge-crossing pairs are measured, but the rule applies everywhere.
3. **default_height_share (0.104 Bay-wide, 469 gated tiles over).** Not footprint mismatch and not a fill bug. `fetch_lidar_bay.py run` died with `BrokenProcessPool` (a worker was killed, memory) in the middle of Sonoma (172 of 208 planned tiles never filled) and Contra Costa (331 of 339), and `run_lidar_bay.sh` then moved on to the next county. Of the 44.8 km2 default area in gated tiles over 5 %, 36.9 km2 (82 %, 313 tiles: 207 Contra Costa, 106 Sonoma) are tiles never attempted. The rest: 123 tiles with a done fill but unsatisfied buildings (4.5 km2, vegetation/coverage rules), 7 failed LPC tiles (1.8 km2), 20 LPC `nolidar` (0.5 km2).
4. **bathymetry shallowness 0.6095** (threshold 0.66 +- 0.03): unchanged, report only. On the R-004 basis (diked cells included) the same bake gives 0.680, inside the band; the wet-mask basis is the stricter reading. No bug found.

## What changed
- `kaiju_geo/vector.py` `resolve_overlaps` + `bake.py` + `config/bake.json buildings.overlap` (enabled): of two overlapping source outlines (outlines and standalone building:parts) the lower one (tag height, untagged lowest, smaller area, way before relation) is excluded when >= 90 % inside the higher one, else clipped to the ground the higher one leaves. Decision is a pure function of OSM geometry+tags, so the tile owning either side reaches the same result; ownership stays with the source outline. Manifest: `count_overlap_clipped/excluded`, `overlap_rule`.
- `kaiju_geo/fetch.py neighbour_3dep` + `bake.py` + `config/bake.json terrain.edge_donors` (enabled, reach 260 m): a Bay-plan tile also reads the cached 3DEP windows of its 8 neighbours that reach its grid. Cache only, no network. Where the neighbour's window ends inside the tile, the existing 50 m valid-fade ramps to CUDEM instead of a cliff at the edge.
- `fetch_lidar_bay.py run`: a broken pool records the tiles in flight as failed, rebuilds the pool (`--max-restarts`, default 5, exit 4 when exhausted); `run_lidar_bay.sh` retries a county (rc 1 or 4) up to 3 times.
- Tests: `tests/test_t106c_overlap.py` (5), `test_t106c_seam_donors.py` (2), `test_t106c_lidar_pool.py` (3).
