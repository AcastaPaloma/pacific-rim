## Finding: the "tie flips" in the exact-depth near and far classes were measurement artifacts, not rendering

Reading the T-182 captures frame by frame (`evidence/T-182/captures/c11slab_exact_layersnap`):

- far, start B06 (4.04 px): in frame 0 the tracker locked to the wrong silhouette. A foreground occluder edge (136 m | 10372 m, moving 2 px/frame) lay 1.5 px from the far class edge (10372 m | 31 km sky). The tracker chose the nearest discontinuity to the commanded position, followed the occluder for two frames, then jumped to the real edge. The real far edge moves 1 px in step with the present shift. No texel flip.
- near, start A16 (4.81 px): frames 137-138 only, when the near corner is 5.9 and 3.9 px from the left output border. The shifted image leaves the guard band there, so the border pixels are fill. No flip in the 126 frames before.

Fix (analysis only, `Tools/Rendering/Pixel/c11_slab.py`, selftest cases added):
1. Edge identity: a candidate boundary counts for a class only when its nearer side is within 2 % of the class point's geometric eye depth (from the sequence meta).
2. Border margin: frames whose class point is within 16 output px (4 texels) of the left/right output border are not scored.

## Camera side

`CameraStabiliser` already steps from the previous snapped position (a step is a whole texel, remainder in [-0.5, 0.5]) and the present shift cancels it. New test `JitterOnAHalfTexelTie_NeverMovesAnExactLayerEdgeByMoreThanTheShiftRounding` (Camera.Tests, 28 pass): +-1e-6 m jitter around the half-texel tie for each layer's own grid moves the apparent edge by no more than the 0.5 px shift rounding. So no hysteresis was added (it would widen remainders beyond +-0.5 texel and change the pinned v1 contract for no measured gain).

## What is still a real texel flip (not fixable by a tie-break)

When a layer depth is not the edge depth (`ref663_*`: reference 663 vs 664.874 m; near layer 124.2 vs 123.49 m, 0.57 %), the edge phase drifts across a rasterised texel boundary once during the sequence: a single 4 px step (A01 frame 36, A03 frame 63, ...). The shift cannot undo it because the rounding of the surface's own phase depends on the surface depth. This is the R-131 any-depth bound (4.8 px at N = 28) and the T-181 "exact or nothing" finding; it belongs to Q-260 and the layer-depth supply (rig supplies exact depths), not to the stabiliser.
