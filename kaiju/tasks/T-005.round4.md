Round 3's report is `evidence/T-005/round3/REPORT.md`. The optimised strides there are feasible from Fr 0.02 to 0.20 under the shipped body data. Joint-space tracking (imp = 2) fell in every run: half speed, weak swing-knee tracking, yaw drift.

## Why
Joint-space replay has no feedback on whole-body momentum or contact forces. A QP solves for both every tick:
- contact wrenches inside the friction cone and CoP limits;
- joint torques within `force_limit_nm`.

This makes the CoM, the centroidal momentum (yaw included) and the swing foot track the optimised stride.
