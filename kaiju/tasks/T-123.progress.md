Decided autonomously (founder asleep); alternatives (a grant delay, an escalating grant count) rejected as harder on honest players.

Repair-then-scrap loses at least 88% of the price.

Debris scrap can never pay for collapsing a protected building (294 $/t x steel share against a 6,000 $/m2 replacement value at 1 t/m2: at most 4.9% of V even for pure steel, against the fee x V that the collapse forfeits); note this guard depends on fee >= 4.9%.
