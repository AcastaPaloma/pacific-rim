# T-206c destruction camera on real geometry (flag -sliceRecordDestructionCam 1; record-only, default off, pixel grid unchanged)
Done: SliceBootstrap.SetImpactCamPose rewritten. Building coll. do not exist (buildings are baked into tile chunk meshes), so "real geometry" = the per-building render meshes (17662 occluders, GeoBuildingData.Mesh, exact segment-triangle test, AABB slab prefilter) plus Physics.RaycastNonAlloc against TerrainCollider / LandmarkStatic colliders. Target = the building the impactor removes (nearest footprint to the impact aim; all 5 reach state 4 and are removed, logged "building removed"). Look point = centre of the target's real mesh bounds (+ its parts); distance solved so the bounds fill 1/3 of the frame height (camera up-axis projection of the 8 corners, FOV from the camera). Pose needs a clear line to the bounds centre and >=5 of 6 bounds points. Relaxation order: stage0 pitch 42/48 (bounds >=60 m tall: 20/28/35, tower against the sky) -> 1 higher pitch 56/64/72 -> 2 same, 1.35x farther -> 3 1.8x farther -> 4/5 near top-down 78/84 deg (1x, 1.5x; centre + 2 points) -> 6 eye-inside test off -> 7 ungated 60-84 deg at 1.6x (logged LAST RESORT) -> smoke destroy view. Events in record.json state stage, distance, frame fraction, visible points, rejection counts.
Runs: 3 of 3 used (1920x1080, 100 s, 3000 frames, 5 impacts, 8 removed, no fall, silent record). run1/run2 evidence in evidence/T-206c/run1,run2 (earlier policy variants), final run3 in evidence/T-206c/ (impact{1..5}_window.png, cut_frame_checks.json, record.json).
