## Cost field input (decided autonomously, founder asleep)
The Track 1 bake (T-102) was not available (`.wt/T-102` has no depth/softness raster yet), so the planner reads an interface, `IBayCostField` (origin, 250 m cells, `DepthM(ix, iy)`, `Softness(ix, iy)`), and the harness supplies `SyntheticBayField` (7 km x 36 km water ellipse, 15 km wadable shelf at the south, deep channel to 120 m, mud-flat band; R-103 6.2 simplified, all *Assumed*). The real provider is one adapter class in Composition that wraps the T-102 raster; no Pack change is needed. Rule: a cell is blocked for a species when depth > 0.4 x height (R-103 3.2), soft ground costs 1 + 1.5 x softness (R-103 6.1); A* 8-connected without corner cutting, string-pulled with 0.15-cell clearance, cached per (height, start cell, goal cell). `PathLength` feeds the raid utility, nearest-infra choice and the synchronised-arrival pace; the transit headings of Anchor/Flanker/Bait/Regroup follow `PackDirective.RoutePos` (the Raider already did) while the slot is farther than `TransitDistanceM`. The harness blocks a kaiju at the first cell deeper than 0.4 x height and slows it by 1/(1 + 1.5 soft) on mud, the same rule the planner uses; the solo arm routes with the same planner.
