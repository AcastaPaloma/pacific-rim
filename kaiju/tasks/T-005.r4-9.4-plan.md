## 7. Risks
1. Jacobian layout is undocumented: base-column order, world vs local frame, reference point (COM or origin), row order = `ArticulationBody.index`; wrong T_base or sign silently destabilises. Mitigate: probe first, assert `J qdot` vs link velocity at startup, fail closed to imp 2 pose.
2. Sign/DOF mapping: planar torques are conjugate to hip = a + th, knee = a - b, ankle = b + f; Unity hip/knee/ankle `Sign` (DofInfo.Sign), `imp2_ff_sign_*` exist because the v1 sign was never settled; planar x-forward vs Unity z, pelvis pitch sign, mirrored A/B <-> L/R at every re-anchor. Mitigate: a swing-leg free-fall check and a one-step sign test per joint in 9.5.
3. Units/mass: sim unit 1e5 (`_unit`), reference 1e7 kg, h from the engine must be multiplied back to SI; sagittal-only model ignores lateral/yaw contact shear (realised friction ratio already 11 % over 0.4 in the planar plant, PhysX patch friction differs); one-tick latency; solver infeasible on touchdown ticks (0.15 %).
