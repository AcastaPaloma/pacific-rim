## Findings
- The prescribed split (Bait/Flanker wide, Anchor 1.6) does not help: with the Anchor at 1.6 the commit gate waits for the Anchor, so the Flanker/Bait range is irrelevant (dev 3.0 and 4.5 identical) and the Bait is still aimed at before the Anchor is in place (dev LH strict 1/6). P4 -0.254 -> -0.241, P5 0.992 -> 0.985, mv2 C1 0.638 -> 0.610. The Anchor is the limiting role, so its range has to be wide for the P4 strike to exist.
- The mirror (Anchor wide) is the first config with P4 PASS, but the margin is +0.0010 (the knife edge the lead refused to bank in T-115h) and it costs P5 0.992 -> 0.815 (CI lower 0.886 -> 0.717) and mv2 C1 0.638 -> 0.545 (below the 0.6 measure threshold; clause was FAIL already). It does not replicate on fresh seeds 4001-4030 (P4 -0.095 vs base -0.004) nor at 900 s (-0.020). Role-aware ranges do not decouple P4 from P5 here.
- 900 s reading (side by side): default P4 -0.254 -> -0.125 (still FAIL): the horizon explains about half of the default miss, not all.
