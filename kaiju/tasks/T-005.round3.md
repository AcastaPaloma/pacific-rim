## Why
Round 2's hand-tuned stance impedance walks only at the legacy step cap (Fr 0.016). Longer steps fold the stance knee. R-008 §3: feed-forward for a stance leg needs contact forces from a consistent model, and Unity's inverse dynamics has none. Option (c) solves one stride offline with the body's own masses, limits and friction. That proves or disproves feasibility before more engine work, and gives joint angles, torques and contact forces that agree with each other.

## Steps (each one small worker; engine-free until 8.5)
- **8.1 Sagittal stride optimisation at Fr 0.10.** Python, kaiju env (numpy, scipy, torch available; no casadi or ipopt). Code in `Tools/GaitOpt/`.
  - Planar multibody from `mech_frame_balanced.json`: upper body lumped on the pelvis, plus thigh, shank and foot per leg.
  - Direct collocation over one step with a periodic, mirrored boundary. The contact schedule is fixed: heel rocker, flat foot, toe rocker, double support.
  - Constraints:
    - joint ROM with a 5° margin;
    - torque ≤ `force_limit_nm` (from `body_tuning.json`);
    - friction cone μ = 0.6 / 1.5 (`friction_dynamic / friction_safety`);
    - CoP inside the active support;
    - average speed = √(0.10·g·l).
  - Objective: Σ τ² (or cost of transport).
  - Output: `Tools/GaitOpt/out/stride_fr010.json` with q, q̇, τ and contact forces per node, plus a feasibility verdict and binding constraints.
  - **Stop rule:** if it is infeasible at Fr 0.10 under these limits, report which limits bind. That is a body-data finding for the founders, not a tuning loop.
- **8.2 Yaw and lateral check** of the 8.1 solution with R-008's `yaw.py` and LIPM-lateral formulas: trunk share needed, waist torque, yaw moment against the free-moment budget.
- **8.3 Continuation** to Fr 0.15 and 0.20, warm-started from 8.1.
- **8.4 Reference as data.** Export the solutions to a config data file that `ReferenceGait` reads at imp = 2, interpolated in Froude. This also closes DEF-T005r2-arch-2 (schedule in code).
- **8.5 Controller tracks the optimised stride** (imp = 2): joint PD plus the optimised τ as feed-forward, with the existing DCM/capture-point step correction. EditMode, dotnet, and ScenarioCli runs in the background.
- **8.6 Continuation in Froude** from the stable gait (stop after three consecutive failed increments), then evidence, eval-physics and eval-arch.
