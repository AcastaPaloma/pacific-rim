## Scope
**Phase A · concept layers (Midjourney, D-023).** A founder has Midjourney open in Chrome (2026-11-14) and the founders accepted the account risk. Build the design layer by layer: (1) silhouette explorations, (2) major forms and proportions as an orthographic turnaround (front / side / back), (3) armour plate breakup and joint mechanics, (4) a mood frame of the mech standing in shallow bay water near a city waterfront in the target dithered limited-palette style. Keep a prompt sheet and every kept output in `art/concepts/mech_frame_balanced/` with `PROVENANCE.md` (tool, exact prompt, parameters, job link or ID, date, selection rationale, AI-generated: yes).
- Prompts describe the look in plain words. **Never** include franchise, studio, artist, character or product names, and do not upload the style reference images to any service.
- Never buy anything, change account settings, or accept new terms. Use the default generation mode and at most 16 generations in total. If a login wall, payment prompt, CAPTCHA or moderation block appears, stop Phase A, record it, and go on with Phase B from the written prompt sheet.

**Phase B · greybox model (Blender).** An original humanoid mech, 80 m tall at 1 Blender unit = 1 m, built by script, split into one rigid mesh per segment of the R-005 table (pelvis, torso, head, upper arms, forearms, hands, thighs, shanks, feet; left/right separate), origin of each part at its proximal joint centre, feet with ≈ 150 m² contact area each, Z-up in Blender and correctly Y-up in Unity. Low polygon count suited to a 1/4–1/6 internal resolution; forms chosen for silhouette. Source `.blend` and exported `.fbx` in `art/models/mech_frame_balanced/` (LFS).

**Phase C · Unity import.** Import settings and a prefab built by an Editor script (never text-edit prefabs), under `Assets/Game/Units/Mech/Art/`, with a part-name → segment-ID mapping asset the physics body can consume. A turnaround capture of the imported model.

## Constraints
- Original design. A reviewer comparing it with well-known giant-robot franchises must not find a recognisable head, chest, colour scheme or emblem.
- True scale in metres. No texture detail smaller than what survives the internal resolution; flat material colours taken from a palette profile.
- Segment dimensions must be consistent with the physics mass model (same lengths and joint positions as the config derived from R-005), within 2%.

Concept contact sheet; Blender turnaround renders with a 1.8 m human and a 67 m bridge-deck height marker for scale; a dimensions report (per-part bounding boxes, joint positions, foot area, total volume) as JSON; the Unity import capture; `EVIDENCE.md`.
