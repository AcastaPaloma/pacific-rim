# T-004 fix cycle 2: C6 ground seams / C7 contrast at 1 km

## Root cause
Tuning only (no code). (1) C6: the auto haze band count was low, so one band step was larger than one palette rung. At a band
boundary the seam dither (band k vs k+1) combined with the LUT dither put non-adjacent rungs (e.g. 3 and 6, skipping 5) in one
4x4 window. Raising `hazeBands` to 16 (defaults) made each band step smaller than a rung step and removed the skips
(rust 0.30 -> 1.0, amber/teal/lavender/crimson 0.98-0.99 -> >= 0.999). (2) C7: with finer bands and the 2.5 L* ladder, the block
(840 m) and surfaces about 1.2x behind it fell in the same or a neighbouring 2.5 L* rung; ΔL* >= 4 needs a coarser rung step
or a steeper ramp at 1 km. rust: ladderMinStepL 5.5 and hazeFullM 5200 (steeper ramp); crimson: 5 bands with a 6.5 rung step.

## Change (PaletteProfileTuning.json only)
- defaults: hazeBands 0 -> 16
- warm_rust_sepia: hazeBands 32, hazeFullM 6500 -> 5200, ladderMinStepL 5.5
- warm_amber_haze: hazeBands 20
- slate_crimson: hazeBands 5, ladderMinStepL 3.0 -> 6.5, ditherSpread 1.2 -> 1.8
- slate_teal, lavender_peach: hazeBands 16 (default) only
