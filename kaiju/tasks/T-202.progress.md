# T-202 progress: physics mech fell in the player demo

## Cause (found)
`SetActorsActive(false)` (the smoke's destroy-view capture at smoke t=12 s and 19.8 s) called `SetActive(false)` on the articulation root GameObject of the physics mech. Deactivating/reactivating an ArticulationBody mid-run collapses the mech. Falls in the pre-fix player runs began ~1.5 s after the destroy-before capture (clock ~17 s, fell ~18.6 s) in every variant that kept the impact; with `-sliceSkip impact,quake,duel,cannon,swing` the mech walked straight until ~26 s (fast) / ~55 s (default) before falling (terrain, long run only). The editor test never took that capture path (no pixel capture in the editor), which is why it passed.
Not causes (bisected): camera cuts / stick yaw, cannon, duel, arm swing, frame rate, ground-height raycast (ray equals terrain sample within 1 cm).
`mech_travel_m` 11180 was the hangar redeploy jump of the proxy (Update-based travel); it now uses COM travel for the physics mech.

## Fixes
- Hide the mech by disabling its Renderers, never SetActive on the articulation root.
- Auto-pilot (smoke / auto only) uses a fixed view yaw so camera cuts cannot turn the stick, and steers to the flattest heading within +-60 deg (terrain gradient > 3 deg avoided); `-sliceAp straight` disables steering.
- Impactor picks the nearest building not already at damage state 4 (quake had destroyed the old target, making the destroy view identical before/after and failing the pixel check).
- TEMPORARY safety net: a mech Falling/Fallen for 2.5 s is rebuilt at the same spot and heading (`mech_respawns`, `mech_fell_ever` in the JSON; off with `-sliceNoRespawn 1`). It did not trigger in the final runs.
- Smoke JSON: `mech_fell_at_s`, `mech_min_y`, `mech_max_y`, `mech_max_speed`, `mech_com_travel_m`, `mech_travel_m` (COM travel, excludes teleports), 1 s trace (0.1 s around 16.5 to 19.5 s) in the log. Bisect aids: `-sliceSkip duel,cannon,swing,impact,quake`, `-sliceAp straight`.
