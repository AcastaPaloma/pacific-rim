# M1 plan: Vertical slice, walk-independent tracks

Status: plan only (2026-11-14). Nothing here is started. IDs come from the block reserved in `ledger/IDS.md`:
T-020–T-039, R-020–R-029, Q-100–Q-129, M-050–M-069. `ledger/LEDGER.md` and `ledger/QUEUE.md` are not edited by this plan;
the orchestrator copies rows in when a track starts.

**M1 gate (orchestrator prompt §9):** all pillar metrics pass (M-001 to M-008 at M1 scope) and the founders sign off on feel.
This plan covers only what can be built and gated without a walking mech. Each track below ends in a feature that meets
§10 (ledger row accepted, linked M-### pass with stored values, panel passed, no modularity violations, behind a flag).

**No-fluff check (prompt §6.3) for every track:** each one serves a pillar, moves a registered metric, has a sourced R-###,
and fits M-008. Tracks 1–4 and 6 are in the §9 M1 scope as written. Track 5 (hangar) is in scope but is held to a stub
(D-017 data carry-over only, no menus, no economy) so it does not become a new system early.

## Track 1 · Golden Gate and SoMa district tiles (P3, P4)

- **Goal.** Two districts on the T-003 pipeline: GG = tiles i 25–27, j 36–38 (9 tiles); SoMa = i 29–31, j 35–36
  (6 tiles, one of which is the existing `L0_30_36`). Defaults from Q-030. Includes the approved Q-069 landmark-roof
  layer (sourced only, nothing invented) and the Golden Gate Bridge as a sourced landmark structure (Q-102).
- **Modules and contracts.** `Pipelines/geo` (Python bake, `geo_tile@v1`); `World.Geo` (`IGeoLayer` LayerApi 1: a new
  `landmarks` layer slots in after `buildings`; `geo_export@v1` gains landmark records). Core `BuildingId` already has a
  `landmark` source. Multi-tile loading and seams in Unity are new (MODULE.md known limit); streaming stays F-042 (M2).
- **Depends on.** Nothing from T-005. T-003 merged. Track 6 for the district form of M-005.
- **Research.** R-020: landmark sources and roof geometry for both districts; bridge dimensions; M-005@v1.2 at district
  scale (viewpoints, default-height share ≤ 2 % by area at M1 per Q-045); seam rule across 15 tiles.
- **Gated on.** M-005@v1.2 (district), M-015 (floating origin; a GG→SoMa traversal is > 4096 m), M-018 (tile render
  budget), M-060 (landmark silhouette IoU, new), M-008 (relative, Mac only while Q-019 is open).
- **Tasks and size.** T-020 bake 14 new tiles (Python only, 2 steps: Haiku run + Sonnet check). T-021 multi-tile load and
  seams in World.Geo (4 steps, Unity). T-022 landmark-roof layer (4). T-023 Golden Gate Bridge structure (3).
  Evaluators: eval-bay, eval-arch, eval-perf. **≈ 13 steps + 1 research.**

## Track 2 · Structural damage, persistent destruction, fault emergence (P2, P3)

- **Goal.** Every OSM/landmark building in the districts has a damage state driven by delivered energy against a sourced
  capacity model; states persist in the world save (F-043); an earthquake ruptures a **real mapped** fault trace and marks
  an emergence point (F-020, D-014). Tested with a placeholder impactor of declared mass and speed, not the mech.
- **Modules and contracts.** New `World.Destruction` (L2: Core contracts + `World.Geo.Contracts` only): `IDamageModel`,
  `BuildingDamageState` keyed by `BuildingId`, event `BuildingDamaged { BuildingId; byte State; float EnergyJ; }` on the
  Core bus (unmanaged, carries IDs). Save: one `ISaveParticipant` section `destruction@v1` in the Core save envelope.
  New `World.Seismic`: reads the existing `faults` layer output; events `QuakeBegan`, `FaultRupture`, `EmergencePoint`.
  Flags `destruction.enabled`, `seismic.enabled`, each in its module's own flag file.
- **Depends on.** Track 1 only for district coverage (works on `L0_30_36` first). No T-005 dependency: the impactor is a
  scripted rigid body. The mech/kaiju hookup (contact → `IDamageModel`) waits for T-005's contact events.
- **Research.** R-021: building capacity and damage states from available attributes (height, levels, footprint, use,
  year), fragility by delivered energy and by ground shaking, debris mass accounting. R-022: rupture length,
  displacement and magnitude scaling on the mapped traces; shaking attenuation to buildings in the district.
- **Gated on.** M-006 (impact energy and momentum clauses, extended as M-006@v2 by Track 6), M-050 (save round trip:
  same damage bytes after save/load), M-051 (damage state vs sourced fragility), M-052 (collapse mass/energy account),
  M-053 (rupture offset from mapped trace), M-054 (rupture scaling), M-008.
- **Tasks and size.** T-024 damage model + contracts, pure C# with dotnet tests (4). T-025 save section + migration (2).
  T-026 damage-state presentation on geo buildings in the pixel style, pre-baked per-state meshes (Q-103) (4, Unity).
  T-027 seismic module: quake, rupture, emergence point (4). T-028 placeholder impactor harness + capture (2, Unity).
  Evaluators: eval-physics, eval-arch, eval-style, eval-perf. **≈ 16 steps + 2 research.**

## Track 3 · Host-authoritative two-player co-op skeleton (P5)

- **Goal.** Host simulates; each client sends intents; host applies them through the intent seat and streams state back.
  Two `PlayerSlot`s, a session handshake that compares `FeatureFlagSnapshot.ToCanonicalString()` and contract versions,
  and a **placeholder body** (kinematic capsule with the mech's declared acceleration and turn limits, no physics walking).
- **Modules and contracts.** New `Net` (L2): `ITransport` (loopback now, Steam per F-063), `SessionHandshake@v1`,
  `IntentPacket@v1`, `StateSnapshot@v1` keyed by `EntityId`, `net_log@v1`. Consumes the `Control` seat/arbiter contracts
  from T-005. New `Units.Placeholder` (test body implementing `ICameraTarget` and the seat). Flag `net.coop`.
- **Depends on.** **Q-100:** the `Control` seat and arbiter contracts on the integration branch behind default-off flags
  (R-008 option A, which does not need the walk to pass). Until then T-029 builds only transport and handshake.
- **Research.** R-023: netcode stack for Unity 6.6 + Steam (NGO, Netcode for Entities, or custom over Steamworks), sync
  model for slow heavy bodies (snapshot interpolation vs prediction), determinism needs, bandwidth at N slots.
- **Gated on.** M-055 (intent-to-host latency at simulated RTT), M-056 (remote-body error at gameplay distance in output
  px), M-057 (bandwidth per client), M-008 (host budget share).
- **Tasks and size.** T-029 Net contracts, loopback transport, handshake, dotnet tests (4). T-030 intent replication into
  the seat + placeholder body (4, Unity, needs Q-100). T-031 two-instance loopback test in **one** Unity process, plus
  one two-process run only when the machine has two free slots (3). Evaluators: eval-arch, eval-perf.
  **≈ 11 steps + 1 research.**

- **Depends on.** Q-100 (as Track 3); Q-106 for the size class. Not on T-005's gait.
- **Research.** R-024: behaviour spec from the breakdown, measurable pack primitives (flank rate, focus fire, target
  switching), M-007@v1 metric spec, brain tick budget.
- **Gated on.** M-007@v1 (behaviour measures; the win-rate clause waits for a policy), M-058 (brain tick cost).
- **Tasks and size.** T-032 brain contracts + scripted state machine + harness, pure C# (5). T-033 `brain_log` capture and
  the M-007 behaviour tool in `Tools/Metrics` (3, Python). Evaluators: eval-ai, eval-arch. **≈ 8 steps + 1 research.**

## Cost and order

Workers = research (one per R) + implementation and run steps (Sonnet medium; Haiku/Sonnet low for runs) + evaluators
(Sonnet medium) + one fix cycle per task (≈ one worker per evaluator that fails). Rough, at Sonnet/Haiku scale.

| Track | Research | Impl + run steps | Evaluators | Fix cycle | Workers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Districts | 1 | 13 | 3 | 3 | **20** |
| 2 Destruction + seismic | 2 | 16 | 4 | 4 | **26** |
| 3 Co-op skeleton | 1 | 11 | 2 | 2 | **16** |
| 4 Scripted brain | 1 | 8 | 2 | 2 | **13** |
| 5 Hangar stub | 1 | 3 | 1 | 1 | **6** |
| 6 Metric tools | 1 | 13 | 2 | 2 | **18** |
| **Total** | 7 | 64 | 14 | 14 | **≈ 99** |

**Most result per token, in order:**
1. **W1 research batch** (6 small Sonnet workers): unblocks everything; each brief is under 40 lines with named files.
2. **Track 1 districts**: reuses a proven pipeline (T-003), the bake is Python-only, the P3/P4 result is visible, and it
   is the M1 track most likely to pass first.
3. **Track 2 destruction core** (T-024, T-025, T-027 are pure C# with dotnet tests, no Unity slot), then its Unity steps.
4. **Track 4 brain** (pure C#, cheap) once Q-100 lands.
5. **Track 6 tools** alongside each track (no track can be accepted without its tool).
6. **Track 3 co-op**: the most Unity-heavy; run late so it does not compete with T-005 for the second slot.
7. **Track 5 hangar** last: smallest result, depends on Track 2's save pattern.
