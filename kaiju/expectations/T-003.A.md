# T-003 expectations, draft A (expect-A): one real SF tile `L0_30_36`

Predicted from the brief, R-004 and GAME_BREAKDOWN; nothing built was read. Tile = BayTM x 61440-63488, z 73728-75776 (downtown, Telegraph Hill, Bay Bridge W2 tower, 39 % water to about -29.5 m). `M-005@v1` = R-004 §6.1-6.8. A criterion passes only on a stored artifact.

## 1. Acceptance criteria

### A. Geographic truth (M-005@v1)
1. **Landmarks, position (M-005.1).** Every R-004 §5 landmark inside the tile (#3 W2, #6 Salesforce, #7 Transamerica, #8 555 California, #9 Millennium, #10 Ferry Building, #11 Coit) has its as-built float64 BayTM anchor within 0.5 m of the import source (a), and p95 <= 5 m, max <= 10 m of the reference (b). Landmarks outside the tile (GG, Sutro, SFO, Alcatraz, summits) are N/A at M0 and listed as such, not silently dropped.
2. **Landmark height (M-005.1d, F-041, GAME_BREAKDOWN §3).** Salesforce 326 m, Transamerica 260 m (incl. the stepped parts, spire included), Millennium 197 m, Coit 64 m above its base, each within max(2 m, 1 %) of the cited value. 555 California is measured and reported but gated only once its 226 vs about 237 m source conflict is settled. W2 is excluded (Q-NEW-i). Salesforce must be the tallest object in the tile.
3. **Terrain at control points (M-005.2).** Median |game - NAVD88| <= 0.5 m and <= 10 % of NGS marks with |delta| > 2 m, over every qualifying mark inside the tile. If fewer than 5 qualify, the report says so and the gate falls back to the pipeline test: 10 000 land points vs the source DEM, RMSE <= 0.10 m, max <= 0.5 m. Coit Tower hill reads about 91 m (EPQS 91.7); Telegraph Hill max about 94.7 m.
4. **Bathymetry (M-005.3).** In-tile wet cells: pipeline RMSE vs CUDEM <= 0.25 m over 10 000 random wet points; deepest point -29.5 m +/- 1 m; plausible depth bands (near-pier shallows, deeper toward the Bay Bridge). The Golden Gate -113 +/- 4 m, Oakland Inner Harbor -15.2 +/- 1 m and Bay-wide 66 +/- 3 % checks lie outside L0_30_36. They must run on the source rasters through the same pipeline code path (a documented run on the GG window), or be reported "not applicable at M0, due at M1". No silent skip.
5. **1:1 scale, no exaggeration (M-005.6).** Vertical scale is exactly 1 (a test builds a known-height point and a known-slope slope and reads metres back). Pair-distance error <= 60 ppm and <= 0.1 m for pairs < 2 km, using the in-tile pairs (#6-#7, #6-#9, #7-#11, #3-#6 and others, at least 6) plus the out-of-tile pairs computed from the projection maths (at least 10 in all). One game unit = 1 m; BayTM@v1 string and WKT2 are the stored CRS; no UTM anywhere.
6. **Shoreline and sea level.** The water surface is at y = +0.969 m NAVD88 (MSL) as written in the manifest. The rendered shoreline follows the OSM coastline: 5.89 km inside the tile, with at most 5 m median offset between the intersection of terrain and water plane and `natural=coastline`. Piers (Ferry Building area, Piers 1-39 area) are dry and solid; water is not painted over land. The SoMa waterfront and Embarcadero are on dry land, Telegraph Hill's east face drops to the water as in reality.
7. **Building height distribution (M-005.4).** Per building |game - `height_m`| <= 0.5 m for >= 99 %; KS D <= 0.02 vs source; p50, p90, p99 within max(0.5 m, 1 %). Fallback validation (c): D <= 0.15, median |e| <= 3 m (Assumed). Every building carries `height_source`; the share per source (osm_height about 69 %, then datasf, osm_levels, default) is reported, and the `default` share is below 10 %.
8. **Building count and footprints (M-005.5).** Area recall and precision >= 0.98; count recall >= 0.99 for buildings >= 20 m². Order of magnitude: about 2046 buildings + 454 parts, 1.24 km². The tile has no building that is absent from the source (no invented buildings), and no building overlapping water.
9. **Skyline (M-005.7).** In-tile viewpoints V4 (Bay east of Ferry Building) and V5 (Coit deck) vs R-src: IoU >= 0.98, MAE <= 1 px; vs R-ind: IoU >= 0.90, MAE <= 3 px (Assumed). V1-V3 lie outside the tile: reported N/A at M0 (see §4).
10. **Faults (F-040, D-014).** The fault layer is fetched (Qfaults layers 4 and 5), normalised (offshore UPPER-case fields), and baked as the global `faults_bay.geojson` (about 3232 features) plus an in-tile clip. The in-tile clip is **expected to be empty** (no Quaternary fault crosses L0_30_36); the manifest must say "0 features, by design", and a test must assert the nearest-trace distances (Golden Gate fault about 11.6 km, Hayward northern about 13.8 km) so nobody mistakes empty for broken.

### B. Surface and IDs
11. **Continuous land-to-seabed surface.** One heightfield, no gap, hole, step or duplicate at the shoreline: maximum adjacent-sample jump across the coastline <= the local source gradient (<= 2 m per 1 m sample, except cliffs listed). The 3DEP-over-CUDEM seam (feather over 10 m) shows no step > 0.5 m (source median seam 0.02 m). Edge rows are sample-exact for neighbours (pixel-is-point, shared edge rows byte-identical across a mirrored synthetic neighbour test).
12. **Stable deterministic IDs (F-043).** `bid` such as `osm:w431972186` is derived from source ids only; two clean runs give identical `bid` sets and identical bytes; building parts keep `part_of`; Core's ID type wraps the string; a test proves ID order and tile placement are independent of input order and thread count. The ID remap policy (Q-NEW-d) is documented.

### C. Pipeline and licences
13. **Re-runnable, idempotent, deterministic.** One documented command per layer plus a bake; second run with the cache populated does no network I/O and produces byte-identical outputs (all `sha256` equal); the build fails loudly, never partly, if a source is unreachable.
14. **Cache and commit hygiene.** `Pipelines/geo/cache/` is git-ignored and absent from every commit (checked with `git ls-files`); the baked tile is committed (large rasters via LFS) under `cache/tiles/v1/L0/30_36/`-equivalent path chosen by the spec; total committed tile size reported (about 25 MB estimated).
15. **Manifest (M-005.8, `geo_tile@v1`).** Every layer lists >= 1 source with `licence`, `attribution`, `fetched_utc`, `source_sha256`; outputs carry `sha256`; `build.git` is a real commit; the Unity importer refuses a tile whose manifest lacks any field (a negative test with a doctored manifest).
16. **Licences and attribution.** Per layer: terrain/seabed CUDEM and 3DEP US-Gov-PD; buildings/roads OSM ODbL-1.0, DataSF PDDL-1.0; faults US-Gov-PD. An attribution asset in Unity lists every distinct attribution string, including "© OpenStreetMap contributors" with the ODbL notice. A script diffs manifests against the asset and reports 100 %. Q-NEW-a (ODbL derivative database) is recorded as an open release-blocker, not hidden.

### D. Streaming, pixel pipeline, budgets, modules
17. **Streaming-ready coordinates (F-042).** Tile data is tile-local with the double-precision tile origin in the manifest; the tile root can be placed at any 2048 m corner and the as-built world position (local + origin) still reproduces criterion 1 exactly; no vertex or collider has |local| > 2048 m (+ margin); no float32 world coordinate beyond 4096 m is produced. Streaming itself is not built.
18. **Survives the pixel pipeline.** Building meshes are watertight extrusions with flat faces and consistent winding; no z-fighting between road, terrain, water and building bases at 10 m, 100 m, 1 km and 3 km camera distances (capture series at those ranges, verified at the T-004 low-res settings if available, else a simulated 1/4-res + nearest-neighbour downsample); roads sit on terrain without flicker; water plane has no coplanar overlap with piers. Silhouettes read as towers, not slivers; flat-shaded captures show a hard-edged, clean skyline.
19. **Budgets (M-008).** Reported and gated against the R-003/R-004 proposal: triangles in the visible tile, draw calls, GPU/CPU memory, bake time, Unity build time. Predicted by me: terrain at native 1 m is about 8.4 M triangles, which is too much for a single tile; expected outcome is LOD or adaptive terrain with a stated triangle count, draw calls batched by material (target < 200 for the tile), and bake <= 5 min on the founders' MacBook. The numbers, not my guesses, decide; any overrun is a recorded defect, not an omission.
20. **Module rules.** World.Geo references only Core contracts and `World.Geo.Contracts`; no cross-module internals (asmdef check by `eval-arch`); `IGeoLayer` implemented for terrain+bathymetry, buildings, roads, faults, water; feature flag default off; config under `Assets/Game/Config/`; `MODULE.md`, a `Pipelines/geo` README, EditMode tests, pytest for projection, tile indexing, ID determinism, manifest completeness; scenes built by Editor API only.
21. **Measurement export.** One batch command exports as-built landmark anchors and heights, building list (`bid`, base, max y), terrain/bathymetry samples at control points and tile stats in float64 BayTM; a Python checker in the kaiju env reads it and emits a pass/fail per M-005 sub-measure. The checker runs from a clean checkout.

## 2. What a San Francisco founder checks first, and "that is not the city"
They open the oblique view from the Bay looking west. First check: **Salesforce Tower is the tallest thing, with the Transamerica Pyramid correctly stepped and pointed, a few blocks north-west, and Millennium Tower leaning-free near Salesforce.** Then Coit Tower on Telegraph Hill, the Ferry Building on the Embarcadero, the waterfront piers, and the Bay Bridge W2 tower standing in the water on the east edge. Next, the Embarcadero curve and the Market Street diagonal, then the grid with Market cutting at an angle, then the steep climb at Telegraph and Nob Hill, then the water depth falling off past the piers.
"That is not the city" if: Salesforce is shorter than Transamerica; the grid is rotated, mirrored or Market Street is not diagonal; Telegraph Hill is a mound rather than a cliff; piers are underwater or the waterfront is a straight line; the Bay Bridge W2 tower is on land; the water sits at the wrong level or the tile reads as a flat plane; north is wrong; downtown is a field of identical boxes; Transamerica is a plain box; buildings float or sink at slopes; the tile is cropped so a tower is cut by the edge.

## 3. What would disappoint them
- A tile that "looks right" but fails the numbers (landmark error 25 m: the UTM-vs-BayTM bug).
- Terrain exaggerated, smoothed into mush, or buildings extruded on a plane with their bases floating on slopes.
- A visible shoreline seam, a trench or wall at the land-water join, or water covering piers.
- Inverted or mirrored axes; an unsourced or hand-tuned height; tall towers missing because OSM parts were dropped.
- The empty fault layer treated as a bug, or a fictional fault drawn through downtown.
- Raw GIS downloads or an untracked 5 GB cache committed; a bake that needs the network every time or differs per run.
- Building IDs that change between runs (breaks F-043).
- A missing OSM credit line.
- A tile that costs 10 M triangles or minutes to load.
- Z-fighting shimmer in the pixel style, or thin sliver faces that crawl.
- Out-of-tile checks silently marked pass.

## 4. Ambiguities, risks and contradictions in R-004 (recommended reading)
1. **Faults vs brief:** brief item 1 asks for faults in the tile, R-004 §7 shows none cross it. Reading: bake the global layer, clip is empty by design (criterion 10).
2. **M-005 sub-measures that cannot run on one tile:** 5.3 (GG, Oakland, Bay-wide), skyline V1-V3, most landmark pairs. Reading: run them through the pipeline on source rasters or as projection maths, label "M0 scope: N/A" in the report; do not count as pass.
3. **Brief says skyline captures "inside this tile":** V1-V3 are not. Reading: V4 and V5 gate at M0; V1-V3 deferred to M1.
4. **Hand-built landmarks vs "no invented buildings":** F-041 landmarks replace footprints but keep `bid`; the replaced and hand-built height must still pass criterion 2.
5. **Self-referential checks:** OSM is both source and reference, DataSF heights were imported into OSM, so 1(a)/7/8 prove the pipeline only. Independent truth comes from NGS, lidar DSM (needs `pdal`, Q-NEW-g), Wikidata dimensions.
6. **Height ambiguity:** Transamerica parts total 260 m with spire; Coit 64 m vs 91 m ground; stated heights above base vs above sea level must be unambiguous in tests.
7. **Terrain triangle load:** 2049² samples is about 8.4 M triangles; R-004 speaks of storage, not rendering. Budget criterion 19 is the safeguard.
8. **ODbL share-alike (Q-NEW-a)** and DataSF stale heights (2010) are known risks; M-005.4(c) thresholds are "Assumed".
9. **`M-005.4` game height = max(y) - base:** roof pitch and parapets vs `height_m` need a stated rule so the 0.5 m gate is not a coin flip.

## 5. Evidence the implementer must produce (`evidence/T-003/`)

- `m005_report.json` plus the exported as-built facts: per sub-measure value, threshold, pass/fail/N-A with reason; building list; control-point samples.

- Double run diff showing byte-identical outputs; pytest and Unity EditMode results; asmdef dependency listing; attribution diff output; `EVIDENCE.md` mapping each criterion number to an artifact.
