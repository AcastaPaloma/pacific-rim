# T-004 expectations, draft B (expect-B): pixel pipeline renderer features and third-person rig stabilisation

Written before any build, independently of draft A. Viewpoint: a founder holding one captured frame beside the reference board asking "is this our game's look?", and a player watching a slow orbit around an 80 m machine with a skyline 20 km away. Sources: T-004 brief, GAME_BREAKDOWN §9/§10, MODULARITY, R-002 (M-003@v1, M-004@v1, `palette_set@v1`, `style_capture@v1`), R-003. Thresholds are quoted as R-002 writes them; most M-004 values are "Assumed, pending calibration" and say so.

## 1. Acceptance criteria (G = gating, R = reported only)

**A. Painted, not filtered (M-003@v1)**
1. G. Palette adherence (M-003a): on every frame of every district capture set, A_2 (CIEDE2000 <= 2.0 to the declared profile) >= 0.95 on `color_output` and >= 0.995 on `color_internal`; any `palette_index` = 255 count equals the internal-capture failures. Negative controls (unquantised, bilinear upscale) must FAIL on the same tool (0.847 / 0.871 in R-002), proving the test can bite.
2. G. Dither in gradients (M-003b): DIG >= 0.15 on >= 80% of evaluated frames; on the sky-gradient and the gradient-lit large surface in the test scene DIG >= 0.15 individually. R: DIG <= 0.5 and ordered index O (see open point 1/conflict C1).
3. G. Value structure by depth (M-003c1): over bands [0,50,150,400,1000,2500,6000,inf) m, no band-to-band mean-L* drop > 2, Spearman rho >= 0.9, far-minus-near span >= 15 L*. Haze calibration scene (M-003c2, `haze_cal`): dark/light pair contrast non-increasing, the 8 km pair <= 50% of the 200 m pair, pair mean L* non-decreasing.
4. G. Posterisation (M-003d): value levels <= 12, hue families <= 3, per frame; accent share 0.1%-15% when present (R). Grid integrity (d-iv): >= 99.5% of s x s output blocks uniform given the sidecar's integer scale and block offset, UI masked.
5. G. Blind panel (M-003f): mean >= 8.0 over >= 12 captures, no profile mean < 7, hidden anchors behave (positives >= 9, negatives <= 3, raters scoring negatives >= 6 excluded). A founder opens the A/B board and cannot name the "filtered" frame within the first glance.
6. G. Resolution rule: 1080p output renders at 480x270 internal (k=4); 1440p at k=5; the grid is a clean integer multiple (no uneven pixels at 1366-style widths: pixel blocks never differ by one output pixel). 4K behaviour per open point 4.

**B. Scale legibility**
7. G. In the style test scene the 1.8 m figure marker at 600 m reads as 1 to 2 internal pixels yet remains a distinct palette colour against its surround; the 80 m block at 1 km is at least 10 pixels taller than the figure's whole stack and shares the frame with the ground, giving the "tiny figure against giant form" composition. Evidence: a fixed `scale_*` capture; M-003e advisory values (horizon <= 0.45 of frame height from the bottom, sky share 0.20-0.50, L*90-L*10 >= 25) reported.
8. G. Haze separates layers: blocks at 1, 3, 10 and 30 km in the scene sit in visibly distinct, successive lighter and lower-contrast value bands, each band readable as a flat poster-like plane with a dithered (not contour-line) seam; the farthest layer is closest to the sky horizon colour so the skyline melts into it. Checked by c1/c2 plus a band-seam test: seam pixels belong to the two adjacent bands only (no off-palette seam colour).
9. G. Near objects are the darkest, most contrasting; a "reversed haze" configuration (control) fails 3 (rho = -1). Proves ordering is by depth, not by object colour.

**C. Behaviour in motion (M-004@v1, all Assumed, pending calibration except static)**
10. G. Static (S0): zero changed pixels over 60 frames with simulation paused (hard rule); a temporal-noise control scores 0.334 so the test is sensitive.
11. G. Yaw (Y1 0.25 px/f, Y2 1.0 px/f) on the orbit: S_px <= 0.01, C_px <= 0.01, F <= 0.001, on the sky and the far skyline too (the sky must not jump when the rig snaps).
12. G. Translation (T1, T2 lateral at the 600 m reference depth): S_px <= 0.12, C_px <= 0.15, F <= 0.005; T3 (1 px/f): S <= 0.12, C <= 0.15, F <= 0.08. The dither on near surfaces (mech, ground within roughly 300 m) must not slide across surfaces as the camera moves: this is what the eye catches first.
13. G. Smooth output: with sub-pixel compensation, `color_output` displacement per frame during T1 and Y1 matches the commanded motion within +-1 output pixel (R-002 M-NEW-a, framed here as an acceptance check, because snapping without compensation reads as stair-step pops). The sequence p95 of single-frame changed-pixel share must not show isolated snap spikes (>= 0.25) in slow motion.
14. G. Camera cut: a teleport frame (`cut = true`) produces no one-frame wrong-offset image and no residual dither phase from the previous position; the first frame after a cut is identical to a static capture of the new pose.
15. R. Animated haze/VFX never "pop" a band: on a slow dolly toward the 10 km block (0.25 px/f), band-edge pixels change index at most once per pixel crossing (no A-B-A reversals: F as in 12). A player must not see haze bands stepping on and off with a thin object at the band boundary.

**D. Artist workflow and toggles**
16. G. A new district palette is a data asset: duplicating a `PaletteProfile` asset, editing colours/ramp/accent and baking its LUT from the asset inspector or a menu item re-skins the test scene with NO code change and NO shader edit; EditMode test: generate a sixth profile from a JSON fixture and capture, A_2 >= 0.95 against it. All five R-002 profiles (warm_rust_sepia, warm_amber_haze, slate_teal, lavender_peach, slate_crimson) exist as assets generated from `palette_set@v1` with the hex strings authoritative and SHA-256 recorded in the capture sidecar.
17. G. Ramp quality (R-002 M-NEW-b): haze ramp used by the renderer has coefficient of variation of consecutive dL* <= 0.35 (uses `ramp_uniform_L`), with the per-profile accent not present in the ramp.
18. G. Stages are separate renderer features, individually toggled by feature flag, default off at module level; the M0 style-frame configuration enables them by config only. EditMode test: every one of the 2^n toggle combinations of the optional stages (haze, supersample/downsample, outline, dither) completes without error, and Haze precedes Quantise, Downsample precedes internal-res stages, Present last. Toggling a stage in the A/B strip changes only that stage's contribution (e.g. dither off: banded not noisy; haze off: M-003c1 fails).
19. G. Existing `PC_RPAsset` is unchanged (diff shows no modification); the pixel pipeline uses its own pipeline/renderer asset created by Editor script.

**E. Rig contract**
20. G. `ICameraRig` and `ThirdPersonRig` exist; the rig follows a target at mech scale (an 80 m, 10,000 t object; speed ~10 m/s) without lateral jitter in the target's screen position beyond one internal texel while following. The rig provides the stabilisation hints (snapped position, quantised rotation, sub-pixel offset, dither origin, reference depth, texel angle, cut flag) to the pipeline through a contract that lives in a lower layer than Camera (MODULARITY §3 L1 vs L3); EditMode/architecture test: Rendering.Pixel assembly references no Camera, Units or World assembly; Camera references no mech code.
21. G. With the rig replaced by a stub returning no hints (the future `CockpitRig` case), the pipeline still renders a valid on-palette frame (criterion 1 holds), merely without snapping.
22. G. Footfall impulse (F-009): publishing a `FootContact`-style event on the Core bus (event contract, no mech reference) shakes the camera in proportion to its magnitude, decays within a stated time, is reproducible given the same event, and is bounded (a unit-test cap). A heavy contact adds visible weight to the orbit; recorded as a short sequence where static framing is restored to within one texel after the decay. The impulse does not break criteria 11 and 13 once decayed, and during the impulse the image changes as a whole (parallax and sub-pixel shift), not as dither crawl.

**F. Capture tool and cost**
23. G. Capture tool: one Unity CLI `-executeMethod` batch invocation, runnable headless on macOS Metal, writes `style_capture@v1` exactly as R-002 §6 specifies (color_internal, color_output, palette_index, depth PNG16 log, mask_animated; sidecar with snap block and `dither_anchor`; `sequence.json`; commit hash; palette SHA). Stills for all five profiles and sequences S0, T1-T3, Y1-Y2 plus `haze_cal`. No TAA, motion blur or bloom on `color_internal`; no post-processing outside the engine. A metric run from the T-002 tools (or from the spec, saying which) reproduces the stored numbers.
24. G. Render cost (M-008 render share, relative regression on the Mac, not M-008 sign-off): custom `Pixel.*` passes <= 0.5 ms GPU p95 and present+UI <= 0.5 ms (R-003 §9 budget); frame timing recorded at 1920x1080 and 2560x1440 output; the pipeline does not add per-frame GC allocations after warm-up (Profiler capture shows 0 B in the render loop). M-008 against a target-class Windows PC stays "unverified" (open point 6), and the criterion text must say so.
25. R. Test scene: built by Editor script, contains forms from 10 m to 30 km, ground plane, sky, a gradient-lit surface, a 1.8 m marker and an 80 m block; one orbit sequence of 20 s at about 5 deg/s is saved as a lossless sequence for the player-eye review.

## 2. Which reference panels to match, and in what respect (style only, D-009/D-022; ref_01 = ref_02)
- ref_01A (warm rust/sepia): value structure and limited hue: one dominant hue, darkest nearest, a single lighter haze layer behind; the shading reads as a few hard-edged planes.
- ref_01B and ref_03B (slate teal and slate blue): layered mist; cloud/mist as clustered masses with soft dithered edges, distant layer barely different from sky.
- ref_01C (lavender with peach fire): the vertical light shafts and dark near form against bright far masses; two complementary hue families plus one accent; fire/cloud clusters in 3 to 4 value steps.
- ref_03D and ref_04A (slate with crimson accent): accent used sparingly, small share (0.4%-14%); everything else a restrained cool value ramp.
- ref_05A (warm orange haze): scale: tiny dark figures in the foreground against a giant form, dust as chunky clustered pixel masses, haze lightening the far shapes. Match the clump size of dust, not its subject.
- ref_03A (warm red/orange): silhouette-first small figure against a glowing giant mass; horizon low.
- ref_03C and ref_04B: posterised value and dusk strip: reported only (excluded from profiles in R-002); ref_06A is an outlier and is not a target.
Match: palette family, pixel size (3 to 5 px blocks at delivered size), clustered dither masses, depth ordering, scale cues, restrained accent. Do not match: any subject, character, armour, glyph, caption, logo or text in the panels.

## 3. What would disappoint them
1. A frame that is a photo-realistic game with a palette filter: smooth gradients inside a posterised palette, no clusters, every surface the same texture of noise.
2. Fine uniform dither covering the whole image (DIG > 0.5): reads as noise, not paint. References dither only in gradients, sky and smoke.
3. A visible mismatch of pixel sizes: UI, particles or the mech at a different pixel scale than the world, or pixels that change size with output resolution.
4. A crawling checkerboard on the mech and nearby ground as the camera moves; swimming sky bands during a turn; a skyline that steps one texel at a time across the screen at random intervals during the orbit.
5. Haze that reads as a fog gradient or as hard contour rings; bands that pop in or out as thin objects cross them; far layers darker or same contrast as near ones.
6. A palette that drifts: colours in the render not in the profile, a muddy accent, three accents, or district assets that need code to re-theme.
7. Scale lost: the figure marker vanishing into noise, or the mech block looking toy-sized because haze and contrast are the same at 100 m and 10 km.
8. A camera that snaps in stair-steps during slow rotation, or that feels light (footfall has no impulse) instead of heavy (P1); an impulse that makes the whole image sparkle.
9. Captures produced with TAA, bloom or a blur upscale, or numbers that cannot be reproduced from stored artifacts.
10. Anything that carries a name, shape or text from the reference panels into assets, test scene, file names or shader comments.

## 4. Disagreements and the reading each criterion takes (flag for scribe)
- C1. Dither type: GAME_BREAKDOWN §9 says ordered (Bayer) dithering; R-002 shows references use clustered, irregular dither (O -0.34 to 0.15, DIG 0.09-0.33) and a pure Bayer 4x4 gives DIG 0.70 (above the R-002 advisory cap 0.5); R-003 defaults to Bayer 4x4 with two-candidate mixing. Criteria read: DIG gate (>= 0.15) is gating, O and DIG <= 0.5 advisory; a dither-type switch (Bayer, blue noise, clustered) is allowed as data if it still passes 2. Default per R-002 Q-NEW-a: Bayer for v1.
- C2. Dither anchoring: R-002 says screen- and direction-locked dither cannot go below about 0.245 changed pixels per pixel of lateral translation under perspective and needs surface-anchored dither near the camera; R-003 chooses screen-space dither with a rotation-anchored origin and says surface-anchored patterns moire past a few hundred metres, and expects the far skyline to crawl at 0.05-0.8 texel/s. Criterion 12 keeps the R-002 gate (S_px <= 0.12) as the target; if the implementer's R-003 approach cannot meet it the gate stays and is Assumed pending calibration (open point 2), not weakened silently.
- C3. Supersample blur: R-003 uses 2x supersample plus box downsample before quantise; R-002 rejects blur only after quantisation. Read consistent: criterion 1 and 4 are measured on the final quantised frame.
- C4. Profile count: GAME_BREAKDOWN §9 names four families; R-002 builds five profiles; R-003 sizes 16-24 colours, R-002's profiles are 11-24. Criterion 16 uses R-002's five (open point 3).
- C5. 4K scale: R-002 integer scale 8 at 4K (480x270 constant); R-003 k = clamp(round(H/270),4,6) (640x360 at 4K); GAME_BREAKDOWN says 1/4 to 1/6. Criterion 6 only fixes 1080p and 1440p (open point 4).
- C6. ID collisions: R-002 and R-003 both propose `M-NEW-a`, `M-NEW-b`, `Q-NEW-a..d` with different meanings; scribe must renumber. This draft refers to R-002's M-NEW-a (output smoothness) and M-NEW-b (ramp uniformity) by content, not number.
- C7. Layering: MODULARITY §4.2 reads as the pipeline reading the rig; §3 forbids L1 depending on L3. Criterion 20 reads the contract as living in L1 with the rig providing it (R-003 Q-NEW-c).
- C8. Reference count: ref_01 and ref_02 identical; panels used as in R-002; no founder claim assumed on a sixth reference.

## 6. Open points for the founders (each with a recommended default; Assumed until answered)
1. Dither look: Bayer 4x4 (as §9) vs blue-noise/clustered (as the references measure). Default: Bayer 4x4 for v1, O advisory, include a blue-noise variant as one data switch for the panel.
2. Translation shimmer: accept surface-anchored dither near the camera and direction-locked for sky/far (R-002 default), or accept the R-003 expected residual crawl in the far skyline? Default: surface-anchored near, direction-locked far; keep T gate 0.12 pending first Unity calibration.
3. Profile set: five (R-002) vs four (§9). Default: five.
4. 4K: constant look (k=8, 480x270) vs finer pixels (k=6)? Default: k=clamp(round(H/270),4,6) per R-003 for now; founders choose by looking at a 4K A/B.
5. Rotation quantisation (about 0.2 deg camera steps): keep for stability or drop for smooth feel (P1)? Default: on behind a flag, re-judged by the feel panel.
6. M-008 verification: no Windows target PC is listed. Default: Mac numbers as relative regressions only; M-008 reported "unverified" until a target-class machine exists.
7. Camera impulse envelope (peak size per tonne, decay time): founders' taste. Default: amplitude from the event's impulse scaled and capped at about 0.5 deg and 0.5 m, decay about 1.2 s, reviewed in the feel panel.
8. Light shafts, clustered smoke and outlines: in this task or later? Default: outlines optional/off, shafts and smoke as a later slice; T-004 provides the screen-door coverage material and haze include so they plug in.
