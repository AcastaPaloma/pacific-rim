# T-006 · M0 style frame and founders' checkpoint packet: expectation draft B

Viewpoints. (1) A founder wakes up, opens ONE image and in five seconds decides whether the night was worth it. (2) The same founder an hour later, with the packet, rating about 10 captures per metric and answering a short list of decisions; the packet must be honest, quick and feed the thresholds. IDs: M-003@v1 sub-measures (a palette, b dither, c1/c2 depth bands, d value/hue/grid, e composition advisory, f blind panel), M-004@v1, M-005, D-022/D-027/F-040/D-007. Thresholds are R-002's as written; nothing is relaxed here.

## A. The hero frame: five-second read (the one image)
1. **H1 A giant machine.** At thumbnail size (about 480 px wide) the mech is the dominant near mass, darkest large form, its silhouette reads as an 80 m humanoid machine, and it occupies roughly 40-60% of frame height. Test: `hero_thumb_test` (named artifact: the frame at 480 px and at 96 px beside a same-size decoy of an unrelated object); the evaluator names "giant machine" from the 96 px version without the label.
2. **H2 A real, recognisable city.** Behind the mech the downtown skyline is recognisably San Francisco: one tower clearly tallest (Salesforce, 326 m), a pointed Transamerica spire, Telegraph Hill with its tower, the Bay Bridge tower to the east side of the view, and a diagonal street grid reading. Test: the blind "which city" check with decoys (as T-003 reported item) run on the hero frame, plus the in-frame skyline check (below, H12). A generic block city fails.
3. **H3 Weight and scale.** The mech wades in water at the tile's sea level, water line between ankle and mid-shin of the real seabed depth there; true-scale 1.8 m figures, cars and a small boat stand in the near field and read as 1-2 px people; the towers are visibly taller than the mech's head (Salesforce 326 m vs mech 80 m), never the reverse. Test: `scale_cues_plate` (crop of the hero at 4x with each cue boxed and its true height printed).
4. **H4 Haze.** Visible distance layering: near darkest, each farther layer lighter and lower in contrast, the downtown towers lighter than the mech, the far shore melting into the sky colour with no seam (M-003c1, c2; 30 km band). Low horizon, large sky (sky share about 0.20-0.50, M-003e advisory).
5. **H5 Pixels.** The grid is visible at 1920x1080 on a monitor (480x270 internal, integer 4x), edges are crisp, no soft or uneven pixels; dither is visible in the sky and haze gradients but not as noise across the whole frame (M-003b DIG >= 0.15 and advisory <= 0.5; M-003d-iv grid >= 0.995).
6. **H6 Style identity.** Placed on the review board it can sit as a lesser panel: two or three hues plus one small accent, posterised bands (M-003d: <= 12 value levels, <= 3 hue families; accent 0.1-15% where present). Proxy panel M-003f by `eval-style` with hidden anchors; founders' own rating is the real one. Aim mean >= 8; if lower, say so on the board.
7. **H7 Original.** No subject, design, armour, banner, character or text from the references appears; mech is the T-007 prefab only; placeholder props are plain primitives listed in `placeholder_props.json` (D-009, D-022). No labels or text inside the image.

## B. On closer inspection
8. **H8 Palette discipline (M-003a).** A2 >= 0.995 on `color_internal`, >= 0.95 on `color_output`, in all five profiles; each profile frame uses only its own palette (SHA recorded).
9. **H9 Dither placement.** Dither sits in gradients (sky, haze seams, lit surfaces); the mech's flat plates and the water far field do not carry full-frame noise; transitions interleave, no hard contour bands; with Dither off DIG < 0.15 (control stored).
10. **H10 Depth bands (M-003c1/c2).** Real radial distance, bands monotone out to 30 km; reversed-haze control fails; no band pops on the push-in.
11. **H11 No cheating.** Buildings, terrain, water and mech are not moved, scaled or restyled for the shot; the mech's height is 80 m and standing depth equals the tile's seabed at that point (both printed in `shot_params.json`). No post-processing outside the engine; no hand-painted pixels; no sky-box paintings; same frame reproducible by one CLI command, two runs bit-identical.
12. **H12 Geography honest.** The in-frame skyline mask from the tile's own data (`skyline_inframe_check`) matches the rendered silhouette; landmark pixel positions of Salesforce, Transamerica, Coit and the W2 tower projected from BayTM agree with the frame within 3 px at 1080p; tile-wise M-005 values are quoted for the tile, not recomputed for the shot. `not_applicable_in_tile` sub-measures stay marked so, never passed.
13. **H13 In motion (M-004@v1).** Orbit and push-in sequences reported in each anchoring mode with S_px, C_px, F against the (Assumed) thresholds; misses shown as misses. Static frame S0 exactly 0.
14. **H14 Cost.** Frame cost and draw calls of tile + mech + pipeline reported as relative numbers, hardware named; "M-008 unverified" stated.

## C. Variants
15. **V1** The hero in all five profiles, identical pose, so only palette differs; each with its M-003 report. **V2** Three alternative framings in the default profile: closer (mech chest and head, towers behind), wider (the mech small against the whole skyline), waterfront looking out (a pier edge with the 1.8 m figures, the mech 600 m off). Each must be a legitimate shot: low horizon, scale cues, no geometry intruding. V3 resolutions: 1920x1080 and 2560x1440 both integer scale.
16. The default profile in the hero is the one with best M-003f proxy among the five, chosen by data and stated; the founders' choice stays open (Q-a).

## D. The checkpoint packet (`checkpoints/M0/`)
17. **K1 Index first.** `README.md` opens with the hero image, then 5-7 numbered decisions (E below), then links. A founder can decide in under ten minutes without opening any other file. Status line at the top: what passed, what failed, what is unmerged, in plain words.
18. **K2 Coverage.** About 10 captures per metric: M-003 (style stills), M-004 (short sequences: lossless frame folders + animated preview at integer scale, 8-10 per set), M-005 (tile views, skylines), and M-001/M-002 walk strips from `task/T-005` labelled "unmerged, not at target pace". If a metric has fewer than 10 usable captures, the sheet says how many and why, never padded with duplicates.
19. **K3 Honesty.** Every capture has a caption with: capture ID, what it is, which stage/control produced it (good/bad end), branch and commit hash, tool and command, whether it is merged. Failed and unmerged work is shown in the same style as passed work, with a red "FAILED" or "UNMERGED" tag, never omitted; the proxy metric values are NOT shown beside the stills on rating sheets (so ratings are blind to the numbers); a separate `answer_key.csv` holds the numbers and is opened only after rating.
20. **K4 Spread.** For each metric the set spans clearly good to clearly bad using the pipeline's own controls (quantise off, bilinear upscale, dither off, reversed haze, stabilisers off, haze off, distance-only changes for M-005; the walk strips' real worst case for M-001/M-002). The metric values of the 10 are spread over the range (min, max, gap check: no gap > 30% of the range). Shuffled IDs so order does not reveal rank.
21. **K5 Rating sheets.** One CSV per metric in the format the `Tools/Metrics calibrate` command reads (capture ID, 1-10 score per founder, notes), plus a one-page guide: what to look at, how to score (use the R-002 rubric bands 1-10 for M-003 verbatim), how to run `calibrate`. Test: `calibrate` run on a filled dummy file from a clean checkout produces proposed thresholds without error; a dry-run on a filled example is included.
22. **K6 Ratings to thresholds.** The guide shows the path: ratings file -> `calibrate` -> `proposed_thresholds.json` per metric with the old and new value side by side, the rating-vs-metric correlation (Spearman) and the cut at which the founders' mean crosses 8 (M-003 / M-001 style) -- and the rule that a threshold changes only on founders' signature in the next checkpoint note (recorded as `R-NEW` calibration, never silently). Any metric whose ratings do not correlate with the number (Spearman < 0.6) is flagged "metric does not match taste".
23. **K7 Weight.** Packet within git size budget (~120 MB committed; bulk frames regenerable by one command that is documented).

## 3. What would disappoint them
- A smooth 3D render with a palette filter; a retro-console look; noisy dither over everything; hard contour banding.
- The skyline unrecognisable (a plain block grid, wrong tallest tower); the mech looks like a toy beside cars; scale cues missing or too large; towers shorter than the mech.
- Mech not visibly in water, or floating or sunk; a visible seam or flicker where water meets buildings; sky bands that crawl.
- Flat, even haze; mech and city the same value so the silhouette dies; accent colour everywhere.
- A packet that hides failures, mixes unmerged work without a tag, repeats near-identical captures, shows numbers before ratings, needs the founders to read code, or lists 20 decisions.
- A hero that required hand-painting, a moved building, an invented skyline or an image outside the engine.

## 5. Open points (recommended default)
- Q-a Which palette is the hero? Default: highest-proxy cold slate profile, five shown.
- Q-b Dither look: Bayer (default) versus clustered; keep both selectable.
- Q-c How many founders rate and are anonymous anchors included? Default: both founders, 2 hidden positive and 2 negative anchors per set (R-002).
- Q-d Include walk strips although unmerged? Default yes, labelled.
- Q-e Time of day: one fixed (D-027, Assumed). Default late-afternoon cold haze.
- Q-f Placeholder props: keep simple primitives; flag them for replacement by real assets later.
