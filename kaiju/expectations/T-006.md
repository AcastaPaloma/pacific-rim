# T-006 · M0 style frame and founders' checkpoint packet — reconciled expectation spec (v1)

Reconciled by the orchestrator from `expectations/T-006.A.md` (A, items n) and `expectations/T-006.B.md` (B, items Hn, Vn, Kn, Dn), 2026-11-14. The cited draft items carry the detail (artifact names, test procedures) and are part of this spec. Thresholds are those of `M-003@v1`, `M-004@v1` (`research/R-002.md`) and the T-003 / T-004 / T-007 specs as written; nothing here relaxes them.
The two readers to satisfy (B): a founder who wakes up, opens ONE image and decides in five seconds whether the night was worth it; and the same founder an hour later with the packet, rating about 10 captures per metric and answering a short list of decisions.

## Acceptance criteria
**A. The hero frame at a glance** (default profile, 1920×1080 output, 480×270 internal)
- **C1 A giant machine.** At 480 px and at 96 px wide the mech is the dominant near mass and reads as a humanoid machine; it occupies 40–60% of frame height; an evaluator names it from the 96 px version beside a decoy (`hero_thumb_test`). (B H1, A6)
- **C2 A real, recognisable city.** The downtown skyline behind it reads as San Francisco: one tower clearly tallest (Salesforce), the pointed pyramid, Telegraph Hill with its tower; the blind "which city" check with decoys is run on the hero frame. A generic block city fails. (B H2)
- **C3 Weight and scale.** The mech stands on the real seabed in water at the tile's sea level; the waterline on the legs equals the reported depth at the mech within 0.25 m and falls between ankle and mid-shin unless the real depth forbids it, in which case the real depth wins and is reported; at least three 1.8 m figures, one car and one small boat at measured true size, each 1–3 internal pixels and at least 10 L* from their surround; the towers are visibly taller than the mech; a `scale_cues_plate` boxes each cue with its true size and overlays an 80 m ruler against the 326 m and 260 m verticals. (A8, A9, A10, B H3)
- **C4 Haze and value.** M-003c1 on the real frame (no band drop > 2 L*, Spearman ≥ 0.9, span ≥ 15 L*) and c2 in this scene's configuration; near darkest, towers lighter than the mech, the far shore melting into the sky with no seam; the mech's mean L* is below that of buildings, terrain and sky and at least 10 L* below the frame median. Reported: the share of the darkest 5% of pixels that fall on the mech and near water. Low horizon, sky share 0.20–0.50 (advisory). (A2, A3, B H4)
- **C5 Pixels and dither.** M-003d-iv ≥ 99.5% uniform blocks at 1080p (s = 4) and 1440p (s = 5); M-003b DIG ≥ 0.15 on the sky and on a lit surface, < 0.15 with the Dither stage off; flat regions carry no dither texture; transitions interleave, no contour lines. (A4, A5, B H5, B H9)
- **C6 Palette discipline.** M-003a (A₂ ≥ 0.995 internal, ≥ 0.95 output) and M-003d-i..iii (≤ 12 value levels, ≤ 3 hue families, one accent of 0.1–15% sitting on something small or near, not on the sky); unquantised and bilinear controls of the same view fail on the same tool. (A1, B H6, B H8)
- **C7 Original.** Nothing from the references' subject matter; the mech is the T-007 prefab only; placeholder props are plain primitives listed in `placeholder_props.json`; no text in the image; a banned-terms check on file names. (A19, B H7)

**B. Truth**
- **C8 Nothing moved, scaled, restyled or painted.** The tile loads unmodified (hashes of terrain, buildings and seabed in the scene equal the baked tile's; all scales 1); the mech is the T-007 prefab at scale 1, 80 ± 0.5 m, rest pose, static, standing in water off the downtown waterfront; no post-processing outside the engine, no hand-painted pixels, no painted sky. (A11, A12, B H11)
- **C9 Geography honest.** The rendered skyline matches the mask from the tile's own data at the hero camera (IoU ≥ 0.98) and the projected positions of the named landmarks agree with the frame within 3 px at 1080p; tile-wide `M-005` values are quoted from the tile's report, not recomputed for the shot; not-applicable sub-measures stay marked so. (A11, B H12)
- **C10 Reproducible.** One CLI command rebuilds the scene and every capture; two runs are bit-identical; `shot_params.json` records camera pose in BayTM, field of view, mech position, water depth at the mech, profile, flags, palette SHA, commit; the scene is built by Editor script; no other module's code is edited; committed captures ≤ 120 MB. (A18, B H11)

**C. Variants**
- **C11 Five-profile strip.** The same frame in all five profiles with identical silhouettes; each passes C4–C6 individually. (A13, B V1)
- **C12 Alternative framings** in the default profile: closer, wider, and from the waterfront looking out; each a legitimate shot (low horizon, scale cues) passing C4–C6 and C8–C9; both 1080p and 1440p at integer scale. (A14, B V2)
- **C13 Review board.** The hero beside the same-family reference panels (text and captions cropped from the panels), the strip and the alternatives, file names only; a second copy in shuffled order for cold rating. (A15, B §2)

**D. Motion and cost**
- **C14 Stability clips** (`M-004@v1`): a slow orbit and a slow push-in around the hero framing in each anchoring mode, lossless frames plus an integer-scale preview; values reported per mode against the Assumed thresholds; a miss is shown as a miss; the static frame is exactly stable. (A16, B H13)
- **C15 Frame cost** (relative only, hardware named, "M-008 unverified" stated): tile + mech + pipeline frame time p50/p95 and draw calls at 1080p and 1440p; zero per-frame GC after warm-up. (A17, B H14)

**E. Checkpoint packet (`checkpoints/M0/`)**
- **C16 Index first.** `README.md` opens with a plain-words status line (what passed, what failed, what is unmerged), the hero image, then at most seven numbered decisions each with a recommended default and what changes otherwise: style-frame approval; palette profile; dither look and anchoring; internal resolution; the walk-gate question; the default framing; do the calibration ratings now or later. A founder can act in ten minutes without opening another file. (A22, B K1, B §E)
- **C17 Coverage and honesty.** About 10 captures per metric for M-003, M-004, M-005, plus M-001/M-002 walk strips from branch `task/T-005` labelled "unmerged, not at target pace"; fewer than 10 is stated with the reason, never padded. Every capture has a caption: ID, what it is, which control or stage produced it, branch and commit, tool and command, merged or not; failed and unmerged work carries a visible tag and is never omitted. (A20, B K2, B K3)
- **C18 Blind and spread.** Capture IDs and file names shown to raters reveal no metric value, profile or toggle; an answer-key file holds them and is opened after rating; each set spans clearly good to clearly bad using the pipeline's own controls. Reported: the spread of metric values across each set and how well the intended order matches the metric. (A20, A21, B K3, B K4)
- **C19 Rating sheets and the path to thresholds.** One sheet per metric in the format `Tools/Metrics calibrate` reads, with the R-002 rubric bands quoted for M-003; a one-page guide; a dry run of `calibrate` on a filled dummy sheet from a clean checkout succeeds and its output is included; the guide states that a threshold changes only on the founders' sign-off, recorded as a calibration entry, and that a metric whose ratings do not track its number is flagged. (A21, B K5, B K6)

## What would disappoint the founders
The union of A §3 and B §3: a smooth render with a palette filter, a retro-console look, or dither noise everywhere; the mech not the heaviest, darkest mass, or toy-like beside the props; a skyline that is not recognisably the city, towers shorter than the mech; a floating or sunk mech, no waterline, a seam at the shore; missing or oversized scale cues; mushy pixels or contour bands; a hero whose numbers are missing or that cannot be rebuilt by one command; a packet that hides failures, shows numbers before ratings, repeats near-identical captures, or buries the decisions.

## Evidence required (`evidence/T-006/` and `checkpoints/M0/`)
The union of A §4 and B §4: `EVIDENCE.md` (criterion → artifact, command, commit); the hero set in `style_capture@v1` with `M-003@v1` reports and the negative controls; `hero_thumb_test`; `scale_cues_plate` and the as-built heights; the waterline close-up and depth report; `placeholder_props.json`; the tile-hash equality log; the in-frame skyline overlay and landmark projections; the five-profile strip, alternatives and both board copies; the orbit and push-in sets with `M-004@v1` reports per mode; timing and draw calls; the two-run hash table; `shot_params.json`; test results; and the packet files with the dry-run `calibrate` log, answer key and caption table.

## Decisions taken at reconciliation (Assumed)
- Hero palette: `slate_teal` unless the style evaluator's proxy scores clearly favour another cold profile; the founders choose from the strip.
- Light: one fixed low sun through haze from the side or behind, so the mech reads as a dark mass (D-027). The drafts differ only on morning versus late afternoon, which a still cannot distinguish beyond sun direction.
- Stand position: the water position off the downtown waterfront with the best line of sight to the skyline within real wading depth; the depth is reported, never edited.
- The pipeline configuration is the T-004 default (Bayer 4×4, hybrid anchoring, 480×270 at 1080p); alternatives appear in the packet.
