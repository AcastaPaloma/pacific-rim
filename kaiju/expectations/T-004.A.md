# T-004 · Expectation draft A (expect-A): pixel pipeline + third-person rig stabilisation

Written before anything is built, from the brief, GAME_BREAKDOWN §9/§10, MODULARITY §4.2/§4.7, R-002 (`M-003@v1`, `M-004@v1`, `palette_set@v1`, `style_capture@v1`) and R-003. Thresholds marked *(Assumed)* are R-002/R-003 proposals pending calibration on the first Unity capture set. All evidence is judged from files in `evidence/T-004/`. "Test scene" = the neutral style test scene (brief scope 5).

## 1. Acceptance criteria (gating unless marked advisory)

**A. Palette and pixel grid (M-003a, d-iv)**
1. For each of the five profiles (`warm_rust_sepia`, `warm_amber_haze`, `slate_teal`, `lavender_peach`, `slate_crimson`) on the test scene, from the three viewpoints (street, ~70 m, ~300 m; ≥12 frames each): M-003a A₂ ≥ 0.995 on `color_internal` and ≥ 0.95 on `color_output` (CIEDE2000 ≤ 2.0 to the declared profile), and the `palette_index` 255-count equals the internal failures. Sky, haze, fire-like emissive and the gradient surface all count; nothing leaks off-palette.
2. Grid integrity (d-iv): ≥ 99.5% of s×s blocks of `color_output` uniform at the declared `output_block_offset_px`, at 1080p (s=4) and 1440p. A bilinear or blurred upscale must fail this test (negative control shipped in evidence).
3. Internal resolution: 1080p output renders at exactly 480×270 (scale 4); 1440p uses integer scale (512×288); no non-integer scale at any tested size. The 4K choice is flagged in §4 and is reported, not gated.
4. Posterisation (d-i, d-ii, d-iii): ≤ 12 value levels, ≤ 3 hue families per frame; if the profile has an accent, its share is 0.1–15% where present (advisory).

**B. Dither (M-003b)**
5. DIG ≥ 0.15 on ≥ 80% of evaluated gradient frames (the lit gradient surface, sky, haze seams). Pipeline with Dither toggled off must score DIG < 0.15 on the same frames (proves the metric sees the stage). DIG ≤ 0.5 and ordered index O are advisory and reported per frame (see §4: Bayer scores ~0.70).
6. No banding without dither in gradients: the gradient-lit large surface and the sky show interleaved transitions between adjacent palette steps, not hard contour lines, in A/B strip and in `palette_index` crops (evaluator-visible at 4x zoom).

**C. Depth haze and value bands (M-003c)**
7. c1 on the test scene with forms from 10 m to 30 km (bands at least 0–50, 50–150, 150–400, 400–1000, 1–2.5, 2.5–6 km, plus 6–15 and 15–30 km added beyond R-002's list): per-band mean L\* has no drop > 2, Spearman ρ ≥ 0.9, far-minus-near span ≥ 15 L\*. Bands follow real radial distance: moving a form 2x farther lightens it, independent of its screen position or size.
8. c2 `haze_cal` scene (dark/light pairs at 200–8000 m): ΔL\* per pair non-increasing (+0.5), 8 km ≤ 50% of 200 m, pair mean L\* non-decreasing. Ramp uniformity: CV of ΔL\* between consecutive haze ramp colours ≤ 0.35 *(Assumed)*, using the profile's uniform ramp.
9. Beyond ~15–30 km everything merges to the sky/horizon band; sky has a posterised, dithered gradient whose horizon colour equals the far-band colour (no visible seam line). Near layers (≤ 150 m) are the darkest per §9.
10. Silhouettes: the 80 m block and the 1.8 m marker are readable in every profile. The block differs from its background by ≥ 1 value band at 150 m and 1 km; the marker (at 150 m, ≥ 2 px tall at 480×270) has ΔL\* ≥ 10 to its local background. Frame silhouette contrast L\*₉₀−L\*₁₀ ≥ 25 (advisory).

**D. Temporal stability (M-004@v1; sequences S0, T1–T3, Y1–Y2, 180 frames, 3 profiles × 2 viewpoints)**
11. Static: S_px = C_px = F = 0 exactly with sim paused, in every profile (no temporal noise anywhere in the chain).
12. Yaw (Y1, Y2): S_px ≤ 0.01, C_px ≤ 0.01, F ≤ 0.001.
13. Translation (T1, T2): S_px ≤ 0.12, C_px ≤ 0.15, F ≤ 0.005; T3 (1 px/frame): S_px ≤ 0.12, C_px ≤ 0.15, F ≤ 0.08. All *(Assumed)*; if a number is missed the evidence reports the achieved value and the stabilisation flags tried (§4 item c).
14. Stabilisation pays for itself: each of the flagged stabilisers (position snap, rotation quantise, dither anchoring) turned off must measurably worsen S_px or F on the matching sequence (A/B numbers stored). Output motion smoothness (R-002 M-NEW-a, advisory): with sub-pixel compensation on, per-frame `color_output` displacement matches the commanded motion within ±1 output px on T1/Y1.

**E. Stages, profiles, rig, capture, cost**
15. Each stage (low-res target + point upscale, haze, supersample/downsample, outline, dither, quantise, present) is a separate renderer feature. Test: toggling any one off via config (no code change) yields a valid frame (no black/magenta/NaN, on-palette where Quantise is on); with Quantise off A₂ must fail, with Haze off c1 must fail, with Dither off DIG < 0.15, with Outline on/off differs only at edges. A/B strip of all toggles in evidence. Default flags are off; the M0 style-frame config switches them on through config.
16. Reorderable: an EditMode test over the installer permutes the feature list; legal orders produce identical-valid frames, illegal ones (Quantise before Haze, Present not last, internal stage before downsample) are rejected or warned, never silently produce garbage. Renderer assets are created by Editor script (both parallel feature lists kept in sync); existing `PC_RPAsset` untouched (diff shows no change).
17. Palette profiles are data: five `PaletteProfile` assets generated from `palette_set@v1` by an Editor script, each with ramp, body colours, accent, haze parameters and LUT baked from it; the sidecar carries the profile SHA-256. Editing one hex in the data and re-running the baker changes the capture and nothing else; adding a sixth profile by data only passes criteria 1–2 with no code change. EditMode tests: LUT bake, nearest-colour maths (engine-free, `dotnet test`), Bayer matrix values.
18. Rig contract: `ICameraRig` + `ThirdPersonRig` follow a moving 80 m target without Camera referencing Units/mech code (asmdef check); at default framing the 80 m block fills roughly half the frame height with a low horizon and large sky *(Assumed)*. The rig supplies stabilisation hints (snapped position, quantised rotation, sub-texel offset, dither origin, reference depth, `cut` flag) through a contract in `Rendering.Pixel.Contracts`, so Rendering.Pixel (L1) never references Camera (L3). A camera cut/teleport resets grid and dither origin with no one-frame garbage. A rig returning "no hints" (future `CockpitRig`) leaves the pipeline working unsnapped.
19. Camera impulse: subscribing to the Core bus, a footfall-style event (by event contract only) produces a visible camera displacement that scales with event magnitude, decays within a bounded time, and never breaks snapping or palette adherence (M-004 sequence with impulses stays within criteria 12–13). Test publishes events with no mech code present.
20. Capture tool: runs under Unity CLI `-executeMethod` without `-nographics`, on macOS Metal, and writes valid `style_capture@v1` (all required keys, sidecar fields, `sequence.json`, rules 1–6: same frame, no TAA/bloom/motion blur, UI off, lossless, scripted camera, commit hash). A schema validator in evidence passes every sidecar; two runs of the same sequence are bit-identical; depth PNG16-log decodes to known test-scene distances within 1%.
21. Blind panel (M-003f): mean ≥ 8.0 over ≥ 12 captures, no profile mean < 7, with positive and negative anchors, ≥ 8 raters. This gates the milestone "founders approve the style frame".
22. Render cost: `Pixel.*` passes ≤ 0.5 ms GPU p95 and present+UI ≤ 0.5 ms *(Assumed)*; frame timing recorded at 1920×1080 and 2560×1440 with per-pass breakdown. Mac numbers are relative only; M-008 is explicitly not claimed until Windows hardware exists.
23. Module hygiene: `MODULE.md` for Rendering.Pixel and Camera; contracts-only dependencies; all tuning values in assets/config; EditMode and a capture/PlayMode test results XML pass; zero compile errors and no unlogged shader warnings in the capture log.

## 3. What would disappoint them
- A generic posterise/colour-reduce filter over a smooth 3D render: right palette, but gradients, glow and smoke still read as a filtered photo.
- A retro-console look (uniform hard 8-bit noise, CRT/scanline tricks, saturated primaries) instead of a painted, limited-hue, hazy picture.
- Banding with hard contour lines in sky, haze seams or the lit surface; or dither covering whole frames so it reads as noise.
- Dither that swims or crawls when the camera yaws or the mech walks; pixels that boil on static frames; far skyline shimmer that is visible at normal viewing speed.
- Haze that ignores distance (screen-position or object-size based), inverted (far darker than near), or that makes the 80 m block vanish at 1 km.
- A mushy upscale: bilinear or non-integer scale, uneven pixel sizes, half-pixel edges, output that is not exactly on-palette.
- Camera that stair-steps visibly (snap without sub-pixel compensation), lags the mech noticeably, or feels floaty rather than heavy (P1).
- Stages that cannot be disabled/reordered, or a palette change that needs a code edit. Any franchise-derived name, shape or text in the test scene, profiles or code (D-009, D-022).
- Evidence that only shows pretty stills with no stored metric values.

## 4. Where sources disagree, and the reading used (flags)
a. **Dither type.** §9 says ordered Bayer; R-002 finds the references clustered or irregular (O −0.34…+0.15) and notes Bayer 4×4 scores DIG 0.70, above its own advisory cap 0.5. Reading: Bayer 4×4 is the default; criterion 5 gates only DIG ≥ 0.15; DIG ≤ 0.5 and O are advisory; the profile data must let a blue-noise or clustered mask be swapped in, and the panel compares them. Flag: the "exactly like the references" intent (D-022) may prefer a non-Bayer mask.
b. **Dither anchoring.** R-002 wants direction-locked dither for sky/far and surface-anchored for near (needed for translation S_px ≤ 0.12); R-003 recommends screen-space dither with a rotation-driven origin and warns surface-anchored patterns moiré beyond a few hundred metres. Reading: the thresholds in criteria 11–13 are R-002's, as written; the implementer picks the anchoring, but must meet them or report the achieved value. Flag: R-002's own synthetic data say screen-space dither cannot go below ~0.245 under translation, so criterion 13 is the likeliest miss.
c. **Shimmer thresholds.** R-002 M-004 (F ≤ 0.001 yaw, ≤ 0.005 translation; S_px, C_px) versus R-003's proposed M-NEW-b (ABA ≤ 1% translation, ≤ 2% rotation, ≥ 50% reduction vs stabilisers off). Reading: R-002 is the registered `M-004@v1` and gates; R-003's ≥ 50% reduction is kept as criterion 14. Both M-NEW-a/M-NEW-b labels collide across the two documents; use the scribe's numbering. Likely miss (c) lets evaluators decide between fixing and relaxing; recommend relax-with-evidence over silent acceptance.
d. **Internal resolution.** §9: 1/4–1/6 of output; R-002: 480×270 at 1080p, scale 8 at 4K, 320×180 as coarse bound; R-003: k = clamp(round(H/270), 4, 6), so 4K gets 640×360. Reading: criterion 3 gates 1080p (480×270) and 1440p (k=5) only; 4K is reported, and R-002's scale 8 would break §9's 1/6 bound.
e. **Haze band spacing.** R-003 posterises transmittance in equal steps (physical edges); R-002 wants uniform-L\* ramps (M-NEW-b CV ≤ 0.35) and fixed distance bands only to 6 km. Reading: criteria 7–8 test outcomes (monotone L\*, contrast falloff) to 30 km, not the band formula; both specs can hold if per-band palettes use the uniform ramp.
f. **Profile count.** §9 lists four families, R-002 five (adds `warm_amber_haze`). Reading: five.
g. **Layering.** MODULARITY §4.2 reads as the pipeline reading the rig; §3 forbids L1 depending on L3. Reading: the hints contract lives in `Rendering.Pixel.Contracts` (R-003); the rig provides it.
h. **Duplicate reference.** ref_01 and ref_02 are identical; both research notes treat the set as five images. Reading: same.
