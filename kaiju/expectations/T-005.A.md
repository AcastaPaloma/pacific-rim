# T-005 expectation draft A: one mech body that walks with physics and intent control

Author: `expect-A`. Predicts what the founders want before anything is built. Sources: GAME_BREAKDOWN §1/§4/§5/§10, MODULARITY, `tasks/T-005.md`, `research/R-001.md` (M-001@v1, M-002@v1, gait_log@v1), `research/R-005.md` (M-006@v1, physics_log@v1, mech_frame@v1). All thresholds quoted as written there. Scenario: `walk_scn`, the T-005 headless scenario (stand 5 s, walk to steady state, turn 90°, walk, stop). Header values mean the values in the log header, read from the mech's config.

## 1. Acceptance criteria

**Walk reads as thousands of tonnes (P1)**
1. M-001.1: steady-walk Fr = v̄²/(g l) in [0.10, 0.30] for every walk command, cruise within ±0.03 of `fr_cruise_nom` (0.20), never above 0.35 in any capture. Test: `walk_fr010/020/030` captures, metric tool report.
2. M-001.2/.3: stride period within [0.85, 1.15] of 2.3 Fr^-0.2 sqrt(l/g); duty factor in [0.60, 0.72] (double support 20-44%). Stride boundaries are successive same-foot `contact_begin` events. About 18 steps/min at cruise; a human-rate cadence is a fail.
3. M-001.4/.5/.6: peak CoM fore-aft acceleration at most 0.30 g (all captures incl. `estop`), lateral at most 0.085 g, net start acceleration at most 0.14 g and at most the power limit.
4. M-001.7/.8/.9: peak yaw rate at most 26.7 deg/s (scaled by sqrt(42.4/l) for other sizes), peak yaw acceleration at most 4.0 deg/s², yaw rate while v̄ > 0.5 v_cruise at most 0.085 g / v̄ (5.3 deg/s at cruise).
5. M-001.10/.11/.12: start 0 to 90% cruise takes 2-4 steps (6.6-13.2 s); normal stop to under 0.3 m/s takes 1.5-3 steps (4.9-9.9 s) with visible lean; emergency stop at least max(3.1 s, m v / F_brake,max).
6. M-001.13 panel: n >= 3, median overall >= 8/10, no scenario median <= 6, on anonymised frame strips and real-time clips (R-001 §7). Evaluator tells to be absent: skating, snap turns, weightless contacts, slow-motion look.
7. Weight is in the body: CoM leads each step and is caught by the landing foot; stance foot stays pinned. Test: `walk_scn` side strip plus `com_p` vs stance-foot `p` plots in the metric report.

**Physics is honest (P2)**
8. No kinematic cheats. Root is never animated, teleported or velocity-set (except floating-origin shifts, logged); feet are not pinned by script; no artificial stabilising force/torque. Test: static check of the implementer's declared list in `EVIDENCE.md`, plus `physics_log@v1` `external_work` = 0 for every locomotion capture (M-006.7 "no step with dE > +0.01 m g H without logged external work"). Evaluators can verify from logs without reading code.
9. Any balance assistance is declared with magnitude and a flag, default off: peak and RMS N and N·m, which joint or body, in which scenarios, and what fraction of ankle forceLimit (1.0e9 N·m) it is. If the walk passes only with assistance on, the verdict says so and reports the metric table both ways.
10. M-006.1 unit sanity: link masses sum to config total within 0.1%, g = (0,-9.81,0), damping 0, physics step and solver settings read back and logged. Config mass total is 10,000 t at 80 m, hull density in [500, 2500] kg/m³ (warn under 1,025).
11. M-006.2 energy drift (passive drop 20 m: loss at most 1.3%, never a gain over 0.1%; pendulum drift at most 3% over 10 periods). M-006.3 momentum (|dp|/|p0| at most 0.5%, |dL|/|L0| at most 2%, KE never increases).
12. M-006.4 ground pressure: double stance each foot 327 kPa ±5%, sum m g ±1%; single stance 654 kPa ±2% (§4 "≈ 650 kPa"); walking vertical impulse per stride = m g T ±2%, peak 1.0-2.0 BW.
13. M-006.5 fall energy: locked mech tilted 5° released: t_fall 8.2 s ±10%, impact omega 0.57 rad/s ±5%, KE_impact = dPE ±3%, total released about 3.6 GJ ±10%. M-006.6 power/torque: |tau| never above forceLimit, saturated steps at most 5% per joint, mean positive power 30-120 MW, peak at most 450 MW, standing mean at most 5 MW. M-006.7 no NaN, no link over 60 m/s. M-006.8 repeatability (see 20).

**Intent control (D-013, F-002)**
14. The pilot supplies only an `IntentFrame` (move, target, effort). The scenario script contains no joint targets or forces. Walk to a target point stops on it: final CoM within one step length of the target (stop planned from stopping distance, M-001.11), logged `intent` vs `com_p`.
15. Intent beyond limits is carried out within limits, never refused silently and never obeyed unphysically: a command for 3x cruise speed, an instant reversal, a 180° heading snap or a stop in 1 step yields the nearest feasible motion (M-001.4-.12 still hold) and an `intent_change`/limit-clamp record saying what was clipped and why (torque, power, traction, tipping). Test: `intent_over_limit` scenario, all M-001 sub-measures still pass on it.
16. Intent beyond what the body can survive (target needing a step outside the reachable region) ends in a controlled fall or a refused move, not in a silent teleport or an explosion (M-006.7).
17. Start, stop and turn: standing to walk is a weight shift then a first step (no step velocity jump); turning is by foot placement, never by spinning a planted foot (M-002 holds during `turn90_inplace`, 90° in at least 6.7 s); `curve` capture passes M-001.9.

**Footfall event, falling, slip**
18. F-009 (M0 part): a `FootContact` event is published on the Core bus in the same physics step as the first step with Fn >= F_on (0.03 m g), carrying foot ID, position, normal, vertical impulse, contact pressure. Same-step in at least 99% of contacts, at most 1 step in 100%; exactly one event per contact (no misses, no duplicates). Test: a test subscriber logs `source_n`, compared with `contact_begin` n; also exercised through T-004's camera hook or `cam_impulse` stub.
19. M-002.1-.5: worst slip per contact < 0.005 L_f (header foot length) in every locomotion capture and `stomp`; p95 < 0.0025 L_f; every step logged at dt <= 0.02 s, float64; contact flag agrees with F_on/F_off hysteresis on at least 99% of steps; zero excluded contacts. Worst-5-contacts table with ids is reported.
20. Determinism: scripted 60 s sequence run 3x on one machine gives CoM divergence at most 1 cm at 60 s and identical footfall step indices (M-006.8); same `gait_log` within tolerance on repeat.
21. Losing balance: forced push above the ankle strategy (0.3 m g for 2 s while standing) triggers a capture step; a push beyond recoverability, or an over-limit intent, produces a fall under physics only (no scripted ragdoll switch, no kinematic collapse), with `fall_start`/`fall_end` events, and no explosion. Fall matches criterion 13 for a limp topple within the stated tolerances. Get-up, brace and wading are not built, and nothing blocks them (hook points documented).

**Architecture and data**
22. D-003: `IPilotSource`, `IntentFrame`, `IIntentArbiter`, `PilotSeat` exist per MODULARITY §4.1, with `SinglePilotArbiter` only. EditMode test: a seat with 2 and with 3 scripted pilots and a test-only arbiter works without touching Units.Mech or Physics.Body. A scripted and a device (Input System) pilot source both exist.
23. D-004: PlayMode test spawns N mechs in one scene (N = 1, 2, 4), each with its own seat, config instance, event IDs, log; none hard-codes 2; a scripted walk of each matches the single-mech run (M-001/M-002 per unit) and no cross-talk (events carry `unit_id`).
24. Config-driven: masses, inertias, joint limits, drive gains, ROM, foot size, power, limits live in config assets under `Assets/Game/Config/` (mech_frame@v1 data). Test: change total mass to 6,000 t and 15,000 t and height to 76/88 m by config only; load succeeds, M-006.1 passes, start time rises with mass when power binds (M-001.12 across at least 2 mass variants). Zero numeric tuning constants in behaviour code (review by `eval-arch`).
25. Modules per MODULARITY §5: Physics.Body (L1) does not reference Control or Units; Control and Units.Mech talk to it only through `*.Contracts` and the Core bus; each module has an asmdef, `MODULE.md`, feature flag default off, EditMode and PlayMode test assemblies; pure logic engine-free under `dotnet test`. Part-name to segment mapping exposed so T-007's model attaches without code change.
26. Logs: `gait_log@v1` and `physics_log@v1` pass the tool's validation (header complete, n contiguous, t = n·dt, feet match header, every `cam_impulse.source_n` exists); invalid log = fail, never pass.

## 3. What would disappoint them

- Gait looks like a person in a suit: fast cadence, bouncy hips, feet landing quickly, over 60 steps/min. Equally bad: slow-motion or underwater, floating CoM, no weight transfer.
- Start or stop that snaps: speed reaching cruise in one step, or stopping dead. Missing lean into starts and stops.
- Turning by sliding or pivoting a planted foot, or the whole body rotating faster than the feet can be placed.
- Footfalls with no weight: no visible settling, the camera-impulse hook not firing on the contact step (late by a frame or two), amplitude not tied to stomp strength.
- A mech that stands because a hidden stabiliser holds it: no sway, never falls, falls only by scripted ragdoll, or a topple that takes 1.2 s (human time) instead of about 8 s.
- A mech that explodes, jitters, vibrates at rest, creeps across the floor on its own, or has leg gains so stiff it looks rigid-bodied.
- Feet skating by a few decimetres per step ("skating stride", fast leg cycle versus speed).
- Intent that either ignores limits (instant 180°) or refuses with no feedback so the player does not know why.
- A walk that works at 10,000 t and breaks at 6,000 or 15,000 t, or only with magic numbers in code.
- Hard-coded two mechs, two feet or one pilot in a way that blocks D-003/D-004.
- Evidence that cannot be reproduced from a command, or logs of different runs than the frame strips.

## 4. Evidence the implementer must produce (in `evidence/T-005/`, all with command and commit)

- `gait/*.gait.jsonl` for `walk_fr010/020/030`, `start`, `stop`, `estop`, `turn90_inplace`, `curve`, `stomp`, `intent_over_limit`, `walk_scn`; `physics/*.physics.jsonl` for M-006 scenes (drop, pendulum, two-body impact, standing pressure, single stance, toppling fall, limp fall, 60 s walk, 60 s determinism x3, origin at x = 2 km).
- Metric reports for every sub-measure of M-001@v1 (13), M-002@v1 (5), M-006@v1 (8): value, threshold, margin, verdict; footfall-sync table (step latency per contact); assisted vs unassisted runs if any assistance exists.
- Frame strips (PNG contact sheets): side and three-quarter views, at least 2 full gait cycles (10 frames per stride, 3 strides) with a scale marker and human-scale props; start, stop, turn, fall strips; one strip showing the over-limit intent response.
- Test XML (EditMode, PlayMode, `dotnet test`); N-mech test log; config-swap test (6,000 / 10,000 / 15,000 t, 76 / 88 m) with metric table.
- `EVIDENCE.md` with artifact, command, commit; a "declared assistance and cheats" section (even if "none"); a "what is not built" list (brace, wade, get-up).
- Built-in check that the F-009 test subscriber saw every `FootContact` and the camera hook received it in the same step.

## 5. Open points for the founders (recommended default)

- **Q-A1** Cruise Fr 0.20 (R-001) or 0.25 (R-005 design point)? Default: 0.20 cruise, band 0.10-0.30, Froude-sweep captures decide at review.
- **Q-A2** Foot shape: 19.6 × 7.65 m or 15 × 10 m at 150 m²? Default: whichever the shipped config uses, with M-002 scaled to header L_f; keep the 150 m² area.
- **Q-A3** Turning on the move: accept the strict 5.3 deg/s cap (a 90° wheel on the move takes about 17 s)? Default: yes for M0; revisit from `curve` captures. It may feel unresponsive.
- **Q-A4** If full dynamic balance is not reached in the cycle, accept declared assistance for M0 (default: yes, if flagged, magnitude reported, and below ankle forceLimit, and not applied in the fall scenarios). Default: assistance may not mask a fall; it must be zero in `stomp` and toppling tests.
- **Q-A5** Panel: the founders score at least `walk_fr020` and the start/stop strips themselves when awake. Default: agent panel gates the milestone, founder score recorded as `Assumed` until given.
- **Q-A6** Reactor power for M0 (200 vs 300 MW) only sets the power ceiling. Default: 300 MW per R-005, limit recomputed from header; not blocking.
- **Q-A7** Light/tall mech corners float (hull density under 1,025). Default: not tested in M0; config load warns.
