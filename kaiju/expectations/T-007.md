# T-007 · Original mech frame (Balanced chassis) — reconciled expectation spec (v1)

Reconciled by the orchestrator from `expectations/T-007.A.md` (A, items AC-n) and `expectations/T-007.B.md` (B, items n), 2026-11-14. Detail in the cited draft items (measurement conventions, test procedures, evidence file contents) is part of this spec. Where the drafts differed the stricter or more specific outcome was taken, as noted.
Conventions (A): H = rest-pose height, sole to top of head; widths on the front orthographic silhouette in rest pose; "L" is the mech's left.
**Single source of dimensions:** `research/R-005/mech_frame_balanced.json` (`mech_frame@v1`). R-005 wins over any window below for joint positions and segment lengths; every conflict is reported in `EVIDENCE.md`, never resolved silently.

## Acceptance criteria
**Originality (D-009, D-022)**
- **C1 Prompt hygiene.** No franchise, studio, artist, character, product or named-design term in any prompt or in `PROVENANCE.md`; a banned-terms check is stored and the evaluator also reads every prompt. (AC-1, B20)
- **C2 No uploads.** No style reference or third-party image was uploaded or used as an image prompt; only this task's own earlier outputs, cited by job ID. (AC-2)
- **C3 Blind resemblance test.** From front, side, 3/4 renders and the small silhouettes, eval-style cannot name an existing giant-robot design that the head, chest, shoulders, colour scheme or silhouette recalls; nothing from the subjects of the style references (horns, helms, capes, fur, tusks, banners, riders). (AC-3, B19)
- **C4 No genre-signature identifiers.** No V-shaped crest or antenna fins, no glowing round chest core, no human-like face, no flag or racing-stripe livery, no emblem, text or number. (AC-4, B19)
- **C5 Own identity.** A design note names 2–3 signature forms in plain words; each is findable in the turnaround and on the greybox at 48 px. (AC-5)

**Silhouette at pixel scale (P4, M-003)**. Test per A: orthographic front/side/3/4, black on white, nearest-neighbour to H = 180, 48, 24 px.
- **C6** 48 px separations as AC-6; **C7** 24 px humanoid-giant read with blind decoy test as AC-7; **C8** 180 px flat-palette plate breakup reads as 3–5 value bands and no feature under 1 m carries the read (AC-8, B11); **C9** machine, not a scaled person: IoU with a same-height human silhouette ≤ 0.75 (AC-9).

**Heavy, heroic proportions (D-008, P1)**, all measured in Blender and reported in `dimensions.json`:
- **C10 Height and markers.** H = 80.0 ± 0.5 m; shoulder line at 62–70 m; the 67 m deck marker crosses the upper chest; the 1.8 m human stands at the foot and the foot is ≥ 7× its height in length. (AC-10, B9, B10)
- **C11 Upper body.** Shoulder width / H = 0.30–0.40; shoulder / hip width = 1.4–2.0; head height / H = 0.05–0.09 and head width / shoulder width ≤ 0.30; forearm max width ≥ 1.1 × upper-arm max width and ≥ fist width; fist width / H ≥ 0.055 with a flat striking face ≥ 6 m². (AC-11, B3, B8)
- **C12 Legs and stance.** Leg length / H = 0.44–0.53; thigh minimum width ≥ 0.10 H and shank ≥ 0.08 H, never thinner mid-length than at the joints; foot length / H = 0.16–0.21, foot width / H = 0.10–0.14, foot width ≥ 1.2 × shank width; stance / shoulder width = 0.70–0.90. (AC-12, B1, B3; B's thigh width is the stricter)
- **C13 Mass where the physics says.** Volume-weighted centroid height within ± 3% H of the R-005 CoM height (fallback window 0.50–0.58 H); on the mid-plane (L/R volume difference < 1%). (AC-13, B2)
- **C14 Feet for ground pressure.** Sole contact area per foot 150 m² ± 10% from the sole polygon, L/R within 1%; flat sole coplanar with the ground (± 0.05 m); length/width 1.3–2.2; ankle centre above the sole within 15% of foot length of the sole centroid; the report prints m·g/A per foot. No heels, claws or narrow toes. (AC-16, B5)
- **C15 Believable joints.** Each of hip, knee, ankle, shoulder, elbow, wrist, neck has a housing form containing the pivot; knees and elbows show an actuator form spanning the joint; continuous load path shoulder → hip. (B6)
- **C16 Panel read.** On the turnaround with scale markers: "thousands of tonnes, heroic, upright; not lanky, hunched or toy-like" ≥ 8/10. (AC-14, B1; A's bar taken to match M-001)

**True scale and mass consistency (D-007, D-008, M-006)**
- **C17** 1 unit = 1 m, scales applied, H in Unity equals H in Blender within 1 cm. (AC-15)
- **C18** Every joint position and segment length within 2% of `mech_frame@v1`, per joint with deviation; part origins at the proximal joint centre within 0.25 m; L/R mirrored within 1 cm. (AC-17, AC-20, AC-22, B14)
- **C19** Every part closed and manifold; total volume 5,000–10,000 m³; implied mass at the config density inside 6–15 kt; per-segment volume fraction within ± 30% of the R-005 mass fraction (segments under 2% exempt), deviations explained. (AC-18, B4)

**Rigid parts for physics and later damage (F-001, D-025)**
- **C20** Exactly 15 rigid parts, one naming scheme identical in `.blend`, `.fbx`, Unity hierarchy and mapping asset; nothing else exported except named helper empties; kinematic tree pelvis → torso → head / upper arm → forearm → hand, pelvis → thigh → shank → foot. (AC-19, AC-20, B12, B13, B15)
- **C21 Range of motion.** Both tests: the four poses of AC-21 (non-adjacent penetration ≤ 0.5 m, no daylight through a joint at 48 px) and the per-joint ranges of B7 (R-005 ranges override; neighbour overlap ≤ 2% of the smaller part).
- **C22 Sockets.** Named socket transforms at each shoulder, hip and forearm hardpoint, L/R identical in local geometry; a swap test replaces one forearm through socket/mapping data alone. (B16)
- **C23 Colliders derivable.** Convex-hull volume / mesh volume ≤ 1.6 per part, or ≤ 3 named convex pieces. (B17)
- **C24 Budget.** ≤ 20,000 triangles, no textures, ≤ 4 flat material slots named by palette role (primary, secondary, joint, accent). (AC-23, B23)

**Concept layers and provenance (D-023)**
- **C25** Four layers kept as files with prompt sheet and `PROVENANCE.md` (tool, exact prompt, parameters, job ID or link, date, selection rationale, AI-generated: yes); layer 3 shows plates, seams and which plates belong to which segment. (AC-24, B18, B21, B22)
- **C26** ≤ 16 generations in total, counted; no purchase, settings change or terms acceptance; a block is recorded with what, when and the fallback, and Phases B and C are still delivered in full. (AC-25, B21)
- **C27 Traceability.** Greybox front and side silhouettes overlaid on the chosen turnaround at equal H show the same major masses (front IoU reported, ≥ 0.6 expected); intentional departures listed with reasons. (AC-26, B22)
- **C28 Mood frame style check.** The §2 check of draft A: ≤ 3 hue families + one accent ≤ 10% of pixels, sky lighter than the mech, water line at ankle to mid-shin, tiny true-scale foreground objects. (A §2, B §2)

**Unity import**
- **C29** No import errors or warnings; Y-up, faces +Z, identity root rotation, all scales 1, H = 80 m. (AC-27, B23)
- **C30** Prefab, import settings and mapping asset made by an Editor script; a second run leaves `git diff` empty; all `.meta` committed; `.blend`/`.fbx` in LFS. The prefab holds meshes, sockets and the mapping only: colliders and ArticulationBody belong to T-005. (AC-28, A Q-g, B23)
- **C31** Mapping asset: 15 unique entries resolving to prefab transforms, IDs spelled as in `mech_frame@v1`; a named EditMode test checks mapping, hierarchy, H, joints against config, axes/scale, socket symmetry and the swap test. Dimensions are read from config, not hard-coded; `Units.Mech` `MODULE.md` lists the assets and IDs. (AC-29, AC-30, B13)

## Style references (style only)
As A §2 and B §2, which agree: cold slate-teal / blue-grey bay fog (ref_01 top-right, ref_03 top-right) with one warm accent; the near giant is the darkest mass against a lighter hazier sky; low horizon; tiny true-scale foreground objects; posterised flat value bands; ordered dither in sky, fog and water.

## What would disappoint the founders
The union of A §3 and B §3: anything recalling a famous robot or the reference characters; genre cliché first impression; lanky or toy-like; a blob at 24–48 px; greeble noise or a featureless mannequin; any faked scale; one fused mesh or wrong pivots; joints that are two boxes touching; a mood frame whose mech differs from the turnaround or lacks scale cues; provenance gaps; a hand-edited prefab.

## Evidence required (`evidence/T-007/`)
The union of A §4 (E-1 to E-8) and B §4: concept contact sheet; banned-terms check; turnarounds with markers at full size and 180/48/24 px (shown ×8) with decoys; `dimensions.json`; pose renders and `rom_report.json`; concept-vs-greybox and human-IoU overlays; Unity 4-view capture, pixel-proxy capture (downscale + palette quantise until the T-004 renderer lands), hierarchy listing, import log, EditMode XML, empty second-run diff; mood frame with cluster histogram; design note; `EVIDENCE.md` mapping each C# to artifact, value, pass/fail, tool, command, commit.

## Decisions taken at reconciliation (Assumed)
Plantigrade humanoid legs; one rigid fist per side; cockpit in the upper chest at about deck height; plates are designed in layer 3 but not split into sub-parts yet; mood frame in shallow water off a port/pier waterfront, no bridge required.

## Questions for the founders (proposed, to be numbered by the scribe)
- **Q-NEW (look of the Balanced chassis):** default in use: industrial-naval (hull-plate armour, exposed hydraulics at knees and elbows, shipyard-built), gunmetal/slate primary with off-white secondary and one small accent, no emblems until a faction identity exists. (A Q-c, B Q-a, Q-b)

## Orchestrator rulings after evaluation cycle 2 (2026-11-14; spec v1.1, appended, nothing above is changed)
Requested by `eval-style` in `evaluations/T-007/eval-style.cycle2.md`.
- **Ruling (a): a rest-pose hand-to-thigh gap is not a style requirement.** C6 as written asks for the arms to be separated from the *torso* by a ≥ 1 px gap along ≥ 40% of the arm's length at 48 px, a crotch gap, and feet wider than the shanks. It does not ask for daylight between hand and thigh. The arms may therefore use the full width available between the thigh and the forearm cap, subject to the range-of-motion criterion C21 and the physics evaluator's clearance findings, which still apply.
- **Ruling (b): C11 shoulder width is measured on the torso and shoulder blocks only** ("outer shoulder armour" in draft A item AC-11), not across the arms. C7 (at 24 px the shoulders are the widest mass) is unchanged.
- **C27:** the 0.6 front IoU is "reported, expected", not a gate; the criterion is the same major masses and signature forms with every departure listed and justified. `eval-style` judged it met in cycle 2.
- Cycle 3 is the last fix cycle for this task under the orchestrator's three-cycle rule.
