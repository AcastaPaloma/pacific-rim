# T-004 · Pixel pipeline renderer features and third-person rig stabilisation — reconciled expectation spec (v1)

Reconciled by the orchestrator from `expectations/T-004.A.md` (A, items n) and `expectations/T-004.B.md` (B, items n), 2026-11-14. The cited draft items carry the detail (frame counts, band lists, sequence names, tolerances) and are part of this spec. Metric thresholds are those of `research/R-002.md` (`M-003@v1`, `M-004@v1`) as written. The viewpoint to satisfy: a founder holding one captured frame beside the reference board asking "is this our game's look?", and a player watching a slow orbit around an 80 m machine with a skyline 20 km away (B).
"Test scene" is the neutral style test scene of the brief (forms from 10 m to 30 km, ground, sky, a gradient-lit surface, a 1.8 m marker, an 80 m block), built by Editor script.

## Acceptance criteria (gating unless marked reported)
**A. Painted, not filtered (`M-003@v1`)**
- **C1 Palette adherence** (M-003a): every frame, every profile: A₂ ≥ 0.995 on `color_internal`, ≥ 0.95 on `color_output`; `palette_index` 255-count equals internal failures; the unquantised and bilinear-upscale controls fail on the same tool. (A1, B1)
- **C2 Grid and resolution** (M-003d-iv): ≥ 99.5% of s×s output blocks uniform at the declared offset; exact integer scale, 480×270 at 1080p, k = 5 at 1440p, never uneven pixels. (A2, A3, B4, B6)
- **C3 Posterisation** (M-003d-i..iii): ≤ 12 value levels, ≤ 3 hue families per frame; accent share 0.1–15% where present (reported). (A4, B4)
- **C4 Dither in gradients** (M-003b): DIG ≥ 0.15 on ≥ 80% of evaluated frames and individually on the sky gradient and the gradient-lit surface; with the Dither stage off the same frames score < 0.15; transitions are interleaved, never hard contour lines. (A5, A6, B2)
- **C5 Value by depth** (M-003c1, c2): c1 with bands extended to 30 km (no drop > 2 L*, Spearman ρ ≥ 0.9, span ≥ 15 L*), by real radial distance independent of screen position; `haze_cal` c2 as written; ramp uniformity CV ≤ 0.35 (Assumed); a reversed-haze control fails. (A7, A8, B3, B9, B17)
- **C6 Layers and sky.** Blocks at 1, 3, 10 and 30 km sit in successive lighter, lower-contrast bands with dithered seams whose pixels belong only to the two adjacent bands; the farthest layer melts into the sky horizon colour with no seam line; layers within 150 m are the darkest. (A9, B8)
- **C7 Scale legibility.** The 80 m block and the 1.8 m marker read in every profile: block ≥ 1 value band from its background at 150 m and 1 km; marker ΔL* ≥ 10 to its surround and 1–2 internal pixels at 600 m; M-003e composition values reported (advisory). (A10, B7)

**B. Behaviour in motion (`M-004@v1`; thresholds Assumed, pending calibration, except static)**
- **C8 Static:** S_px = C_px = F = 0 exactly over the S0 sequence, every profile; a temporal-noise control is detected. (A11, B10)
- **C9 Yaw** (Y1, Y2): S_px ≤ 0.01, C_px ≤ 0.01, F ≤ 0.001, sky and far skyline included. (A12, B11)
- **C10 Translation** (T1, T2): S_px ≤ 0.12, C_px ≤ 0.15, F ≤ 0.005; T3: F ≤ 0.08. Dither must not slide across near surfaces. (A13, B12)
- **C11 Smooth output.** With sub-pixel compensation, per-frame `color_output` displacement matches the commanded motion within ± 1 output pixel on T1 and Y1; no isolated snap spikes. (A14, B13)
- **C12 Stabilisers pay for themselves.** Each stabiliser (position snap, rotation quantise, each dither anchoring mode) switched off measurably worsens S_px or F on the matching sequence; A/B numbers stored for every anchoring mode. (A14)
- **C13 Camera cut.** A teleport frame produces no wrong-offset image and no residual dither phase: the first frame after a cut equals a static capture of the new pose. (A18, B14)
- **Reported:** band-pop check on a slow dolly toward the 10 km block (B15).
- **How a miss on C9/C10 is handled.** These thresholds were derived on a synthetic ray-caster and R-002 says they must be recalibrated on the first Unity captures. If all anchoring modes and stabilisers are implemented with A/B evidence and the best mode still misses, the evaluator records the defect with the achieved values, and the orchestrator sends it to research and the founders' calibration packet rather than through repeated fix cycles. Nothing is relaxed silently.

**C. Stages, profiles, workflow**
- **C14 Separate, toggleable stages.** Each stage is its own renderer feature with a default-off flag, enabled for the M0 configuration by config only; every toggle combination of the optional stages renders a valid frame; toggling a stage changes only its contribution (Quantise off → C1 fails; Haze off → C5 fails; Dither off → C4 fails); A/B strip. (A15, B18)
- **C15 Reorderable, safely.** Legal orders render; illegal orders (Quantise before Haze, Present not last, an internal-resolution stage before the downsample) are rejected or warned, never silently wrong. Renderer and pipeline assets are created by Editor script; `PC_RPAsset` is untouched (diff). (A16, B19)
- **C16 Palettes are data.** Five `PaletteProfile` assets generated from `palette_set@v1` (hex authoritative, SHA-256 in the sidecar), each with ramp, body colours, accent, haze parameters and a baked LUT; a sixth profile from a JSON fixture passes C1–C2 with no code or shader change; editing one colour and re-baking changes the capture and nothing else. EditMode tests for LUT bake, nearest-colour maths (engine-free, `dotnet test`) and the Bayer matrix. (A17, B16)

**D. Rig**
- **C17 Rig contract.** `ICameraRig` and `ThirdPersonRig` follow a moving 80 m target (≈ 10 m/s) with no target-position jitter beyond one internal texel; hints (snapped position, quantised rotation, sub-texel offset, dither origin, reference depth, cut flag) flow through `Rendering.Pixel.Contracts`; assembly test: Rendering.Pixel references no Camera, Units or World assembly and Camera references no mech code; a rig giving no hints (the future `CockpitRig`) still yields a valid on-palette frame. (A18, B20, B21)
- **C18 Footfall impulse** (F-009): a footfall-style event on the Core bus, by event contract only, moves the camera in proportion to its magnitude, bounded and decaying, reproducibly; framing returns to within one texel; the image moves as a whole and C9/C11 hold once decayed. (A19, B22)

**E. Capture and cost**
- **C19 Capture tool.** One Unity CLI `-executeMethod` batch run on macOS Metal writes valid `style_capture@v1` (all keys, sidecars, `sequence.json`, commit hash, palette SHA; no TAA, bloom or motion blur; nothing post-processed outside the engine) for stills of all five profiles, `haze_cal` and S0/T1–T3/Y1–Y2; sidecars pass the validator; two runs are bit-identical; depth decodes to known distances within 1%. (A20, B23)
- **C20 Render cost** (relative regression only; M-008 stays unverified until target-class Windows hardware exists, and the evidence must say so): `Pixel.*` passes ≤ 0.5 ms GPU p95 and present ≤ 0.5 ms (Assumed) at 1920×1080 and 2560×1440 with per-pass breakdown; zero per-frame GC allocation in the render loop after warm-up. (A22, B24)
- **C21 Module hygiene.** `MODULE.md` for Rendering.Pixel and Camera; contracts-only dependencies; tuning values in assets/config; tests pass; zero compile errors and no unexplained shader warnings; no name, shape or text from the references anywhere. (A23, B §3.10)

**Milestone gate, not a task gate:** the blind panel M-003f (mean ≥ 8.0 over ≥ 12 captures, ≥ 8 raters, hidden anchors) is the founders' style-frame approval. For this task `eval-style` applies the same rubric with anchors and records its scores as an agent proxy; the panel board is prepared for the founders. (A21, B5)

## Style references to match (style only)
A §2 and B §2 agree: the lavender/peach panel for grid crispness, clustered dithered masses and near-dark/far-light layering; the slate-teal and slate-blue panels for mist and low-contrast far bands; the warm rust panels for single-hue posterised bands and dark silhouettes; the warm-haze panel for scale cues and chunky dust; the slate-with-crimson panels for a sparing accent. Excluded outliers per R-002 are not targets.

## What would disappoint the founders
The union of A §3 and B §3: a palette filter over a smooth render; a retro-console look; uniform dither noise over the whole frame; hard contour banding; crawling dither on the mech and ground; swimming sky bands in a turn; haze that ignores distance or pops; a mushy or uneven upscale; mismatched pixel scales; lost scale cues; a stair-stepping or weightless camera; palettes that need code to change; captures with TAA or bloom; numbers that cannot be reproduced.

## Evidence required (`evidence/T-004/`)
The union of A §5 and B §5: `EVIDENCE.md` (criterion → artifact, command, tool, commit); `style_capture@v1` sets for the five profiles (stills from three viewpoints, `haze_cal`, S0/T1–T3/Y1–Y2) plus negative controls (unquantised, bilinear, reversed haze, temporal noise, stabilisers off, dither off, each anchoring mode); metric JSON for every sub-measure of `M-003@v1` and `M-004@v1` with the tool and commit used; the stage A/B strip and reorder test result; swatch sheets and a 30 km depth-ramp strip per profile; the side-by-side board with reference panels and anchors; a 20 s orbit with one footfall impulse; the scale capture; test XML; frame timing CSV with per-pass GPU times and the GC capture, hardware named; the `PC_RPAsset` no-diff proof; `MODULE.md` files.

## Questions for the founders (proposed; defaults in use)
- **Dither look:** Bayer 4×4 as §9 says, or clustered/blue-noise as the references measure? Default Bayer, alternatives switchable by data and shown side by side.
- **Translation shimmer:** surface-anchored dither near the camera with direction-locked sky, or accept residual far-skyline crawl? Default: the hybrid; threshold 0.12 kept pending calibration on Unity captures.
- **Rotation quantisation** (camera turns in about 0.2° steps): keep for stability or drop for smoothness? Default: on behind a flag; judged with the feel panel.
- **4K internal resolution:** constant 270 lines (k = 8) or §9's range (k = 6)? Default k = clamp(round(H/270), 4, 6).
- **Profiles:** five versus the four families named in §9. Default five.
- **Target PC for M-008** and whether to obtain Windows hardware. Default: RTX 3060-class at 1080p60; M-008 unverified until then.
- **Camera impulse envelope** (default cap about 0.5° and 0.5 m, decay about 1.2 s) and **default framing** (default: the 80 m mech fills about half the frame height, low horizon).
- **Outlines, light shafts, clustered smoke:** outlines optional and off in M0; shafts and smoke in a later slice.
