# T-003 · One real San Francisco tile (`L0_30_36`) — reconciled expectation spec (v1)

Reconciled by the orchestrator from `expectations/T-003.A.md` (A, items n and §4.n) and `expectations/T-003.B.md` (B, items n and §3.n), 2026-11-14. The cited draft items carry the detail and are part of this spec. Thresholds are those of `M-005@v1` in `research/R-004.md` §6 as written. Tile: BayTM x 61440–63488, z 73728–75776 (downtown waterfront, Telegraph Hill, Bay Bridge W2 tower, about 39% water).
Founder view (B): "I put an 80 m mech in the water off the Ferry Building and look west. That must be San Francisco: Salesforce towering over everything, Transamerica's spire, Coit on its hill, the Bay Bridge tower behind me, and water I can wade. Then I knock a tower over and the save remembers it. Then you build the other 4,600 tiles the same way."

## Readings taken where R-004, the brief and the breakdown disagree (both drafts agree on all of these)
- **Sub-measures that cannot run on one tile** (M-005.3 Golden Gate, Oakland and Bay-wide boxes; skyline viewpoints V1–V3; out-of-tile landmarks): the checker reports `not_applicable_in_tile` with the reason and they are due at M1. They are never counted as a pass. V4 and V5 gate at M0.
- **Faults:** no Quaternary fault crosses this tile. The global fault layer is fetched and baked; the tile clip is valid and empty by design, the manifest says so, and a test asserts the nearest-trace distances so that empty is not mistaken for broken.
- **Baked data location:** the brief requires committed baked tiles; R-004 placed them under the git-ignored cache. Baked `geo_tile@v1` output is committed (LFS for rasters) in a folder outside `Pipelines/geo/cache/`; raw downloads stay ignored.
- **Landmarks at M0** are data extrusions keeping their `bid`; hand-crafted meshes (F-041) come later behind the same `bid`. Heights are stated unambiguously as above-base or above-datum.
- **Self-reference:** OSM is both source and reference for several checks, which prove the pipeline only. Independent truth comes from NGS marks, the lidar surface (needs `pdal`) and cited landmark dimensions. If `pdal` cannot be installed in the kaiju env from conda-forge, the independent comparisons are reported "not run", never passed.
- **Water:** static mean sea level at +0.969 m NAVD88; flat world; no tides at M0.

## Acceptance criteria
**A. Geographic truth (`M-005@v1`)**
- **C1 Landmark positions** (M-005.1): every in-tile landmark of R-004 §5 (W2, Salesforce, Transamerica, 555 California, Millennium, Ferry Building, Coit) within 0.5 m of the import source and p95 ≤ 5 m, max ≤ 10 m of the reference. (A1, B1)
- **C2 Landmark heights** (M-005.1d): Salesforce 326 m, Transamerica 260 m, Millennium 197 m, Coit 64 m above its base, each within max(2 m, 1%); 555 California and W2 measured and reported but not gated while their sources conflict; Salesforce is the tallest object in the tile. (A2, B6)
- **C3 Terrain** (M-005.2): NGS marks in the tile: median |Δ| ≤ 0.5 m and ≤ 10% beyond 2 m; with fewer than 5 qualifying marks the count is reported and the gate is the pipeline check (10,000 land points versus the source: RMSE ≤ 0.10 m, max ≤ 0.5 m). Thresholds are never lowered. (A3, B2)
- **C4 Bathymetry** (M-005.3, tile-scoped): RMSE versus CUDEM ≤ 0.25 m on 10,000 wet points; tile minimum within 1 m of the source minimum (about −29.5 m). (A4, B3)
- **C5 Building heights** (M-005.4): |Δ| ≤ 0.5 m for ≥ 99%; KS D ≤ 0.02; p50/p90/p99 within max(0.5 m, 1%); the fallback validation reported with its Assumed status; every building carries `height_source`, shares per source reported, `default` share < 10%; a stated rule for roof shape versus `height_m`. (A7, A §4.9, B4, B24)
- **C6 Footprints** (M-005.5): area recall and precision ≥ 0.98; count recall ≥ 0.99 for buildings ≥ 20 m²; no building absent from the source, none overlapping water. (A8, B5)
- **C7 1:1 scale** (M-005.6): ≥ 10 landmark pairs: |d_game/d_geo − 1| ≤ 60 ppm and ≤ 0.1 m under 2 km; vertical scale exactly 1; one unit = 1 m; BayTM@v1 is the stored CRS, no UTM anywhere. (A5, B6)
- **C8 Skyline** (M-005.7): V4 and V5 versus R-src IoU ≥ 0.98, MAE ≤ 1 px; versus R-ind IoU ≥ 0.90, MAE ≤ 3 px (Assumed). (A9, B7)
- **C9 Licences and attribution** (M-005.8): complete manifests for 100% of layers; a Unity attribution asset holding every distinct attribution string including "© OpenStreetMap contributors" with the ODbL notice; a script diffs manifests against it; ODbL layers marked as derivative-database candidates; no layer with an unclear commercial licence. (A15, A16, B8, B21)

**B. Reads as San Francisco, at giant scale**
- **C10 Shoreline and sea level.** Water surface at +0.969 m NAVD88; the terrain/water intersection follows the OSM coastline with median offset ≤ 5 m; piers dry and solid; no water over land. (A6)
- **C11 One continuous surface.** A single land-to-seabed heightfield with no gap, hole, step or double surface: profile at 50 shoreline crossings, max adjacent-sample jump < 1.5 m except documented seawalls and piers; 3DEP/CUDEM seam median |Δ| ≤ 0.1 m and no step > 0.5 m; no water-plane cell above ground. (A11, B12)
- **C12 Buildings sit right.** Every base within 0.5 m of local terrain; watertight flat-faced extrusions with consistent winding; no z-fighting between roads, terrain, water and building bases at 10 m, 100 m, 1 km and 3 km (also at a 1/4-resolution nearest-neighbour downsample or the T-004 pipeline if available); towers read as towers, not slivers. (A18, B13)
- **C13 Roads and faults.** Roads present with class and width, on the terrain without flicker, bridge and tunnel segments flagged (the Bay Bridge approach is a deck, not a ground road); the global `faults_bay.geojson` baked with its fields, the in-tile clip empty by design. (A10, B14, B15)
- **Reported (founders will look at these first):** the height-relation plate with an 80 m mech silhouette beside the towers and W2 (B9); the wading depth map and histogram with the deepest point (B11); un-styled V4 and V5 captures at full size and at 270 rows, plus V4 from a 70 m eye height; a blind "which city" check by the bay evaluator with decoys (B10).

**C. Built to extend**
- **C14 Building identity and separability** (F-043): `bid` derived from source IDs only, identical across two independent bakes, across input order and thread count, and across tile-boundary clipping (owner-tile rule exercised); parts linked by `part_of` so a stepped tower is one structure; Core's ID type wraps the ID; **any building can be removed individually at runtime by `bid` without re-baking**, and removing 20 random buildings leaves all others and the terrain byte-identical in the export. (A12, B16)
- **C15 Deterministic, idempotent pipeline.** One documented command per layer plus a bake; a second run with a populated cache does no network I/O, reports "up to date" and gives byte-identical outputs; an unreachable source fails loudly, never partly. (A13, B17)
- **C16 `geo_tile@v1` exactly, fit for the Bay.** 2048 m tiles, 2049² samples pixel-is-point, shared edge rows byte-identical with a synthetic neighbour from the same source, tile-local float32 with a float64 origin that is a multiple of 2048 m, LOD field present; the bake is parameterised by tile index and a second tile (the planned M1 tile) bakes in Python with no code change. (A11, B18)
- **C17 Floating-origin readiness** (F-042): placing the tile at any tile-corner origin reproduces C1 exactly; shifting the Unity origin by a tile multiple changes as-built float64 positions by ≤ 1 mm; no in-scene |position| beyond 4096 m. Streaming itself is not built. (A17, B19)
- **C18 Manifest and hygiene.** Every layer: source product, URL, licence, attribution, fetch time, source hash; output hashes; build script and commit. The importer refuses a tile whose manifest lacks any field (negative test). `Pipelines/geo/cache/` absent from every commit; committed tile size reported. (A14, A15, B20)
- **C19 Measurement export.** One batch command exports as-built facts in float64 BayTM (landmark anchors and heights, building list with `bid`/base/top, terrain and bathymetry samples at control points, tile statistics); a Python checker in the kaiju env emits pass / fail / not-applicable per `M-005@v1` sub-measure from a clean checkout. (A21, B §4)
- **C20 Budgets** (proposed by the drafts because research gave none; Assumed until the founders define the target PC): ≤ 1.5 M triangles for the tile at gameplay LOD (native 1 m terrain would be about 8.4 M, so an LOD or adaptive mesh with a stated error is expected); ≤ 1,500 draw calls, with the batched figure reported against a target of 200; committed tile data ≤ 100 MB; bake ≤ 5 min; editor build of the tile ≤ 60 s; an extrapolation to about 70 M1 tiles and the full Bay. Any overrun is a recorded defect with a named mitigation. (A19, B22)
- **C21 Module rules.** World.Geo (L2) references only Core contracts and its own; `IGeoLayer` implemented for terrain+bathymetry, buildings, roads, faults, water; default-off flag; config under `Assets/Game/Config/`; `MODULE.md`; a Pipelines/geo README; EditMode tests; `pytest` for projection, tile indexing, ID determinism, manifest completeness; scenes built through Editor API only; `.meta` files committed. (A20, B23)

## What would disappoint the founders
The union of A §2–§3 and B §2: Salesforce not clearly dominant or shorter than Transamerica; Transamerica as a plain box; Coit missing or Telegraph Hill a mound; a rotated or mirrored grid, Market Street not diagonal; piers under water or a straight waterfront; a visible seam, trench or wall at the shoreline; buildings floating or sunk on slopes; a field of identical boxes; a tile that looks right but fails the numbers (the UTM-versus-BayTM error); all buildings fused into one mesh; IDs that change between runs; z-fighting or sliver faces that crawl under the pixel pipeline; an empty fault layer nobody explained; a bake that needs the network or differs per run; raw caches committed; a missing OSM credit; out-of-tile checks silently marked pass.

## Evidence required (`evidence/T-003/`)
The union of A §5 and B §4: the manifest, CRS file and manifest-completeness log; the doctored-manifest rejection; `m005_report.json` with every sub-measure (value, threshold, pass / fail / not-applicable with reason) and the as-built export; skyline captures V4 and V5 (full size and 270 rows, plus the 70 m eye variant) with R-src and R-ind masks, overlays and IoU/MAE; top-down, oblique (three bearings), shoreline close-up and the z-fighting distance series; the depth map and histogram; the height-versus-mech plate; the fault map with distances; the two-run hash table and "up to date" log; the second-tile bake log; the synthetic-neighbour seam check; the floating-origin check; tile statistics (triangles, draw calls, memory, disk, counts, `height_source` shares, bake and build times); pytest and Unity test XML; the asmdef dependency listing; the attribution diff; a `known_gaps.md` (recent buildings missing from the source); `EVIDENCE.md` (criterion → artifact, command, commit).
