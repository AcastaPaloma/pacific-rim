# T-006 · M0 style frame and founders' checkpoint packet: expectation draft A

Viewpoint: two founders wake up, open one image, and ask "is this our game, and is this the Bay?" Then they sit down with the checkpoint packet and rate things. Everything below serves that morning. Panel naming for references: ref_01 A rust (top-left), B slate-teal (top-right), C lavender/peach (bottom); ref_03 A rust-red, B slate-blue with clouds, C sepia, D slate with crimson (per `research/R-002.md` panel ids). Thresholds are `M-003@v1` etc. as written; nothing here relaxes them.

## 1. Acceptance criteria (gating unless marked reported)
**A. The hero frame as a picture** (`hero_default`, default profile, 1920x1080 output)
1. **Palette and accent** (M-003a, d-i..iii): A2 >= 0.95 on output, >= 0.995 on internal; <= 12 value levels; <= 3 hue families; exactly one accent, 0.1-15% of the frame, and it sits on something small or near (a lamp, a hull, a light on the mech), not on the sky. Unquantised and bilinear controls of the same view fail the same tool.
2. **Value by depth** (M-003c1): over the real frame, with bands out to the farthest visible skyline, no band drop > 2 L*, Spearman >= 0.9, span >= 15 L*. By eye: water and mech legs darkest, downtown mid-value, far hills and sky nearly one colour. The mech must be the darkest large mass in the frame: its mean L* is below every other object class (terrain, buildings, water, sky) and below the frame median by >= 10 L*; the darkest-5%-of-pixels mask falls mostly on the mech (>= 50% of those pixels).
3. **Skyline melts into haze** (M-003c2 `haze_cal` rerun in this scene's config, M-003e advisory): the farthest buildings differ from sky by <= half the contrast of the nearest buildings; no hard seam line between the last layer and the horizon; band seams are dithered, not contours.
4. **Crisp pixels** (M-003d-iv): >= 99.5% of s x s blocks uniform on output at 1080p (s = 4) and 1440p (s = 5); no soft or uneven pixels anywhere including the water and the mech.
5. **Dither only where there is a gradient** (M-003b): DIG >= 0.15 on the sky and on at least one lit surface; with the Dither stage off the same frame scores < 0.15; flat regions (mech plates, wall faces in shade) show no dither texture (reported: DIG <= 0.5 over the frame; advisory).
6. **Composition** (M-003e advisory, D-006): horizon in the lower 45% of the frame, sky share 0.20-0.50, silhouette contrast L*90 - L*10 >= 25, mech silhouette readable at 48 px wide.
7. **Blind panel** (M-003f): `eval-style` proxy scores the hero >= 8 with hidden anchors; the founders' own rating is the real gate. No profile mean < 7 across the strip.

**B. Scale and truth**
8. **The 80 m machine against real towers.** The mech is 80 m +/- 0.5 m in the scene (as-built export). Salesforce Tower (326 m) and Transamerica (260 m) in frame measure those heights above their base within max(2 m, 1%) (T-003 C2), and Salesforce is visibly taller than the mech's head and the tallest thing in frame; the machine's head is well below the tower tops, and the 67 m deck marker (T-007 C10) falls on its upper chest. A scale-plate image overlays an 80 m ruler against the 326 m and 260 m verticals from the same camera.
9. **Water on the legs.** Sea level +0.969 m NAVD88; the mech's stand position and the real seabed depth there are reported; waterline on the legs matches `depth_at_mech` within 0.25 m on the as-built export, and the waterline falls at ankle to mid-shin (as in the T-007 mood intent) unless the real depth forbids it, in which case the reported depth wins and the frame is not nudged. The sole is on the seabed, not floating or sunk.
10. **Tiny true-scale figures.** At least three 1.8 m figures, one car (about 4.5 m) and one small boat (about 6-10 m) in the foreground, each at measured true size (as-built list) and each 1-3 internal pixels wide or tall at the figure distance; they read as dark specks against a lighter surround (marker dL* >= 10).
11. **Nothing moved or restyled.** Tile `L0_30_36` loaded unmodified: building, terrain and seabed hashes in the scene equal the baked tile's; zero scale on any building or terrain object; the mech scale is 1.0. The skyline in frame (V4/V5 style check at the hero camera) is the real one: IoU against the tile's own data mask >= 0.98, and the named landmarks (Salesforce, Transamerica, Coit, Bay Bridge tower) are identifiable in the hero or in at least one alternative framing. No invented buildings; placeholder props are the only additions and are listed.
12. **Mech is static and honest.** Rest pose, no physics body, no animation, as the T-007 prefab (hash equal); stands off the downtown waterfront in water, not on a pier or street.

**C. Variants and composition for choice**
13. **Five-profile strip.** Same camera, same frame, all five profiles (`warm_rust_sepia`, `warm_amber_haze`, `slate_teal`, `lavender_peach`, `slate_crimson`); each passes items 1, 2, 4, 5 individually; geometry identical pixel for pixel in silhouettes (only colour changes); the strip is labelled by profile id.
14. **Alternative framings**, default profile: closer (mech fills about 70% of height), wider (city and bay dominant, mech about 25%), and from the waterfront looking out (figures large in the foreground, mech and skyline behind). Each passes items 1-5 and 10-11.
15. **Beside the references.** A board puts the hero next to the same-family reference panels (section 2) and the five-profile strip; file names only, no scores, order randomised in a second copy for cold rating.

**D. Motion, cost, reproducibility**
16. **Stability clips** (M-004@v1): a slow orbit and a slow push-in around the hero framing in each dither anchoring mode; sequence metrics reported per mode; the best mode meets T1/Y1 thresholds of `expectations/T-004.md` C9/C10, or the miss is recorded with achieved values and not relaxed. The orbit shows no sliding dither on the mech, no crawling skyline, no band pop. Lossless frame folders plus an integer-scale preview.
17. **Frame cost** (M-008, relative only, labelled "no target PC yet"): tile + mech + pipeline GPU ms p50/p95 and draw calls at 1080p and 1440p, `Pixel.*` within the T-004 C20 budget, tile triangle and draw-call numbers against the T-003 C20 budget, zero per-frame GC after warm-up.
18. **Reproducible.** One CLI command rebuilds the scene and every capture; two runs are bit-identical (hash table); sidecar records camera pose in BayTM, FOV, mech position, water depth, profile id, flags, palette SHA, commit. Built by Editor script; no hand-painted or externally post-processed pixel; no module code edited. Committed capture volume <= 120 MB.
19. **Originality** (D-009, D-022): no name, shape, text or iconography from the references in the scene, props or files; the mech is the T-007 original.

**E. Checkpoint packet** (`checkpoints/M0/`)
20. **About 10 captures per metric** for M-003, M-004, M-005, plus M-001/M-002 walk strips from `evidence/T-005/` labelled "not at target pace" (read-only, no merge). Each set spans clearly good to clearly bad, with the bad end made by named pipeline toggles (dither off, no haze, bilinear upscale, unquantised, stabilisers off) and the set shows which toggle made it; verified: the tool's own value on the first capture is clearly better than on the last, and the order matches the metric on >= 80% of adjacent pairs.
21. **Rating sheets** per metric in the exact ratings-file format that `Tools/Metrics calibrate` reads (capture id, 1-10 per founder, notes); a dry run of `calibrate` on a filled dummy sheet succeeds; capture ids are blind (no metric value, no profile or toggle in the file name shown to raters; a separate key file holds them).
22. **Guide and index.** A one-page guide (what to look at, how to rate, how to run `calibrate`) and `README.md` listing every decision asked with links: style-frame approval, palette choice, dither look and anchoring, internal resolution, walk-gate question, plus the open points in section 5. Each decision has a recommended default and a "what changes if you choose otherwise" line.

## 2. Reference panels to sit beside (style only)
- Default (cold) profile: ref_01 B (slate-teal mist, low-contrast far layers, dark near mass) and ref_03 B (slate-blue layered haze, clustered dithered cloud edges): for haze ramp and layering.
- Warm profile: ref_03 C (sepia, single-hue posterised bands) and ref_01 A (rust field with one dark silhouette): for flat value bands and a dark mass against a lighter field.
- Lavender/peach: ref_01 C: for crisp pixel grid, clustered dither masses, tiny figures at foot of a huge form.
- Crimson accent: ref_03 D: for sparing accent on slate.
Respect only: grid crispness, limited hues plus one accent, near-dark/far-light, scale cues. Subject matter (creatures, riders, armour, banners, text, logos) is out. Ref_02 equals ref_01 and is not shown twice; fan-art lettering is cropped from any board.

## 3. What would disappoint them
- A smooth 3D render with a palette filter; or a retro-console look; or dither noise over everything.
- The mech not the heaviest, darkest mass: lost in haze, or lit like a toy, or a pale cut-out.
- Salesforce shorter than the mech's reach or lost in haze so it cannot be told from a box; Transamerica a plain slab; no recognisable San Francisco.
- A sea of identical boxes, floating or sunk buildings, a straight fake shoreline, a seam or trench at the water line.
- Mech floating above or buried under the water, no waterline, or legs standing in a puddle that cannot be real depth.
- No scale cues, or props that are huge, glowing or look pasted on; invented landmarks.
- Mushy or uneven pixels, hard contour bands in the sky, dither that crawls in the orbit clip.
- Hero that looks right but whose numbers are missing; a "frame" that cannot be rebuilt by one command.
- A packet that is a pile of files: scores visible before rating, no good-to-bad spread, sheets the tool cannot read, decisions buried.
- Anything that looks like another franchise's robot or world.

## 5. Open points for the founders (recommended default)
1. **Hero palette.** Default: `slate_teal` (cold bay fog, closest to the mood frame and ref_01 B), with `warm_amber_haze` as the strip's close second; founders choose from the five-profile strip. Fixed per district (D-027, Assumed).
2. **Framing.** Default: low horizon, mech about 50% of frame height (T-004 default), centred-left on the thirds, Salesforce visible behind its left shoulder, waterfront figures bottom edge. Alternatives per item 14.
3. **Time of day.** Default: low-sun hazy morning, soft light, long cool shadows, accent from a warm lamp or boat light; no night or weather at M0 (D-027).
4. **Mech prominence against the city.** Default: dominant but not dwarfing: the mech about 80% of the height of the downtown skyline mass in frame so the 326 m tower still reads taller. Alternative: close framing with the city as backdrop.
5. **Placeholder props.** Default: primitive-built 1.8 m figures (dark specks), one car, one small boat with a mast, one lamp post, all flagged placeholder; no cranes, no named ships, no text.
6. **Dither look and anchoring.** Default: Bayer 4x4 with the hybrid (surface-anchored near, direction-locked far); blue-noise and clustered shown in the packet.
7. **Internal resolution.** Default 480x270 at 1080p (k = 4); 320x180 shown as the coarse alternative.
8. **Stand position.** Default: the water position off the downtown waterfront giving the best skyline line-of-sight within real wading depth; the reported depth is not edited to flatter the frame.
9. **M-001/M-002 strips** in the packet: default include, labelled "not at target pace", with the walk-gate question as an explicit decision.
