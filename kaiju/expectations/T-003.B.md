# T-003 expectation draft B: one real SF tile (terrain, bathymetry, buildings, roads, faults)

Author: `expect-B`. Pillar P3 (with P4 and P2 as consumers). Tile = `L0_30_36` per R-004 §4 (Assumed accepted). Written before anything is built; independent of draft A.

Founder view: "I put an 80 m mech in the water off the Ferry Building and look west. That must be San Francisco: Salesforce towering over everything, Transamerica's spire, Coit on its hill, the Bay Bridge tower behind me, and water I can wade. Then I knock a tower over and the save remembers it. Then you build the other 4,600 tiles the same way."

## 1. Acceptance criteria (all gating unless marked "reported")

### 1A. Accuracy (M-005@v1, thresholds as written in R-004 §6)
1. **Landmark positions (M-005.1).** Every §5 landmark inside the tile (rows 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11; row 16 cranes are outside M0) has as-built BayTM float64 anchor within **≤ 0.5 m of the import source** and **p95 ≤ 5 m, max ≤ 10 m vs the independent reference**. Rows with open source conflicts (row 3, Q-NEW-i) are excluded from 1(d) height only, never from position.
2. **Terrain (M-005.2).** NGS control points inside the tile: **median |Δ| ≤ 0.5 m and ≤ 10 % of marks with |Δ| > 2 m**; pipeline check on 10,000 random land points: **RMSE ≤ 0.10 m, max ≤ 0.5 m**. If the tile holds fewer than 5 qualifying marks the checker reports the count and the criterion falls back to the pipeline check alone (see §3, item 4).
3. **Bathymetry (M-005.3, tile-scoped).** Pipeline RMSE vs CUDEM on 10,000 wet points **≤ 0.25 m**; as-built tile minimum is within 1 m of the CUDEM tile minimum (R-004: about −29.5 m at 8 m resampling; the 1 m surface may go slightly deeper). The Golden Gate, Oakland and Bay-wide boxes are **not testable on this tile**: the checker must emit `not_applicable_in_tile`, never a silent pass.
4. **Building heights (M-005.4).** Per building **|Δ| ≤ 0.5 m for ≥ 99 %**; KS **D ≤ 0.02**; p50, p90, p99 each within max(0.5 m, 1 %). Fallback validation (c) (KS D ≤ 0.15, median |e| ≤ 3 m) reported with its Assumed status.
5. **Footprints (M-005.5).** Area recall and precision **≥ 0.98**; count recall **≥ 0.99** for source buildings ≥ 20 m².
6. **1:1 scale (M-005.6).** At least 10 landmark pairs (those of §6.6 that fall in or near the tile, plus out-of-tile reference pairs computed from the transform alone): **|d_game/d_geo − 1| ≤ 60 ppm**, and **≤ 0.1 m for pairs under 2 km** (Salesforce-Transamerica, 790.91 m). No vertical exaggeration: Salesforce top minus base reads 326 m within max(2 m, 1 %).
7. **Skyline (M-005.7).** Captures for V4 and V5 (both inside the tile). Versus R-src **IoU ≥ 0.98, MAE ≤ 1 px**; versus R-ind **IoU ≥ 0.90, MAE ≤ 3 px (Assumed)**. V1 to V3 are outside the tile: the criterion is "the export and checker accept them and report `outside_built_area`", not a pass.
8. **Licences and attribution (M-005.8).** 100 % of tile layers have complete manifests; credits text contains every distinct `attribution` string, including "© OpenStreetMap contributors" with the ODbL notice. Binary pass.

### 1B. Giant-scale and "reads as San Francisco" (outcomes the founders will actually look at)
9. **Height relations to the machine (reported at M0, gating at the review).** A top-down plus side elevation overlay places an 80 m mech silhouette beside Salesforce (326 m), Transamerica (260 m), 555 California (226 m), Millennium (197 m), Coit Tower on its hill (summit about 91 m plus 64 m) and Bay Bridge W2. The as-built heights match the table; Salesforce is visibly more than 4 times the mech and the mech's head is below the W2 tower top. This is a measured artifact, not a judgement.
10. **Recognisable skyline from the water.** Un-styled V4 capture (Bay east of the Ferry Building, looking west) shows, left to right by azimuth, a distinct Salesforce Tower as the tallest mass, the stepped Transamerica spire, and Telegraph Hill with Coit; a blind reviewer given the V4 and V5 captures plus three decoys names "San Francisco" and Salesforce correctly (reported; panel of eval-bay, 2 reviewers). The silhouette must stay readable when reduced to the 1/4 to 1/6 pixel target (a V4 capture downsampled to 270 rows is also supplied).
11. **Wading depths.** A depth map of the tile (water surface at +0.969 m NAVD88 minus bed) and a printed histogram: share of water by depth bands (0-2, 2-5, 5-10, 10-20, > 20 m), deepest cell, and the length of shoreline with depth under 8 m within 100 m of shore. Outcome: the Ferry Building to Bay Bridge reach is wadable by an 80 m machine everywhere in the tile (all depths < 40 m, below mech knee-to-hip range, no cell deeper than the mech's own height), and the report states the deepest point and where it is.
12. **One continuous surface, seamless shoreline.** A single terrain-plus-bathymetry surface crosses the waterline with no step, hole or double surface: sample a profile at 50 shoreline crossings; max adjacent-sample vertical jump below 1.5 m except at documented seawalls/piers; the 3DEP-to-CUDEM seam median |Δ| ≤ 0.1 m (R-004 measured 0.02 m) with no visible lines in an oblique capture. Water mask and surface agree: no water-plane cell above ground, no dry cell below MSL except documented diked land (piers solid per R-004 §1.5). Capture the waterline under oblique light at the Embarcadero.
13. **Buildings read as buildings, no ghosts.** Extruded footprints with real heights, flat tops or declared roof shape, no floating or buried blocks (every building base within 0.5 m of local terrain `base_navd88_m`), no z-fighting at 5 sampled orbit distances (50 m to 3 km), clean silhouettes (no jagged cut-off at tile edge beyond the clipping rule in 3.3). Piers and the Embarcadero waterfront stand on terrain, not in the water.
14. **Roads.** 4131 OSM ways (183 km, 60.9 km drivable) present with class and width; roads sit on the terrain without z-fight; bridge and tunnel segments flagged (Bay Bridge approach at the east edge is a bridge, not a ground road). Piers/footways may be ground decals, but drivable classes are visible in a top-down capture.
15. **Faults.** Fault fetch and bake run end to end and the global `faults_bay.geojson` (≈ 3,232 features, principal traces San Andreas, Hayward, Calaveras, Rodgers Creek, San Gregorio) exists with fields `fault_name, section, linetype, slip_rate, slip_sense, age`. Because **no Quaternary fault crosses `L0_30_36`**, the tile's `faults.geojson` is valid and empty (or holds only sub-metre clips) and the manifest says why; evidence includes a map plot of the nearest faults and distances (Golden Gate fault about 11.6 km, Hayward Northern about 13.8 km) to the tile.

### 1C. Extension, streaming, destruction (for the people who build on this)
16. **Per-building identity and separability (F-043).** Every building and part has a stable `bid` (`osm:w…`) that is **identical across two independent bake runs and across tile-boundary clipping**; owner-tile rule exercised with a boundary-straddling building. Each building is its own addressable mesh/collider object (no merged mega-mesh that cannot be removed individually) with a `part_of` link so Transamerica's stepped parts destroy as one structure. Test: delete any 20 random buildings, the other buildings and the terrain are byte-identical in the export.
17. **Determinism and idempotence.** Re-running fetch+bake from the cache twice produces byte-identical `terrain.*`, `buildings.geojson`, `roads.geojson`, `faults.geojson` and manifest hashes (timestamps confined to the manifest's `built_utc`/`fetched_utc`). Second run reports "up to date" and does no network work.
18. **Spec fit for the whole Bay and streaming.** Tile data is `geo_tile@v1` exactly (R-004 §3): BayTM@v1 WKT2, 2048 m tile, 2049² samples, pixel-is-point so the east row of tile 30 equals the west row of tile 31 (verified with a synthetic neighbour built from the same CUDEM source), tile-local float32 coordinates with a float64 tile origin that is a multiple of 2048 m, LOD level field present. The bake is parameterised by tile index: baking a second tile (the planned M1 `L0_26_37`) from the same scripts needs no code change; the implementer proves it by baking at least one more tile in Python (data only; need not be imported).
19. **Floating-origin readiness.** Importer places the tile by origin offset; moving the Unity origin by a tile-corner multiple (an exact integer) leaves as-built positions in float64 BayTM unchanged to ≤ 1 mm, and all in-scene |position| ≤ 4096 m (M-NEW-a precondition).
20. **Manifest fit.** Manifest contains for every layer: ≥ 1 source with product, item, url, licence (SPDX or `US-Gov-PD`), attribution, `fetched_utc`, `source_sha256`; output `sha256`; build script and git hash. The importer **refuses** a tile whose manifest lacks any of these (unit test with a corrupted manifest).
21. **Licences.** ODbL layers (buildings, roads, water) marked as derivative-database candidates and listed in a `LICENCES` summary; a Q-### for Q-NEW-a is carried. No layer with unclear commercial licence is shipped (Overture, Microsoft footprints not used at M0).
22. **Budgets (M-008).** Report and meet: tile triangles, draw calls, GPU memory, baked size on disk, bake time. Proposed gates: **≤ 1.5 M triangles and ≤ 1,500 draw calls for the tile at the gameplay LOD**, **committed tile data ≤ 100 MB** (LFS for rasters), full-tile build in the editor under 60 s, and a stated extrapolation to ≈ 70 M1 tiles and 4,624 tiles. Anything over budget carries a named mitigation, not a silent pass. Perf is measured by eval-perf from the evidence, not claimed.
23. **Module rules.** `World.Geo` is L2 and references only `Core` contracts and its own; `World.Geo.Contracts` holds `IGeoLayer` and the data types; feature flag default off; tuning values under `Assets/Game/Config/`; EditMode tests green; `MODULE.md` and a Pipelines/geo README present; no hand-edited `.unity`/`.prefab`; `.meta` committed; `pytest` for projection, tile indexing, ID determinism, manifest completeness green.
24. **Missing heights.** Every building whose height is not `osm_height` carries `height_source` ∈ {datasf, osm_levels, default}; counts per source reported (R-004: about 25 % lack OSM height). No building is silently invented.

## 2. What would disappoint them
- A skyline that is "a lot of boxes": Salesforce not clearly dominant, Transamerica as a plain block, Coit missing or buried, Telegraph Hill flattened, piers fused with water.
- Salesforce at 0.2 m (stale DataSF) or Transamerica shrunk because a fallback overrode an OSM height; 181 Fremont absent.
- Water shown as one flat blue plane over a land-coloured sea floor with a visible band at the shoreline, or a Bay so shallow nothing is ever above the mech's knee while the shoreline is a cliff.
- All buildings fused into one mesh, so "destroy them one by one" needs a re-bake; IDs that change when OSM updates or when the tile boundary shifts.
- Tile scale off by even a few hundred ppm (UTM instead of BayTM) so the second tile will not line up with the first.
- Tower base floating above or sunk below the 3DEP surface (buildings on Telegraph Hill sit in the slope, not on a plane).
- Geometry that crawls under the pixel pipeline: z-fighting roads, razor-thin parts, dense tiny polygons that dither to noise.
- Nothing tells the founders the fault layer is empty here, so they conclude it failed.
- A pipeline that only works from a hand-edited cache, or whose bake gives different bytes each run.
- Credits screen missing the OSM line (a legal blocker on ship).

## 3. Ambiguities, risks, contradictions in R-004 and the brief (with recommended reading)
1. **M-005.3 boxes lie outside M0.** The Golden Gate, Oakland and Bay-wide checks cannot run in `L0_30_36`. Reading: they are gated at the M1 tile (`L0_26_37`); M0 gates the pipeline RMSE and the tile minimum; the checker must say "not applicable", never pass vacuously.
2. **Viewpoints V1 to V3 are outside the tile** but the brief demands captures "from the R-004 viewpoints inside this tile". Reading: V4 and V5 gate; V1 to V3 deferred to M1/M2 and listed as pending.
3. **M-005.7 R-ind needs PDAL, not yet in `environment.yml`** (Q-NEW-g). Risk: the independent comparison cannot be produced. Reading: add `pdal` as the default says; if it fails, the evaluator must be told the gate is not run (not passed), and the DataSF 2010 heights serve as a degraded reference.
4. **NGS control points in a downtown tile** are few (the baseline was 13 over 5 miles) and benchmarks on buildings are filtered out. Reading: if fewer than 5, report and rely on the pipeline check; do not lower the thresholds.
5. **R-ind includes vegetation and the "hand-crafted landmark" meshes (F-041)**, so IoU 0.90 is taste-dependent. Reading: accept as Assumed, with a recorded calibration run; M0 landmarks are data extrusions (no hand-crafted meshes), so M0 is the cleanest the metric will ever be.
6. **Hand-crafted landmark replacement (F-041).** `bid` of a replaced building must stay (R-004 §6.5 allows it). Reading: the extruded OSM building is the M0 landmark; hand-crafting happens later behind the same `bid`.
7. **Eye height of 10 m in V4** is not a mech's eye. Reading: also capture V4 at about 70 m eye height (80 m mech head) from the same position, as a non-gating, reported pair.
8. **Flat world and 196 m curvature at 50 km** (Q-NEW-b) does not affect this tile. Reading: out of scope, listed only.
9. **Water level**: R-004 uses a static +0.969 m MSL; the brief says "sea-level water plane". Reading: static MSL, tides later (Q-NEW-f); noted in the manifest.
10. **Transamerica in stepped parts (60 to 260 m)** gives several `bid`s for one tower, while M-005.5 counts footprint matches by `bid`. Reading: parts share a `part_of` parent; the count rule uses buildings, and the destruction owner is the parent.
11. **Brief says "committed baked tile data under the path the spec gives"** while R-004 puts the cache under the git-ignored `Pipelines/geo/cache/tiles/v1/`. Contradiction. Reading: baked `geo_tile@v1` output goes in a committed folder (LFS) separate from `cache/`; raw downloads stay ignored.

## 4. Evidence the implementer must produce (`evidence/T-003/`)
- `manifest.json` (+ `crs/baytm_v1.wkt`) and a manifest-completeness test log; `LICENCES` and the credits-text diff output.
- M-005.1 to 8 raw outputs (JSON/CSV) from the Python checker with inputs and thresholds echoed; `not_applicable`/`pending` flags stated per sub-measure; the float64 BayTM export from Unity with `bid`, anchors, heights, sampled control points.
- Skyline captures V4, V5 (full-res and 270-row), the R-src and R-ind masks, the overlay images and IoU/MAE; the 70 m-eye V4 variant.
- Top-down (terrain relief + water + buildings), oblique (waterfront at three bearings), shoreline close-up, depth map and histogram, height-versus-mech elevation plot, fault map with distances.
- Two-run determinism hash table; second-run "up to date" log; second-tile bake log (`L0_26_37` or other); synthetic-neighbour seam check; floating-origin shift check.
- Tile statistics: triangles, draw calls, memory, disk, building/road counts, `height_source` counts, bake time, command and commit hash for each; pytest and Unity EditMode XML; deliberately-corrupted-manifest rejection log.
- `EVIDENCE.md` listing, for every criterion above, the exact artifact path and the producing command.

## 5. Open points for the founders (recommended default, marked Assumed)
1. Accept `L0_30_36` as M0 (Ferry Building, towers, W2 tower). Default: yes (Assumed).
2. Skyline "recognisable" panel: two reviewers, three decoys. Default: yes; the numeric IoU gates stay primary.
3. Triangle and draw-call gates 1.5 M / 1,500 and ≤ 100 MB committed data. Default: use these until R-003 supplies a budget; revise by orchestrator.
4. Eye heights for gameplay skyline: add a 70 m mech-eye V4. Default: yes, reported only.
5. Do we want a fictional fault through downtown for spectacle (Q-NEW-h)? Default: no; real offshore San Andreas and Hayward faults only, per P3.
6. Roads at M0: ground-hugging ribbons or true mesh with bridge decks? Default: ribbons on the terrain, with the Bay Bridge approach as a separate deck; refine when destruction needs it.
7. OSM share-alike (Q-NEW-a): default to publishing OSM-derived layers under ODbL, legal review before Steam.
8. Pre-2010 stale heights: should recent buildings missing from OSM be added by hand (e.g. 181 Fremont)? Default: no invented buildings at M0 (brief); list candidates in a `known_gaps.md` for F-041.
