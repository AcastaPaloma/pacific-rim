# T-005 expectation draft B: one mech body that walks with physics and intent control

Author: expect-B, written before any build, without reading the other draft, evidence, evaluations or code. Pillars: P1, P2. IDs: F-001, F-002, F-003 (M0 part), F-009 (event only), D-003, D-004, D-007, D-008, D-013, D-018 (M0 part), M-001@v1, M-002@v1, M-006@v1.
Test names below are proposed; the implementer may rename them but must keep one named test or artifact per criterion.

## 0. What the founders picture
Pick up the controller, push the stick forward. Nothing visible happens for a moment, then the hips shift, one foot lifts slowly, and a 80 m machine commits to a first step it cannot take back. It takes about 7 to 13 s to reach cruise (~9 m/s) over 2 to 4 steps. Releasing the stick does not stop it: it carries on 1.5 to 3 steps, leaning, then plants and settles. A turn is a sequence of foot placements over several seconds, never a spin. Every footfall is a definite event with a weight-sized thud. It can be knocked over, and then it falls slowly and truly.

## 1. Acceptance criteria
Verdict rule for 1 to 6: all listed sub-measures PASS in one commit, values stored next to thresholds (M-001@v1 verdict is "all 13 pass").

**Feel: timing and limits (M-001@v1)**
1. Cruise gait. Walk captures `walk_fr010/020/030`: M-001.1 Fr in [0.10, 0.30] (never > 0.35), cruise command within ±0.03 of `fr_cruise_nom`; M-001.2 stride period ratio in [0.85, 1.15]; M-001.3 duty factor in [0.60, 0.72]. Artifact: metric tool output per capture.
2. Start. Capture `start`: M-001.10 0 to 90 % of cruise in 2 to 4 steps (6.6 to 13.2 s); M-001.6 net start acceleration <= 1.38 m/s². Also (new, proposed, see Q-b): the first visible response to a stick push (CoM begins moving toward the first stance foot, or swing foot unloads) is logged with its delay in seconds, and is <= 1.5 s. Test `Start_FirstResponseDelay`.
3. Stop. Captures `stop`, `estop`: M-001.11 normal stop in 1.5 to 3 steps (4.9 to 9.9 s); M-001.12 emergency stop >= max(3.1 s, m·v/F_brake,max); M-001.4 peak fore-aft CoM accel <= 0.30 g in every capture, including a stop and a stick snap to neutral. Stopping is by stepping and leaning, visible in the strip (CoM ahead of the foot, then caught).
4. Turn. Captures `turn90_inplace`, `curve`: M-001.7 peak yaw rate <= 26.7 °/s; M-001.8 yaw accel <= 4.0 °/s²; M-001.9 yaw rate while above half cruise <= 0.085 g / v (5.3 °/s at cruise); M-001.5 lateral accel <= 0.085 g. A 90° turn in place takes >= 6.7 s and is made by foot placement; no loaded foot twists (M-002 covers it).
5. Commitment. Test `Intent_Reversal`: a full reversal of the stick mid-stride during swing must not change the swing foot's landing place by teleport or snap; the reversal is carried out through the next footfall(s). Pass: swing-foot target moves continuously, no instantaneous change of any foot or root velocity > 0.30 g·dt, and the stop or reversal respects 3 and 4.
6. Panel. M-001.13 median overall >= 8/10, no scenario median <= 6, n >= 3, rubric as in R-001 §7, from the frame strips and a real-time clip of cruise, start, stop, 90° turn, curve.

**Feet (M-002@v1)**
7. No sliding. M-002.1 max slip per contact < 0.5 % of `L_f` read from the header (0.098 m for 19.6 m); M-002.2 p95 reported (< 0.0025 L_f); M-002.3 every step logged at fixed dt <= 0.02 s in float64; M-002.4 `contact` flag agrees with the hysteresis on >= 99 % of steps; M-002.5 zero excluded contacts in locomotion. Worst 5 contacts listed with ids. Must hold for every capture, including the turn and the push of criterion 11.

**Honesty of the physics (M-006@v1, P2)**
8. All of M-006.1 to M-006.8 pass in one commit with the R-005 §8 thresholds: sanity (masses, g exactly 9.81, damping 0), passive drop energy loss <= 1.3 % (never a gain > 0.1 %), two-body momentum, ground pressure (single stance 654 kPa ± 2 %, double 327 kPa ± 5 %, stride impulse = m g T ± 2 %), fall energy (ω_impact 0.57 rad/s ± 5 %, t_fall 8.2 s ± 10 %, total ~3.6 GJ ± 10 %), torque and power (saturation <= 5 % of steps, mean positive power 30 to 120 MW, peak <= 450 MW), no solver explosions (zero NaN, zero clamps outside falls), repeatability (3 runs, CoM within 1 cm at 60 s, identical footfall step indices). Same scripted run used for M-001 must also show standing mean |P| <= 5 MW.
9. No hidden cheats. Test `Honesty_NoKinematicCheats`: during every scenario the root pose is never written, no body is pinned or teleported, no velocity is overwritten, no drag or damping beyond the declared drive damping (read-back damping = 0). Any assistance (stabilising force or torque, kinematic constraint, friction boost, gain beyond forceLimit) must be listed in `physics_log` as `external_work` (J per step) and in EVIDENCE.md with peak N and N·m and the fraction of steps active, behind a default-off flag, and the walk must be re-run with the flag off with the results reported side by side. Zero declared assistance is the target; a declared assist is acceptable only if it is small and quantified (Q-d).
10. Mass-honest masses. Body is built from the config asset only (R-005 segment table, `mech_frame@v1`); sum of link masses equals config within 0.1 %; changing config values changes behaviour with no code edit.

**Limits and abuse**
11. Over-commanded intent. Test `Limits_OverCommand`: full-stick from rest, instant 180° target, a target beyond reach, and stick jitter at 30 Hz. The body must clamp to the criterion 2 to 4 limits (no value above limit in the log), never fall, and never exceed forceLimit (M-006.6). The log shows the intent as received and the command as limited, so the clamp is visible and its size is known.
12. Shove. Test `Limits_Shove`: horizontal push of 0.05 m·g for 2 s while standing is absorbed without a step; 0.3 m·g for 2 s causes a capture step, not a fall (R-005 §5.4 item 4). Push applied as a real external force on the body (logged in `ext_f`), with the contact excluded from M-002 only under the stated rule. A larger shove (>= 0.6 m·g for 2 s, value reported) must end in a fall.
13. Slope. Test `Limits_Slope`: walk on a rigid ramp of 3° and 5° (up and down, when the ramp scene exists; else state "not built, Q"); it either walks within criteria 1, 3, 7 or declares the max slope it fails at; it must not slide down while standing (CoM drift <= 5 cm in 60 s) and must fail by falling, not by exploding.
14. Falling. Test `Fall_LossOfBalance`: when balance is truly lost (shove from 12, or an inhibited foot-placement), the body goes limp-to-braced-down under physics: no recovery by force, the fall is slow (seconds, matching 8.2 s from 5°, 5.6 s from 15°), energy released matches §4 (see 15), no joint violates its range, and it ends at rest with every link asleep disabled (sleep threshold 0). `Fall` is emitted as an event for later damage.
15. §4 agreement. Fall energy released stored with the CoM height used; accepted if within ±10 % of m·g·h_cm for that height and within the §4 "≈ 4 GJ" order (3.6 to 4.4 GJ for the reference).

**Footfall event (F-009)**
16. Exact step. Test `FootContact_ExactStep`: for every contact in every capture exactly one `FootContact` bus event is published, in the same simulation step that the log first shows contact (L_step = 0 in >= 99 %, <= 1 in 100 %, matching R-001 §6); it carries foot id, position, normal, vertical impulse, contact pressure, and (proposed) approach velocity, CoP, step index. A test subscriber standing in for the T-004 camera records one impulse per event, no misses, no duplicates, and the amplitude correlates with vertical impulse over a spread >= 3x (Pearson r >= 0.90; a separate M-NEW-a, informational here). Lift-off is also an event.
17. Plausible footfall size. Per walk contact: peak vertical force in 1.0 to 2.0 body weights; touchdown vertical speed reported (target <= 1.0 m/s, Q-NEW-c); heel-first contact visible in the strip.

**Extension points (D-003, D-004)**
18. Control contract. Exactly the MODULARITY §4.1 types exist in `Control.Contracts`: `IPilotSource`, `PilotInput`, `IntentFrame {move, target, effort}`, `IIntentArbiter`, `PilotSeat`; `SinglePilotArbiter` is the only arbiter. Test `Seat_TwoPilotsSwapArbiter`: a test arbiter (e.g. one that merges two scripted sources) is swapped in without editing any class outside the test assembly, and the mech walks. The body never reads a device directly; a scripted source and the Input System source are interchangeable and produce identical logs from identical inputs.
19. N mechs. Test `Mechs_NIndependent`: spawn 1, 2 and 4 mechs from the same config in one scene (separated, one on a different mass/height); each is controlled by its own seat; no static singletons; every log, event and foot id carries the unit id; foot count comes from config (a test with 3 or 4 foot entries builds and logs without code change). Behaviour of mech A is unchanged within the M-006.8 tolerance whether B exists or not.
20. Layers. `Physics.Body` has no reference to Control or Units; cross-module use only via `*.Contracts` and the Core bus (assembly-reference test, `Arch_NoForbiddenRefs`); every module has `MODULE.md`; feature flags default off; no tuning numbers in behaviour code (grep test over the config keys).
21. Visual attach point. A part-name to segment mapping is exposed by config so the T-007 model can attach with no code change. Primitive visuals scale in the frame strip with a visible 1.8 m figure marker.

**Later systems**
22. Parts removed. Test `Damage_PartRemoval`: remove or detach an arm, a hand, or the head (and, as a harder case, a thigh) at runtime through the body contract, in standing and walking. The articulation stays valid (no NaN, no explosion, M-006.7 holds), total mass, CoM and inertia update from the actual links, the controller reads them (no cached values), and it either keeps walking with a visible, logged change in gait (lean, shorter step) or falls under physics. Never a freeze or exception. Event `PartDetached` carries the link id and mass.
23. External impulses. Test `Impulse_Any`: a one-shot impulse of 1e7 to 1e8 N·s at a named link (strike, throw) is accepted through the body contract; momentum before and after the application is within 0.5 %, and response (stagger, step, fall) is physical. Slip exclusion applies only as in M-002.
24. Mass range. Test `Mass_Sweep`: the same code and the same gain rules (formulas in config, not hand-picked numbers) walk at 6,000, 10,000 and 15,000 t and at H = 76, 80, 88 m, reproducing the §10 table with gains derived from mass and inertia. All pass criteria 1, 3, 7, 8 at each corner; stop time t* in band at every corner and time-to-stop grows with mass where power binds (M-001.12). Corners that fail must be named with reason; light tall corners (hull density < 1,025 kg/m³) only warn.
25. Repeatability. Same build, same machine, scripted walk scenario run 3 times: CoM within 1 cm at 60 s, identical footfall step indices, identical `gait_log` event order (M-006.8). Logs are byte-comparable apart from timestamps.

## 2. Disagreements and which value I use
- Cruise Fr: R-001 nominal 0.20 (cruise within ±0.03, M-001.1); R-005 tunes and measures at Fr 0.25 (10.15 m/s; M-006.6, M-006.8 walks). 0.25 is inside the band but outside ±0.03 of 0.20. I use R-001 for M-001 (0.20 nominal) and require M-006.6 power to be measured at the same nominal and also, as a stress case, at 0.25. Flag Q-a.
- Stop: R-005 §5.2 says a capture-point stop completes in one to two steps; R-001 M-001.11 demands >= 1.5 steps normal. I use R-001 (floor 1.5 steps).
- CoM height and foot: R-001 44.8 m and 19.6 x 7.65 m foot (slip threshold 0.098 m); R-005 42.5 m and 15 x 10 m foot (slip 0.075 m); §4 ~40 m. Both keep 150 m². Criteria use the values in the config/`mech_frame` actually built, copied to the log headers; F_on/F_off and thresholds scale with the header. The two research files and `mech_frame_balanced.json` must agree in the build, and the mismatch is stated in EVIDENCE.md. 3.6 to 4.4 GJ accepted for fall energy.
- Sample rate: R-001 assumes 50 Hz logs (dt <= 0.02); R-005 uses 1/60 s. 1/60 satisfies "<= 0.02"; I use 1/60.
- Contact thresholds: R-001 F_on 0.03 m·g / F_off 0.01 m·g (M-002.4), R-005 footfall at 0.05 m·g·dt impulse and lift-off at 0.02 m·g for 2 steps. The event must follow one rule that reproduces M-002.4 agreement >= 99 %; I take R-001's rule and require the log header to declare which.
- Power: R-001 P = 200 MW (assumed), R-005 mean 30 to 120 MW, peak 450 MW, Q-NEW-b 300 MW. For T-005 I use the R-005 measured bands and treat 200 MW as the available budget; not blocking.
- Gait loads: R-005 allows a ≈ 0.2 g from friction (mu 0.6 foot); R-001 uses 0.30 g and mu 0.4. I use the stricter pair: accel cap 0.30 g only if grip allows, and the body never commands more than mu_avail/1.5 allows.
- Panel in M-001.13 is a judgement metric, so it cannot be the sole gate for 1 to 5.

## 3. What would disappoint them
- A stick push that makes the mech move within a quarter second, or a stick release that stops it within a stride. Instant response means P1 failed.
- A mech that moves 9 m/s with feet cycling like a person (cadence > 60 steps/min) or crawls so slowly it reads as slow motion (Fr < 0.1).
- Skating: the body travels while a foot is planted, or feet slide on turns.
- Turning by spinning the torso or root with the feet dragged round; a 90° turn that takes 2 s.
- Perfect balance without cause: a push of any size never matters, or the mech is held upright by an invisible force.
- Falling like a toy: a fast topple, a bounce, a settle with jitter, a body that lies twitching, or a fall energy that disagrees with 4 GJ.
- Landing without weight: footfall with no distinguishable stomp strength, event arriving a frame late, or two events for one step.
- Gaits that need a retune whenever mass changes, or a body that breaks (NaN, flying limbs, stretched joints) when an arm is removed.
- Hard-coded two feet, two players, 80 m, 10,000 t, or one global mech.
- Honesty hidden in tuning: damping set to look heavy, drag on the root, or gravity or time scale changes.
- Evidence that is only a video, a mean without the worst case, or a PASS without values.
- The mech jitters or hums at rest (standing mean |P| high, CoM wandering).

- Test results XML for every named test above (EditMode and PlayMode) and the dependency-test output.
- Frame strips (PNG): >= 2 full gait cycles side and three-quarter, start, stop, turn, fall, shove, part removal, each with a 1.8 m figure and a scale bar; plus a real-time clip for the panel and the panel's scored table.
- Config diff showing the 6,000 / 10,000 / 15,000 t and 76 / 88 m variants and the `Mass_Sweep` table.
- N-mech capture (4 mechs) and a swap-arbiter test log.

## 5. Open points (recommended default, Assumed)
- Q-a. Nominal cruise Fr: 0.20 (R-001) or 0.25 (R-005)? Default 0.20 for the shipped command, test both, founders pick from the calibration sweep.
- Q-b. Input-to-first-motion delay: not bounded by any research file. Default <= 1.5 s to a visible response, <= 6.6 s to cruise start as per M-001.10. Founders may want it snappier at the expense of weight (P1).
- Q-c. Is the 7 to 13 s start and 90° turn >= 6.7 s too sluggish to be fun? Default keep (derived limits, A-pending-calibration); calibration captures (R-001 §9 a to d) go to the founders.
- Q-d. How much assisted balance is tolerable if full dynamic balance misses the cycle? Default: none allowed to pass M-001 silently; assist <= 1 % of m·g in force or 1 % of the ankle limit in torque, always declared, flag-gated, and a stated Q opened.
- Q-e. Heel-strike harshness (Q-NEW-c): default 1.0 m/s touchdown speed.
- Q-f. Lowest allowed mass for H = 76 to 88 m given hull density (Q-NEW-a): default 8,500 t x (H/80)³ so mechs sink; the 6,000 t corner still runs but warns.
- Q-g. Slopes and soil: M0 is rigid ground. Default slope test only if a ramp scene exists; no sinkage expected (Physics.Contact later).
- Q-h. Shove magnitude defining "fall" (default 0.6 m·g for 2 s) and the part-removal set (default: a hand, an arm, the head; thigh as stretch).
