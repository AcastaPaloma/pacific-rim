# T-005 · One mech body that walks with physics and intent control — reconciled expectation spec (v1)

Reconciled by the orchestrator from `expectations/T-005.A.md` (A, items n) and `expectations/T-005.B.md` (B, items n), 2026-11-14. The cited draft items carry the detail (capture names, tolerances, test procedures) and are part of this spec. Metric thresholds are those written in `research/R-001.md` (`M-001@v1`, `M-002@v1`) and `research/R-005.md` (`M-006@v1`); they are `Assumed, pending calibration` where the research says so.
What the founders picture: B §0 (push the stick; a pause; the hips shift; a first step that cannot be taken back; 7–13 s to cruise; 1.5–3 steps to stop; turns made of foot placements; every footfall a definite event; it can be knocked over and then falls slowly and truly).

## Acceptance criteria (gating configuration: 80 m, 10,000 t, rigid flat ground, **all assistance off**)
**Feel (M-001@v1; verdict rule: all 13 sub-measures pass in one commit, values stored next to thresholds)**
- **C1 Cruise gait:** M-001.1, .2, .3 on `walk_fr010/020/030`. (A1, A2, B1)
- **C2 Start:** M-001.10 and M-001.6 on `start`; weight shift then first step, no velocity jump. (A5, A17, B2)
- **C3 Stop:** M-001.11 on `stop`, M-001.12 on `estop`, M-001.4 in every capture; stopping by stepping and leaning. (A3, A5, B3)
- **C4 Turn:** M-001.7, .8, .9, .5 on `turn90_inplace` and `curve`; by foot placement, no loaded foot twists. `walk_scn` turns in place after slowing. (A4, A17, B4)
- **C5 Commitment:** a stick reversal mid-swing never teleports or snaps a foot or the root; it is carried out through the next footfalls. (B5)
- **C6 Weight in the body:** CoM leads each step and is caught by the landing foot; visible in the side strip and the CoM-vs-stance-foot plot. (A7)
- **C7 Panel:** M-001.13, three independent raters using the R-001 §7 rubric on anonymised strips and a real-time clip: median ≥ 8/10, no scenario median ≤ 6. The panel is necessary but never sufficient: it cannot rescue a failed numeric sub-measure. Founder scores replace the agent panel at calibration. (A6, B6)

**Feet (M-002@v1)**
- **C8** M-002.1–.5 in every locomotion capture, the turn, `stomp` and the shove test; worst five contacts listed with IDs. (A19, B7)

**Honest physics (M-006@v1, P2)**
- **C9** M-006.1–.8 all pass in one commit with the R-005 §8 thresholds (unit sanity, energy drift, momentum, ground pressure, fall energy, torque/power, no solver explosion, repeatability); standing mean |P| ≤ 5 MW. (A10–A13, A20, B8, B25)
- **C10 No hidden cheats.** The root is never written, no body pinned or teleported, no velocity overwritten, no drag or damping beyond the declared drive damping; `external_work` is 0 in every locomotion capture. `EVIDENCE.md` has a "declared assistance and cheats" section even if it says "none". (A8, B9)
- **C11 Assistance rule.** The gate is judged with assistance **off**. If the walk only passes with an assist, that is not a pass: the assist is listed with peak N and N·m, RMS, fraction of steps and fraction of ankle force limit, behind a default-off flag, the flag-off and flag-on metric tables are reported side by side, and the task goes back through the loop (orchestrator §5.5). An assist is never active in fall, `stomp` or toppling tests. (A9, B9; see Q on assistance)
- **C12 Config-built body.** Built only from the config asset; link masses sum to the config total within 0.1%; no tuning numbers in behaviour code; gains come from the R-005 formulas evaluated on config mass and inertia. (A24, B10, B20)

**Intent control (D-013, F-002)**
- **C13** The pilot supplies only an `IntentFrame`; scenario scripts contain no joint targets or forces. Walking to a target point ends within one step length of it. (A14)
- **C14 Over-commanded intent** (3× cruise speed, instant reversal, 180° heading snap, stop in one step, a target out of reach, 30 Hz stick jitter): carried out as the nearest feasible motion with all M-001 limits still holding, never obeyed unphysically and never refused silently; the log shows intent as received and command as limited, with the limiting cause. An unsurvivable intent ends in a controlled refusal or a physical fall, never a teleport or explosion. (A15, A16, B11)

**Balance and falling (F-003 M0 part)**
- **C15 Shove.** A real external force on the body, logged: 0.05 m·g for 2 s absorbed without stepping; 0.3 m·g for 2 s answered with a capture step, not a fall; a larger shove (value reported, default ≥ 0.6 m·g for 2 s) ends in a fall. (A21, B12)
- **C16 Falling.** Loss of balance produces a fall under physics only: no scripted ragdoll switch, no recovery by force, slow (matching the M-006.5 times), energy as in the table above, no joint beyond its range, ends at rest without jitter; `fall_start`/`fall_end` events for later damage. Brace, wading and get-up are not built and not blocked (hook points documented). (A21, B14, B15)

**Footfall event (F-009 M0 part)**
- **C17** Exactly one `FootContact` per contact, published on the Core bus in the simulation step in which the log first shows contact (same step in ≥ 99% of contacts, ≤ 1 step in 100%), carrying foot ID, unit ID, step index, position, normal, vertical impulse, contact pressure, approach velocity; lift-off is also an event. A test subscriber standing in for the camera records one impulse per event with no misses or duplicates. (A18, B16)
- **C18 Plausible footfalls.** Peak vertical force per walking contact 1.0–2.0 body weights (M-006.4); touchdown speed reported; heel-first contact visible. (A12, B17)

**Extension points and architecture (D-003, D-004, MODULARITY)**
- **C19 Control contract.** Exactly the `MODULARITY.md` §4.1 types in `Control.Contracts`, `SinglePilotArbiter` the only shipped arbiter; a test arbiter merging 2 and 3 scripted pilots is swapped in without editing anything outside the test assembly; scripted and Input System sources are interchangeable; the body never reads a device. (A22, B18)
- **C20 N mechs.** 1, 2 and 4 mechs in one scene, each with its own seat, config instance and log; every event and foot ID carries the unit ID; foot count comes from config; no static singletons; mech A's behaviour is unchanged within the M-006.8 tolerance whether B exists or not. (A23, B19)
- **C21 Layers and docs.** `Physics.Body` references neither Control nor Units; cross-module use only via `*.Contracts` and the Core bus (assembly-reference test); asmdef, `MODULE.md`, default-off flag, EditMode and PlayMode test assemblies per module; pure logic engine-free under `dotnet test`. (A25, B20)
- **C22 Visual attach point.** A part-name → segment mapping lets the T-007 model attach with no code change; primitive visuals in the strips include a 1.8 m figure marker and a scale bar. (A25, B21)
- **C23 Logs validate.** `gait_log@v1` and `physics_log@v1` pass the validators; an invalid log is a fail. (A26)
- **C24 Repeatability.** Three runs of the scripted 60 s sequence: CoM within 1 cm at 60 s, identical footfall step indices and event order. (A20, B25)

**Reported, not gating for M0 (must be run or explicitly marked "not built" with a reason; failures become ledger findings, not task failures)**
- **R1 Mass and height corners** (6,000 / 15,000 t, 76 / 88 m) by config only: must load and pass M-006.1 (gating part, A24); the full walk table at each corner is reported, with failing corners named and explained (B24).
- **R2 Parts removed** at runtime (hand, arm, head; thigh as stretch): no NaN or explosion, mass/CoM/inertia update from the actual links (B22). Damage is a later task; this checks nothing blocks it.
- **R3 External impulse** of 1e7–1e8 N·s at a named link through the body contract, momentum within 0.5% (B23).
- **R4 Slope** 3° and 5° if a ramp scene exists (B13).
- **R5 Input-to-first-visible-response delay** in seconds, and the latency table of B §4 (stick-to-first-response, to steady gait, release-to-stop) (B2). Proposed metric, unregistered: cannot fail.
- **R6 Footfall amplitude correlation** with vertical impulse (R-001's proposed footfall-sync metric): informational until registered (B16).

## What would disappoint the founders
The union of A §3 and B §3. In short: a person in a suit (fast cadence, bouncy hips) or slow-motion; instant response to the stick or a stop within a stride; skating feet; spin turns; a mech held up by an invisible hand that never falls, or one that topples in a second like a toy; jitter, hum or creep at rest; weightless footfalls or a late event; intent that ignores limits or refuses without saying why; a walk that needs hand retuning when mass changes; anything hard-coded to two feet, two players, 80 m or 10,000 t; "heaviness" faked with damping, drag, gravity or time scale; evidence that is a video without values or a mean without the worst case.

## Questions for the founders (proposed, to be numbered by the scribe; defaults in use)
- **Q-NEW (cruise pace):** nominal cruise Froude 0.20 (≈ 9 m/s, 6.6 s stride) versus 0.25. Default 0.20; the Froude sweep captures go into the calibration packet.
- **Q-NEW (sluggishness versus weight):** start in 7–13 s, 90° turn in ≥ 6.7 s, 5.3 °/s turning at cruise, and the first visible response to the stick (default bound 1.5 s, unregistered). Are the derived limits fun? Default: keep the derived limits; decide from the captures.
- **Q-NEW (assisted balance):** if full dynamic balance cannot be reached, is a small declared stabilising assist acceptable (draft A: yes if flagged and reported; draft B: at most 1% of m·g or of the ankle torque limit, never silent)? The drafts disagree and this touches P2, so it is the founders' call. Default in use: the gate is judged with assistance off.
- **Q-NEW (foot shape):** 15 × 10 m (R-005, in the shared frame table) versus 19.6 × 7.65 m (R-001), both 150 m². Default: 15 × 10 m.
- **Q-NEW (minimum mass):** at 6,000 t the hull is lighter than sea water and the mech would float (R-005). Default: the 6,000 t corner loads with a warning; a floor near 8,500 t × (H/80)³ is proposed.

## Round 2 after re-research (2026-11-14; spec v1.1, appended, nothing above is changed)
Three fix cycles ended with an honest unassisted walk at steady Froude 0.023, and the task was escalated to research under the three-cycle rule. `research/R-008.md` found: the controller binds now (a flat-footed stance driven only by a force loop is capped at Froude 0.005–0.025); the body as given can reach Froude 0.20 with about 5° of joint margin, but only with human-like heel and toe roll, about 10° of forward pelvic tilt and trunk counter-rotation cancelling 80–87% of the legs' angular momentum; no data or threshold change is needed and no assistance is recommended. The loop re-enters at implementation with the research's approach; the fix-cycle counter restarts for round 2.
- **Target for round 2.** Every criterion C1–C24 stands. The registered metric is unchanged: band [0.10, 0.30], nominal cruise 0.20 ± 0.03. R-008's estimate for this approach is Froude 0.10–0.15, so round 2 is expected to bring the walk into the band, not necessarily to the nominal; whether M0 may be accepted at the band floor is the founders' decision (recorded with the walk-gate question), not the implementer's or the evaluators'.
- **Ruling on C20 (mech independence), proposed by R-008 and adopted as Assumed pending the founders:** exact independence between articulations sharing one PhysX scene and ground is not achievable, because float-level differences are amplified by the gait. C20's last clause is tested as: mech A is bit-identical whether or not other mechs exist while those mechs are not walking; when they walk, the first divergence is ≤ 1e-4 m and the divergence after 20 s lies within the spread of a self-perturbation ensemble of mech A alone (the ensemble and its size stated in the test). The original 1 cm figure is kept as a reported value.
- **`M-006@v1.1` (Assumed, pending founder calibration), from R-007 and R-008:** M-006.5 for a limp fall gates on passivity (cumulative energy gain ≤ 1%, no single step above 7.8e7 J) and on 3.6 GJ ± 10% released to rest for the reference body; friction and joint-limit work the engine does not report are stated, not gated. Whole-capture reporting is mandatory for every sub-measure (no trimmed windows). Reports carry both v1 and v1.1 blocks.
- **Still the founders' call:** any stabilising assist, any change to the body data, any change to the registered band, and whether the modules may be merged as infrastructure before the gate is met.
