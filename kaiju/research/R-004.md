# R-004: GIS data sources, tile scheme, and the M-005 metric spec

Agent: `research-geo` · Date: 2026-11-14 · Status: Proposed
Requirements covered (quoted in full):
- F-040: "1:1 Bay Area generated from open data: terrain (USGS elevation), seabed (NOAA bathymetry), buildings (OpenStreetMap footprints and heights), roads, faults (USGS fault database)" (P3, Accepted)
- F-041: "Hand-crafted landmarks at true scale: both bridges, downtown towers, the port of Oakland, Alcatraz, etc." (P3, P4)
- F-042: "World streaming with a floating origin. float32 resolution at 100 km is $2^{-7}$ m ≈ 0.8 cm, enough to make joints and IK jitter, so the origin must be shifted" (P3)
- D-010: "World: the **full Bay Area at 1:1 scale**, open world, with kaiju roaming"
- D-014: "Kaiju emerge along real Bay Area fault lines (San Andreas, Hayward, etc.), with an earthquake before each emergence"
- M-005 | Bay is accurate | "Landmark positions within X m of GIS data. Building height distribution matches OSM. Skyline silhouette from known viewpoints matches reference photos"

**Question.** Which open products build a 1:1 Bay (terrain, seabed, buildings, roads, faults, water), in what coordinate system and tile layout, which tile is M0, and how is "Bay is accurate" measured?

**Headline.** One seamless topobathy surface exists and is the right base: NOAA NCEI **CUDEM 1/9 arc-second** (NAD83 + NAVD88, ~3 m, COG tiles on S3, US public domain). Its land agrees with the 3DEP 1 m lidar DEM to a median of 0.02 m, so 3DEP 1 m can be overlaid on land with no visible seam. Buildings and roads come from **OSM** (68.8 % of M0 buildings carry `height`, 74.5 % `height` or `building:levels`). Missing heights fall back to **DataSF footprints** (PDDL), with a caveat: DataSF heights are from 2010 and read 0.2 m at the Salesforce Tower site. Faults come from the **USGS Qfaults ArcGIS service**. The world uses a custom **Bay Transverse Mercator** (scale error 0 to +55 ppm; UTM 10N is −196 to −400 ppm). Tiles are **2048 m**. The M0 tile is **BayTM tile (30,36)**: downtown, Telegraph Hill, the Bay Bridge W2 tower, and 39 % water down to −29.5 m.

Evidence: every sample below was fetched on 2026-11-14 with `/opt/miniconda3/envs/kaiju/bin/python` into `Pipelines/geo/cache/research/` (git-ignored). Filenames are given as `cache:<name>`.

### 1.2 Terrain + bathymetry: CUDEM is the single seamless surface (recommended)
- **Access.** Bulk directory `https://coast.noaa.gov/htdata/raster2/elevation/NCEI_ninth_Topobathy_2014_8483/` [7]. Files are mirrored on S3 as `https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/dem/NCEI_ninth_Topobathy_2014_8483/CA/ncei19_n{LAT}x{LATfrac}_w{LON}x{LONfrac}_{YEAR}v1.tif`, listed in `urllist8483.txt`. The name gives the NW corner of a 0.25° tile (e.g. `ncei19_n38x00_w122x50_2022v1.tif` covers 37.75–38.00 N, 122.50–122.25 W). The files are Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFFs (512² blocks, overviews 2/4/8/16), so `rasterio` can do windowed HTTP reads with `/vsicurl/`. No API key is needed.
- **Spec (verified from the file).** CRS EPSG:5498 = NAD83 + NAVD88 height. Grid 3.0864e-5° (≈ 3.43 m N–S × 2.71 m E–W at 37.8°N). 8112 × 8112 float32 cells per tile, nodata −9999. Description tag "Topography-Bathymetry", dated 2022-06-24. The metadata states NAVD88 (EPSG:5703), "Not subject to copyright protection within the United States", and DOI 10.25921/ds9v-ky35 [6].
- **Accuracy (stated).** "No quantitative vertical accuracy analysis has been performed". Topographic lidar is assumed better than 0.5 m RMSE. Horizontal accuracy is ≤ 1 m for GNSS-era sources [6]. The framework is described in Amante et al. 2023 [8].
- **Sample (`cache:cudem19_m0.tif`, `cache:cudem19_gg.tif`; 2.9 s for both windows).**
  - M0 window (972 × 680 cells): elevation −29.0 to +113.3 m; 26 % of cells below 0.
  - Golden Gate window: minimum **−112.0 m** at 37.8164 N, 122.4900 W; 80 % of cells below 0. USGS gives 113 m for the scoured bedrock channel [27], so the difference is about 1 m.
  - Spot statistics in §6.3: Oakland Inner Harbor median −15.1 m against a −50 ft MLLW project depth [28]; San Pablo Bay shoal median −2.8 m.
- **Size.** M0 tile: about 1.2 Mcells, so ≈ 2–5 MB per 2 km tile at native resolution. Full Bay: 26 tiles intersect the world box, **5.00 GB** (HEAD request sum).
- **Seam with 3DEP 1 m (measured).** Over 3.9 M land cells in the downtown window, 3DEP − CUDEM has median **+0.02 m**, MAD 0.10 m and p95 |Δ| 1.61 m (the tail is building edges and cliffs). Over water, 3DEP is hydro-flattened to a constant **−1.07 m** or is nodata (33 % of water cells), so it carries no bathymetry. Merge rule: use 3DEP 1 m where it is valid and CUDEM > MHW (+1.80 m NAVD88), use CUDEM elsewhere, and feather over 10 m.
- **CoNED SF Bay TBDEM (evaluated, not recommended).**
  - What it is: a 2 m UTM 10N/NAD83 seamless surface built from 42 datasets (1922–2010), published 2013-03-22, public domain [9][10]. FGDC metadata was fetched (`cache:coned_sfbay_fgdc.xml`): 63638 × 63120 cells, bounds 123.30–121.85 W, 37.32–38.47 N.
  - Access: one **12.39 GB zip** (`TOPOBATHY_SAN_FRANCISCO_ELEV_METERS.zip`) holding a single deflate-compressed GeoTIFF; the local header shows compression method 8. Random access is impossible, so no sample was read.
  - Why not: its land data predate 2010 (no Salesforce-era grading, older lidar), and it would cost 12 GB to get one tile. CUDEM 2022 is newer, tiled, COG and windowable. Keep CoNED only as an independent check for the bathymetry sub-measure (§6.3) once someone fetches it in bulk.

### 1.3 Land detail: 3DEP 1 m and 1/3 arc-second
- **Discovery.** TNM Access API `https://tnmaccess.nationalmap.gov/api/v1/products?datasets=Digital%20Elevation%20Model%20(DEM)%201%20meter&bbox=W,S,E,N&outputFormat=JSON` [1]. Downtown returned 4 overlapping projects for UTM tile x55y419. The newest is `CA_SanFrancisco_B23` (published 2024-08-26), at `https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/Elevation/1m/Projects/CA_SanFrancisco_B23/TIFF/USGS_1M_10_x55y419_CA_SanFrancisco_B23.tif`. Tiles are 10 × 10 km in UTM 10N, about 86.5 MB each.
- **Spec (verified).** EPSG:26910 (NAD83 / UTM 10N). The vertical datum is NAVD88 (GEOID18) per the project report [4]; the TIFF CRS does not carry it. 1.0 m grid, 10012 × 10012 float32, nodata −999999, 512² tiles, overviews 2–32 (COG).
- **Accuracy.** QL2 lidar requires non-vegetated RMSEz ≤ 10 cm; USGS quotes 0.53 m RMSE for the dynamic service and 0.82 m for the 1/3″ seamless layer (CONUS, 2022) [3].
- **Sample (`cache:dem1m_3dep_downtown.tif`, 13.8 MB, 12.8 s).** 2303 × 2234 cells, −9.86 to +94.75 m, 7 % nodata.
- **py3dep (v0.19.0).** `check_3dep_availability(bbox)` returned 1 m, 3 m, 10 m, 30 m and topobathy all available. `get_dem(bbox, 10, crs=4326)` returned a 333 × 353 array, −1.27 to +114.7 m, in 4.8 s; note it came back in EPSG:5070 despite the `crs` argument, so reproject explicitly. `elevation_bycoords(..., source="tep")` returned 91.55 m at Coit Tower and 255.24 m at Sutro Tower in 0.7 s. EPQS `https://epqs.nationalmap.gov/v1/json?x=…&y=…&units=Meters&wkid=4326` gave 91.71 m at Coit Tower at 1 m resolution.
- **Size.** M0: a 2048² float32 window is about 14 MB deflated. Full Bay: **151 unique 10 km tiles, 33.8 GB** taking the newest project per tile (TNM query over the world box, 334 items).
- **Role.** Land detail for urban and played tiles (curbs, cuts, Telegraph Hill cliffs at 1 m instead of 3 m). Fallback is the 1/3″ seamless layer (≈ 10 m) via py3dep for LOD tiles.

### 1.4 Buildings and heights
- **Production access.** Geofabrik `https://download.geofabrik.de/north-america/us/california/norcal-latest.osm.pbf`: **655.9 MB**, refreshed daily (Last-Modified 2026-11-14 02:10 GMT) [16]. Clip with `osmium extract -b` and read with pyosmium. This gives a reproducible snapshot with a replication timestamp, which F-043 persistent building IDs need.
- **Development access.** Overpass `https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter` [13]. The first query got a 502 (server at capacity); the retry succeeded. Overpass is not suitable for the full-Bay build.
- **M0 sample (`cache:osm_m0_buildings.json`, 2.7 MB, OSM base 2026-11-14T11:27Z).** Counted by representative point inside tile (30,36):
  - 2046 `building` features and 454 `building:part`; footprint area 1.242 km².
  - Tag coverage: `height` 68.8 %, `building:levels` 15.5 %, either **74.5 %** (area-weighted height coverage 74.6 %). Parts with `height`: 32.8 %.
  - Height quantiles: p10 8.0, p25 10, p50 13, p75 21, p90 55.3, p99 171 m; maximum 326 m (Salesforce Tower `w431972186`, `height=326`, levels 61). Transamerica `w24222973` is 260 m and is modelled in stepped parts of 60/120/180/240/260 m.
- **DataSF fallback** (`https://data.sfgov.org/resource/ynuv-fyni.geojson?$where=within_box(shape,N,W,S,E)`, Socrata).
  - Licence: **ODC PDDL 1.0**, attribution "City and County of San Francisco" (verified from `api/views/ynuv-fyni.json`) [17]. Footprints come from a 2010 Pictometry model; heights are LiDAR zonal statistics (`hgt_median_m`, `peak_1st_m`, `gnd_min_m` in NAVD88 m).
  - M0: 1969 footprints, 1.137 km², 100 % with heights.
  - **Stale:** the footprint at the Salesforce Tower site reads `hgt_median_m` = 0.2 m (vacant lot in 2010), and 181 Fremont is missing.
  - Agreement with OSM on 1350 matched buildings (≥ 50 % overlap): median |Δ| 0.28 m, p90 0.54 m, MAE 2.63 m. The tight core is expected: OSM SF heights were largely imported from this data in 2016 [18], so the two are **not independent** for heights.
  - Area recall: 91.6 % of DataSF footprint area is covered by OSM, and 84.8 % of OSM area by DataSF (OSM is newer).
  - Centroid offset for 140 large footprints (> 1500 m², IoU > 0.6): median 1.88 m, p90 5.43 m, p95 7.52 m; median IoU 0.88.
- **Height fallback order** (measured on M0):
  1. OSM `height`.
  2. DataSF `hgt_median_m`, used only if the footprint IoU with the OSM footprint is > 0.6 and `peak_1st_m − gnd_min_m` > 3 m (rejects post-2010 sites).
  3. `building:levels` × **4.22 m**. This is the median h/levels on 202 buildings with both tags (IQR 3.56–4.71). It is weak: median |e| 5.9 m, p90 25 m.
  4. Default of 3 levels (12.7 m), flagged `height_source=default`.
- **Other fallbacks (licence check for a commercial game).**
  - Microsoft US Building Footprints: **ODbL**, no heights [20].
  - Overture buildings: **ODbL** because it contains OSM; credit "© OpenStreetMap contributors, Overture Maps Foundation" plus per-source credits (Microsoft, Esri Community Maps, Google Open Buildings) [19]. It adds coverage, not better SF heights.
  - DataSF (PDDL) is the only no-strings height source. All are usable commercially; ODbL adds share-alike on derivative databases (Q-NEW-a).
- **Size.** M0: about 2.5 MB of vector data. Full Bay: on the order of 1.5–2 M buildings inside the NorCal PBF (estimated from the M0 density of 2046 per 4.2 km² over the urban fraction; not measured).

### 1.5 Roads, coastline, water
- **Roads, M0 (`cache:osm_m0_roads_coast.json`).** 4131 `highway` ways, 183 km in total: footway 101.6, tertiary 16.0, service 13.1, residential 12.0, secondary 9.5, primary 5.9 km. Drivable classes total 60.9 km; `lanes` is tagged on 57 %, `width` on 0 %. Width comes from `lanes` × 3.5 m, else a class default.
- **Coastline.** 96 `natural=coastline` ways (110 km) in an SF box, 5.89 km inside the M0 tile.

## 2. Coordinate system for a 1:1 world

### 2.1 Projection choice (computed with pyproj 3.8 / PROJ 9.9 on a 61 × 49 grid over the world box 123.05–121.55 W, 37.15–38.35 N)

| CRS | Point-scale error over the Bay | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| UTM 10N (EPSG:26910/32610) | **−400 to −196 ppm** (mean \|err\| 335 ppm) | Salesforce −365 ppm. GG→San Jose (74.8 km) is 24.9 m short. Fails the 1:1 scale check (§6.6). |
| CA State Plane III (EPSG:2227) | −71 to −16 ppm | US survey feet; convergence up to −1.56° |
| CA Albers (EPSG:3310) | equal-area, **3206 ppm anisotropy** | Not conformal: shapes shear. Rejected. |
| **BayTM, k = 1** (recommended) | **0 to +55 ppm** (mean 18.5) | Salesforce +0.9 ppm; GG→Diablo error +0.18 m over 50 km |
| BayTM, k = 0.99997 | −30 to +25 ppm | Halves the peak error but breaks "1 game metre = 1 m" at the centre |
| Oblique stereographic, k = 0.999985 | −15 to +39 ppm | Marginal gain, less tooling support |

**Recommendation: BayTM@v1.** It is Transverse Mercator on NAD83(2011), with origin 37.75 N, 122.30 W (open Bay water west of Alameda) and k = 1:
```
+proj=tmerc +lat_0=37.75 +lon_0=-122.3 +k=1 +x_0=70912 +y_0=69632 +ellps=GRS80 +units=m +no_defs
```
- **Base datum.** Store the authoritative form as WKT2: `PROJCRS["kaiju BayTM v1 (NAD83(2011))", BASEGEOGCRS EPSG:6318, …]` compounded with NAVD88 height EPSG:5703. This WKT was generated with `pyproj.crs.ProjectedCRS(TransverseMercatorConversion(...), geodetic_crs=CRS("EPSG:6318"))`. The PROJ string alone loses the datum and reads as "unknown, based on GRS80".
- **False origin.** The offsets 70912 = 34.625 × 2048 and 69632 = 34 × 2048 put tile edges on the M0 tile chosen in §4, and keep all world coordinates positive (≈ 4.3–137.5 km E, 3.3–136.5 km N).
- **Why TM over a local tangent plane.** A tangent (ENU) plane keeps heights relative to a plane, so terrain 75 km away would sit d²/2R = **441 m** below the plane. A projection keeps orthometric heights on a flat world, which is what gameplay physics needs (gravity = −y everywhere). Visual curvature is a separate rendering question (Q-NEW-b).
- **Datum caveat.** NAD83(2011) and ITRF2014 differ by **1.50 m** horizontally at SF (pyproj TransformerGroup, epoch 2020). OSM is nominally WGS84, but in practice it is traced from mixed-datum imagery, and pyproj's EPSG:4326→6318 transform is a null (ballpark) operation. Treat OSM coordinates as NAD83 and carry ±1.5 m as datum uncertainty in M-005 (§6.1). Meridian convergence over the world is ±0.47°, so grid north (+z) ≠ true north; the sun and compass must apply γ.

### 2.2 Axis mapping into Unity
- Unity x = BayTM easting (m), y = NAVD88 orthometric height (m), z = BayTM northing (m). The ENU→(x,U,z) swap turns right-handed ENU into Unity's left-handed frame with no mirroring; Cesium for Unity uses the same convention.
- Yaw 0 = +z = grid north. One Unity unit = 1 m. Measured scale error: +1.0 ppm (Salesforce–Transamerica, 790.91 m) to +3.6 ppm (Sutro–Diablo, 49.47 km).

### 2.3 Vertical datum and sea level
NOAA CO-OPS station **9414290 San Francisco**, NTDE epoch 1983–2001, accepted 2003-04-17, metres above station datum [11]:

| Datum | Above STND | Relative to NAVD88 |
|---|---|---|
| MHHW | 3.602 | **+1.798** |
| MHW | 3.416 | +1.612 |
| **MSL** | 2.773 | **+0.969** |
| MLLW | 1.822 | **+0.018** |
| NAVD88 | 1.804 | 0 |

- **Static water plane:** y = **+0.969 m** (MSL, Q-NEW-f).
- **Depth conversions:** nautical-chart depths (MLLW) convert to NAVD88 by −0.018 m. USACE "−50 ft MLLW" is −15.22 m NAVD88.
- **GPS heights:** GEOID18 undulation at SF is N = −32.49 m (NGS mark HT0686 [12]), so ellipsoid h = H_NAVD88 − 32.49 m. Any WGS84 ellipsoid height (GPS, Cesium tiles) must be converted.

### 2.4 Floating origin (F-042)
- **Representation.** World state is stored as float64 BayTM. Unity transforms are float32 relative to a floating origin O, which is always a **tile corner** (a multiple of 2048 m). Tile-local coordinates are therefore exact small integers plus a fraction, and a rebase shifts by exact integers.
- **Rebase rule.** Rebase when the focus point is > 1024 m from O, so |p| < ~3 km for everything within one tile of the focus. The float32 ulp at 2048–4096 m is 2^−12 m = **0.24 mm**; at 4096–8192 m it is 0.49 mm. Compare the 7.8 mm ulp at 65.5–131 km that F-042 flags.
- **Multiplayer.** Host physics in one PhysX scene cannot serve two mechs 50 km apart without precision loss. Either use one `PhysicsScene` per activity region, each with its own origin, or bound player separation (Q-NEW-c).

## 3. Tile scheme and the `geo_tile@v1` data spec

### 3.1 Grid and indexing
- **Base tiles (L0).** 2048 × 2048 m in BayTM. Index i = ⌊x/2048⌋, j = ⌊z/2048⌋. The world box falls in i, j ∈ [0, 67], so the grid is 68 × 68 = 4624 tiles (139.3 km square). Tile ID `L0_{i:02d}_{j:02d}` (M0 = `L0_30_36`).
- **LOD pyramid.** Level L covers 2048·2^L m with the same 2049² samples. L0 is 1 m, L1 2 m, L2 4 m, L3 8 m, L4 16 m (32 km tiles, the horizon layer).
- **Why 2048 m.**
  - The heightmap is 2049² = 2^11 + 1, a native Unity Terrain heightmap resolution, at exactly 1 m spacing.
  - Tile corners are exact float32 and float64 values, which keeps floating-origin rebases exact.
  - About 70 L0 tiles cover the M1 districts.
  - One L0 tile holds roughly 25 mech body lengths.
- **Seam rule.** Samples sit on grid nodes (pixel-is-point) at x0 + k·1 m, k = 0…2048, so neighbours share their edge rows exactly. Vector features are clipped to the tile but carry a global ID, and a building is owned by the tile containing its representative point.

### 3.2 Per-layer cached formats (in `Pipelines/geo/cache/tiles/v1/L{L}/{i}_{j}/`)

| File | Content | Format | M0 size (est.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `terrain.tif` | Merged topobathy surface, master copy | COG float32, 2049², BayTM + NAVD88 WKT2, deflate + predictor 3 | ≈ 10–14 MB |
| `terrain.r16` | Unity import | uint16 little-endian RAW, row 0 = **south** edge, col 0 = west. h = h_min + v·(h_max − h_min)/65535. M0 range −30…+95 m gives a 1.9 mm step; worst Bay tile (Diablo, ~900 m range) 1.4 cm. | 8.4 MB |
| `water.png` | 8-bit mask, 2048² cell-centred: 255 = water surface at MSL, 0 = land/pier | PNG | < 0.5 MB |
| `buildings.geojson` | One feature per building/part. Fields: `bid` (e.g. `osm:w431972186`), `part_of`, `height_m`, `min_height_m`, `levels`, `roof_shape`, `base_navd88_m` (min terrain under footprint), `height_source` ∈ {osm_height, datasf, osm_levels, default}, `name` | GeoJSON with legacy `crs` member = BayTM@v1, metres (GDAL-readable; simple for a C# parser) | ≈ 2.5 MB |
| `roads.geojson` | Fields: `wid`, `class`, `lanes`, `width_m`, `bridge`, `tunnel`, `layer` | GeoJSON (BayTM) | ≈ 1 MB |
| `faults.geojson` | Clip of the global fault layer. Fields: `fault_name`, `section`, `linetype`, `slip_rate`, `slip_sense`, `age` | GeoJSON (BayTM) | 0–50 kB |
| `manifest.json` | See §3.3 | JSON | 2 kB |

- **Global layers** (not tiled): `faults_bay.geojson` (3232 features, 4 MB) and `landmarks.json` (§5).
- **Full Bay estimate.** 4624 L0 tiles × ~20 MB ≈ 90 GB if every tile were at 1 m; ≈ 39 GB is the terrain `.r16` alone. Default: 1 m only for urban and shoreline tiles, L1 (2 m) elsewhere (Q-NEW-e).

**Manifest rules.**
- Every layer lists ≥ 1 source, each with `licence` (an SPDX id or `US-Gov-PD`), `attribution`, `fetched_utc` and `source_sha256`.

- The importer refuses a tile whose manifest lacks any of these; this is sub-measure M-005.8.
