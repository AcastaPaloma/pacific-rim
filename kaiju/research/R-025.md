# R-025: Hangar site (Track 5, Q-104, M-059 draft)

Requirement (D-017): "Between fights: hangar for repair, refit and upgrades. Damage carries over."
Scope here: site, M-059, and what the stub omits. Tile arithmetic uses only the L0_30_36 corners in R-004 §3.3/§4
(SW -122.407531, 37.786854; NE -122.384302, 37.805325; 2048 m tiles, no rotation over a few tiles). Computed with
Python (pyproj, equirectangular offset from those corners, error well under 100 m near SoMa, a few hundred m at the
Presidio). Positions below are from agency records unless marked *Assumed*. T-034 must confirm each against baked
footprints. The Pier 70 and Crissy tiles straddle a tile edge (stated in the table).

## 1. Fit rule
The body is 80 m (brief). No enclosed Bay structure clears 80 m standing: tallest found is Hangar One, 198 ft
(60 m) to the trusses (HAER, NASA; clear height unverified). So every candidate is judged as open-air (no roof, 80 m
vertical free) or as a prone/kneeling service pose (*Assumed*, to be chosen in Q-NEW-a).

## 3. Ranking and recommendation (Q-104)
1. **Crissy Field airfield, L0_27_36 (recommended).** Inside the GG tiles, so no extra bake (true for both L0_27_36 and L0_27_37), and it is the only
   candidate inside either district. Largest open footprint (113,000 m², about 7x the Pier 70 dock plan area of 15,600 m²), no roof
   to collide with, and a real former airfield, matching the "former airfield" idea in Q-002. Its hangars are
   small, so the stub places a *Assumed* open-air hangar pad on the apron and does not model the historic
   buildings.
2. Pier 70 Dry Dock 2, L0_30_34 / L0_31_34. Nearest dock to SoMa, but one extra tile bake and the dock is slated for removal.
3. Treasure Island Building 2, L0_31_37. One extra tile, enclosed, 24 m clear: fails the standing pose.
4. Hangar One: far outside both districts.
Neither SoMa nor GG contains a sourced enclosed site tall enough. Q-NEW-a: confirm that an open-air pad satisfies
"hangar" for M1 (the alternative is a prone-pose roofed site, #3).

## 4. M-059 draft: damage and repair carry-over (gates T-034, hangar@v1)
**Definition.** For a body B, the hangar state H(B) is the serialised `Units.Placeholder` damage record plus the repair
state (per part: current integrity, repaired flag, repair-queue entry). M-059 = number of bitwise differences between
H(B) taken at the end of a fight (or hangar session) and H(B) read back after save, process restart and load into the
next phase. Related: M-050 covers save/load alone. M-059 adds the phase change fight, hangar, fight.

**Procedure.** (all values *Assumed*, to be fixed by T-034)
1. Fixed seed S. Start with an undamaged body. Fight 1: replay a scripted hit list (>= 20 hits across >= 6 parts,
   including one part driven to 0).
2. Save `hangar@v1`. Compute SHA-256 of H(B). Restart the process. Load. Recompute. Record equality.
3. Hangar: run a fixed repair script (repair 2 named parts, leave the rest). Check repaired parts equal the target
   integrity exactly, and unrepaired parts are bitwise unchanged from step 2. Save, restart, load, hash.
4. Fight 2: replay a second scripted hit list that includes re-hitting a repaired part and an unrepaired part.
   Check the unrepaired part continues from its carried value (not from full).
5. Repeat steps 2-4 for 3 full cycles (fight, hangar, fight, hangar, fight, hangar). Run with 3 seeds.

**Threshold.** Zero bitwise differences in every comparison over 3 cycles and 3 seeds (float compared as raw bits,
so no tolerance). Any difference, any part reset to full, or any repair applied to the wrong part fails the measure.
A negative control must fail: loading a save with one damage byte flipped must give a nonzero count.

## 5. What the stub leaves out (no-fluff gate)
- **Refit** (D-017, F-060): no part swap, no loadout change. Reason: needs the part catalogue and mass/balance
  rules, which do not exist before T-005.
- **Upgrades:** no progression, no unlocks.
- **Economy:** no repair cost, time, resources or currency. Repair is instant and free in the debug panel.
- **UI:** debug panel only (per Track 5). No hangar scene art, no historic building models.
- **Site fidelity:** the Crissy apron pad is a spawn anchor on the baked terrain, not a surveyed hangar structure.
- **Co-op:** single body, single player. Multi-body hangar waits for Track 3 follow-up.
- **Persistence outside the cycle:** no cross-session campaign save beyond `hangar@v1`.
Delivered: site anchor, spawn, damage and repair state carried over, gated by M-059.
