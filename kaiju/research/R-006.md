# R-006 · M-005@v1.1: what the first use on the M0 tile taught us (M-005)

Agent `research-geo`, 2026-11-14. Inputs: `research/R-004.md` §5–§6 (M-005@v1), `evaluations/T-003/eval-bay.md` (DEF-T003-bay-1…7), `evaluations/T-003/eval-perf.md` (DEF-T003-perf-1) and `expectations/T-003.md` (C5, C8, C11, C20). I read no implementation code.
**Question.** Four parts of the metric failed for reasons inside the metric itself. Seam: 0.457 m against 0.1 m, with no visible step. Default-height share: 13.3 % against 10 %. Skyline vs R-ind: IoU 0.845 / 0.635 against 0.90. Draw calls were estimated, not measured. How should M-005 change so that each sub-measure measures what a player sees or a physics body feels, with thresholds derived from the sources?
**Note on provenance.** The 0.1 m seam limit (C11) and the 10 % default-share limit (C5) came from the T-003 acceptance criteria, not from R-004 §6. v1.1 moves both into M-005: the seam check becomes M-005.9 and the default share becomes M-005.4(d), so they are versioned and sourced like the rest.

## 1. Shoreline seam: measure the built surface, not the disagreement between sources
**Finding (high confidence).** The v1 figure is the median |3DEP − CUDEM| inside the 10 m feather band (DEF-bay-6). The two sources' own accuracy statements predict a disagreement of that size, so the 0.1 m limit could never be met:
- 3DEP land surface, CA_SanFrancisco_B23 lidar:
  - flown in spring 2023 (collection start 2023-04-20);
  - built to the ASPRS 2014 10-cm RMSEz class, required NVA 19.6 cm at 95 %;
  - tested NVA RMSEz 1.73 cm on 30 bare-earth/urban checkpoints [1].
- CUDEM 1/9″ topobathy:
  - topography "assumed to be, at worst, better than 0.5 meters RMSE";
  - bathymetry accuracy varies by source, and no quantitative vertical analysis has been done [2].
- Derivation. If the errors are independent, the expected 95 % disagreement is 1.96·√(0.0173² + 0.5²) = **0.98 m**. The measured median of 0.457 m sits inside that envelope. A 0.1 m limit is 5× tighter than CUDEM's own stated RMSE.

**What a player or a physics body meets is the built heightfield h_b.** Across the band, the blend can only add discontinuities that neither source has. A linear feather of disagreement Δ over L metres adds |Δ|/L to each 1 m node step (with L = 10 m, 0.1 m per metre for every 1 m of Δ).
**Perceptual derivation.** At the low-res target (1/4 of 1080p ≈ 270 rows, 60° vertical FOV; R-004 §6.1), one row spans 0.222° = 3.88 mrad. At 50 m that is 0.19 m. So a 0.2 m step is at most one pixel beyond 50 m.

**Proposal: M-005.9 Shoreline surface continuity.**
- **Domain.** Every adjacent pair of 1 m nodes (both axes) inside the blend band, plus 5 m either side.
- **Excess jump.** E = |h_b(i+1) − h_b(i)| − max_s |h_s(i+1) − h_s(i)|, where s ∈ {3DEP, CUDEM}, both resampled to the same nodes. E is the part of a step that neither source explains.
- **Thresholds.**
  - **p99 E ≤ 0.20 m.** Basis: the B23 required NVA of 19.6 cm at 95 % [1] is the smallest vertical difference the land source certifies, and it is ≈ 1 low-res pixel at 50 m.
  - **max E ≤ 0.50 m**, the C11 cap, kept. Basis: it equals CUDEM's stated topo RMSE [2].
  - Documented seawalls and piers are excluded, as in C11.
- **Kept from C11.** The profile check at 50 shoreline crossings (max adjacent jump < 1.5 m except documented structures) and "no water cell above ground".
- **Informational, not gating:**
  - D = median |3DEP − CUDEM| in the band, with p95, shown against the 0.98 m envelope;
  - the share of band nodes where |Δ|/L > 0.2 m. That share tells the implementer where a wider feather would help: L ≥ |Δ| / 0.2 m.
- **M0 status.** The evaluator measured an extra blend step of at most 0.489 m. That passes the max cap. The p99 has to be computed with the new procedure.

## 2. Default-height share: what to count, how parts attach, what fills the gaps
**Finding.** The 13.3 % (334 of 2,503 by count; 10.1 % by area) has three parts:
- 7 features that should not exist as buildings (DEF-bay-1);
- 26 `building:part`s that do not inherit from their parent;
- small structures with no OSM height or levels (median 117 m², p90 739 m²), because the R-004 IoU > 0.6 DataSF rule rejects row houses that OSM splits.

The evaluator estimates that a containment rule alone brings the share to 6.4 % (DEF-bay-5).

**Unit and denominator.**
- **Unit = structure:** a building outline plus its parts, matching `part_of` in C14. A structure is "default" if its tallest element's height comes from the last-resort default.
- **Gate on footprint-area share; report count share and perimeter share** (perimeter is the façade-length proxy).
  - Why area: visual weight and skyline impact grow with size, and by count a 20 m² shed weighs as much as a block.
  - Why not "visible façade area": façade area = perimeter × height, and the height is exactly the unknown, so perimeter is the honest proxy.
- **Excluded before counting** (as built and as counted):
  - `location=underground`;
  - `layer<0` together with `building:levels:underground` or `level<0`;
  - `building=ship` and features whose base is below MSL inside the water mask (the San Francisco Belle case);
  - `building:part=roof` / `building=roof`, which get their height from `min_height`/parent and are canopies, not massing;
  - footprints < 20 m², consistent with the M-005.5 count rule.

**Part→parent matching (in order).**
1. An explicit `type=building` relation (roles `outline`/`part`) wins.
2. Otherwise containment: part P belongs to outline B if area(P∩B) ≥ 0.9·area(P). The 10 % allowance covers digitising slivers. If several outlines qualify, the smallest-area B wins.
3. Unmatched parts become their own structure.

**Inheritance** (closes DEF-bay-2):
- a part with only `building:levels` = n gets n × (H_parent / N_parent), capped at H_parent;
- a part with neither tag gets H_parent;
- in both cases `height_source = parent`, which is not counted as default.

**DataSF match** (replaces "IoU > 0.6"):
- accept a DataSF footprint if IoU > 0.6, **or** if ≥ 0.8 of the OSM footprint lies inside one DataSF footprint (row houses split in OSM);
- keep R-004's `peak_1st_m − gnd_min_m` > 3 m guard against post-2010 sites;
- source `datasf_contain`.

**Open, commercially usable fill sources.** Licences were checked; priority is after OSM `height` and DataSF:

| Source | What it gives | Licence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3DEP CA_SanFrancisco_B23 lidar (2023) | height = p90 of (first-return DSM − DTM) over the footprint eroded 1 m | US Government public domain (R-004 §6.7) [1] | Best fill: current and measured. It is also the skyline/roof reference, so lidar-filled structures are reported separately in every lidar comparison (Q-NEW-a). |
| SF Assessor Historical Secured Property Tax Rolls (DataSF `wv5m-vpq2`) | `number_of_stories`, `year_property_built`, parcel-centroid point → join by point-in-footprint; height = stories × 4.22 m (R-004 §1.4) | PDDL [3] | The Assessor gives no warranty of accuracy [3]. Weak, like levels (median \|e\| 5.9 m, R-004). Useful for `year_property_built` too. |
| Overture Maps buildings | conflated OSM, Esri Community Maps, Microsoft ML and Google Open Buildings footprints; some heights derived from 3DEP lidar | ODbL; some underlying sources require their own credit [4] | Adds little over the first two in SF and brings extra attribution duties. Not recommended. |

**Threshold (M-005.4(d)).**
- **M0: default area share ≤ 10 %** (the v1 number, now on area; **Assumed, pending calibration**).
- **From M1, once the lidar fill exists: ≤ 2 % by area** (Assumed). With lidar, a default should remain only where lidar has no clean roof, for example under canopy.
- Count share and perimeter share are reported.
- Why: the default (3 × 4.22 = 12.66 m) has the weakest provenance in the chain, and the "field of identical boxes" founder risk scales with how much area it covers.

## 3. Skyline reference R-ind, specified precisely
**Facts.**
- B23 was flown in spring 2023 [1].
- B23 delivers only classes 1 (processed, unclassified), 2 (ground), 7 (low noise), 9 (water), 17 (bridge deck), 18 (high noise) and 20 (ignored ground) [1].
- **Neither buildings (6) nor vegetation (3–5) are classified, so trees and buildings are both class 1.** Dropping classes cannot remove vegetation in this project.
- ASPRS LAS 1.4 standard classes [5]:
  - 0 never classified, 1 unassigned, 2 ground, 3/4/5 low/medium/high vegetation, 6 building, 7 low point (noise);
  - 9 water, 10 rail, 11 road surface, 13–16 wires and towers, 17 bridge deck, 18 high noise;
  - 20 ignored ground, 21 snow, 22 temporal exclusion;
  - 23–63 reserved, 64–255 user definable.
  - (8 and 12 are reserved in LAS 1.4: use flags.)

**Recipe (DSM at 0.5 m on BayTM, DTM from class 2 + 20, water from MSL).**
1. **Points.** Keep first returns (`ReturnNumber == 1`) whose class is not in {7, 18, 22}, and drop any point with the withheld flag set. Where classes 3–5 exist (other projects), drop them too. Use one `filters.expression`, for example `ReturnNumber == 1 && Classification != 7 && Classification != 18 && Classification != 22`, not several `filters.range` terms on one dimension, which PDAL ORs (DEF-bay-3).
2. **Cell value.** The 95th percentile of the kept returns per cell, not the max. That removes single-point spikes such as the 340–357 m cells over a 331 m crown.
3. **Vegetation.** Class 1 includes trees, so mask them by return structure:
   - mark a cell vegetation if > 50 % of its first returns have `NumberOfReturns ≥ 2` (canopy is penetrated; roofs and walls mostly give single returns);
   - set vegetation cells to the DTM;
   - the threshold is **Assumed, pending calibration** against Pioneer Park and Redwood Park, which the evaluator named.
4. **Thin temporary structures.**
   - Apply a morphological opening with a 3 × 3 m kernel to cells more than 20 m above the DTM. This removes crane masts, booms and lattice elements narrower than 3 m.
   - The footprints of the R-004 §5 landmarks, dilated by 5 m, are exempt so that spires survive.
   - The kernel size is **Assumed**.
5. **Change mask (survey date vs build data).**
   - Any structure where |lidar p90 height − build height| > 10 m on ≥ 300 m², or tagged `building=construction`, or with `start_date`/`year_property_built` after 2023-04-20, is removed from *both* renders: its footprint + 5 m is replaced by DTM.
   - Masked structures are listed in the report.
   - **In-tile towers completed after the survey: none identified.** The tile's recent towers were all complete before spring 2023: Salesforce Tower 2018, 181 Fremont May 2018 [6], One Steuart Lane 2020–2021 [7].
   - 530 Sansome (41 storeys, in the tile) was approved in November 2025 and is expected in 2028 [8].
   - An exhaustive post-2023 completion list could not be sourced; the change mask makes the metric robust to that gap.

**Image and viewpoints.**
- **Camera.**
  - Rectilinear pinhole, 1024 × 576, pitch 0, roll 0, pixel-centre convention.
  - Heading = BayTM grid azimuth, clockwise from +z. Grid convergence here is ≈ Δλ·sin φ ≈ 0.09°·0.61 = 0.055° (derivation), which is negligible.
  - Flat world, no curvature, in both renders. Sky = no hit within 15 km.
- **V4** (BayTM 63300, 74900, eye 10 m): **heading 257.6°, hFOV 75°** (was 270°, 60°).
  - Computed bearings / elevation angles: Salesforce 227.4° / 14.4°, Millennium 227.8°, 555 California 248.9°, Transamerica 260.2°, Coit 287.9° / 4.6°.
  - The 60.5° spread no longer fits a 60° FOV. 257.6° ± 37.5° keeps all of them in frame with ≥ 7° margin, and gives the founder scene "Salesforce towering, Coit on its hill" (DEF-bay-7).
  - vFOV is 46.7° and 1 px = 1.28 mrad.
- **V4m.** Same as V4 at a 70 m eye (mech head). Report only.
- **V5** (Coit deck, 146 m): heading 170°, hFOV 60° kept. Salesforce at 150.7° and Millennium at 147.5° are inside the 140–200° frame. 1 px = 1.02 mrad.
- V1–V3 are unchanged (M1).

**Comparison band (replaces "highest reference row to horizon").**
- The horizon row is the image centre row (pitch 0).
- **Skyline columns:** columns where either profile is ≥ 2 px above the horizon.
- IoU is computed over the skyline columns, from the horizon row to max(top rows) + 2 px. MAE is computed over the skyline columns, in px and in mrad.
- A view gates only if ≥ 25 % of its columns are skyline columns (**Assumed**). Otherwise it is reported. This removes the V5 fragility, where rows 161–288 made a few pixels look large.

**Registration.** Allow a column shift of ±1 px (±1.0–1.3 mrad ≈ 1.5–2 m at 1.5 km). This matches the ±1.5 m datum ambiguity (R-004 §2.1) and the evaluator's best-shift result of 1–2 m. Report the best shift; a best shift beyond ±1 px is a registration defect.

**Noise floor and thresholds.**
- The floor F_v is the same metric computed between two independent references:
  - R-ind (above);
  - R-ind2: DataSF 2010 footprints extruded to `hgt_median_m` on the same DTM (PDDL; independent of OSM and of the 2023 point processing), with the same change mask.
- Also report the spread of R-ind against itself under recipe perturbations (p95 vs max cell value; 0.5 vs 1 m cells; ±1.5 m eye shift) as the within-reference noise.
- **Gate:** IoU_game ≥ IoU(F_v) − 0.02 and MAE_game ≤ MAE(F_v) + 1 px, and in any case IoU ≥ 0.80. All three numbers are **Assumed, pending calibration** at the first founder checkpoint.
- **R-src gate unchanged** (IoU ≥ 0.98, MAE ≤ 1 px).

## 4. Draw-call measurement for tile budgets (procedure only)
- **Graphics device required.**
  - Run batch mode without `-nographics`. With that flag Unity creates no graphics device, so nothing renders and no rendering counter is meaningful [9].
  - Use `unity run … -executeMethod`, which enters Play mode on a fixed scene.
- **Counters.** Read the Rendering-module counters with `ProfilerRecorder` in `ProfilerCategory.Render` [10]:
  - `Draw Calls Count`: the total draw calls issued in the frame, including non-batched, dynamic-batched and static-batched;
  - `Batches Count`;
  - `SetPass Calls Count`: shader-pass switches, which are the CPU cost [10][11];
  - `Triangles Count`, `Vertices Count`;
  - `Shadow Casters Count`, one entry per caster per light [10].
  - These are frame totals over everything the engine rendered in that frame.
  - `Draw Calls Count`, `SetPass Calls Count` and `Vertices Count` are the recorder names used in Unity's sample [10]; check the other names against the Profiler Module Details pane once (6000.6.9f1).
- **Capture protocol.**
  - Gameplay camera only, at 1920 × 1080.
  - URP asset, quality level, shadow distance and cascades, depth-prepass mode and SRP Batcher state are all recorded in the report.
  - 30 warm-up frames, then 120 recorded frames per view (V4, V5 and the three obliques). Report the median and the max.
- **Budget figure.**
  - The C20 budget is checked on the max per view of `Draw Calls Count`; `Batches` and `SetPass` are reported beside it.
  - The renderer-count estimate becomes informational (DEF-perf-1).
- **Per-pass breakdown.** Once per change to the URP asset, capture the frame with the Frame Debugger and list the draw counts per pass: MainLightShadow, depth prepass and DrawOpaqueObjects. The Frame Debugger shows only passes that contain draws [12].
