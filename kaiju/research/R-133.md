## 1. Why, and what M1 left out
R-022 (section "What Q-105 leaves out") states BSSA14's site term gives amplification only, so SoMa fill and the waterfront damage is a
lower bound. Loma Prieta 1989 showed ground failure on fill (Marina District, Treasure Island, Oakland port) [S1, S2 (search snippets; PDFs not re-read)].
Gameplay (P2 physical truth, P3 real Bay): a quake before emergence should visibly tilt/sink fill blocks, and a 100 m mech standing on fill
should feel it.

## 2. Minimal physical model (Seed-Idriss simplified procedure, deterministic)
Cyclic stress ratio (Seed and Idriss 1971, as updated by Youd et al. 2001 NCEER [S3, not re-read]):
CSR = 0.65 (a_max/g) (sigma_v / sigma_v') r_d, with r_d = 1 - 0.00765 z (z <= 9.15 m), 1.174 - 0.0267 z (9.15 < z <= 23 m) [S3].
Magnitude scaling: CSR_7.5 = CSR / MSF, MSF = 10^2.24 / Mw^2.56 [S3]. Overburden factor K_sigma (<= 1.0) [S3].
Resistance, SPT route: CRR_7.5 from (N1)60cs via the clean-sand baseline curve; closed form (Youd 2001 eq. for (N1)60cs < 30):
CRR_7.5 = 1/(34 - N) + N/135 + 50/(10 N + 45)^2 - 1/200 with N = (N1)60cs [S3].
Resistance, Vs route (Andrus and Stokoe 2000): CRR_7.5 = a (Vs1/100)^2 + b [1/(Vs1c - Vs1) - 1/Vs1c], with a = 0.022, b = 2.8, Vs1c = 215 m/s (fines < 5 %) [S4, not re-read; verify constants].
Factor of safety FS = CRR_7.5 * MSF * K_sigma / CSR. FS < 1 liquefies; FS in [1, 1.2] marginal (**Assumed** gameplay band).
Inputs per cell: a_max = PGA from the existing `IShakingField` (ShakingSample.PgaG, Mw from `QuakeScenario`); depth to water table, layer
thickness, unit weight, (N1)60cs or Vs1. These come from the zone layer below, not from the player. No new GMPE needed.
Consequences (all **Assumed** unless noted; each gets a sourced calibration in T-186 step 2):
| Effect | Minimal model | Output to Destruction |
|---|---|---|
| Bearing capacity loss | Residual strength ratio r_u(FS) mapped linearly: r_u = 1 at FS <= 1, 0 at FS >= 1.4; bearing factor = 1 - 0.8 r_u (**Assumed**) | per-building `GroundFailure` record: capacity factor on `CapacityModel` |
| Settlement / sinkage | Post-liquefaction volumetric strain from Ishihara and Yoshimine 1992 chart (strain 1 to 5 % by FS and N) [S5, not re-read] x liquefied thickness | `SinkM` per cell |
| Differential tilt | Settlement gradient across footprint (cell lattice 10 m); tilt = dSink / footprint width; > 1/100 yellow, > 1/50 red-tag (**Assumed**) | `TiltRad` per building; feeds damage state bump |
| Lateral spread | Youd, Hansen, Bartlett 2002 multilinear regression (M, R, free-face ratio W, slope S, T15, F15, D5015) [S6, not re-read]; only where a free face exists (shoreline, fill edge) | `SpreadM` vector per cell; piers/seawalls |
| Sand boils / ejecta | Cosmetic: visual only where FS < 1 and a thin non-liquefied cap < 3 m (Ishihara 1985 cap criterion) [S7, not re-read] | VFX hint, no gameplay mass |
Damage hook: a building adds a one-shot `ApplyGroundFailure(id, capacityFactor, tiltRad, sinkM)` after the shaking draw; state never lowered,
deterministic per BuildingId (same convention as `DamageModel`). Debris/energy accounting: tilt/sink move the building down the state ladder
by raising the effective demand (equivalent PGA scale 1/capacityFactor), so M-051/M-052 invariants stay intact.
Mech foot sinkage (F-003, wading body, T-005): `GroundSupport(x,z) -> {BearingFactor, SinkM}` query; a foot load of mass/contact area
(order 10,000 t on ~ 200 m^2 = ~ 0.5 MPa, **Assumed**) exceeds a liquefied soil (residual strength ~ 0.01 to 0.05 MPa, **Assumed**) by an order
of magnitude, so a liquefied block swallows a foot; sink rate = bearing-deficit relaxation with time constant **Assumed** 5 s.
Timing: liquefaction onset after strong shaking begins plus a few seconds (Assumed); effects persist until a re-consolidation clock
(minutes to hours; set to the session, **Assumed**). Because the destruction bridge deliberately has no clock (MODULE.md), the first cut is
instantaneous at `FaultRupture` plus a fixed delay field on the event.

## 3. Data pipeline
Needed: a per-tile susceptibility/depth layer. Sources (identified; licences must be confirmed at T-186 step 1):
- CGS Seismic Hazard Zones (liquefaction zones, SF Bay quads incl. San Francisco North/South, Oakland West, Alameda; official regulatory maps; polygons as GIS downloads via CGS "Seismic Hazard Zone Report/Map" data / Earthquake Zones of Required Investigation viewer) [S8, S9]. State agency data: expect open use with attribution; **licence terms not read, confirm** (Assumed open).
- USGS SF Bay Area liquefaction hazard maps (Knudsen, Witter et al.; probabilistic liquefaction susceptibility for scenarios of 7.0-ish and PGA; USGS public domain) [S2, not re-read].
- Vs30 grid: USGS Vs30 (Wald and Allen 2007 topographic proxy, public domain) or Thompson et al. 2014 California Vs30 [S10, not re-read]; closes R-022 Q-NEW-c.
- Water table depth: Bay shoreline sea level plus topographic depth proxy (Assumed), refine with CGS reports where polygon attributes list historical high groundwater depth.
- Geologic age/type: USGS SF geology (Open-File 1997-715 `sf_geo`) [S11] for artificial fill (af), Bay mud (Qhbm), dune sand (Qd).
Pipeline (Python, `Pipelines/geo`, offline): CGS polygons clipped to BayTM L0 tiles -> `ground@v1` layer: raster at 10 m (class: none / low / high susceptibility, depth-to-water, (N1)60cs or Vs1 class median, free-face distance). Class values are derived defaults per geologic unit with sourced ranges; no invention of site borings. Flagged, never fabricated: cells with no CGS coverage default to "not susceptible" with `coverage=0` (as the height-fill policy of Q-202).
Known SF Bay hot spots to validate against [S1, S2, S12]: Marina District (1906 fill and 1989 damage), SoMa / Mission Bay fill, South of Market lots, Treasure Island and Yerba Buena (hydraulic fill, 1989 sand boils), Oakland port and airport fills, Alameda, Foster City / Bay Farm.

## 4. Engine-free module design
New `World.Ground` (L2, new assemblies `Game.World.Ground.Contracts` + `Game.World.Ground`, references Core, Geo.Contracts, Seismic.Contracts only; Destruction consumes via a bridge in its own assembly as the existing `SeismicShakingBridge`, no reverse dependency). Contracts: `IGroundField` (`TryGetSupport(x,z,out GroundSample{FS, BearingFactor, SinkM, TiltRad, SpreadX, SpreadZ, Susceptible})` after a given EventId), event `GroundFailureOccurred` (unmanaged, tile + counts). Flag `ground.liquefaction` (default off). Save: derived from the event seed, so no section (unless a mech sinks: out of scope).
Tests: golden-hand-computed FS for 6 textbook cases (Youd 2001 worked examples), monotonic in PGA, Mw, N; MSF anchors; determinism (byte-identical over 3 runs); r_d continuity at 9.15 m.

## 5. Validation (acceptance, proposed metric numbers allocated later)
- Proposed metric "liquefaction extent": for a scenario at Loma Prieta (Mw 6.9, PGA at Marina ~ 0.2 g by Assumed lookup from the ShakingField) the fraction of cells flagged FS < 1 inside the 1989 documented zones (Marina, Treasure Island, Oakland port) >= 0.7 and the fraction flagged outside CGS zones <= 0.05. (**Assumed thresholds**; a sourced list of documented 1989 sites with polygons is T-186 step 1.)
- Proposed metric "FS closed form": 6 worked examples to 1e-6 relative.
- Proposed metric "ground-to-damage consistency": buildings in FS < 1 cells show state >= baseline state in 100 % of cases (never lowered).
- Proposed metric "budget": ground query <= 2 microseconds per cell lookup; bake adds <= 5 % to tile bake time; contributes to M-008.
