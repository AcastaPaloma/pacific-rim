# R-027: real-scale cannon ballistics and melee impact energy for an ~80 m mech (T-040)

Scope: numbers behind `Assets/Game/Combat` (engine-free). *Assumed* = no source, a tunable default; every Assumed value lives in a spec struct, never in logic.
Sources: [1] en.wikipedia.org/wiki/16%22/50_caliber_Mark_7_gun (fetched 2026-11-15). [2] en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M777_howitzer (fetched). [3] en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drag_coefficient (fetched). [4] Walilko, Viano, Bir, Br J Sports Med 2005 (PMC1725037), via search abstract only; figures not checked against the full paper. [5] Froude similarity: standard dimensional analysis (speed ~ sqrt(g L), mass ~ L^3), here applied by us.

## 1. Cannon scale
- Why battleship scale: a 12.7 mm heavy machine gun is the largest arm a 1.8 m person carries. Scaling a ~80 m body by 80/1.8 = 44x in length gives 44x calibre = ~560 mm and ~85,000x projectile mass. The nearest real gun class is the 406 mm naval gun [1]: AP shell 1,225 kg (infobox; table says 1,200 kg), muzzle velocity 762 m/s (2,500 ft/s), charge 300 kg (6 bags of 50 kg), max range ~38.7 km on the ship mount. Muzzle energy = 0.5 * 1225 * 762^2 = 356 MJ.
- Field-artillery lower bound [2]: 155 mm M777, muzzle velocity 827 m/s (Charge 8S), range 23.5 km (M795). Shell mass not given by [2]; the M795 mass of ~46 kg is Assumed from memory (not verified).
- Two cannon presets: `Heavy` (406 mm class: 1,225 kg, 762 m/s, 0.406 m) and `Light` (155 mm class: 46 kg Assumed, 827 m/s, 0.155 m).
- Drag: F = 0.5 rho v^2 Cd A [3]. rho = 1.225 kg/m3 (ISA sea level; SF is at sea level; Assumed constant with altitude, shells reach ~10 km, which overstates drag aloft: real range comes out shorter, accepted). Speed of sound 340.29 m/s (ISA, Assumed). Cd(Mach) table is Assumed in shape (subsonic ~0.15, transonic rise to ~0.38 near Mach 1.1, falling to ~0.25 at Mach 3), standing in for the standard G7 curve [not fetched]. Vacuum range at 45 deg for 762 m/s = v^2/g = 59.2 km; the real 38.7 km [1] is 0.65 of vacuum. Combat accepts a 45 deg range in 0.45..0.85 of vacuum for the Heavy preset (band Assumed, wide because the Cd table is Assumed); measured with the Assumed Cd table (T-040 test log): 32.0 km, ratio 0.54 (real 0.65, so the table is ~20% draggier than the real shell; accepted, retune when a G7 table is sourced).
- Recoil impulse: J = (m_shell + k_gas * m_charge) * v_muzzle, k_gas = 1.25 (Assumed, Hornig-style gas momentum factor; unverified). Heavy: (1225 + 1.25*300) * 762 = 1.22 MN s. A free mech of 2e6 kg (Assumed) takes dv = 0.61 m/s; the real body consumes the impulse later (stance stability is T-005's gate). [1] gives no recoil data.
- Fixed step: semi-explicit RK4 on (x, v) in double with only + - * / and sqrt, so the same inputs give bit-equal results in any process on IEEE-754 hardware. No Exp/Pow/Sin in the integrator.

## 2. Melee impact energy
- Human reference [4]: fist 9.14 m/s, effective mass 2.9 kg, force 3,427 N (all means). Energy 0.5*2.9*9.14^2 = 121 J.
- Froude scaling [5] to 44x: speed x sqrt(44) = 6.6 -> ~61 m/s; mass x 44^3 = 85,000 -> ~247 t. KE = ~460 MJ, the same order as the 406 mm muzzle energy (356 MJ): a full-force melee blow and a heavy shell are comparable energy sources. This is an upper bound (Froude-similar actuators are not structurally realistic).
- Defaults (Assumed): arm 60,000 kg uniform rod about the shoulder, fist/blade 20,000 kg at the tip, reach 40 m, peak tip speed 30 m/s, so I = (m_arm/3 + m_tip) L^2 = 6.4e7 kg m^2 (tip-effective mass 40,000 kg), KE_max = 0.5*I*w^2 = 18 MJ at 30 m/s; `Max` preset uses 61 m/s tip speed (75 MJ for these masses; 460 MJ needs the full 247 t).
- Impact model (rigid pivot, point contact at radius r): m_eff(r) = I / r^2; relative normal speed v = w r - v_target.n; target effective mass M (building: its mass M0 from Destruction; kaiju: declared; 0 = immovable); mu = m_eff M / (m_eff + M) (mu = m_eff when immovable); impulse J = mu (1 + e) v; energy dissipated dE = 0.5 mu (1 - e^2) v^2, which is the ΔKE the Destruction contract expects ("ΔKE of the physics contact record"). Restitution e = 0.1 for masonry/steel (Assumed). Bounds: 0 <= dE <= 0.5 mu v^2 <= 0.5 m_eff v^2 = swing KE; arm angular speed after = w - J r / I >= 0 when J is capped (an impact cannot reverse the arm beyond e).
- Cannon shells use the same law with m_eff = m_shell, M = target mass, e = 0 for AP into a building (Assumed, full plastic).

## 3. Open items

- Kaiju HP per MJ (default 0.15 HP/MJ: a 1000 HP kaiju dies from ~19 full heavy shells) is Assumed; calibrate with the kaiju design.

## 4. Metric drafts (spec step 1 only; cannot fail anything until a tool and calibration exist)
- **M-061@v1 ballistic accuracy vs analytic** (gating, headless dotnet): vacuum flights at 5..85 deg, 20 s, dt 0.02, relative error of x and y vs closed form; drag-free parabola, vertical tanh fall and horizontal hyperbolic flight, each vs closed form; RK4 error ratio >= 12 when dt halves; two runs bit-equal. Draft threshold: relative error < 1e-9 (measured 3.7e-14). Source: tests in `Combat.Tests/BallisticsTests`.
- **M-062@v1 impact energy bounds** (gating, headless dotnet): for every resolved impact 0 <= E <= 0.5 m_eff v_n^2 (<= swing KE or muzzle KE) and the two-body balance holds to 1e-9 relative over 2,000 seeded cases; melee grid (4 target masses x 4 durations) worst E / E_swing_max = 0.89 (< 1). Draft threshold: E / E_bound <= 1 + 1e-9.
