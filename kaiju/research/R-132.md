## 1. Scale of the problem
A 6,000 t mech foot or a 10,000 t kaiju contact delivers megajoules to tens of megajoules in tens of milliseconds. Geometric similarity says every resonance of a struck body scales as 1/length, i.e. as mass^(-1/3) at fixed density, so a 6e6 kg body rings roughly 180x lower than a 1 kg one. Content therefore sits at 5 to 60 Hz: partly infrasonic (below 20 Hz), which is felt (and used by the camera impulse / haptics), not heard on laptop speakers. Design consequence: the audible part of a footfall is the high-order modes, the contact transient and the debris it throws, plus a synthetic "psychoacoustic" upper layer. [A, reasoning from [S] modal scaling, van den Doel and Pai]

## 2. Source level from energy
Acoustic power = eta * E / T (eta radiated fraction, T burst duration). Sound power level Lw = 10 log10(P / 1e-12 W). On the ground (hemisphere) Lp(1 m) = Lw - 10 log10(2 pi) = Lw - 7.98. [S: standard acoustics; Lp/Lw definitions, ISO 3744 style hemisphere] Clamp at 194 dB (1 atm peak; linear acoustics ends; real shocks propagate non-linearly and lose level faster). [S: physical limit]
Efficiencies eta and T per kind are tuning [A]: footfall 2e-3 over 80 ms, impact 1e-2 / 50 ms, structure failure 5e-3 / 0.6 s, debris rain 1e-3 / 1.5 s, muzzle blast 5e-2 / 20 ms, crack 1e-3 / 5 ms, quake 1e-4 / 8 s. Worked example [C]: 3e7 J foot contact -> P = 2e-3*3e7/0.08 = 7.5e5 W -> Lw 178.8 dB -> Lp(1 m) 170.8 dB (clamped region starts above 194). At 100 m: 130.8 dB; 1 km: 110.8 dB before absorption.
Blast scaling: air blast scales with W^(1/3) (Hopkinson-Cranz cube root), Kinney and Graham 1985 give the overpressure curves; use them as a calibration check of the muzzle model, not for runtime. [S: https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-642-86682-1_8 ; Hopkinson scaling summary https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/12/18/9139/htm]

## 3. Distance, atmosphere, delay, Doppler
- Spherical spreading: 20 log10(r / 1 m), 6.02 dB per doubling. [S]
- Atmospheric absorption: ISO 9613-1:1993 pure-tone coefficient alpha(f, T, RH, p) (dB/m), implemented from the closed form (oxygen and nitrogen relaxation frequencies). Spot values computed from the formula [C] (T, RH 70 percent): at 10 C: 1 kHz 3.66, 4 kHz 33.1, 8 kHz 118 dB/km (these match the ISO table I recalled; the standard itself is paywalled: https://www.iso.org/standard/17426.html, formula transcription https://sengpielaudio.com/AirdampingFormula.htm, so the table match is [C]/[A] until the standard is bought). At 20 C: 1 kHz 4.98, 4 kHz 23.1 dB/km. 16 Hz: 0.006 dB/km (infrasound travels). At 1 km and 15 C 75 percent the 8 kHz band is down ~90 dB: distant footfalls are all low end. The voice's low-pass corner is the 1/3-octave where path absorption reaches 6 dB.
- Speed of sound c = 343.2 sqrt(T/293.15) m/s [S ideal gas]; delay r/c: 1 km at 15 C = 2.94 s [C]. This delay is the single most important cue for scale and is applied per voice (event tick time plus delay), never "cheated" to zero; M-009 is still met because the visual/impulse effects fire at the contact frame and the *sound* leaves the source at the contact frame.
- Doppler: f_obs/f_src = (c + v_listener_away)/(c - v_source_toward), projected on the source-listener line. Clamp to [0.25, 4] and bound the denominator at 0.1c: a shell above Mach 1 produces no pitch glide but an N-wave crack on passage. [S: classical Doppler]
- Temperature/humidity default for the Bay: 15 C, 75 percent RH [A]. Wind and gradients ignored (refraction shadows are a later layer) [A].

## 4. Footfalls and impacts
Contact time grows with size (Hertz contact t_c ~ m^(1/3) at fixed material and speed; for a foot hitting soil/asphalt tens of ms) so the excitation low-passes at about 1/(pi t_c) and the spectral centroid falls with mass: implemented as an amplitude roll-off 1/(1+(f/fc)^2) on the modal set. [A model; Hertz contact as modal excitation is the standard in https://persianney.com/kvdoelcsubc/publications/modalpaper.pdf and FoleyAutomatic https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~kry/pubs/foleyautomatic/foleyautomatic.pdf]. Per-foot variation: seed hash detunes every mode by up to 1 percent (deterministic). Layering: (1) sub thump (sine burst at 0.5 to 2x f1 of the soil/structure, pitch-drop envelope), (2) modal body, (3) filtered-noise grit, (4) debris granular tail from the debris account. Footfall of a mech should use the Body module's `VerticalImpulseNs` (and later `FootLoaded` peak) as the gain driver, because a heavy foot's harshness is impulse, not mean pressure.

## 5. Structural failure, collapse, glass
Steel: bar-like inharmonic modes (free-free beam ratios 1, 2.756, 5.404, 8.933 [S: Euler-Bernoulli free-free bar]), long T60. Concrete: heavily damped, dull, many clustered modes [A ratios]. Glass: plate modes, high brightness, medium T60, plus a granular shatter burst (hundreds of 1 to 5 ms grains in 50 to 150 ms). Failure sound = crack transient (noise burst, 5 to 20 ms, band 500 Hz to 4 kHz, scales with building mass) + low modal groan + debris rain. Collapse: debris mass from `BuildingDamaged.DebrisKg` drives a granular generator: grain count ~ DebrisKg / mean_chunk_kg, each grain a modal hit with material = concrete/steel/glass mix, random delays from a hash-based schedule (deterministic), gain by chunk mass. Bonneel et al. show impact sounds for debris-like events are well served by modal/frequency-domain methods at 5 to 8x speed-up with slight quality loss. [S: https://www.springerprofessional.de/doi/10.1145/1399504.1360623 (search snippet; paper not read in full)]

## 6. Earthquakes
P and S arrive at distance R after R/vp and R/vs; with vp 6000 and vs 3500 m/s [A upper-crust values] a site 30 km from the hypocentre gets P at 5.0 s, S at 8.6 s. The seismic module already publishes Mw, origin and hypocentre in `QuakeBegan` (`Mw`, `OriginTimeS`, `HypoX/HypoZ/HypoDepthM`) and a PGA field (`IShakingField.TryGetShaking`). Spectrum: Brune corner frequency fc = 0.49 beta (dSigma / M0)^(1/3) [S: Brune 1970 JGR 75:4997], M0 = 10^(1.5 Mw + 9.1) N m [S: Hanks and Kanamori 1979 JGR 84:2348]; with beta 3500 m/s and dSigma 3 MPa [A] Mw 7.2 gives fc about 0.06 Hz: the source is entirely infrasonic. The audible quake is therefore (a) the building and glass response (creaks, rattle: modal hits triggered by PGA exceedance), (b) a synthetic low rumble: filtered noise with a 20 to 80 Hz band whose bandwidth and gain rise with Mw and local PGA, level = 60 dB + 20 log10(PGA/0.01 g) clamped to 0..120 dB [A]. P arrives as a short thump, S as the rolling rumble that starts the shaking. Loudness is driven by the local `ShakingSample.PgaG`, not by epicentral distance directly.

## 7. Weapons (406 mm cannon)
- Muzzle blast: filtered noise burst with an N-wave-like pressure shape; level from `WeaponFired` energy (propellant + shell KE, from `ProjectileMassKg` and muzzle speed) with eta 5e-2 [A]; dominant 60 to 150 Hz plus a short broadband crack; at 100 m the blast is far past 140 dB so the perceptual map and limiter do the work.
- Supersonic crack: a projectile above Mach 1 trails a Mach cone; a listener off-axis hears the N-wave when the cone passes, *before* the muzzle blast (blast travels at c from the gun, the shell outruns it): the audio planner should schedule the crack at the cone-arrival time (geometric: needs shell state from Combat; deferred to T-197). [S: classical shock/Mach cone; timing derivation Computed]
- Projectile whistle: a Doppler-shifted band tone for subsonic phases only; level low [A].
- Impact: reuse `ImpactImpulse.EnergyJ` and `ImpulseNs`; material from the target kind (building = concrete/steel/glass mix, mech/kaiju = steel/hide).
- Recoil: `RecoilImpulse` already goes to the camera; audio adds a low thump at the muzzle.

## 8. Procedural synthesis design (the decision)
Procedural first; samples are a later staged layer. Reasons: no external licences or money; massive range of mass and energy (a single sample cannot span 8 orders of magnitude without audible resampling artefacts); determinism and exact-frame triggering are natural; tiny build size. [A, decision]
- Modal bank: up to 8 modes per hit, y(t) = sum a_k exp(-6.9 t / T60_k) sin(2 pi f_k t) (van den Doel and Pai 1998, https://persianney.com/kvdoelcsubc/publications/modalpaper.pdf [S]). f_k = f1(material) * ratio_k * m^(-1/3) * (1 + detune). Recursive resonators (two-pole, one multiply-add per mode per sample) make 32 voices x 8 modes affordable in `OnAudioFilterRead`; the offline `ModalSynth.Render` here is the oracle for the adapter.
- Filtered noise bursts: blasts, cracks, wind, wade (state-variable filters, deterministic xorshift seeded per event).
- Granular: debris, shatter, gravel; grain scheduler seeded by event seed.
- Sample layers (stage 2, later, original recordings or tool-generated only, Q-265): swap or add per-kind layers by profile; the planner and voice budget do not change.
- Distance cue: low-pass at the absorption corner + gain by inverse square + delay; no samples needed for any of it.

## 9. Loudness mapping, headroom, limiter, voice budget
- Physical SPL at the listener is far outside any speaker range (110+ dB at 1 km). Perceptual compression maps it to the mix: linear in dB from (40 dB SPL -> -60 dBFS) to (140 dB SPL -> -3 dBFS), above 140 clamps (ceiling), below 40 silent. Slope 0.57 dB-mix per dB-SPL (a compression ratio ~1.75:1) [A, tunable]. Rationale: preserves ordering (louder is louder) and the audible gap between a nearby and a distant footfall while keeping dynamic range playable. Reference: SPL definition re 20 uPa [S].
- Headroom: sum of voices can exceed 0 dBFS. Master limiter: brick-wall, instant attack, 250 ms exponential release, ceiling 0.89 (-1 dBFS), stereo-linked gain; deterministic and state is only the gain. A guaranteed |y| <= ceiling is tested.
- Voice budget: 32 simultaneous voices, 6 reserved for critical kinds (quake, muzzle blast, foot contact). Priority score = (SPL at listener - 25 dB audible floor) + kind bonus (quake 12, foot/muzzle/impact 8, structure 6, crack/splash 3, debris/wade 0, ambience -6); below floor = culled before allocation (audibility culling). Steal the lowest score only if the newcomer beats it by 3 dB (hysteresis, avoids chatter); ties break by older tick then lower event id; non-critical cannot steal critical. [A all numbers; design pattern is common practice]
- Many debris events: coalesce per building per 100 ms into one granular voice whose grain count scales with debris mass (adapter job, T-196).

## 10. Spatialisation and occlusion
- Panning: constant-power pan from azimuth (sin of angle relative to listener heading) plus Woodworth ITD = (a/c)(theta + sin theta), a = 0.0875 m [S: Woodworth 1938 (textbook formula)]. Full HRTF is a stage-2 option (Unity spatializer plugin) not needed for pixel-art top-down camera; the listener for audio is the camera focus, with heading from the camera yaw [A].
- Occlusion: adapter counts building footprints intersected by the straight source-listener segment (2D line vs district footprints, cheap, cached per source cell); each blocker adds 8 dB (cap 30 dB) and halves the low-pass cutoff (cap 4 halvings). Diffraction over rooftops of 80 m mechs makes real occlusion small for tall sources; this crude model is deliberately conservative [A]. ISO 9613-2 barrier attenuation (https://www.iso.org/standard/20649.html) is the later upgrade [S for the existence of the method, not implemented].
- Water: splash = noise burst + bubble modes at 1 to 4 kHz scaled with m^(-1/3); wade = filtered noise modulated by foot submersion [A]. Bay ambience: low wind noise, gulls omitted (samples), ship horns and bridge hum later as sample layer [A].
