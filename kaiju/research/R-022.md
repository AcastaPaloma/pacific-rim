# R-022: fault rupture and emergence on real traces (M1 Track 2, F-020, D-014, Q-030)

Requirement (F-020): "Emergence: earthquake, then fault rupture, then kaiju rises along real fault traces."
Default (Q-030): no fictional faults; emergences on the real offshore San Andreas / Golden Gate fault and the Hayward fault.
Trace names and distances are R-004's, not re-measured (Q-NEW-a).
Tags: [S#] sourced (list at end). *Assumed* = no source.

## 1. Traces near the GG and SoMa tiles; segment used
Data fields available from `bake_faults.py` (`faults_bay.geojson`, BayTM@v1): `fault_name, section, linetype, slip_rate, slip_sense,
age, mapped_scale, fault_id, source_layer (4 onshore / 5 offshore), objectid`. Pieces are short (median 359 m); the director must
dissolve by `fault_name`+`section` and prefer well-constrained `linetype` [R-004 §7].

Nearest mapped traces (R-004 §7, Qfaults 2020 [S1]); no Quaternary fault crosses either district:
| From | Trace | Distance |
|---|---|---|
| GG district (i25-27, j36-38) | Golden Gate fault (offshore, San Andreas zone) | 2.3 km |
| GG district | San Andreas, San Francisco section (offshore) | 5.1 km |
| GG district | Potato Patch; San Gregorio-Hosgri | 6.9 km; 8.4 km |
| M0 tile `L0_30_36` (inside SoMa district) | Golden Gate fault | 11.6 km |
| M0 tile | Serra fault zone; San Andreas SF section; Hayward Northern section | 12.7; 13.6; 13.8 km |

The Hayward trace is ≈ 14 km (M0 tile figure, used for the SoMa district; *Assumed* for the district edge) from SoMa and farther from GG.
Both districts therefore have a mapped trace within 2.3-14 km. The stop condition (no trace within playable distance) is not met.

**Segment chosen for the M1 emergence: offshore San Andreas fault zone, San Francisco section, with the Golden Gate fault as the
nearest vertex source.** Reasons: (a) closest to GG (2.3 km) and still 11.6 km from SoMa, so both districts shake and the deep-channel
danger (F-045) stays in play; (b) San Andreas is right-lateral, slip rate > 5 mm/yr, historic (1906) [R-004 §7, S5]; (c) it needs no
fictional splay (Q-030). Hayward (257 km zone, > 5 mm/yr [R-004 §7]) is the secondary, land-based option for a visible surface
scarp, ≈ 14 km from SoMa. Both are selectable by `fault_id`; the table in §2 gives values for both magnitudes.

Emergence point rule (deterministic): on the dissolved trace, the vertex inside the ruptured interval that is nearest to the target
district's boundary. For GG that is on the Golden Gate fault / SF section, 2.3-5.1 km offshore (*Assumed*: R-004 distances are tile-edge
distances, vertex confirmed by the director at build time).

## 2. Scaling: rupture length, displacement, magnitude (Wells & Coppersmith 1994, strike-slip, Table 2A [S2])
Coefficients read from the paper's scanned Table 2A (OCR, cross-checked with the figure legends). SRL = surface rupture length (km),
MD / AD = maximum / average surface displacement (m), SS = strike-slip, n = events, σ in magnitude or log10 units.
| Relation (SS) | a | b | σ | n | Valid range |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M = a + b log SRL | 5.16 | 1.12 | 0.28 | 43 | M 5.6-8.1, SRL 1.3-432 km |
| log SRL = a + b M | -3.55 | 0.74 | 0.23 | 43 | same |
| M = a + b log MD | 6.81 | 0.78 | 0.29 | 43 | MD 0.01-14.6 m |
| log MD = a + b M | -7.03 | 1.03 | 0.34 | 43 | same |
| M = a + b log AD | 7.04 | 0.89 | 0.28 | 29 | AD 0.05-8.0 m |
| log AD = a + b M | -6.32 | 0.90 | 0.28 | 29 | same |
| log W = a + b M (width, km) | -0.76 | 0.27 | 0.14 | 87 | M 4.8-8.1 |
| M = a + b log A (area, km²) | 3.98 | 1.02 | 0.23 | 83 | 3-5184 km² |

Paper statement used: average displacement per event ≈ half the maximum [S2]. Hanks & Bakun 2002 show WC94 underestimates large
strike-slip events in area scaling [S6]; treat M > 7.5 as extrapolation.

Computed values (my arithmetic from the table, median, `scratchpad` script, not a sourced result):
| Mw | SRL (km) | MD (m) | AD (m) | W (km) | Area from M (km²) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7.0 | 42.7 | 1.51 | 0.95 | 13.5 | 914 |
| **7.2 (M1 default)** | **60.0** | **2.43** | **1.45** | 15.3 | 1436 |
| 7.9 (1906 size) | 197.7 | 12.8 | 6.17 | 23.6 | 6970 (above 5184 km² range) |

SRL × W (918 km² at 7.2) differs from the area regression by 1.6x (independent fits); M-054 uses SRL and displacement only.

Anchors: 1906 San Andreas, Mw 7.8-7.9, ≈ 477 km (older estimate ≈ 430 km), maximum offset ≈ 6 m (geodetic up to 8.5 m), average ≈ 3 m
[S5]. WC94 from 477 km gives M 8.15 (0.25 above 7.9, inside 1σ); AD at 7.9 is 6.2 m vs 3 m observed (0.31 dex, ≈ 1.1σ). Hayward: HayWired
M7.0 scenario ruptures ≈ 52 mi (84 km) with average slip ≈ 1.3 m [S7]; WC94 gives 42.7 km and 0.95 m, so the scenario is long by 0.29 dex
(1.3σ) and 0.14 dex in slip. Use the HayWired length when Hayward is chosen and report the deviation (M-054 warn band).

**M1 default scenario: Mw 7.2, SRL 60 km on the San Andreas SF section / Golden Gate fault reach, right-lateral, MD 2.4 m, AD 1.5 m.**
Why 7.2: strong enough to drive fragility in both districts (§3), well inside the 5.6-8.1 regression range, and the 60 km interval
fits a ≈ 100 km+ dissolved offshore trace (*Assumed*: trace length not re-measured; if shorter, shorten Mw to the trace via the
SRL relation, never stretch the trace). Surface-offset slip profile along strike: tapered, *Assumed* (elliptical, peak = MD at the
hypocentre-centred midpoint, mean = AD); WC94 gives only the two scalars.

## 3. Shaking at the district buildings (for R-021 fragility)
**Model.** BSSA14 NGA-West2 GMPE [S3]: ln Y = FE(M, mechanism) + FP(Rjb, M) + FS(Vs30, Rjb, M).
- FE: e1 (strike-slip) + e4(M-Mh) + e5(M-Mh)² for M ≤ Mh; e1 + e6(M-Mh) above.
- FP = [c1 + c2(M-Mref)] ln(R/Rref) + c3(R-Rref), R = √(Rjb² + h²), Mref = 4.5, Rref = 1 km.
- FS = clin ln(min(Vs30,Vc)/760) + f2 ln((PGAr+0.1)/0.1), f2 = f4[exp(f5(min(Vs30,760)-360)) - exp(400 f5)]; PGAr = PGA at Vs30 = 760.
- Y: PGA in g, PGV in cm/s. Vertical strike-slip fault: Rjb = horizontal distance to the ruptured polyline.
- Coefficients (as transcribed in OpenQuake `zalachoris_rathje_2019` table, 3 significant figures [S4]; the BSSA14 PDF host had an
  expired certificate so I did not read the paper's own table; tolerance for a 3-s.f. rounding is below 1 %):
| | e1 | e4 | e5 | e6 | Mh | c1 | c2 | c3 | h | clin | Vc | f4 | f5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PGA | 0.486 | 1.43 | 0.0505 | -0.166 | 5.5 | -1.13 | 0.192 | -0.00809 | 4.5 | -0.60 | 1500 | -0.15 | -0.00701 |
| PGV | 5.08 | 1.07 | -0.154 | 0.225 | 6.2 | -1.24 | 0.149 | -0.00344 | 5.3 | -0.84 | 1300 | -0.10 | -0.00844 |

Omitted: basin-depth term δz1 (set to 0, *Assumed*; SF Bay sediments would add amplification), and aleatory sigma (not retrieved;
the median is deterministic, add σ only if R-021 wants a spread: Q-NEW-d).

**Intensity.** MMI from PGV, Worden et al. 2012 [S8] (equation as listed by CGS MS-48 [S9]): MMI = 3.78 + 1.47 log10 PGV for
log10 PGV ≤ 0.53; MMI = 2.89 + 3.16 log10 PGV above (PGV in cm/s). CGS warns the conversion carries significant uncertainty [S9].

**Median shaking for the M1 scenario** (my computation from the coefficients above; Vs30 760 m/s = rock, 270 m/s = soft fill/SoMa
waterfront, both *Assumed*, Vs30 map not retrieved: Q-NEW-c). Distances are R-004 values taken as Rjb, requiring the rupture to pass
the nearest vertex (true for the default interval, *Assumed*).
| Site (Rjb) | Mw | Vs30 760: PGA g / PGV cm/s / MMI | Vs30 270: PGA g / PGV cm/s / MMI |
|---|---|---|---|
| GG edge, Golden Gate fault (2.3 km) | 7.2 | 0.44 / 46 / 8.1 | 0.52 / 76 / 8.8 |
| GG, SA SF section (5.1 km) | 7.2 | 0.36 / 37 / 7.8 | 0.44 / 64 / 8.6 |
| SoMa, Golden Gate fault (11.6 km) | 7.2 | 0.24 / 23 / 7.2 | 0.32 / 42 / 8.0 |
| SoMa, SA SF section (13.6 km) | 7.2 | 0.22 / 20 / 7.0 | 0.29 / 38 / 7.9 |
| 20 km | 7.2 | 0.17 / 15 / 6.6 | 0.24 / 29 / 7.5 |
| SoMa, 11.6 km | 7.0 | 0.22 / 20 / 7.0 | 0.30 / 38 / 7.9 |

**Interface for R-021:** per building, `PgaG`, `PgvCmS`, `Mmi` (float) computed from Rjb to the ruptured polyline, Mw and the building's
Vs30. R-021's curves should be indexed on PGA or MMI; both are supplied. Inputs available from the geo layer: building position
(BayTM), `BuildingId`. Vs30 is not a geo-layer output today (Q-NEW-c).

## 4. Event sequence, timing, payloads (World.Seismic, unmanaged structs on the Core bus)
All times are simulation seconds from `QuakeBegan` (t = 0). Speeds are *Assumed* standard values, no source retrieved:
rupture speed 2.8 km/s (≈ 0.8 x shear speed 3.5 km/s), P 6.0 km/s, S 3.5 km/s.
| t (s) | Event | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | `QuakeBegan` | Origin; shaking field defined from Mw and the planned rupture; nothing is damaged until the S wave arrives |
| 0 | `FaultRupture` (start) | Slip starts at the hypocentre vertex and propagates bilaterally along the dissolved trace at 2.8 km/s |
| SRL/2 / 2.8 ≈ 10.7 | rupture reaches both ends | Duration for 60 km = 10.7 s bilateral (21.4 s if unilateral, *Assumed* bilateral) |
| Rhyp / 3.5 | S wave at a building | Per-building shaking start: GG edge ≈ 1.5-3 s (Rhyp ≈ 8-10 km with an 8 km hypocentre depth, *Assumed*), SoMa ≈ 4-5 s |
| rupture end + 10 (*Assumed*) | shaking ends | Total shaking window ≈ 20 s at SoMa |
| shaking end + 5 (*Assumed*) | `EmergencePoint` | Kaiju rises on the vertex chosen in §1; presentation starts |

`QuakeBegan { uint EventId; float Mw; double OriginTimeS; float HypoX, HypoZ, HypoDepthM; uint FaultId; float RuptureSpeedMs; uint Seed; }`
`FaultRupture { uint EventId; uint FaultId; ushort SectionIdx; int VertexStart, VertexEnd; float SrlKm, MdM, AdM; byte SlipSense; float StartS, EndS; }`
(polyline stays in the faults layer, referenced by ID and vertex range; slip profile regenerated from `MdM`, `AdM`, seed, so events stay unmanaged)
`EmergencePoint { uint EventId; double X, Z; float HeightM; uint FaultId; int VertexIdx; float TimeS; float DistToGGm, DistToSoMaM; }`
Rules: IDs, section and slip sense come from baked fields (`fault_id`, `section`, `slip_sense`); slip rate may weight section choice
(R-004 §7). `QuakeBegan` precedes `FaultRupture`; `EmergencePoint` fires at most once per `EventId`.

## 5. Draft M-053 and M-054 (for the orchestrator; IDs per brief)
### M-053: rupture offset from the mapped trace
- **Definition.** For a game rupture, the perpendicular distance between the rupture centreline and the mapped Qfaults trace it claims
  to follow. No fault may appear that has no source `fault_id` (no fictional trace, Q-030).
- **Procedure.** (1) Load `faults_bay.geojson` in BayTM; dissolve by `fault_name`+`section`. (2) Sample the rupture polyline every 10 m in
  BayTM float64 (before floating-origin shifts). (3) For each sample, find the nearest point of the source trace (shapely distance).
  (4) Report max and p95. (5) Check each rupture vertex range maps to an existing `fault_id`. (6) Report disclosed positional uncertainty from
  `mapped_scale` (not a pass criterion): 0.5 mm at map scale, i.e. 12 m at 1:24k up to 375 m at 1:750k (*Assumed* cartographic rule,
  the Qfaults scale range is from R-004 §7). Also verify the right-lateral offset applied across the trace equals the slip profile to ≤ 5 %.
- **Threshold.** Fidelity of reproduction, not geologic accuracy: **p95 ≤ 1.0 m and max ≤ 2.0 m** against the baked trace (*Assumed*: pipeline
  rounding and the sample step; revisit after the first run), **all** vertex ranges mapped to a source `fault_id` (100 %), and the
  baked `faults_bay.geojson` hash equals the manifest hash.
- **Source.** USGS Qfaults [S1] (trace and its `mapped_scale`); R-004 §7 for the layer.

### M-054: length-displacement-magnitude consistency
- **Definition.** The rupture's declared Mw, surface rupture length, and its average and maximum displacement are mutually consistent with
  the WC94 strike-slip regressions (§2).
- **Procedure.** From the event: SRL = arc length of the ruptured vertex range (km), Mw declared, AD = mean of the slip profile, MD = its maximum.
  Compute: dM_L = Mw - (5.16 + 1.12 log10 SRL); dAD = log10 AD - (-6.32 + 0.90 Mw); dMD = log10 MD - (-7.03 + 1.03 Mw).
  Also require SRL ≤ trace length, and Mw, SRL, MD, AD inside the validity ranges of §2.
- **Threshold.** **Pass** if |dM_L| ≤ 0.28, |dAD| ≤ 0.28 and |dMD| ≤ 0.34 (one WC94 σ each). **Warn** between 1σ and 2σ (record and
  justify, e.g. the HayWired length). **Fail** beyond 2σ (|dM_L| > 0.56, |dAD| > 0.56, |dMD| > 0.68) or out of range. Default M1 scenario has deviations
  of ≈ 0 by construction; the test also runs the HayWired Hayward and the 1906 anchors (§2) to prove the check discriminates (HayWired
  length would show dM_L ≈ +0.31, a warn).
- **Source.** Wells & Coppersmith 1994 Table 2A [S2]; anchors from [S5], [S7]. Tolerance is statistical; if deviation is used to claim
  "accurate", state it as median ± σ.

### What Q-105 (no tsunami, no liquefaction in M1) leaves out
- **Liquefaction and lateral spreading.** SoMa fill and waterfront (Vs30 low) are the area where 1906 and 1989 damage concentrated
  (*Assumed* from general knowledge, no source retrieved). BSSA14's site term gives amplification only, so R-021 damage at SoMa fill
  will be a lower bound. Ground-failure damage must stay out of the M-051 pass claim.
- **Tsunami.** A strike-slip Mw 7.2 offshore generates little seafloor uplift; submarine landslide tsunami is not modelled (*Assumed*).
  The `EmergencePoint` offshore therefore needs no wave field in M1; wading depth comes from the CUDEM water layer.
- Also absent: fire following, aftershocks, afterslip, directivity and basin-edge effects. Offshore surface rupture is invisible:
  the default presentation is a water disturbance, not a scarp across streets.
