## 2. The offline model and its validation

The test scene is all neutral greys, and the pipeline is haze (sRGB lerp toward the airlight by a banded amount) -> value grade -> **a ladder of 5-9 rungs** (`ladderMinStepL`). I ported `PaletteLutBaker.Ladder/Grade/ProjectRatio` and `PixelHaze.shader` (`research/R-131/pixmodel.py`). The ported rung L* equal the captured ones (rust 17.3/22.9/31.9/39.6/46.5/53.9/65.7; crimson 9.9/19.0/27.2/36.4/49.7), which is why the layer means in `tasks/T-004.c6layers.md` are rung values.

Replay (`replay.py`): each non-sky pixel of the committed stills (`evidence/T-004/captures/m003_stills`, 15 sets x 12 frames) is inverted to the interval of scene albedo that reproduces its observed rung under the current curve; a candidate curve then re-renders it. Inferred albedos: ground sRGB grey 0.240 (0.2275-0.2525), box faces 0.272 (0.266-0.279), identical across all five profiles; 94-98 % of ground and 97-99 % of upright pixels agree with a single value. Sky and accent pixels keep their observed value.

Validation: replaying the current curve reproduces the committed numbers exactly: C7 1 km 6/9 (min 1.35), 6/9 (0.00), 9/9 (6.35), 9/9 (8.27), 1/9 (0.00); scale marker dL 33.8/32.9/27.6/30.1/22.6; c1 max drop 0.94/0.04/0.72/0.00/0.00 and rho 0.964/0.964/0.964/1.000/0.991; c2 pass. The C7 masks are the committed `block_contrast` masks; c1 calls `kaiju_metrics.style.measures.depth_banding`; c2 calls `haze_pairs`.
Not modelled (unit-level risks, section 3.5): DIG, dither/seam purity inside the 4x4 windows (checked only as "adjacent rung per band step"), the sky, the accent pixels.

## 3. C7 versus layer ordering

### 3.1 Why it looked impossible, and what is really binding
- The metric: the 80 m block face vs the mean L* of the three texels beside its silhouette that show surfaces more than 1.15x farther or sky. For the block at 926-998 m that ring is ground at 1.15-2.5x plus layer boxes at about 3x and 10x (ring z/block-z percentiles 1.15, 1.6, 2.5, 3.2-5.8, 10).
- Output has only 5-9 rungs, so dL is a count of rung steps. The ring mean must sit at least 4 L* above the block; rung gaps are 5.5-13 L*, so more than about half of the ring pixels must be one rung up.
- A smooth curve spends about one rung per 1.1 ln-units of distance (rust: 7 rungs over ln(30000/130)). A ground pixel 1.2x farther than the block gains 0.2 ln-units, so at the block's own distance the contrast is decided by *where a rung boundary falls*, not by slope. T-004 called this "boundary coincidence" and tuned 5 iterations by hand. A search over the free parameters finds many alignments, and the number of bands is the lever T-004 did not use (rust 64, amber 32; the earlier gamma trials kept bands low and moved the knee into thin bands).
- The 1 km figure is therefore parameter-stable only to about 10 % in start/full (section 3.4); the gate is a property of the test geometry. I do not recommend any threshold change.

## 4. C11 off the reference plane

### 4.1 Mechanism (derived, then checked against the sidecars)
Setup (`c11_sim.py`): reference depth z_ref 664.9 m, snap grid G = one texel at z_ref (1.5994 m), k = 4 output px per texel, camera lateral speed 0.0902 G/frame (the sidecars' 0.361 px/frame at the tower edge). The rig renders from a snapped position S (a multiple of G) and presents with a uniform whole-output-pixel shift equal to the remainder (`present_shift_output_px`, round(4 x remainder)). For a surface at rho = z_ref/z: rendered edge b = round(rho (X - S)/G + phi), output k b - shift, commanded -k rho c.
- Reference plane (rho = 1): S steps by one texel so the raster phase is locked and the shift cancels the remainder: error <= 0.5 px quantisation, 0.93 px in the model (measured 1.04).
- Far (rho -> 0): the uniform shift moves a surface that did not move: error = shift, up to 1 texel = 4 px (model 5.7 worst phase / 4.8 median; measured 3.4).
- Near (rho = 5.35, z 124 m): rendered position is static between snaps while the true position moves rho x 0.0902 texel/frame, and re-rasterises with a new phase at every snap: error about 4.35 texel = 17 px (model 14.4 / 11.7; measured 10.6 over 28 in-view frames).
Calibration is therefore order-of-magnitude (model near/far a little above the measured maxima because the measured near class only sees 28 frames).

### 4.2 What each technique gives (model, px, max over 16 edge phases; worst phase / median)
| Technique | near (124 m) | ref (663 m) | far (10.4 km) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current: single snap, uniform shift | 14.4 / 11.7 | 4.8 (tie flips) / 0.93 | 5.7 / 4.8 |
| Finer snap grid G/2, G/4, G/8 | 9.9 / 7.8, 6.2 / 4.6, 6.2 / 4.2 | 2.8-4.8 / 2.8-3.0 | 5.8 / 3.9, 4.8 / 3.4, 3.9 / 3.0 |
| Depth-aware shift in Present (per-pixel parallax of its own depth, single snap) | 4.5 / 3.4 | 4.8 / 0.93 | 4.0 / 3.0 |
| Per-layer snap, layer reference depth = surface depth (3 layers at the class depths) | **0.67** | **0.96** | **0.66** |
| Per-layer snap, N log-spaced layers 30 m-30 km, worst over all surface depths | N=3 13.1, N=6 7.4, N=12 5.4, N=20 4.9, N=28 4.9 | | |
Notes: worst-phase "tie flips" are edge phases that sit exactly on a rounding boundary and change by one whole texel (4 px). The depth-aware row removes the parallax term but leaves the raster-phase error.

### 4.3 Why 1 px at all depths cannot be reached by shifting
A depth discontinuity in a texel raster sits on a 4 px grid; the metric subtracts only the first frame, so the phase must be *constant*. Phase is constant only if the snap step moves the surface by a whole number of texels: rho x (S step / G) integer. That holds at rho = 1 (the reference), at integer ratios, and for a layer snapped to its own grid at its own depth. Anywhere else the phase wanders by up to 1 texel (4 px) however small the position grid or however exact the shift. More layers help only at their reference depths (the N-layer row stays near 5 px for worst depth). The sub-pixel present pass is a shift, so it cannot remove it. Rotation-only compensation (the Y1 case, uniform for every depth) is already what the pass does and is not applicable to the translation case.
Sources (search 2026-11-15, snippets only): Texel Splatting, perspective-stable 3D pixel art, arXiv 2603.14587 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14587) states the orthographic-only validity of camera snapping and proposes world-space texel quads; community camera-snap-plus-subpixel-offset practice (UPixelator docs, https://github.com/Radivarig/UPixelator_Documentation; Godot threads https://godotforums.org/d/36180-subpixel-snapping-in-a-3d-pixel-art-game, https://godotengine.org/qa/97389/for-game-rendered-low-resolution-would-make-subpixel-camera): single snapped camera and a remainder offset, orthographic. Unity Pixel Perfect Camera docs (https://docs.unity3d.com/Manual/urp/2d-pixelperfect-ref.html): 2D sprites only. I did not fetch t3ssel8r's video; its method is the single-plane snap and offset above (not verified).

### 4.4 Recommendation
1. Step A (cheap, T-180): depth-aware shift in Present, shift = round(k rho remainder) from the linear depth already bound; predicted near 4.5, far 4.0, ref unchanged. Does not reach 1 px; removes the dominant error and is a prerequisite for B.
2. Step B (T-181, Opus-designed): per-layer snapped slabs (near / main / far) each with its own snap grid and shift; predicted 0.7-1.0 px at the slab reference depths. Costs 3 scene passes (draw-call budget C15 to be re-measured), and objects crossing a slab boundary seam (ground at the boundary changes phase): put the boundaries at haze band edges.
3. The T1 classes are at 124 m, 663 m and 10.4 km. A 3-slab design passes C11 *because* the slabs sit at those depths; for continuous depth the model shows about 5 px worst case. That is the founders' scope question (Q-260), not a number I change. **Decided autonomously (founder asleep): keep the C11 gate unchanged; implement A then B with slab reference depths from the rig's design depths (mech/target, 600 m skyline, far); report the any-depth figure beside the gate; do not claim C11 for arbitrary depth.**
4. Rejected: finer snap grid (reference plane degrades to 3-5 px, shimmer M-004 worsens); per-pixel reprojection alone (above); halo or any change to the measurement.

## 5. Risks of C11
Disocclusion tearing at depth edges when the shift differs between near and far; slab seam phase jumps; extra passes against the 60 fps budget (M-008) and perf-1..5 evidence not available on Metal batch; M-004 crawl/flicker (a finer snap grid would break it); the model is a lateral-translation, single-axis abstraction: yaw (Y1) and diagonal motion are not simulated.
