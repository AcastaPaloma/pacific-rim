# R-100: Full 1:1 Bay geo bake: tile coverage, data volume, height sources, landmarks, infrastructure, M-005@v2 plan

Agent: M2 Track 1 worker (T-100) · Date: 2026-11-15 · Status: Proposed (founder asleep, §15: every question below is decided with its default)
Requirements: F-040, F-041 ("both bridges, downtown towers, the port of Oakland, Alcatraz, etc."), F-044 ("bridges, power, port cranes, airport"), D-010 (full Bay at 1:1), M-005 ("Bay is accurate"). Decisions served: Q-200 (Bay tiles bake into the git-ignored cache), Q-201 (every L0 tile in the R-004 world box with land or Bay/estuary water), Q-202 (lidar DSM fill wherever 3DEP lidar exists; county footprints only with a compatible licence; never invented).
Builds on `research/R-004.md` (cited as R-004 §n). Evidence is the committed `bay_manifest@v1` (`Assets/StreamingAssets/Geo/global/v1/bay_manifest.json`, 1.3 MB) and its inputs `Pipelines/geo/config/bay.json`, both produced by `Pipelines/geo/bay_manifest.py`.

**Question.** Which tiles are "the Bay", what does each need, how much data and time does the whole set cost, where do building heights outside SF come from, what is the source for every F-041 landmark and F-044 infrastructure class, and how is M-005 measured at Bay scale and the bake proven reproducible?

**Headline.**
- The world box holds **4311 L0 tiles** (of the 68 x 68 = 4624 grid; 313 are outside): **1048 urban, 2006 rural, 149 water-only (Bay/estuary), 1108 ocean-only**. 3054 tiles have land; 1.87 M OSM buildings sit in them, 98.5 % in the urban class.
- Static downloads are **40.4 GB** (CUDEM 5.00, 3DEP 1 m 33.80, OSM PBF 0.66, water polygons 0.91; the last two are already cached). Lidar height fill adds **about 40-100 GB** of EPT reads (Assumed, pilot in T-103). Baked output is about **86 GB** in the git-ignored cache (about 20 MB per tile, not the 5-15 MB of the M2 plan). Disk free: 438 GB.
- **Two coverage holes the plan did not know about:** (1) CUDEM 1/9" covers only 26 of the 0.25-degree cells; **702 land tiles** (north of 38.25 N, east of 121.75 W, south edge) and **458 ocean tiles** have no CUDEM. Land tiles can use 3DEP 1 m alone; the ocean tiles need another bathymetry source (Q-NEW-b). (2) The existing fetch hard-codes `_2022v1`; the files are **2022v1, 2023v1 and 2025v1** by area.
- No county publishes a licence-clean footprint-plus-height layer except Santa Clara (no use limits stated) and Marin (heights from elevations, no licence stated). The default is lidar DSM fill everywhere (Q-202); 80 tiles with buildings lie outside every EPT lidar project in the public index and fall back to levels x 4.22 m and then a flagged default.

## 1. Tile coverage set (Q-201)

### 1.1 Method (all offline, deterministic, `bay_manifest.py build`, 8 s)
- Tiles: every L0 tile whose square intersects the world box (123.05-121.55 W, 37.15-38.35 N) transformed into BayTM (R-004 §2.1): extent x 4.28-137.5 km, z 3.04-136.5 km. 4051 tiles are fully inside; 260 are cut by the box edge (`in_box_fraction` < 1) and still listed because their data are needed to seam neighbours.
- Land fraction per tile: 1 minus the union of OSM water polygons (osmdata split 4326, sha256 22d97b20...) clipped to the tile [1]. Land = dry side of the OSM coastline, so Bay salt ponds and the Delta count as land.
- Bay versus ocean water: a tile with no land is **water_only** when at least 50 % of its water lies in `bay_region_polygons_lonlat` (the Bay and Delta east of the Golden Gate mouth Point Bonita-Lands End, plus Tomales Bay, Bolinas Lagoon, Drakes Estero, Bodega Harbor), else **ocean_only**.
- Buildings per tile: one pass over the cached NorCal PBF counting `building` ways by first-node tile (3 min, relations ignored, so counts run a few percent low) [2].
- Thresholds (config, Assumed): land >= 0.1 % of the tile (4190 m2); urban >= 100 OSM building ways (1048 of 3054 land tiles qualify; 713 have >= 500); rural otherwise.

Throughput measured 2026-11-15 from this machine, single stream, 80 MB range reads: prd-tnm S3 (3DEP) **6.4 MB/s**, NOAA S3 (CUDEM) **10.8 MB/s**, Geofabrik **9.1 MB/s**. Four parallel streams were not measured; scale linearly until the link saturates (Assumed).

| 3DEP 1 m DEM [6] | 151 UTM tiles, newest project per tile (CA_AlamedaCounty_2021_B21 26 tiles 6.3 GB; CA_SolanoCounty_A23 21, 5.1 GB; CA_CaliforniaGaps_B23 21, 4.1 GB; CA_NorthernCA_B22 20, 4.8 GB; CA_NoCal_Wildfires_B5b_QL1_2018 17, 3.9 GB; CA_SantaClaraCounty_2020_A20 10, 3.7 GB; others 36 tiles 5.9 GB) | **33.80 GB** | 88 min / 22 min | windows cached only |

| Faults, NGS, tide datums | small | < 0.1 GB | minutes | faults cached |

- **Whole-tile versus window reads.** The existing `fetch_3dep.py` reads a window per bake tile (14 MB deflated for downtown). 3054 land tiles x about 10 MB is about 30 GB, the same as the 151 whole files; whole files are resumable, shared by up to 25 tiles each and fetched once. **Default: T-101 downloads whole 3DEP and CUDEM files** (checksummed, resumable) and the bake reads local windows.
- **Lidar EPT volume (Assumed).** The only measurement is the SF downtown tile: 60 M first returns kept in 259-456 s, 6.4 GB resident [lidar logs, T-020b]. Reading whole tiles at that density for 1048 urban tiles would cost 0.2-0.5 TB and about 70 h serial. **Default: read only 128 m cells that contain a building whose height is still `default` after OSM and DataSF** (about 75 % of buildings have no `height` tag: 458,683 of 1.87 M have one, 29,433 have only levels). Footprints cover 5-15 % of a tile, so the estimate is **40-100 GB and 15-25 h at 4 workers**. A five-tile pilot in T-103 (one per county type) replaces this estimate with a measurement before T-103 commits to the plan.
- **Bake.** Existing logs: 7.7-37 s per tile (median about 9 s; SF dense tiles 15-37 s), peak memory 6.9 GB on the SF tile (24 GB machine, 15 cores). At 3 workers: **about 4-5 h** for 4311 tiles, ocean tiles being fastest. Output about 19-24 MB per tile (`terrain.tif` 12 MB, `terrain.r16` 8.4 MB, vectors 1-4 MB): **about 86 GB** for the set.
- Disk plan: 40 GB raw + 40-100 GB lidar cache + 86 GB baked = 170-230 GB of 438 GB free. Lidar DSM cache entries (40-100 MB each as npz) can be deleted after the bake because `height_source=lidar` is stored in the tile.

## 3. Building heights outside San Francisco (Q-202)

OSM carries a `height` on 24.5 % of buildings Bay-wide (458,683 of 1,874,513 building ways) and levels on 1.6 %, so about 74 % rely on a fallback. DataSF [8] exists only inside San Francisco (146 tiles). The fallback chain per building becomes: OSM `height` -> DataSF (SF only) -> **lidar DSM fill** (T-020b: p95 first return minus terrain over the eroded footprint, vegetation rejected) -> levels x 4.22 m -> flagged default. County footprint layers are inputs only if their licence is compatible (checked below); none is needed to meet the default-height share.

| County | Open building layer found | Height attribute | Licence / terms | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Santa Clara | "Building Outlines 2020" REST layer (lidar project deliverable, Sanborn, 2020) [9][10] | `Base_Heigh`, `Building_H` | metadata: no use limitations, credit courtesy to USGS and Sanborn; Open Data layer says "AS IS" [9] | candidate cross-check and gap filler; confirm licence text with the county (Q-NEW-a) |
| Marin | "Building Footprints" feature service (2004 stereo + 2014 ortho, edited 2023) [11] | `BaseElevationMinFeet`, `PeakElevationMaxFeet` | no licence or copyright text on the service [11] | **not used until a licence is shown**; a layer without a licence is all rights reserved by default |
| Sonoma | "Buildings" layer, building outlines [12] | none (name, type) | attribution "County of Sonoma" required [12] | footprints only, no heights; OSM covers it (Sonoma import [13]); not needed |
| San Mateo | county GIS download "Building Footprints", exported daily [14] | not listed | page states "All rights reserved" [14] | **incompatible**: not used |
| Alameda | none found on the county portal (Hayward-only 2018 layer in library catalogues) [15] | n/a | n/a | lidar fill; 3 EPT projects (AlamedaCo 1-3 2021) cover the county |
| Contra Costa | none found (2007 school outlines only) [15] | n/a | n/a | lidar fill; eastern fringe has no EPT (48 tiles) |
| Napa | county ArcGIS "Building_Footprints_Public" feature layer; 2014 Berkeley copy [16] | not verified | no licence found; "incomplete, use with caution" [16] | lidar fill; revisit if licence appears |
| Solano | 2015 footprints in Stanford repository, public domain, by the Solano regional GIS consortium [17] | none | public domain, "not legally authoritative" [17] | footprints only; OSM is newer; not needed |

National alternatives (R-004 §1.4): Microsoft US Footprints and Overture buildings are ODbL (compatible with the existing OSM share-alike posture, no heights) [R-004 §1.4]. USGS-derived heights exist for Overture but were not verified for these counties; **not used** (independent lidar fill is already the better, per-building-flagged source).

**Why lidar is enough.** Of the 2452 tiles with buildings, 2370 (96.7 %) overlap at least one EPT project. The 82 without are 61 rural, 19 urban (Contra Costa 48 of them, then Sacramento 23, Yolo 5, San Joaquin 3, ocean fringe 3). They hold 12,413 OSM buildings (0.7 % of the Bay), so the Bay-wide 2 % clause is safe even if all took the default, but the **per-county** M-005@v2 clause (<= 2 %) must be checked for Contra Costa after T-103 (Q-NEW-d: the EPT index lags the TNM lidar point-cloud index; T-103 queries TNM `Lidar Point Cloud (LPC)` for these 82 tiles first).

## 7. M-100 draft: bake reproducibility

- **Definition.** Two independent bakes of the same manifest fingerprint produce byte-identical outputs.
- **Procedure.** (1) `bay_manifest.py build` twice: identical bytes (tested). (2) Pick N = 48 tiles stratified by class (24 urban, 12 rural, 6 water-only, 6 ocean-only) with a fixed seed derived from the manifest fingerprint. Bake them twice in fresh processes with different `--threads` and different tile order; delete the cache `bake/` directory between runs, keeping raw downloads. (3) Hash every output file except `build_info.json` (git revision, wall time). (4) After the full Bay bake, a second full run with the committed manifest and an unchanged raw cache must report `up to date` for 4311 of 4311 tiles (input fingerprint no-op).
- **Threshold.** 100 % of files byte-identical in (2), 4311 of 4311 no-ops in (4), zero network use in the second run (`--offline` guard). No tolerance.
- **Source.** Existing T-003 determinism rules (sorted ids, mm-rounded coordinates, canonical JSON, input fingerprint) and `tests/test_ids_and_bake.py` re-bake identity; scaled from 1 tile to the Bay. The manifest fingerprint is sha256 over config, water-polygon sha256 and the manifest code hash.
- **Failure rule.** A difference is a bug in the bake; never widen the sample or add a tolerance.
