### 1.4 Scaling rule for 76–88 m and 6,000–15,000 t
Geometric similarity in length, independent mass: lengths and positions × H/80; masses × M/10⁷; inertias × (M/10⁷)(H/80)²; foot area × (H/80)²; ROM unchanged. Gains scale with inertia (k ∝ M H² at fixed ω_n; ω_n for Froude-similar timing ∝ H^−½ gives k ∝ M H). Torques ∝ M H, powers ∝ M H^½, ground pressure ∝ M/H². Hull density ∝ M/H³: at 76 m/6,000 t it is 862 kg/m³, at 80 m/6,000 t 739, at 88 m/6,000 t 556 kg/m³, **below sea water (1,025)**, so a light, tall mech would float when fully submerged in the Golden Gate channel (F-045). Hull density ≥ 1,025 needs M ≥ 8,320 t × (H/80)³ for this hull. Proposed Q-NEW-a.

## 3. PD drive tuning at this scale

### 3.1 Unity API, verified against the 6000.6 scripting reference
- `ArticulationBody` has `jointType`, `xDrive`/`yDrive`/`zDrive`, `twistLock`, `swingYLock`, `swingZLock`, `mass`, `inertiaTensor`, `inertiaTensorRotation`, `automaticCenterOfMass`, `automaticInertiaTensor`, `centerOfMass`, `solverIterations`, `solverVelocityIterations`, `sleepThreshold`, `maxAngularVelocity`, `maxLinearVelocity`, `maxJointVelocity`, `maxDepenetrationVelocity`, `jointFriction`, `linearDamping`, `angularDamping`, `collisionDetectionMode`, `jointPosition`/`jointVelocity`/`jointForce`, `driveForce`, and methods `GetDriveForces`, `GetJointForces`, `GetJointGravityForces`, `GetJointCoriolisCentrifugalForces`, `GetJointForcesForAcceleration`, `SetDriveTarget(s)`, `SetDriveTargetVelocity`, `SetDriveStiffness`, `SetDriveDamping`, `SetDriveForceLimit`, `SetJointForces`, `TeleportRoot`, `WakeUp` [5].
- `ArticulationDrive` fields: `stiffness`, `damping`, `forceLimit`, `target`, `targetVelocity`, `driveType`, `lowerLimit`, `upperLimit` [6]. Units as documented: **target in degrees** for angular drives; **stiffness N·m/rad**; **damping N·m·s/rad**; **forceLimit N·m**; **targetVelocity rad/s** [6]. `ArticulationDriveType` = Force, Acceleration, Target, Velocity [6].
- Documented law: "F = stiffness * (currentPosition - target) - damping * (currentVelocity - targetVelocity)" [6]. As written the spring term has the non-restoring sign; the physical (and PhysX) law is τ = k(θ_t − θ) + d(θ̇_t − θ̇), clamped to ±forceLimit, with PhysX drives **implicit** ("can handle very large gains") [8]. **Risk R1:** the degree/radian mix (target in deg, k per rad, targetVelocity in rad/s while the doc's own example writes "90 rad/s") must be measured, not trusted: build one revolute link of known I, set k, read the oscillation period; ω_meas = √(k/I) confirms rad, √(57.3 k/I) means per-degree.
- Use **driveType = Force**. Acceleration drives make the response independent of link mass [8], which would hide a lost limb or added armour (P2 violation). Target/Velocity types track "almost kinematically" [6]: a fake, banned for gameplay bodies.

### 3.2 Gain choice
For a joint whose moving side has effective inertia I about the joint axis, target natural frequency ω_n and damping ratio ζ:
k = I ω_n², d = 2 ζ I ω_n, forceLimit = 1.2–1.5 × the required peak torque (§3.3).
Choose ζ = 1 (critically damped: no overshoot, which reads as weight, P1) and ω_n ≈ 5–7 × the gait frequency: the stride at Fr 0.25 takes 6.3 s (0.16 Hz), so **ω_n = 2π × 1.0 Hz for swing limbs, 0.5 Hz for whole-body (stance) joints**. Feed-forward the gravity and inertial torques (`GetJointGravityForces`, `GetJointForcesForAcceleration`, applied via `SetJointForces`/`jointForce`) so the PD only corrects errors; PhysX notes inverse-dynamics results exclude the drives [8]. Effective inertias from the rest pose (script output):

| knee, swing | 1.90e8 | 1.9e9 | 1.2e9 | 7.5e9 | 2.4e9 |

**Say it plainly: these gains are 1e8–1e12 N·m/rad and 1e8–1e11 N·m·s/rad**, i.e. 6–9 orders of magnitude above the defaults in any tutorial; at 88 m/15,000 t the stance ankle reaches 1.3e12. Gains must be **scheduled by phase** (stance vs swing): the stance-ankle gain on an airborne foot gives ω = √(7.3e11/8.7e6) = 289 rad/s, ω·Δt = 4.8 at 60 Hz, unstable for any explicit PD; PhysX's implicit drive survives it but over-stiffens the chain and fights other constraints ("the constraint resolved last ... overwrites" [8]). Do **not** implement PD as explicit `AddTorque`; if a custom PD is ever needed outside the drives, use stable PD (Tan et al.), which evaluates the spring at the next-step state [9].

### 3.3 Required torques and powers
Dynamic similarity: at equal Froude number, joint torque per unit mass scales with length and power per unit mass with √length [10]. Length ratio 42/0.93 = 45.2 (√ = 6.72). Human peaks at Fr ≈ 0.25 (≈ 1.5 m/s): ankle 1.4–1.7 N·m/kg and 2.5–3.5 W/kg; knee 0.5–0.9 N·m/kg, 0.8–1.0 W/kg; hip 0.8–1.35 N·m/kg, 0.7–1.45 W/kg [11][12][13] (sources disagree; range kept).

**Recommended forceLimit (Assumed):** ankle 1.0e9 (saturates near the foot-tipping limit), knee 8e8, hip 1.2e9, waist 5e8, shoulder 3e8, elbow 1.5e8 N·m. Standing still costs almost nothing (straight knee, CoM over the feet): static torques ≈ 0 except balance corrections bounded by the ankle row above.
**Power vs reactor.** Mean positive mechanical power = CoT_mech × m g v; human mechanical CoT ≈ 0.05–0.1 (net metabolic ≈ 0.3 [14] at ≈ 25 % muscle efficiency) gives **50–100 MW** at 10.15 m/s; joint peaks sum to ≈ 270–400 MW for ≈ 0.5 s at push-off. No reactor figure exists in the design (D-019 names a "reactor power budget" only). Benchmark: one naval A4W reactor is 550 MWt and drives ≈ 104 MW of shaft power [15]. Proposed default (Q-NEW-b, Assumed): **300 MW continuous electrical, plus a 1.5 GJ buffer (≈ 150 MW for 10 s) for peaks**; walking at Fr 0.25 then takes ≈ 25–40 % of the reactor after ≈ 80 % drivetrain efficiency (no regeneration assumed), leaving room for thrusters (F-004) only in bursts. Peak joint angular speeds: human 4–6 rad/s × 1/6.72 ≈ 0.6–0.9 rad/s.

### 3.4 Drive setup recipe (per link, at build time from `mech_frame@v1`)
1. `automaticCenterOfMass = automaticInertiaTensor = false`; set `mass`, `centerOfMass` (segment CoM relative to the link), `inertiaTensor` (principal values), `inertiaTensorRotation = identity` in the rest pose. Collider-derived inertia would follow mesh art, not the config (MODULARITY §1.3).
2. `anchorRotation` so that anchor X = the joint's first DOF axis (flexion, world +x in rest pose); `matchAnchors = true`.
3. Lock unused DOFs (`twistLock`/`swingYLock`/`swingZLock` = Locked); active DOFs Limited with `lowerLimit`/`upperLimit` from `rom_deg` × `unity_sign` (swap min/max when the sign is −1).
4. Drive: `driveType = Force`; k, d from §3.2 for the joint's current phase; `forceLimit` from §3.3; `target` in degrees.
5. Each step: write targets with `SetDriveTargets` (one call for the hierarchy), feed-forward with `SetJointForces`, read back `GetDriveForces`/`GetJointForces` for the log. Index with `GetDofStartIndices`; never assume DOF order.

## 5. Walking control for M0

### 5.1 Options
| Approach | Robustness | Cost | Feel / tunability | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SIMBICON-style FSM + PD + linear foot-placement feedback [23] | robust to pushes and terrain in sim; few parameters | low | phase-based, readable gait; few gains | **core** |
| LIPM / capture-point (DCM) foot placement [24][25] | analytic step location that guarantees stopping; handles intent changes | low | clean speed/stop control; physically interpretable | **foot-placement law** |
| Virtual-force (Jacobian-transpose) stance control + gravity compensation [26] | generalises across bodies and speeds | medium | intuitive (gains in N, N/m) | **stance-leg law** |
| ZMP preview control [27] | excellent on flat ground with exact model | medium–high; needs accurate dynamics and a footstep preview | stiff, robotic; poor with pushes | defer |
| Learned controllers (DeepMimic-style) [28] | high when trained | high: training per body and mass; retrain after damage (F-007) | style from clips; hard to tune by hand | later, for kaiju |

**Recommendation:** a Coros-style controller [26]: SIMBICON finite-state machine, capture-point step placement, Jacobian-transpose virtual forces on the stance leg, PD tracking through ArticulationDrives toward IK targets (F-001), gravity feed-forward. It is analytic, needs no training, survives mass changes from damage (it reads masses from the articulation at runtime) and every gain has a physical meaning.

### 5.2 Control loop (each physics step, 60 Hz)
1. **Intent → command** (D-013, `MODULARITY` §4.1). `IntentFrame{move, target, effort}` from `IIntentArbiter`. Walk: heading ψ_d toward `target`, speed v_d = effort × v_max (v_max, a_max, turn-rate limits from R-001), ramped so |v̇_d| ≤ min(a_max, (μ_avail/1.5 − 0.2) g) and stopping distance d_stop = v²/(2a) ≥ distance to target triggers Stop. Brace, Turn, Stop are moves of their own.
2. **State estimate**: CoM x, ẋ from link masses (`worldCenterOfMass`, `linearVelocity`), stance foot from contact events, LIPM ω₀ = √(g/z_c) = √(9.81/42.5) = 0.48 1/s (time constant 2.08 s), DCM ξ = x + ẋ/ω₀.
3. **State machine**: Stand → (shift CoM over first stance foot) → Swing_L ↔ DoubleSupport ↔ Swing_R → Stop → Stand; plus Brace, Falling, Fallen, GetUp. Swing ends on the **footfall contact event**, not on a timer (time-out T_swing + 0.5 s). Nominal step at Fr 0.25 (Alexander & Jayes stride λ/L = 2.3 Fr^0.3 [10]): stride 63.7 m, step 31.9 m, step time 3.14 s, swing ≈ 2.4 s, double support ≈ 0.7 s.
4. **Foot placement (DCM)**: ξ̇ = ω₀(ξ − p). At touchdown place the foot at p_next = ξ_end − b, with b = s/(e^{ω₀T} − 1) along the heading (s = v_d T; at Fr 0.25, e^{ω₀T} = 4.52, b = 9.1 m) and ± half stance width (6.5 m) laterally, rotated by the heading. To stop: put the foot at ξ (b = 0): at 10.15 m/s ξ is 21.1 m ahead of the CoM, inside the 48 m reach (2 L sin 35°), so the mech stops in one to two steps. If ξ leaves the reachable region → Falling.
5. **Swing foot**: horizontal minimum-jerk path from lift-off to p_next; vertical clearance bump 3–5 m (scaled human foot lift; clears cars and debris); sole kept parallel to the ground, 5° dorsiflexed for heel-first contact; touchdown vertical speed ≤ 1.0 m/s (Assumed, pending calibration: harder reads heavier but raises impulse, §6). Analytic two-link IK (hip, knee) + ankle → drive targets (degrees) with swing gains.
6. **Stance leg**: virtual force F = K_p(x_d − x) + K_d(v_d − ẋ) on the CoM (K_p = m ω_c², K_d = 2 m ω_c, ω_c = 2π×0.3 Hz; with m = 1e7: 3.6e7 N/m and 3.8e7 N·s/m), mapped τ = Jᵀ F over stance ankle, knee and hip; torso pitch/roll held by the stance hip (SIMBICON: τ_stance hip = −τ_torso − τ_swing hip [23]); gravity feed-forward. Ankle torque clamped so the CoP stays 10 % inside the sole: |τ_pitch| ≤ F_n × (0.9 × 9.75 m toe or 0.9 × 5.25 m heel), |τ_roll| ≤ F_n × 4.5 m.
7. **Turning**: heading change per step |Δψ| ≤ ω_turn,max × T_step (R-001 limit), applied as stance-hip yaw during single support and a rotated swing-foot target; turn in place = short steps (s ≤ 0.3 L) with alternating toe-in/toe-out; the foot never twists while loaded (avoid slip, M-002).
8. **Limits enforcement**: before committing a step, check predicted joint torques against forceLimit and predicted power against the power subsystem's grant (`ISubsystem` Power, F-008); if over, lower v_d or lengthen T. This is "within mass and balance limits" (F-002).
9. **Brace**: stance width 13 → 20 m, knees 20–40° flexed (knee torque 1.3–2.6e8 per leg), CoM lowered 2–6 m, stance gains to 1 Hz.

### 5.3 Planted-foot friction (no slide) and contact frames (F-009)
Required friction: μ_req = |F_t|/F_n. Human level walking needs ≈ 0.2 peak (required coefficient of friction) [29]; with dynamic similarity the same ratio holds at 80 m. Horizontal CoM acceleration adds a/g: a = 0.25/0.5/1.0/2.0 m/s² needs 0.23/0.25/0.30/0.40 in total. Rule: **μ_avail ≥ 1.5 μ_req**, so with μ_d = 0.6 the controller may command at most a ≈ 0.2 g; on mud (μ ≈ 0.3, Assumed) the ceiling is ≈ 0 extra, so it shortens steps. Contact frames: set `providesContacts = true` on sole colliders and read `Physics.ContactEvent` (all contact pairs of the step, `ContactPairHeader` → `ContactPair` → `ContactPairPoint`) [30]. Footfall = first step where, after a swing state, the sole's summed normal impulse over the step J_n > 0.05 m g Δt (5 % of body weight) **and** separation ≤ 0; record `t_contact = t_step − Δt + separation_prev / v_approach` (sub-step interpolation). Lift-off = F_n < 0.02 m g for 2 consecutive steps (hysteresis). Speculative CCD reports contacts with separation > 0: ignore them for the event.

### 5.4 Bring-up order (each step is a PlayMode test that must pass before the next)
1. Single revolute link: drive-unit check (R1), k and d against the predicted period and decay.
2. Mech on a fixed root (`immovable` pelvis): every joint tracks a 0.1 Hz sine of ±10° with gravity feed-forward; tracking error ≤ 1°.
3. Free root, standing on two feet, drives holding the rest pose: M-006.1 and M-006.4 pass; CoM drift ≤ 5 cm over 60 s.
4. Push recovery while standing: a 0.05 m g horizontal push for 2 s is absorbed by the ankle strategy without a step; 0.3 m g for 2 s (above the ankle-strategy ceiling of toe lever / CoM height = 9.75 / 42.5 = 0.23 g) triggers a capture step instead of a fall.
5. Weight shift and a single step, then continuous walking at 0.5, 0.75, 1.0 × v(Fr 0.25); turn; stop on target; brace.
6. Falls: limp topple (M-006.5), then fall detection from DCM leaving the reachable region.

## 7. Fall energetics
- §4 check: m g h_cm = 1e7 × 9.81 × 42.54 = **4.17 GJ ≈ 1.00 t TNT** ✓ (1 t TNT = 4.184 GJ). Energy actually released from standing to prone (CoM ends ≈ 6 m up, torso half-depth 5 m + ground armour) is **3.59 GJ ≈ 0.86 t TNT**.
- Rigid toppling about the toe line (r = 43.6 m from pivot to CoM, I_pivot = I_cm + m r² = 2.27e10 kg·m²): λ = √(m g r / I_pivot) = 0.434 1/s; impact angular speed ω = √(2 m g (r − 6) / I_pivot) = **0.57 rad/s**; CoM speed at impact **24.9 m/s**, head top **45.6 m/s** (≈ a 106 m free fall for the head). Backward over the heels: 0.57 rad/s, 24.5 / 45.8 m/s.
- Time to fall from an initial tilt θ₀ (θ̈ = λ² sin θ, integrated numerically): **11.9 s from 1°, 8.2 s from 5°, 5.6 s from 15°.** (Human ≈ 1.2 s from 5°, consistent with t ∝ √L: 8.2/6.7.) A slow topple is physically correct, P1-positive, and leaves 5–8 s for thruster fall arrest (F-004).

### 8.9 `physics_log@v1`
JSON Lines, one header line then one record per physics step, plus event records. It **extends** a `gait_log@v1`-style log: same `run_id`, `step` (integer physics step) and `t` (s = step × Δt) keys so the two join row-for-row; it never redefines a gait_log field.

- Step record: `run_id`, `step`, `t`, `com` [3], `com_vel` [3], `ke_lin`, `ke_rot`, `pe`, `e_total`, `p` [3], `L_com` [3], `max_link_speed`, `max_joint_speed`, `clamps` (count), `nan` (bool), `joints` {id: {q_deg[], qd[], target_deg[], tau_drive[], tau_total[], power, saturated}}, `feet` {L/R: {contact, Jn, Jt [2], cop [3], n_points, mu_util, pressure_kpa}}, `power_total_pos`, `power_total_abs`, `external_work` (J this step: thrusters, scripted forces).
