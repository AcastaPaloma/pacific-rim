## 2. Panel inventory

Method (`analyse_panels.py`):
- **Grid detection.** Take strong edges (top 5% of |∂I|) and score each candidate period p ∈ [2, 12] px with the mean resultant length R = |mean(e^{2πi·x/p})| of edge positions (circular statistics). Choose the largest p with R ≥ 0.85·max, because sub-harmonics p/2 and p/3 score equally. R ≥ 0.08 counts as detected; 0.05–0.08 with consistent x/y and p ≤ 8 counts as weak. Posterised panels with no grid score 0.01–0.045.
- **De-noising.** Detected-grid panels are area-resampled to the native grid. Other panels get a 3×3 median filter and 2× decimation.
- **Palette.** Over-segment with k-means (k=64, CIELAB), drop clusters under 0.1%, then merge centroids agglomeratively while any pair is within ΔE00 < 3 (≈3 JND; entries this close are not separate palette colours to a viewer, see [2]). The "knee k" column is the Kneedle knee [6] of the k-means distortion for k = 2..24, i.e. the dominant-colour count.
- **Value bands.** Count peaks of the L* histogram (prominence ≥ 0.2%, spacing ≥ 4). Value levels = distinct L* among entries ≥ 0.5% share, merged at ΔL* < 4.
- **Hue families.** Peaks of the chroma-weighted circular hue histogram (C* ≥ 8, σ = 15°) holding ≥ 10% of the mass.
- **Accent.** One entry with 0.1–20% share, C* ≥ 12, and either C* ≥ 1.5× the median chroma or ≥ 60° from the dominant hue.

## 4. M-003@v1: matches the style references

Every sub-measure runs on one **capture set** (§6) of ≥ 12 frames per district profile from fixed, scripted viewpoints (street level, mech eye height ~70 m, elevated 300 m), with the UI disabled.

M-003 passes only if (a), (b), (c), (d-i to d-iv) and (f) all pass; (e) is advisory.

### (a) Palette adherence
- **Definition.** A_T = share of non-masked pixels whose CIEDE2000 [1] distance to the nearest colour of the frame's declared profile (`style.palette_profile_id`) is ≤ T.
- **Procedure.** Decode the PNG, convert sRGB to CIELAB (D65), compute the nearest-colour ΔE00 (`m003.py: adherence`). If a `palette_index` capture is present, its 255 count must equal the internal-capture failures.
- **Threshold.**
  - T = 2.0; gate A_2 ≥ 0.95 on `color_output` (this is the M-003 seed text) and A_2 ≥ 0.995 on `color_internal`.
  - The second number is Assumed: the LUT quantiser emits exact palette colours, so only additive VFX or UI can leak.
- **Derivation.**
  - The CIEDE2000 JND is ≈ 1 by design [2]. CIELAB ΔE\*ab JND ≈ 2.3 [3]; the interpretation is disputed, which is one more reason to use ΔE00.
  - 8-bit rounding of any sRGB colour costs ≤ 0.88 ΔE00, and a 1-code-value error ≤ 1.22 (measured, 200k random colours), so T = 2 never rejects a legitimate encoding.
- **Controls** (`m003_validation.json`, slate_teal profile, synthetic scene):

| Capture | A_2 | Result |
|---|---|---|
| quantised (Bayer, no dither or white noise) | 1.000 | pass |
| continuous, unquantised ramp render | 0.847 | **fail** (A_5 = 0.998 would pass, so T = 5 is too lax) |
| bilinear upscale to output | 0.871 | **fail** |

- The reference self-adherence (F2) does **not** set the gate. The references are lossy (raw at T=2: 0.63–0.85; ref_06A 0.16) and pass only at T = 3–5. They are reported to show that the extracted palettes describe them: ≥ 0.92 at T = 3 for 10 of 11 panels.

### (b) Dithering present in gradients
- **Definition**, on the `palette_index` image (or `color_internal` mapped to indices) at internal resolution:
  - A *gradient pixel* has a low-passed L\* slope (Gaussian σ = 3 px) in [0.25, 4] L\*/px.
  - An *interleaved window* (4×4) meets all of: top colour < 90%; ≤ 4 distinct indices; ≥ 35% of 4-neighbour pairs differ; mean |L\* − box₄(L\*)| ≥ 1, i.e. a real palette step.
  - **DIG** = share of gradient pixels inside interleaved windows.
  - **Ordered index O** = ρ(lag 2) − ρ(lag 1) of the de-screened L\* residual inside interleaved windows, averaged over x and y. Bayer and checker patterns score > 0.5, white noise ≈ 0, clustered pixel masses < 0 (Bayer matrix: [4]; halftone background: [5]).
- **Procedure.** `m003.py: dither_metrics`. Frames whose gradient share is < 5% are skipped.
- **Threshold.** DIG ≥ 0.15 on ≥ 80% of evaluated frames. O is **advisory** until Q-NEW-a is answered. Advisory upper bound DIG ≤ 0.5, because no reference panel exceeds 0.33 and a whole frame of dither reads as noise.

### (c) Value banding by depth is monotonic

**c1, gameplay frames.**
- Exclude sky (z ≥ 0.99·far).
- Use log depth bands [0, 50, 150, 400, 1000, 2500, 6000, ∞) m, keeping bands with ≥ 1% of pixels.
- Compute mean L\* per band.
- **Pass:** no band-to-band drop > 2 L\*, Spearman ρ(band, L\*) ≥ 0.9, and far-minus-near span ≥ 15 L\* (Assumed).

### (e) Composition statistics (advisory, style not content)
- **Sky share** = share of z ≥ 0.99·far. Advisory band 0.20–0.50. In the references, a top-connected low-texture heuristic gives 0.23–0.46 where a sky exists (01A 0.46, 01C 0.23, 03A 0.40, 03B 0.41, 03C 0.38, 03D 0.30). The heuristic is weak (it returns 0 for misty or full-frame panels).
- **Geometric horizon height** from pose: h = 0.5 + tan(pitch)/(2·tan(vfov/2)), as a fraction from the bottom. Advisory: ≤ 0.45, because §9 says "horizon low, sky large".
- **Silhouette contrast** = L\*₉₀ − L\*₁₀ of the frame. Advisory: ≥ 25. References 17–77, median 46.
- None of these gate M-003. They feed the panel discussion.

### (f) Blind panel rubric, style match 1–10
- **Protocol** (ITU-T P.910 ACR-style single-stimulus [11], adapted):
  - Each evaluator (≥ 8: founders plus blinded agent evaluators; P.910 lab tests use more, so the result is labelled "small panel") sees one capture beside a fixed reference board of the 11 panels.
  - Order is random; build and source are hidden.
  - Each set includes 2 hidden positive anchors (crops of reference panels; expected ≥ 9) and 2 negative anchors (unquantised render of the same view; expected ≤ 3). A rater who scores the negative anchors ≥ 6 is excluded.
- **Pass:** panel mean ≥ 8.0 over ≥ 12 captures, and no profile mean < 7.
- **Score bands:**
  - **1–2:** Reads as a standard 3D render. Smooth gradients, unlimited colours, no pixel grid.
  - **3–4:** Pixelated or posterised, but hues are wrong for the profile, there is no haze ramp, and near and far have equal contrast.
  - **5–6:** Correct palette family and visible grid, but dithering is missing or mechanical everywhere, flat banding in sky and smoke, weak depth layering, silhouettes muddy.
  - **7:** Palette, grid and haze correct. One clear miss: dither texture, cloud or smoke treatment, accent overuse, or composition (high horizon, no scale figures).
  - **8:** Could sit on the board as a lesser panel: 2–3 hues plus accent, posterised value bands, dithered or clustered transitions, near-dark to far-light layering, readable silhouette.
  - **9:** Indistinguishable in style from the stronger panels. Clustered pixel clouds, light shafts or haze where fitting, tiny scale cues.
  - **10:** Would be picked as a reference panel itself.

## 5. M-004@v1: pixel stability (shimmer and crawl)

**Capture protocol** (sequences per §6):
- **Frames.** 180 frames per sequence at a fixed dt of 1/60 s. Simulation, animation and VFX time are frozen, or animated pixels are flagged in `mask_animated`. Each camera move is scripted and logged in `motion`.
- **Sequences** for each of 3 profiles and 2 viewpoints (eye height ~70 m; street level). Speeds are in internal px/frame, measured at the 600 m reference depth for translation and at screen centre for yaw. At 320×180 with a 60° horizontal FOV, one pixel is 2.165 m at 600 m, or 0.207°.

| Sequence | Speed (px/frame) | Real-world equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| S0 static | — | 60 frames |
| T1 lateral translation | 0.1 | 13 m/s |
| T2 lateral translation | 0.25 | 32 m/s |
| T3 lateral translation | 1.0 | 130 m/s |
| Y1 yaw | 0.25 | 3.1°/s |
| Y2 yaw | 1.0 | 12.4°/s |

**Metrics** (`m004.py`):
1. **Reprojection.** Back-project each pixel of frame t to world space using `depth` and pose, then project into frame t−k and take the nearest pixel. A pixel is valid if it is in bounds and passes the occlusion test |z_src − z_expected| ≤ 5%. Sky reprojects by direction.
   - This is the warping-error construction of [7], with ground-truth flow from depth and pose instead of estimated flow.
   - Warping error alone rewards blur [7]; that is harmless here, because M-003 forbids blur.
2. **Shimmer S_px.** Share of valid pixels whose palette index changed between frame t and frame t−k, with k = ⌈1/speed⌉. This is the change per pixel of camera motion.
   - Consecutive-frame change (k = 1) under-reports slow drift: 0.0023 at 0.1 px/frame against 0.248 per pixel.
3. **Crawl C_px.** S_px restricted to interleaved (dithered) pixels: the dither pattern sliding over the surfaces it shades (the "shower-door" effect).
4. **Flicker F.** Share of valid pixels with index A→B→A over t−2, t−1, t. Large-area flashing is separately capped by WCAG 2.3.1 (≤ 3 flashes in any 1 s) [13].
5. **Static S0.** Plain frame difference.

**Thresholds** (all **Assumed, pending calibration** against a first Unity capture set; static is a hard rule):

| Motion | S_px | C_px | F |
|---|---|---|---|
| S0 static | 0 | 0 | 0 (exact; the temporal-noise negative control scores 0.334) |
| Y1–Y2 yaw | ≤ 0.01 | ≤ 0.01 | ≤ 0.001 |
| T1–T2 translation | ≤ 0.12 | ≤ 0.15 | ≤ 0.005 |
| T3 translation | ≤ 0.12 | ≤ 0.15 | ≤ 0.08 |

Derivation:
- **Yaw.** 4× the best measured result (direction-locked dither plus yaw snapping: 0.0025–0.0029). It is 50× below screen-locked dither (0.51).
- **Translation.**
  - 1.25× the surface-anchored upper bound (0.095).
  - Screen-space or reference-depth-locked dither scores 0.245–0.30 at every speed, because parallax moves near surfaces several pixels while the screen pattern stays put [9].
  - Locking the pattern to the reference-depth shift is *worse* (0.296) than leaving it screen-fixed (0.246), because the sky and far ridges then crawl.
- **Flicker.** Snapping removes A→B→A reversals at slow speeds: 0.111 → 0.000 at 0.25 px/frame.

Implementation guidance this implies (for `Rendering.Pixel`; not a design decision):
- Snap the camera position to the texel grid at a reference depth, and snap yaw/pitch to whole-pixel angles [8][10]. Pass the sub-pixel remainder to the upscale pass so the output moves smoothly [10].
- Anchor dither to view direction: the sphere-mapped pattern Pope used for stability under rotation [8].
- Anchor dither for near geometry to surfaces (object or texture space), or accept failing T-thresholds. This is Q-NEW-b.
- A screen-fixed skybox jumps when the camera snaps [10], so the sky must use the same direction-locked mapping.
- Unity's 2D Pixel Perfect Camera documentation notes that point filtering keeps pixels sharp but can shimmer [10]. It is not usable for this 3D perspective camera; it is cited only for the snapping concept.

## 6. Capture contract `style_capture@v1`

This is the contract between the renderer implementer (writes captures) and the metric-tool implementer (reads them).

**Directory layout.** `captures/<capture_set_id>/<sequence_id>/` holds `sequence.json` plus, per frame, `f<index:06d>.json` (sidecar) and the image files it names. A complete example sidecar is in `research/R-002/style_capture_example.json`.

**Files written per frame:**

| Key | Format | Resolution | Content | Used by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `color_internal` | PNG, 8-bit sRGB RGB, no alpha | internal (e.g. 480×270) | final palette-quantised image before upscale, without UI | M-003a, b, c, d, e; M-004 |
| `color_output` | PNG, 8-bit sRGB RGB | output (e.g. 1920×1080) | presented frame after upscale and sub-pixel compensation, UI layer off | M-003a, d-iv; panel |
| `palette_index` | PNG, 8-bit grey | internal | index into the profile's `colours[]`; 255 = not quantised | M-003a, b; M-004 |
| `depth` | PNG16 log (required) or EXR float32 (optional) | internal | eye-space linear depth z (m) along camera forward. PNG16: z = near·(far/near)^(v/65535), v = 65535 means sky. Relative precision 1.7×10⁻⁴ (0.17 m at 1 km) with near = 0.3, far = 20 km | M-003c, e; M-004 |
| `mask_animated` | PNG, 8-bit | internal | 255 = animated element excluded from M-004 | M-004 |
| `mask_ui` | PNG, 8-bit, optional | output | 255 = UI pixel (only if UI cannot be disabled) | M-003 |
| `motion_vectors` | EXR, optional | internal | renderer motion vectors; when present, used to cross-check the depth-and-pose reprojection | M-004 |

**Sidecar fields** (all required unless marked optional):
- `schema` (= `style_capture@v1`), `capture_set_id`, `sequence_id`.
- `frame{index, sequence_length, sim_time_s, dt_s, sim_paused}`.
- `build{git_commit, unity_version, render_pipeline, feature_flags}`.
- `style{palette_set, palette_profile_id, palette_sha256, district_id, time_of_day}`.
- `resolution{internal_wh, output_wh, integer_scale, output_block_offset_px, subpixel_offset_internal_px}`.
- `camera{rig, projection, hfov_deg, vfov_deg, near_m, far_m, origin_offset_m, position_local_m, rotation_xyzw, yaw_pitch_roll_deg, snap{enabled, reference_depth_m, position_grid_m, yaw_step_deg, pitch_step_deg, snapped_position_local_m, snapped_yaw_pitch_roll_deg, dither_anchor ∈ {screen, direction, surface}}}`.
  - The pose is the **rendered (snapped) pose**, in floating-origin local coordinates plus `origin_offset_m` (F-042).
- `conventions{handedness, up, forward, pixel_origin}`.
- `motion{type ∈ {static, translate, yaw, pitch, dolly, scripted}, axis, speed_internal_px_per_frame, speed_si, scripted}`.
- `files{...}` (keys above; null if absent) and `encodings{...}`.

**`sequence.json`** fields:
- `schema`, `capture_set_id`, `sequence_id`, `purpose` (M-003 | M-004 | haze_cal), `frame_count`, `motion`.
- The list of frame sidecars.
- The renderer settings hash.

**Rules:**
1. All files of a frame come from the same rendered frame.
2. No TAA, motion blur or bloom on `color_internal`.
3. The UI is off, or masked.
4. Captures are lossless PNG/EXR only.
5. The capture script, not player input, drives the camera.
6. Every capture set records the commit that produced it (AGENT_PROTOCOL §4).

## 7. Limits and risks

- **Lossy references.** The references are lossy, resampled reproductions (JPEG-like ringing, non-integer scaling). Grid detection fails on 6 of 11 panels, and the palettes include re-encoding intermediates. The ΔE00-3 merge and the 0.1% floor limit this. Confidence is medium on per-panel colour counts and high on hue families and ramps.
- **Synthetic stand-in.** The M-003c/M-004 validation uses a numpy ray-caster (boxes, a haze ramp and a 4×4 Bayer matrix), not URP. Absolute thresholds must be recalibrated on the first Unity capture set, hence Assumed.
- **Ramp L\* spacing.** Steps in L\* are uneven (largest near the light end). This made apparent contrast non-monotone at 1–4 km in c2. See M-NEW-b.
