## 1. C1: a real slot-geometry defect, fix built but OFF (it costs P4 and P1)
- Cause (dev seeds): Bait slot -51 deg and Anchor slot -34 deg on the same side of the approach axis, the lone Flanker at +70 deg committing from 336 m, outside its 300 m D_eng. The first Charge then saw {Anchor, Bait} engaged with a 17-23 deg spread: unflanked commit (15 of 31 multi-engaged commits in LLH/LLMH/LLMHH).
- Fix (`PackConfig.BaitFirst`): Bait 100 deg off the axis, every striker 0..70 deg on the other side (>= 100 deg from the Bait), strikers hold outside weapon range and commit from the hold. Hold-out: C1 0.818 -> 0.898, every composition's CI lower bound >= 0.57.
- Cost on the same hold-out seeds: P4 +0.001 (PASS) -> -0.077 (FAIL, LH guard), P1 0.705 (PASS) -> 0.654 (FAIL; LL hunt ticks with fit roles fall when the Strider Flanker holds outside its D_eng, so the raid-Raider ticks of mixed weigh more). The default stays OFF; the default output is bit-identical to 68deb4f.

## 2. C2 (LL) and P1 (LL:L) are one evaluator-reading artefact, not pack defects
- `Tools/m007_bay.py` puts infrastructure entities into its target table (`tpos` from `infra` rows), so a decision row whose target is an infrastructure id counts as (a) a fire target in FF (C2) and (b) an engagement in P1 / P6.
- C2: the 2-Strider pack on hunt_mv@v2 leaves the 3-4 mechs for a raid (R-103 3.3 utility: strength 1.0 vs 7.5-10) for most of the run; the Raider's target is the raid target, the escort's a mech, so ff = 0.5 on those ticks. During hunt ticks the LL Bait and Flanker share their target 99.4 % of the time (dev, `diag_c2`).
- P1: in `mixed` an LL pack raids SFO; R-103 4.2 raid slots require one Raider and both Striders have Raider fit 0.3 < 0.6; the evaluator counts the Raider at its target as engaged. No pack behaviour can make an LL raid pass this reading (idling instead fails I2).
- With infrastructure ids removed from decision targets (`Tools/m007_bay_diag_noinfra.py`, diagnostic only), hold-out seeds: **C2 PASS** FE 0.889 (CI 0.864..0.913), LL 0.970..0.999; **P1 PASS** min fit 0.895 (evidence/T-113b/holdout/diag_noinfra_mv2.json, diag_noinfra_v1.json; same result on the it2 logs, holdout_it2/).
- **Decision needed (lead):** R-024 3.1-3.2 define FF and Engaged over the kaiju's combat targets (mechs). Recommended: an M-007@v2 tool correction (not a threshold or clause change) that ignores infrastructure targets in FF, Engaged, P1 role time and P6, logged as a decision with this evidence. Alternative rejected: making a raiding escort report the raid target as its own target would let raid ticks inflate FF (the control does not raid with a target), which games C2 the other way.

## 3. P4, P5, P6: real hunt-dynamics problems, not solved in 3 iterations
Mech model (R-103 6.3, harness): fires only at the nearest kaiju within 300 m, 3 s lock after the turret (0.6 rad/s) is on target, lock reset on retarget; 6 hp/s. Kaiju turn 0.5 rad/s, accelerate 2 m/s^2. Engagement radius D_eng = 5 H: Strider 300 m (= the weapon range), Breacher 500, Bastion 800.
- **P5 (KX vs noroles)**: the kill exchange is set by the seconds the mech spends locked and firing. The Bait only pays if the strike lands while it still holds the lock; in practice the Bait entered range 20-30 s before the gate opened (it stages at 1.05 x range = 315 m next to a patrolling mech whose board position is extrapolated) and retreated (hp < 0.6, R-103 4.2) before the strikers arrived from 340-760 m (runs of 15-40 s from rest). 25 % of all kaiju damage, in every arm, came from mechs that were nobody's target while members walked past them.
- **P6 (participation)**: the Bastion Anchor is inside its 800 m D_eng while holding its standoff slot, which starts the engagement clock; Striders are "engaged" only inside 300 m, i.e. inside weapon range, so a Strider cannot be both engaged and safe while it waits. Long waits (60-120 s engagements) push Strider shares under 50 % (81 of 100 bad pairs are Striders, dev it2).
- **P4 (2 x nobait)**: Bait-targeted opportunities succeed 0.59-0.61 (passes the 0.40 part), but the role-agnostic rate includes every 3 s aim episode on strikers and on members passing non-focus mechs, which rarely get a strike, so the role-agnostic rate stays ~1.8 x nobait.
- Tried (dev seeds 1001-1010): bait-first geometry (kept), strict Anchor hold tolerance, a pincer that alternates the nearest threat between the Bait and the nearest striker every 4 s (+/- 30 m), steering around non-focus mechs, holding every striker outside its own D_eng. The last raised P6 a little but collapsed C1 and P4 (nobody engaged at the commit instant) and was rejected; avoidance helped the noroles control more than the candidate (KX 0.470 -> 0.534 vs 0.449 -> 0.473), the pincer could not alternate in practice (a stopped Bait needs > 4 s to re-pass a 12-15 m/s striker at 2 m/s^2). Numbers: tasks/T-113b.progress.md.
- **Research questions**: (1) a timed hunt: plan the ring-up so that the Bait's lock and the strikers' arrival coincide (strikers launch at range r_s so t_run(r_s) ~ lock + traverse time), the Anchor reaching its slot last; (2) whether P6's engagement interval should start at the first member inside D_eng (an Anchor at standoff starts it for everyone) or at the first damage exchanged; (3) a Bait that dips in and out of range at the band edge (draws aim without completing the lock) needs turn-rate-feasible paths (0.5 rad/s); (4) threat-aware transit (avoid non-focus mechs) is a correct behaviour but raises both arms; P5 then needs a role-specific gain.

## 4. I3: scenario artefact in mixed plus a throughput-bound raid
- Mixed: from the Pacifica spawn the nearest target (SFO, airport, weight 3, 2000 hp) is also the best by R-103 3.3 utility and is the only one destroyed within 3600 s, in every pair (dev: ival 3.0 = 3.0 in 54/54 pairs; hold-out I3 = 1.001 for base, it1 and it2 alike). No goal-selection policy can beat nearest-first there, so mixed only dilutes the ratio.
- Raid: candidate and nearestinfra destroy ~2 targets each in 6000 s; only Raider slots damage (1-2 of n members), so the run is throughput bound and target order moves the total by ~2 % (dev 363.7 vs 356.0 ival).
- Pooled 1.20 would need +26 % raid value at equal throughput (dev). Recommended: (a) a mixed scenario variant whose nearest target is not the best (for example a low-value crane cluster nearer than a power node), justified by this evidence and logged as a decision, or a horizon long enough for a second target; (b) a raid-throughput research item: whether escorts should demolish when no defender is known (raises both arms; the ordering advantage then has room to show).
