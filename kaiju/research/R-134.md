## 1. Sources of waves relevant to the Bay
- **Distant (far-field) subduction tsunamis**: Alaska 1964 (Mw 9.2). Travel time to San Francisco about 5 h [S1 (search snippet: ~5 h)]. Bay effect: damage mostly to small craft and marinas; Bay wave height reports conflict (about 1.1 m = 3.7 ft peak [S2] vs "no larger than the normal tidal range" [S3]; both snippets, not re-read) so treat 1964 as a low-amplitude, high-current marina event. Crescent City, outside the Bay, took the damage (about 11 deaths) [S2, S3].
- **Tohoku 2011**: heavy harbour damage at Santa Cruz and Crescent City (about USD 100 million along the coast, snippet [S4], not re-read). A gameplay and validation anchor for harbours, not for the open Bay.
- **Regulatory maps**: California Geological Survey / Cal OES tsunami inundation maps and the 2021 Tsunami Hazard Area maps; SAFRR scenario (Mw 9.1 Alaska-Aleutian) fact sheet USGS FS 2013-3081 [S5] for Bay inundation (West Oakland, Lake Merritt area, North Beach/Fisherman's Wharf per SF OEM summary [S6, snippet]). Data are state/federal; licence to confirm (Assumed open with attribution).
- **Near-field**: the San Andreas and Hayward faults are strike-slip, so seafloor uplift at Mw 7.2 is small (R-022). A tsunami would need (a) a submarine landslide triggered by shaking (Assumed plausible, no source retrieved) or (b) the offshore Cascadia event. The brief's idea "Mw 7+ offshore San Andreas" mostly yields a **landslide-generated or seiche-like** local wave, not a basin-scale tsunami. Decision: model a *scripted* near-field source (landslide/rupture-displacement hump with sourced amplitude from a literature scenario) as the gameplay source, honest labelling "scenario, not forecast".
- **Seiche**: Bay natural periods from basin length and depth; Golden Gate entrance and shallow shoals act as filters (Gulf of Alaska 1964 ringing in the Bay was dominated by Golden Gate tidal exchange; **Assumed** details). Estimate: Merian formula T = 2L/sqrt(g h). For the South Bay L about 60 km, mean depth 5 m: T = 2*60000/7.0 = 17,100 s = 4.8 h (my arithmetic, **Assumed** geometry); central Bay (L about 25 km, h about 12 m) T about 1.3 h (my arithmetic, Assumed geometry). These are validation targets for the solver's own free decay, to be refined against NOAA tide-gauge spectra (Presidio, Alameda, Redwood City) [S7, not read].

## 2. Solver design (engine-free, deterministic, GPU-friendly later)
Equations: depth-averaged nonlinear shallow-water equations (SWE) on a regular grid, h and hu, hv, bed b(x,y); well-balanced hydrostatic reconstruction (Audusse et al. 2004) with HLL fluxes, first-order space then MUSCL second-order; wetting/drying via a minimum depth threshold h_min = 0.01 m with velocity regularisation; Manning friction n = 0.025 in water, 0.03 to 0.1 on land by land cover (**Assumed**, to be refined with the OSM land-cover class; Bricker et al. 2015 review as a source [S8, not re-read]). Linear SWE variant (eta, u) for far-field open-boundary propagation and an LOD far-field clone.
Determinism: fixed iteration order, fixed time step sequence derived from CFL on integer bit patterns; no atomics; same results on every run (the golden test).
Forcing: initial hump (eta0) from a sourced analytic/EDGE source; open boundary at the grid edge (Sommerfeld radiation, or sponge layer 10 cells, Assumed); far-field forcing as a time series at the Golden Gate/offshore boundary from a table (NOAA MOST results table; tide-gauge-like).
Resolution and time-step budget (CFL dt = C dx / (|u| + sqrt(g h)), C = 0.5; my arithmetic):
| Region | depth h | dx | wave speed c | dt max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shallow bay | 5 m | 50 m | 7.0 m/s | 3.6 s |
| Shallow bay, fine | 5 m | 25 m | 7.0 m/s | 1.8 s |
| Golden Gate throat | 100 m | 100 m | 31 m/s | 1.6 s |
| Inundation (land) | 1 m | 10 m | 3.1 m/s | 1.6 s |
Whole Bay 70 x 30 km at 50 m = 840,000 cells; at 1.6 s steps, about 5,600 steps per 2.5 h. Cost per step about 840 k cells x 100 flops = 84 Mflop; 5,600 steps = 0.47 Tflop: a few seconds to tens of seconds on a CPU core in C# offline, so a **precomputed (baked) scenario** field is feasible at 50 m for the whole Bay, and a *live* 10 m local grid is feasible only near the player (e.g., 4 km x 4 km = 160 k cells at 10 m, dt 1.6 s -> 0.1 ms per cell step for 16 ms budget: **too slow on CPU in one frame**; run at 4 steps/s on a worker thread; GPU port later). Decision: **bake-then-play** first: precompute eta, u, v at 25 s snapshots on a 50 m grid (and 10 m nest at the districts), interpolate in game; live solver later (M3+).
LOD for M-008: far-field grid (50 m) always baked; near-field 10 m nest only inside the physics ring; hydrodynamic damage evaluated only for buildings within the inundation polygon, in tile batches; budget gate: <= 0.5 ms main-thread per frame, <= 2 ms worker (**Assumed**).

## 3. Coupling

## 4. Validation targets (acceptance metrics; thresholds Assumed unless noted)
1. Analytical: lake-at-rest preservation (max |u| < 1e-10 m/s), 1D dam break (Stoker), Thacker oscillating paraboloid wetting/drying (L2 error <= 2 % after 5 periods), solitary wave runup on a plane beach (Synolakis 1987 runup law) [S11, not re-read].
2. Seiche decay: free-decay periods of the South and Central Bay within 20 % of Merian estimates and tide-gauge spectra.
3. 1964 Alaska at the Golden Gate: with a table forcing from NOAA MOST/historical (about 1 m amplitude at the Presidio; verify), predicted Bay maximum eta within a factor of 1.5 of the observed range 0.3 to 1.1 m; current speeds in marinas (Marin) > 1 m/s qualitative (damage observed).
4. 2011 Tohoku at Crescent City: NOAA MOST/NCTR results (peak about 2.5 m and maximum current > 4 knots reported; **not verified**; fetch via NCTR) as a *port-scale* check at one harbour; optional.
5. CGS/Cal OES inundation polygon: for the SAFRR Mw 9.1 Alaska scenario, area-overlap IoU >= 0.6 of the modelled inundation versus the published polygon on SF waterfront and Oakland (Assumed threshold; maps have bounded accuracy).
6. Mass conservation to 1e-9 relative, determinism golden, nesting boundary error.
Proposed metrics (numbers allocated later): "SWE verification suite", "Bay seiche period", "1964 and SAFRR agreement", "tsunami budget".
