## 1. Damage-state scale (answer to Q-103 prior)
Five discrete states, Hazus-style [S1] (Hazus: None, Slight, Moderate, Extensive, Complete):

| `State` (byte) | Name | Meaning in play | Hazus-style definition [S1, unverified wording] |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | None | intact | no damage |
| 1 | Slight | cracks, glass, cladding | minor cracking, no structural loss |
| 2 | Moderate | partial wall/parapet loss | cracking of frame/walls, some residual drift |
| 3 | Extensive | heavy damage, part of floors down | major structural damage, partial collapse risk |
| 4 | Complete | collapsed or in imminent danger | brittle failure / loss of stability; a fraction of floor area has collapsed [S5] |

State is **monotone non-decreasing** within a world save (no repair in M1; Assumed). Presentation (T-026) uses 5 pre-baked meshes per class, matching the byte.

## 2. Capacity model from `geo_export@v1` attributes
### 2.1 Derived quantities (per building)
- Levels `n`: `building:levels` if present, else `round(h / 4.22 m)` (4.22 m/level is the measured median on 202 SF buildings, IQR 3.56-4.71) [R-004 §1.4]; min 1.
- Height `H` (m, `height_m`), footprint area `A` (m², from the export footprint), floor area `A_f = A n`.
- Seismic/dead mass `M0 = ρ_f A_f`, `ρ_f` = **600 kg/m²** of floor. Basis: a 150 mm (6 in) RC slab at 2,400 kg/m³ is 366 kg/m² (reasoned; 75 psf slab figure appears in a student design report, weak); frame, walls, cladding and partitions bring it to 600 *Assumed*. Light low-rise use group (houses, `use` = residential, n ≤ 3): 250 kg/m² *Assumed* (wood frame is far lighter).
- Rise class: **A** low (n ≤ 3), **B** mid (4-7), **C** high (≥ 8). Hazus uses the same low/mid/high rise split for its model building types [S1, unverified bounds]. Tallest class C buildings (Salesforce 326 m, R-004 §1.4) use the same law.
- Effective modal mass `m_e = 0.75 M0` (Hazus uses a modal mass coefficient α₂ in the capacity curve [S1][S2]; 0.75 *Assumed*).
- Elastic period `T = 0.0488 H^0.75` (s, H in m): the ASCE 7 approximate-period form for "all other systems", Ct = 0.02, x = 0.75, H in ft, equals 0.0488 in SI [S3, unverified; recalled].

### 2.2 Capacity curve and energy per damage state
Hazus capacity curve: linear to a yield point (Dy, Ay), fully plastic between yield and ultimate [S1][S2][S8]. Used here as elastic-perfectly-plastic:
- `Ay = Cy g`, `Cy` (yield strength coefficient, g): A **0.25**, B **0.15**, C **0.10** *Assumed* (Hazus tabulates design strength per type and code level in Table 5.x; values to be loaded from the CSV of Q-NEW-a [S1]).
- `Dy = Ay T² / (4π²)`.
- Damage-state thresholds as roof drift ratio `θ_k`: Slight 0.2 %, Moderate 0.5 %, Extensive 1.5 %, Complete 4 % *Assumed* (Hazus gives medians as spectral displacements per type [S1]). Spectral displacement `Sd_k = 0.75 θ_k H` (roof-to-spectral factor 0.75 *Assumed*).
- Energy to reach state k (work under the pushover curve, J):
  `E_k = m_e Ay [ ½ Dy + max(Sd_k - Dy, 0) ] ` for `Sd_k ≥ Dy`, else `½ m_e Ay Sd_k²/Dy`.
- Capacity scatter: `E_k,i = E_k · exp(β_E z_i)`, `β_E = 0.5` *Assumed* (Hazus uses β ≈ 0.6-0.9 on Sd per type [S1, unverified]); `z_i` is a standard normal from a hash of `BuildingId` (§3), so it is reproducible and not saved.

Worked values (computed with the above, `/opt/miniconda3/envs/kaiju/bin/python`): 

| Building | A, n, H | M0 | T | E1..E4 (Slight..Complete) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| house (A) | 100 m², 2, 7 m | 0.12 kt (ρ_f 600; 0.05 kt at 250) | 0.21 s | 2, 6, 17, 46 kJ (at 600 kg/m²) |
| mid-rise (B) | 600 m², 6, 25 m | 2.2 kt | 0.55 s | 0.1, 0.2, 0.7, 1.8 MJ |
| tower (C) | 1,500 m², 40, 160 m | 36 kt | 2.2 s | 4.8, 14, 46, 126 MJ |
(kt = 1,000 t.) Caveat: the model is a **global lateral** pushover. Local punching or shear failure at the contact point (a foot through a wall) needs far less energy; the global figure is an upper bound on what a hit must deliver for a global state. Local failure is out of scope (Q-NEW-c).

### 2.3 Energy delivered by an impactor (links to M-006@v1)
- Impactor mass `m`, normal approach speed `v_n` (building effective mass `M_b = m_e`, or the full `M0` for a tight base): perfectly plastic contact (restitution e), energy lost into the target:
  `E_del = ½ μ v_n² (1 - e²)`, `μ = m M_b / (m + M_b)`.
  This is the quantity M-006.3 verifies for the engine: for e = 0 head-on, KE_after/KE_before = m₁/(m₁+m₂) (so the lost fraction is m₂/(m₁+m₂)) and linear momentum conserved to ≤ 0.5 %, angular ≤ 2 % [R-005 §8, M-006.3]. Contact events in `physics_log@v1` carry `impact {bodies, J, ΔKE}` [R-005 §8.9]; **`EnergyJ` of `BuildingDamaged` = that `ΔKE`** (no second energy computation; one source of truth).
- Sanity (reference mech 1e7 kg [R-005 §1]) on the mid-rise above (`M_b` = 1.62e6 kg): `v = 5 m/s` gives `E_del` ≈ 22 MJ ≫ E4 (1.8 MJ): complete. `v = 2 m/s` gives 3.6 MJ > E4: complete. On the tower: `v = 5` gives ≈ 98 MJ (state 3, E3 = 46 < 98 < E4 = 126). Walking footfall: `1e7 kg` at touchdown 1 m/s with 20 % engaged mass, impulse ≈ 2e6 N·s [R-005 §6]: `½·2e6·1² = 1 MJ` order, so a footfall on a house (E4 = 46 kJ) is Complete; on the tower, Slight.
- Fall energy scale: a standing mech toppling releases 3.6 GJ, CoM at 24.9 m/s, head 45.6 m/s [R-005 §7]; every sub-class here is Complete under that, by 1-2 orders of magnitude (Assumed consequence; gameplay may cap `EnergyJ` per contact).
- **Cumulative energy**: `E_cum += E_del` per contact; state = max k with `E_cum ≥ E_k,i`. Hysteretic damage accumulation is *Assumed*; Hazus treats a single demand [S1]. This makes repeated small hits matter (P2) and keeps the model deterministic.
- **Leverage check (P2)**: a lateral impulse `J` at height `z` gives overturning moment impulse `J z`; for a rigid block, compare to `M0 g b/2` per second of loading (b = footprint dimension along the push). Kept as an optional second trigger for Complete (rocking); not used in M-051/052. Q-NEW-c.

### 2.4 Ground-shaking fragility (earthquake route, F-020)
Hazus form [S1][S4]: `P(DS ≥ k | IM) = Φ( ln(IM / θ_k) / β )`, IM = PGA (g) (the equivalent-PGA form; Hazus derives the PGA medians from Sd medians with an assumed M≈7 western-US Site Class D spectrum [S4]). Medians per rise class (*Assumed*; load from Hazus Tables 5.16a-d via ShakeCast lookup [S4], pre/low/moderate/high code):

| Class | k=1 | k=2 | k=3 | k=4 | β |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A low | 0.25 g | 0.45 g | 0.85 g | 1.40 g | 0.7 |
| B mid | 0.15 g | 0.30 g | 0.55 g | 0.90 g | 0.7 |
| C high | 0.12 g | 0.25 g | 0.45 g | 0.75 g | 0.7 |

`code_era` (needs Q-NEW-b data): pre-1980 multiplies medians by 0.6, post-2000 by 1.2 *Assumed*. R-022 supplies the PGA at each building. Draw: `u_i` uniform from the ID hash of (BuildingId, event index); state = max k with `u_i < P_k` and monotone. Ground-shaking damage also adds `E_cum = max(E_cum, E_state)` so both routes share one state. The two routes use different tables (energy vs PGA) on purpose; they are not cross-calibrated (Q-NEW-d).

### 2.5 Determinism
`z_i`, `u_i` come from `IdHash.Mix` over the `BuildingId` and an integer event index (Core: deterministic hash, never string/object hashing) [Core MODULE.md]. No `System.Random`, no wall clock.

## 3. Debris, mass and energy on collapse (gameplay scale)
- **Mass conservation.** Per building: `M0 = M_standing + M_debris`, with `M_debris(k)` = `f_d(k) M0`; `f_d` = [0, 0.01, 0.05, 0.25, 0.85] for k = 0..4 *Assumed*. Hazus estimates debris empirically per damage state and building type [S6]; for Complete, the collapsed share is by floor area [S5] (Assumed 0.15 of floor area collapses completely; the rest of the "debris" at k=4 is facade, contents and partial-floor failure). Debris is monotone and **incremental**: moving from state j to k adds `(f_d(k) - f_d(j)) M0`. The removed standing mass is exactly that added to debris pieces. Contents are ignored (Assumed).
- **Geometry.** Bulk rubble density `ρ_r` = 1,200 kg/m³ *Assumed* (loose concrete/masonry rubble; a bulking factor of ≈ 2 on solid concrete at 2,400). Volume `V_d = M_debris/ρ_r`. Spread: the heap occupies the footprint plus a ring of width up to `0.5 H` (Assumed); pile height `h_p = V_d / A_spread`.
- **Energy account** (Complete, or any increment), SI joules:
  `ΔPE = M_d g (H_c - h_c)` where `H_c`, `h_c` are the debris-mass centroid heights before and after (for a uniform building `H_c = H/2`; heap `h_c = h_p/2`);
  `ΔPE + W_ext = E_frac + E_ground + KE_ej + E_res`, with
  - `W_ext` = work by the impactor (the `E_del` already counted into state); included once only,
  - `E_frac = e_c M_d` fracture and comminution, `e_c` = 0.5 MJ/t... i.e. **5e2 J/kg** *Assumed* (order of crushing concrete; no source found),
  - `E_ground` inelastic ground impact of falling pieces (restitution 0 after landing),
  - `KE_ej` kinetic energy of ejected pieces at landing, capped at 10 % of ΔPE *Assumed*,
  - `E_res` unaccounted, must be ≥ 0 and small.
  Example (mid-rise, Complete, `M_d = 0.85 M0 = 1.84 kt`, `H/2 = 12.5 m`, `h_p ≈ 4 m`): `ΔPE = 1.84e6 · 9.81 · (12.5 - 2) ≈ 1.9e8 J = 190 MJ`; `E_frac = 0.92 MJ`. So collapse releases roughly 100× the structural capacity energy E4 (1.8 MJ): consistent with a building that falls once its global capacity is exceeded (Assumed reading).
- **Gameplay-scale rules.** Debris is spawned as bodies in `n_d` ≤ 64 pieces per building (budget M-008 is Track-owned); piece masses sum to `M_d` exactly (last piece takes the remainder). `n_d` and per-piece shape are presentation; the account uses sums only.

## 4. Draft metrics
Thresholds marked *A* are Assumed pending first runs.

### M-050 Persistent destruction save round trip (F-043)
- **Definition.** The destruction section of the world save restores every building's damage state and cumulative energy bit-exactly, directly and across one section migration.
- **Procedure.** (1) Load M0 sample buildings (2,046 + parts, R-004 §1.4) and 20,000 synthetic `BuildingId`s of all three sources; apply seeded random damage (states, `e_cum_j`, `debris_kg`, `last_step`). (2) `Capture`, write, read, `Restore` into a fresh `DamageStore`, `Capture` again: compare canonical bytes of the `destruction@v1` section (Core `SaveCodec` canonical output) [Core MODULE.md]. (3) Run under cultures `de-DE` and `tr-TR`. (4) Migration: a committed golden `destruction_v1.json` (never rewritten except `KAIJU_WRITE_GOLDEN=1`) is migrated v1→v2 by a test migration that adds one defaulted field; compare per-ID `state` and `e_cum_j` bits before and after, and re-save then reload the v2 file. (5) Failure injection: a stream failure mid-write leaves the previous save loadable and unchanged [Core MODULE.md: `.tmp` + `File.Replace`]. (6) A building not in the loaded world (removed by `GeoBuildingRemoved`) keeps its record.
- **Threshold.** Section bytes identical after save/load/save: **0 differing bytes**; per-ID `state` equal and `e_cum_j` equal as IEEE-754 bit patterns: **0 mismatches** in direct and migrated runs; record count equal; unrelated sections byte-identical; unknown/newer version refused untouched (`SectionTooNew`).
- **Source.** F-043; Core save contract (canonical bytes, Float64 as bits, validate-then-apply) [Core MODULE.md].

### M-051 Damage state versus fragility
- **Definition.** The state the model assigns follows the sourced fragility law: for each class and intensity, the fraction of buildings at state ≥ k equals `Φ(ln(IM/θ_k)/β)`.
- **Procedure.** Per class (A, B, C), `N = 10,000` synthetic buildings with distinct IDs; IM grid PGA = {0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.4, 0.8, 1.6} g; independent fresh run per grid point (no accumulation); empirical `F_k = (#state ≥ k)/N`. Energy route: the same with `E_del` on a log grid from 0.1 E_1 to 10 E_4 in 13 points, predicted `Φ(ln(E/E_k)/β_E)`. Also: monotone (state never decreases), table medians equal the loaded CSV (Q-NEW-a), determinism (two runs identical).
- **Threshold.** `max |F_k - Φ| ≤ 0.015` (3σ binomial at N = 10,000, worst case p = 0.5: 3·√(0.25/10,000)); at the median `0.5 ± 0.015`; zero decreases; two runs bit-equal; the loaded medians equal the Hazus table values **within 1 %** once Q-NEW-a lands (until then the test checks against the *Assumed* table and is marked provisional).
- **Source.** Hazus lognormal fragility form [S1][S4]; binomial tolerance (derived).

### M-052 Collapse mass and energy account
- **Definition.** On any transition to state ≥ 3, debris mass and energy balance with stated residuals.
- **Procedure.** For 5 archetypes (house, mid-rise, tower, wide low warehouse, slender tower) and every transition (j→k), run the debris step: `M_standing + Σ piece masses = M0`; `ΔPE + W_ext - (E_frac + E_ground + KE_ej)`; piece count ≤ 64; heap height from `ρ_r`; repeat as a two-step transition (1→3→4) and compare with direct 1→4.
- **Threshold.** `|M0 - (M_standing + M_debris)| / M0 ≤ 1e-6`; sum of piece masses equals `M_debris` to 1e-6 relative; `E_res / ΔPE ∈ [0, 0.05]` *A*; `KE_ej ≤ 0.10 ΔPE` *A*; no energy creation (`E_frac + E_ground + KE_ej ≤ ΔPE + W_ext`); two-step and direct totals equal within 1e-6; debris monotone.
- **Source.** Conservation of mass and energy (Newtonian; M-006.2/.3 style checks) [R-005 §8]; Hazus debris is state-based [S6]; remaining coefficients *Assumed*.

## 5. Contract fields (Core rules: unmanaged structs, IDs only, no strings/arrays/references [Core MODULE.md])
### `BuildingDamaged` (M1 plan fixes the first three fields) [plans/M1.md Track 2]
`struct BuildingDamaged : IEvent { BuildingId BuildingId; byte State; float EnergyJ; }`
Proposed extensions (Q-NEW-e; one added field costs nothing on the bus): `byte PrevState; byte Cause` (0 impact, 1 shaking, 2 fault rupture, 3 debris); `EntityId Source` (impactor, `default` = none); `float DebrisKg` (increment). All unmanaged. Published once per state **change** (and none per sub-threshold energy increment), same step, before the outermost `Publish` returns. `EnergyJ` = `ΔKE` of the contact record (§2.3). Float is enough for the event; the save keeps float64.

### `destruction@v1` section (one `ISaveParticipant`, section name `destruction`, version 1)
Records keyed by canonical building ID string `bld:<osm-way|osm-relation|landmark>:<u64>` (ordinal order, one per line). Sparse: only buildings with `state > 0` or `e_cum_j > 0`.
| Field | Core type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `state` | Int64 | 0-4 (byte widened; range-checked on load) |
| `e_cum_j` | Float64 (IEEE bits) | cumulative delivered energy, J |
| `debris_kg` | Float64 | debris mass emitted so far, kg (zero until state ≥ 1 with f_d > 0) |
| `last_step` | Int64 | clock step of the last change |
| `cause` | Int64 | last cause (as in the event) |
Section header: `model_rev` (Int64, version of the capacity/fragility tables the state was computed with). Not saved: capacity scatter `z_i` (recomputed from the ID), meshes, debris bodies (recomputed from `debris_kg` and state at load; Assumed). Migration `v1→v2` adds fields only, defaults documented; `IDamageModel` must tolerate unknown IDs (buildings absent from the loaded tile) by retaining the record.
