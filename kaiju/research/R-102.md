## 1. Headline

1. **Three original quadruped-derived plans, all four-footed in locomotion.** Working codenames (not final names, none from any franchise): **STRIDER** (light, 60 m, lean digitigrade quadruped, long raised neck, whip tail; role **bait / flank**), **BREACHER** (mid, 100 m reared, plantigrade quadruped that rears onto hind limbs and a thick tail tripod to slam and grapple; role **flank / breaker**), **BASTION** (heavy, 160 m, domed pillar-legged quadruped with a low head and a dorsal crest; role **anchor**).
2. **Masses from structure (§3): 4,190 t / 35,000 t / 164,000 t** (0.42, 3.5, 16 times the 10,000 t mech). Method: sum of ellipsoid and frustum primitives × effective density 900 / 980 / 1,050 kg/m³; the identical recipe returns 5.2 m³ for an African elephant (live mass 4–6 t, [4][5]).
3. **A real-material body fails at all three sizes.** Extrapolating real bone (Campione and Evans scaling [6]) with elephant posture gives peak bone stress 730–1,080 MPa against a failure stress of ≈ 200 MPa [2]: safety factor 0.19–0.29 (§4). Muscle needs 2–4.6 MPa per limb section against ≈ 0.3 MPa isometric [3]. For reference the largest real land animal studied is an 80 t sauropod [1]; the bone-limited ceiling is 10⁵–10⁶ kg [7]. The fiction must therefore assume (*Assumed*): **kaiju skeletal composite 600 MPa compressive yield (3× cortical bone, steel-grade) and kaiju muscle 6.0 MPa isometric (20× vertebrate muscle, still below hydraulic actuators)**. With those two numbers, shared by all species, every species passes SF ≥ 2 at its charge speed (§4.3).
4. **Bipeds are excluded** for all three sizes because a single supporting limb carries 3× the load of a quadruped limb: it would need 1.3–1.9 GPa and 6.6–15 MPa muscle (§4.4). That cap (600 MPa, 6 MPa) is what decides the body plan, not taste. The 10,000 t mech is a biped only because its frame is steel and hydraulic by D-008, not tissue.
5. **Froude-band gait (§5):** cruise Fr 0.30 / 0.20 / 0.10 (stride 5.1 / 6.8 / 11.2 s), charge Fr 0.80 / 0.35 / 0.15 (**15.3 / 12.4 / 11.6 m/s**, 55 / 45 / 42 km/h), all ≥ the mech's 9.1 m/s cruise (R-001). No gait has an aerial phase beyond Strider's brief gallop (Fr ≤ 0.8 < 1, [4]).
6. **Impulse budgets (§6) are comparable to the mech's:** melee blows 20 MJ to 1.0 GJ (mech: 18 MJ default, 75 MJ max, 460 MJ upper bound [R-027]); body charges 0.35 / 0.60 / 0.63 GJ of contact energy and 5.0×10⁷ / 1.1×10⁸ / 1.2×10⁸ N·s against a 10,000 t mech (mech's own cruise momentum 9.1×10⁷ N·s). BREACHER's rearing slam, 3.0 GJ, is the same order as a mech toppling (3.6 GJ).
7. **Ground pressure 0.86–1.1 MPa static** (mech 0.65 MPa), 3–4× Bay-soil ultimate capacity: every species sinks and craters (§8). Most of the Bay (< 5 m) is a wade for all three; the Golden Gate (≈ 100 m) is a swim for STRIDER and BREACHER (ρ < seawater) and a bottom walk for BASTION with the head near the surface (§7).

## 2. Method and reference animals

- **Reference animal (calibration): African elephant**, shoulder 3.0 m, live mass ≈ 4.5 t (4–6 t; recalled, not re-verified, *Assumed*). Elephants are the heaviest living graviportal walkers; lateral-sequence walk, duty factor > 0.5 at every speed, aerial phase only at Fr ≈ 1 [4]. Largest model for kaiju stress: Sellers et al. 80 t *Argentinosaurus*, simulated walking just over 2 m/s [1].
- **Bone-failure and safety factor:** cortical bone fails at 200 MN/m² (large animals) to 233 (small), no size trend [2]; peak locomotor stress is 25–50 % of failure, SF 2–4 [3]. Posture (erect limbs for large animals) keeps stress roughly constant at the price of agility and acceleration [8].
- **Muscle:** peak isometric stress ≈ 200–300 kPa [9][10]. I use 300 kPa as the real ceiling.
- **Limb-load model (my model, Assumed; every coefficient listed):**
  - Per-limb peak force `F = φ m g K(Fr)`, φ = 0.30 (most loaded of four limbs; static share 0.25), `K(Fr) = 0.9 + 1.2 Fr` (anchors: human walking Fr 0.25 → ≈ 1.2 BW, human running Fr ≈ 1.5 → ≈ 2.7 BW; recalled, not re-verified).
  - Bone as a tube (ID/OD q = 0.4), stress `σ = F/A + F e L (D/2)/I` with lateral offset `e` as a fraction of leg length (`e = 0.20` columnar, 0.25 semi-erect, 0.30 digitigrade/crouched; Biewener's posture trend [8]). **Calibration:** with Campione bone size [6] a 4.5 t elephant (D = 10.6 cm per bone, leg 1.7 m) gives SF 4.5 at Fr 0.2 and SF 2.4 at Fr 1.0 with `e = 0.20`, inside the measured 2–4 [3]. The muscle check gives 0.24 MPa at walk and 0.44 MPa at Fr 1 for a 50 % muscle share of the limb section: elephants sit at 80–150 % of isometric stress, i.e. they are already muscle-limited, consistent with Hokkanen's claim that muscle weakening is the more serious limit [7].
  - Muscle force `F_m = F / EMA`, EMA 0.9 (columnar; elephants ≈ 0.8 recalled from Biewener's EMA scaling [8], *Assumed*); required stress `σ_m = F_m / (0.5 A_limb)`, `A_limb` at mid thigh.
- **Gait:** Froude `Fr = v²/(g l)` with l = hip height, stride period `T = 2.3 Fr^-0.2 √(l/g)` (Alexander mammal fit, R-001 §1 [11][12]). Same dimensionless method as R-001; the M-001 tool normalises by header values so it applies to kaiju unchanged (R-001 §9.4).

## 3. Body plans and mass from structure

**Recipe (as GAME_BREAKDOWN §4, but from explicit primitives instead of scaling a human).** `V = V_trunk(ellipsoid a,b,c) + V_head(ellipsoid) + V_neck(frustum L,R,r) + V_tail(frustum) + 4 (V_upper limb + V_lower limb)(frustums) + V_extra`; `V_extra` = armour, crest, dorsal and facial plates and sail (volume only, from a Q-NEW below). `m = ρ_eff V`. **Validation:** elephant with the same recipe (trunk 1.5×0.65×0.85, limb 0.9 m + 0.8 m, R 0.28 to 0.13) gives V = 5.23 m³; at ρ = 0.86 it is 4.5 t and at 1.0 it is 5.2 t, inside the 4–6 t range (accuracy ±15 %).

**Effective density.** Live tissue ≈ 1,000 kg/m³ (convex-hull reconstructions apply 893 kg/m³ to mammals [5]); air sacs of ≥ 10 % of body volume lighten sauropod-like bodies [13]. STRIDER 900 (pneumatic bones, large air sacs, almost no armour), BREACHER 980 (lung-air 4 %, osteoderm back), **BASTION 1,050** (armoured, dense: just denser than seawater 1,025, so it sinks, §7). All *Assumed*; the three values are within the sourced range 0.89–1.1.

Why these plans (the stress argument, §4.4, decides quadruped; shape follows pack role and silhouette):
- **STRIDER**: lean limbs (limb radius 0.09 leg length) and `e = 0.30` because a cursorial animal runs crouched [8]; light mass keeps absolute loads smallest; a long neck gives the 60 m height without leg mass.
- **BREACHER**: heavier per limb, `e = 0.25`, thick arms (R 7 m at the shoulder) to slam and grapple; the rearing mode loads the two hind limbs at φ = 0.5 and works (§4.4), only because the tail carries the tripod load and rearing is time-limited (≤ 20 s, Q-NEW-2). The sloth-like reared brawler (ground sloths fed reared) is a natural, non-franchise precedent.
- **BASTION**: elephant-like columns (limb radius 0.11 leg length, R_top 10.5 m), `e = 0.20` (most erect), foot pad 360 m². Short neck because blood pressure and neck-muscle loads scale badly at this size [7]; the head is carried low for the same reason and the crest (60 m of thin plate above the back) supplies the 160 m silhouette cheaply in mass.

*Originality (D-009).* None of the three is a bipedal upright reptile, an ape, a winged form or an insectoid; the quadruped-plus-reared-forelimb plan comes from ground-sloth and elephant biomechanics; surface design (plates, crest, skin colour) is left to art with a no-franchise-reference rule (Q-NEW-6).

## 4. Limb cross-sections, peak stress, and where real material fails

### 4.1 Limb geometry (derived from §3)
Mid-thigh section `A_limb = π R_mid²`: STRIDER R 2.35 m, 17 m²; BREACHER 5.8 m, 106 m²; BASTION 9.0 m, 254 m² (elephant 0.225 m, 0.16 m²). Design bone area share 15 % of the limb section (elephants ≈ 7 %), tube q = 0.4: bone D = 2.0 / 4.9 / 7.6 m (D/L 0.066 / 0.109 / 0.083 of leg length; real extrapolation 0.042 / 0.061 / 0.051).

### 4.2 Real-material extrapolation (nature's trend, same posture)
Real bone sizes from Campione and Evans `log₁₀ M(g) = 2.749 log₁₀ C_{H+F}(mm) − 1.104` [6] (published coefficient 2.754 − 1.097; difference < 1 %; recalled, verify against the article):

|---|---|---|---|
| Real bone D (per bone) | 1.27 m | 2.75 m | 4.82 m |
| Peak limb force walk / charge (MN), `0.30 m g K` | 15.5 / 22.9 | 117 / 136 | 492 / 521 |
| **Real-bone σ_peak walk / charge (MPa)** | 729 / 1,076 | 689 / 798 | 878 / 930 |
| **Real-bone SF at 200 MPa, walk / charge** | **0.27 / 0.19** | **0.29 / 0.25** | **0.23 / 0.22** |
| Real-muscle stress needed (MPa) walk / charge | 2.0 / 2.9 (6.6× / 9.8× isometric) | 2.5 / 2.9 (8.2× / 9.5×) | 4.3 / 4.6 (14× / 15×) |

All three break their bones and tear their muscles by factor 3–5 and 7–15. Where it breaks first when sizes grow isometrically from the elephant (same posture, Campione bone growth): bone SF reaches 1 at ≈ 2,500 t (Fr 0.12 walk) and muscle exceeds 300 kPa already at ≈ 17 t (the elephant is at the muscle limit). These crossing points are model outputs, not literature values; the literature ceiling is 10⁵–10⁶ kg from bone [7] and the best simulated animal is 80 t [1]. The model therefore says nature's route past ≈ 20 t is posture and fat limbs; the kaiju need the next step: stronger tissue.

### 4.3 Fiction material (the Assumed numbers) and the design check
**Assumed, shared by all species** (single tissue keeps the fiction one paragraph long; Q-NEW-1):
- Skeletal composite compressive yield **σ_b = 600 MPa** (3× cortical bone [2]; structural steel S355 is 355 MPa and Ti-6Al-4V ≈ 880 MPa, i.e. the mech's class of material). Elastic modulus and toughness stay at bone-like ratios; damage via F-024 uses the same yield (§9).
- Muscle peak isometric stress **σ_m = 6.0 MPa** (20× vertebrate [9]; hydraulic cylinders operate at 20–30 MPa), utilisation ≤ 0.85 at charge (so ≤ 5.1 MPa in use).
- Design limb: bone 15 % of the section, muscle 50 %, 35 % tendon, hide, vessels, fat (elephant-like).

Design stresses (same model, design bone dimensions):

| | STRIDER | BREACHER | BASTION |
|---|---|---|---|
| σ_bone walk / charge (MPa) | 192 / 284 | 125 / 144 | 228 / 241 |
| **SF at 600 MPa, walk / charge** | **3.1 / 2.1** | **4.8 / 4.2** | **2.6 / 2.5** |
| σ_muscle needed walk / charge (MPa), utilisation of 6.0 | 2.0 / 2.9 (0.33 / 0.49) | 2.5 / 2.9 (0.41 / 0.48) | 4.3 / 4.6 (0.72 / 0.76) |
| Charge Fr cap from stress | 0.80 | 0.35 | 0.15 |

Charge speed caps are set by the first of bone SF 2.0 and muscle utilisation 0.85, then rounded down; BASTION is the most stressed (0.76) and would need 6.5 MPa to gallop faster than Fr 0.15. **Where the fiction sits relative to the mech:** the mech needs steel columns and hydraulics for 98 MN on one leg; a BASTION limb carries 5× that in compression through a 7.6 m bone column, so the fiction is "tissue with an engineering material's strength", not magic; the same single-tissue parameter set feeds F-024.

### 4.4 Why not a biped (body plan decision, Q-NEW-1)
One supporting limb carries φ = 1.0 in single support (a quadruped limb 0.30). Same model, same design bone:

| Case | σ_bone (MPa) / SF-2 requirement | σ_muscle (MPa) |
|---|---|---|
| STRIDER biped walk / charge | 642 / 947 → needs 1.28 / 1.89 GPa | 6.6 / 9.8 |
| BREACHER biped walk / charge | 416 / 481 → 0.83 / 0.96 GPa | 8.2 / 9.5 |
| BASTION biped walk / charge | 760 / 805 → 1.52 / 1.61 GPa | 14.3 / 15.2 |
| **Rear mode** (φ = 0.5 per hind limb, Fr 0.05) STRIDER / BREACHER / BASTION | 244 / 175 / 358 → 0.49 / **0.35** / **0.72** GPa | 2.5 / **3.5** / **6.7** |

Every biped walk or charge exceeds the 600 MPa and 6 MPa caps; only a time-limited rear mode passes for STRIDER (not needed) and BREACHER, and fails for BASTION (0.72 GPa, 6.7 MPa), so BASTION never rears. A species-specific stronger tissue would be a second fiction knob for every species and is rejected (it narrows nothing, but breaks the "one shared tissue" rule). An earlier-considered six-limb plan was dropped: extra limb pairs lower per-limb load by only 1.5× and cost 50 % more articulation chain for T-117.

## 6. Attacks: impulse and energy budgets

**Reference budgets (10,000 t mech).** Melee fist: 18 MJ default, 75 MJ at max tip speed, 460 MJ Froude-similar upper bound (R-027). 406 mm cannon: 356 MJ muzzle (R-027). Toppling fall: 3.6 GJ (R-021/§4). Mech charge at cruise: 0.42 GJ, momentum 9.1×10⁷ N·s. Mech reduced mass in a two-body hit: `μ = m_k m_mech/(m_k + m_mech)`; transfer energy `½ μ v²`, impulse `J = (1 + e) μ v`, e = 0.1.

**Bound used for every attack (same idea as M-062):** `E = min(½ m_eff v², m_muscle · w · A)`; `w` = 70 J/kg muscle work per contraction (recalled, *Assumed*), `A` = 4 elastic amplification (*Assumed*); tip speed `v = η · 9.14 √(L/0.7) m/s` (R-027 Froude-scaled human fist, η = 0.3–0.5 for large animals, *Assumed*). Gravity-fed attacks use `m g Δh`.

| Bay soil presumptive allowable (kPa, IBC via R-005 §[33]) | clay/silt 72, sand 96, sandy gravel 144, sedimentary rock 192, crystalline rock 575; mud and fill none | | | |
| Ultimate ≈ 3× allowable | 215–430 kPa soil, 1.7 MPa rock | | | |

All three exceed the ultimate capacity of every soil and of rock under a charging foot, so footfalls crater (F-009) and feet sink; BASTION's foot is 21 m across so it sinks less per unit load and its crater is wide and shallow. Elephant calibration: ≈ 0.1 m² per foot gives ≈ 0.11 MPa static (recalled, *Assumed*), the kaiju are 8–10× higher, which is the price of 10⁵× mass (area ∝ L², weight ∝ L³). Sinkage depth is a soil-model question for T-117 (Q-NEW-5), not fixed here.

## 9. Structural damage (F-024) and the shared parts model

The kaiju skeleton uses the same part graph as the mech (limb segments, joints as failure planes, armour plates). Critical stress per part = **600 MPa** composite yield (§4.3); damage to a limb segment adds load to the three remaining limbs. **Crippling thresholds** (derived, per leg): losing one limb of four raises the other three limbs' φ from 0.30 to 0.40 (×1.33): STRIDER charge SF 2.1 → 1.6 (limp, Fr cap 0.45), BREACHER 4.2 → 3.1, BASTION 2.5 → 1.9 (cap 0.12). Losing two limbs doubles the load (φ → 0.60): walk SF falls to 1.5 (STRIDER), 2.4 (BREACHER), 1.3 (BASTION), so a two-limb loss forces a crawl (Fr ≤ 0.05) or a fall. These are inputs to M-103.5 and the same impulse limits as the mech's `IBodyDamageRecord` (T-030c).

### 11.3 M-103 (draft): species physical consistency
**Definition.** Each species' mass, limb stress, gait, attack, water and ground numbers recomputed from its `KaijuSpeciesSpec@v1` agree with the spec's declared values and respect the sourced bounds; every non-sourced number is declared Assumed and shared.

**Procedure.** `Tools/kaiju_spec_check.py` (and a mirrored dotnet test) loads each `species/*.json` and recomputes:
1. Volume from primitives → mass `m = ρ V`.
2. Per-limb force, bone stress, muscle stress at walk, charge and rear mode.
3. Gait quantities from `l` and `Fr`.
4. Attack energy bound `min(KE, muscle work)` and impulse.
5. Floating state, ground pressure.

| Sub-measure | Threshold | Source |
|---|---|---|
| M-103.1 Mass from structure | `|m_spec − ρ V| / m ≤ 1 %`; ρ ∈ [850, 1,100] kg/m³; recipe validation (elephant primitives) ∈ [3, 6] t at ρ 0.86–1.1; mass in D-026 height category (60 / 100 / 160 ± 10 m ⇒ height field ∈ category) | §3 [4][5] |
| M-103.2 Cross-species consistency | mass ratio between consecutive species ∈ [0.5, 2.5] × (H₂/H₁)³ | §3 |
| M-103.3 Bone stress | SF = σ_b/σ_peak ≥ 2.5 walk, ≥ 2.0 at charge cap, with σ_b the shared tissue value; real-material extrapolation is reported (never gated) and must be < 1 for every species (documents why the tissue is needed) | §4 [2][3] |
| M-103.4 Muscle stress | utilisation `σ_m,req/σ_m,iso` ≤ 0.85 at charge, ≤ 0.85 at rear mode | §4 [9] |
| M-103.5 Assumed-tissue declaration | `bone_yield_Pa`, `muscle_isometric_Pa` identical for all species and listed in `assumed_fields[]`; ratio to the real values ≤ 3.5× (bone) and ≤ 25× (muscle) | §4.3 (Assumed) |
| M-103.6 Gait Froude | cruise Fr ∈ [0.10, 0.30], charge Fr ≤ cap ≤ 1.0, stride period within ±15 % of `2.3 Fr^-0.2 √(l/g)`, duty factor in band; measured via the M-001 tool on `gait_log@v1` | §5 [11][12] |
| M-103.7 Attack energy | `E ≤ min(KE, muscle work) (1 + 10⁻⁹)`; each attack's `E_cap` ≤ 10 × the mech upper-bound melee (460 MJ) except gravity-fed attacks, which must satisfy `E = m g Δh` | §6, M-062 |
| M-103.8 Water | `floats` agrees with ρ vs ρ_w; draft and belly clearance reproduce within 1 % | §7 [14] |
| M-103.9 Ground pressure | static pressure ≤ 1.2 MPa and recomputed within 1 %; > soil ultimate flagged "sinks" | §8 |
| M-103.10 Limb bone cap | bone D recomputed from `bone_area_share × limb_area` ≤ 0.9 × limb diameter | §4.1 |

**Verdict.** PASS only if every sub-measure passes for every species. Provisional values for the three codenames: M-103.3 min SF 2.1 (STRIDER charge), M-103.4 max 0.76 (BASTION), M-103.9 max 1.12 MPa. Thresholds are *Assumed, pending calibration* (SF 2.0 charge from the lower end of Biewener's 2–4 [3]).

## 12. Open questions with recommended defaults (decided autonomously; founder asleep, §15)

- **Q-NEW-1 Fiction material.** One shared tissue: 600 MPa bone, 6.0 MPa muscle. **Default: as specified** (a species-specific tissue would make each species an exception; a steel frame is already the mech's justification).
- **Q-NEW-2 Rearing and slam limits.** BREACHER rear ≤ 20 s, slam cooldown 40 s, BASTION never rears. **Default: as specified**; rear-mode SF 3.4.

- **Q-NEW-4 Deep water.** Swim mode (≤ 3 m/s), STRIDER and BREACHER float; BASTION bottom-walks. **Default: as specified**; M-103.8 gates the data, T-117 builds it.
- **Q-NEW-5 Sinkage model.** Soil sinkage model deferred to T-117 (need Bay mud shear strength and bearing; recalled ≈ 10–30 kPa undrained for soft Bay mud, unverified). **Default:** use `pressure / ultimate` as the drive until T-117 adds a soil table.
- **Q-NEW-6 Naming and surface design.** Codenames STRIDER / BREACHER / BASTION are placeholders; final names and surface art through art review against D-009. **Default:** no species resembles an upright biped reptile, an ape or an insectoid; an art checklist, not this document, owns the rest.
- **Q-NEW-7 Height category definition.** H = top of head or crest in the standard pose; BREACHER counts reared (62 m on all fours). **Default: as specified** (the alternative would put it near 60 m and break the 100 m class).
- **Q-NEW-8 Six-limb and winged variants.** Not adopted (§4.4). **Default:** revisit after M-103 passes.
- **Q-NEW-9 Kaiju yaw and lateral limits are scaled from the mech biped.** **Default:** use R-001's formulas; recalibrate with captures like R-001 §9's.
- **M-NEW-1 Silhouette separation metric** (pairwise height-normalised silhouette overlap < 0.6, readable at 1 and 3 px/m). **Default:** draft in R-103/T-112 with art.
