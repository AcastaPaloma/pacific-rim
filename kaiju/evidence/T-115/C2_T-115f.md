## Why C2 could not discriminate in hunt@v1
ff = share of kaiju that have a target who share the most-shared target. In hunt@v1 mechs patrol 3 km out in a 2 km ring and are met one at a time, so at most one mech is in anyone's sight: every control kaiju has the same single candidate, ff ~ 0.975 (T-115e). The clause denominator 1-ff_nf then explodes. This is a property of the geometry (one visible target), not of the control brain.
