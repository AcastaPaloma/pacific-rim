## What binds / diagnosis
1. Lower row 0 makes it WORSE monotonically: v5 row 0 (Fr 0.02) 30.8 s, v6g (0.015) 17.6, v6e (0.010) 18.0, v6 (0.005) 14.6. A slower, shorter reference gives the plant less forward DCM momentum; the 15 m foot lands 5 m ahead of a CoM that moves 0.1-0.9 m/s, so the CoM is behind the support and falls backwards at the first load transfer. The r7 diagnosis (swing cannot execute the row) is therefore incomplete: the slow rows remove the swing-torque problem (hip 0.5-0.6 of limit, apex 3.5-4.1 m reached) but expose a forward-momentum / load-transfer problem the reference cannot fix.
2. The swing still takes 3.5-3.9 s and lands 1.7-3.6 m short even with swing hip at 0.57 of its limit in the row: the executed swing is not torque limited by the row but by the QP (whole-body trade with the stance leg, IK out of reach 40-90 ticks).
3. Torque rows on the swing (swlim) change nothing measurable; the retime of the whole clock is harmful, retime of the foot curve alone is neutral.
