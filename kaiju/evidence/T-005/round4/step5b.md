## What binds (precise)
1. Lateral and yaw are no longer what fails: with stage 2 the lateral speed stays within +-1.2 m/s and realised friction > mu drops from 24 % to 14-18 % of contact ticks; the QP side tracks its yaw target (commanded = target to 1 %). Realised yaw still drifts (to +20 deg in the best run): in cycle 1 the commanded whole-body yaw moment (-1e8 N m for 5 s) met a realised rate of ~0 because the legacy trunk-yaw PD (pelvis to the planted foot's yaw) cancelled it at the stance hip; removing that (cycle 2) did not help (24.2 -> 21.1 s).
2. The start binds, in two ways. (a) The time-warped Fr 0.10 stride is dynamically infeasible below about s 0.7: a 28 m step at 1.9 m/s needs a 10-15 s single support; the CoM stalls and reverses (forward speed -1.75 m/s at 12 s, best run), then the QP pumps it to 5.8 m/s and the next touchdown at that speed falls (24.2 s). (b) The speed-matched ladder removes the stride mismatch but not the state mismatch: at the hand-off (double support, 3.3-3.8 m/s on the 2.88 m/s row) the CoM sits several metres off the row's node-0 CoM relative to the landed heel; the CoM task brakes at the friction cap (frc 0.90, 3.8 -> 1.2 m/s in 0.7 s), then the trunk pitches forward to 24 deg with both hips at -LIM (1.18e9 N m) and it falls 2.9 s after the hand-off, for every ladder variant.
3. Within each QP tick the hips sit at +-LIM with opposite signs in double support (internal force, friction at the cap); w_tau up to 100 does not change it, so the torque saturation is the CoM / trunk tasks' demand, not an unregularised null space.
