## Ruled out (with the data)
- PhysX friction / patch / restitution: realised SS |ft|/fn 0.2-0.4 vs PhysX mu_s 0.8 / mu_d 0.6; the braking is COMMANDED (fr = QP cmd at 0.95 of qp_mu 0.5), decel ~4 m/s2 = the QP cap. Loaded-foot "slip" 1-4 m/s is yaw pivoting about a sole corner (foot yaw 17 -> 51 deg in one stance), a consequence of edge loading, not the sagittal loss.
- 1-tick / order-of-operations latency, unmodelled Coriolis, qdd = 0 sub-block, stance PD scale: CoM and stance joints track the row to 1.5 m / 1-3 deg for 3.4 s of each step; the PD-scale seeds (1e-4) only sample the spread.
- Time warp / phase clock drift: ts = 1 on the row; the clock re-anchors every touchdown; the failure is the re-anchor itself after a misplaced foot.
- Friction margin of the reference (0.84-0.90 of mu 0.4): the post-anchor demand is 1.7-6x any cone; qp_mu 0.4 (9.5h J8) is not better.
- The ankle soft lock (4 % M-row error): not the trigger; the HARD ankle ROM stop is (the planar b5 plant has no ROM: its ankle reaches -20.4..-22.3 freely, `g5_010_A/g5_009_A.npz`).
