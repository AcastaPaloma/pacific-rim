## Diagnosis (from the logs, then confirmed with qp.k)
1. Saturation: yes. In every 2-6 s swing the swing hip sits at +-0.98 LIM for 14-54 % of the ticks (knee at -LIM in the same window, ankle 0-45 %); a 0.65 s swing 95 %. While |ff| is at LIM the drive force limit (LIM - |ff|, ArticulatedBody.cs:380) leaves the PD <= 2 % LIM (24e6 N m): the swing leg is then pure QP torque. The knee folds because the hip flexion torque (+1.18e9) whips the thigh while the knee extension torque (-0.78e9) is also at its limit: the shank lags (knee 45-60 deg vs ref 25 max).
2. Why the QP asks for it: the swing HEEL task (kp = om_sw^2 = 16 /s2, kd 8 /s, weight 1e3) tracks a WORLD target (reference + xoff): heel errors 2-6 m in a normal swing give commanded accelerations of 90-250 m/s2 (9-25 g; the hip can deliver ~40 m/s2 at the foot), after an off-schedule re-anchor errors reach 10-46 m and 500-960 m/s2. Posture weight (10) cannot compete. Not blend: T_blend 0.2 s only delays the task after lift-off; the stance tasks are 1e6 and do not outweigh it.
3. qp_pd_scale 0.25: PD torque is the same size as the QP torque on the swing hip / knee (mean |PD| 300-580e6 vs |QP| 500-780e6, PD > QP in 20-40 % of ticks) and pulls to the LEGACY imp 2 foot path, not the QP's. But removing it (swing scale 0 / 0.05) or retargeting it to the QP reference (qp_pd_ref) is WORSE (13.6-19.5 s): the PD is a needed stabiliser, not the cause.
4. Touchdown logic: ContactSchedule accepts ANY point of the swing leg (toe included) at tau > 0.6 ts. In the 9.5j logs 35 % of the swings end toe-first (foot pitch -22 .. -86 deg, cop at the toe, 1-9 m high), 53 % last < 1.5 s (median 1.37 s vs 3.3 s ref); each scuff re-anchors (xoff, clock; rematch restarts at node 2-3 s) so the next swing's heel target is 10-46 m off: the 0.5-1.5 s swings are the consequence of the swing-heel saturation + early toe contact, not an independent cause.
