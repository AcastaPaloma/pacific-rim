## Model (a, b, c)
a) Legs + two straight arms (0.70e6 kg each, 13.5 m lateral, CoM 18 m below the shoulder) give Mz = dL_z/dt about the CoM (legs +-6.5 m, as yaw_check.py). Arm swing = N extra variables: psi(k) on the leg-A side, -psi(k) on the leg-B side, anti-periodic per step. The arm reaction is added to the 3 sagittal dyn rows (Newton-Euler force of the swinging arm minus the same arm rigid in the trunk lump), shoulder torque <= 0.9 x 3e8 N m (Lagrange row of psi).
b) Ground free torsion Mt = Mz - M0, M0 = moment about the CoM of the planned shear (sagittal Ft at the foot's lateral offset + lateral-LIPM Fy). One cone per node: Ft_tot^2 + Fy_tot^2 + (Mt / (0.85 r))^2 <= (mu Fn_tot)^2, r = sole mean radius (flat 4.86 m / edge 2.5 m, yaw_check.py), mu 0.40, on top of the per-point sagittal/lateral rows. c) all v3 rows unchanged.
