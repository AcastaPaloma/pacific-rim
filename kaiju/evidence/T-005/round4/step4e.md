# T-005 r4 step 9.4e: rig node-0 pose assertion fixed (cause: test pose setup order, not the mapping)
Cause: `Rig.SetPose` (WholeBodyProbeTests.cs) wrote the joint positions and THEN called `TeleportRoot(pos, rot)`. With a non-identity root rotation the engine keeps the world orientation of the spherical joints attached to the root (waist, hip_L, hip_R) and rewrites their joint coordinates by -rotation: the legs lose the pelvis pitch (foot absolute pitch f - th, torso unpitched), which is exactly the step4d hypothesis. Hip positions did follow the rotation, which is why only the orientations were off.
Probes (EditMode, fresh rig each, gravity 0, colliders off, 2 runs, probes removed again): one variable at a time, world pitch of pelvis/torso/thigh/shank/foot after one step. Joint alone (hip 0.3, knee 0.5, ankle 0.4, no root rotation): link pitches exactly -0.3 / +0.5 / +0.4 (mapping hip Sign -1, knee +1, ankle +1 and planar pitches th, -a, -b, +f confirmed). Pelvis pitch alone 0.3: pelvis 0.3, thigh/shank/foot 0.0, hip_L/hip_R/waist joint coordinate -0.3 (not 0). th + hip 0.3 (thigh must be vertical): thigh -0.3, hip coordinate -0.6.
Order test (th = 0.3, hip 0.3): joints,TeleportRoot -> wrong (above); TeleportRoot,joints -> thigh 0.0, torso 0.3, joint coordinate -0.3 (correct); TeleportRoot,step,joints, joints,Tele,joints, Tele,joints,Tele -> same correct result.
Fix: `Rig.SetPose` calls `TeleportRoot` first, then the joint positions, then zeroes the root velocities. No change to the controller, driver, reference, mapping (hip = a + th etc.), thresholds or any config; TolPose stays 0.25 m. The driver's own th is read from the root rotation and was never affected.
Result: sole points of the rig at node 0 vs the reference points: worst 0.007 m (was 6.27 m); Apply status Optimal; M_ss worst entry 0; J_p qd_S 1.9e-4 of max speed.
