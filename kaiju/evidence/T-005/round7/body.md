# T-005 r7 body check: toe lever <= 7 m (A), ankle limit >= 1.6e9 N m (B), both (C), on the best round-7 controller: no variant is stable and none beats the default body (30.8 s); recommendation: no body change
Method: experiment flags only (`exp_toe_m`, `exp_ankle_limit_nm` in the walk-tuning variant JSON, applied by `MechConfig.WithExperiments()` in the scenario CLI; `body_tuning.json`, `mech_frame_balanced.json` untouched). Toe trim: foot box shortened at the toe (heel -5.25 m kept, toe 9.75 -> 7.0 m, sole 15 -> 12.25 m, area 150 -> 122.5 m^2, mass kept, inertia of the box). Rows re-optimised for each foot / ankle with the same hard limits (CoP in [heel + 1.5, toe - 2.5] m, F_z <= 1.5 W, T <= 3 s; chains `a7_*` toe 7 m, `c7_*` toe 7 m + ankle 1.6e9; files gait_reference_qp_ladder_v5a/v5c.json, selected by qp_ref_v5 = 2 / 4; variant frame / body json in `Tools/GaitOpt/out/r7/frame_toe7.json`, `body_ank16.json`; exporter tolerance 5e-4, dynamics residuals 1.1e-5 .. 1.9e-4). Controller = best r7 (L7: 7b base + qp_ref_v5 + qp_r7_clock + qp_plan_lmax 1.0), 4 seeds (`summary_run10.txt`).
Offline: the toe-7 m foot has feasible rows at the same limits for Fr 0.02-0.09 (a7_090 residual 1.1e-5, T 2.0 s, L 12.2 m); Fr 0.10 at T >= 2.0 s is infeasible for the default foot, toe 7 m and toe 7 m + ankle 1.6e9 (residual 7.5e-3, 4.1e-2) and for ankle 1.6e9 alone (b16_100, 1.6e-2): at T < 2 s the default foot is feasible (h2b), the variants were not re-run below 2 s. So in the planar stride model the CoP window / foot length is not what limits the speed.

## Gameplay cost of adopting each (assessment, not measured beyond the fall times above)
- A, toe lever 7 m (foot 12.25 m, -18 % sole area): smaller forward support polygon, so less forward lean the ankle can hold; standing and slow-down stability margin forward falls from 9.75 to 7 m of the 42 m CoM height (tilt-over angle 13 -> 9.5 deg), more stepping at stops and in turns, lower top Froude for a given step; sole area is also the torsion budget of the yaw model (r_flat), so turning grip falls with area unless the foot is widened. Speed: the offline row at Fr 0.09 still exists. In the Unity test the survival was 5-10 s shorter.
- B, ankle limit 1.6e9 N m (+60 %): stiffer, quicker stance balance and a CoP range of 16 m at 1 W (vs 10 m), higher heel-toe rocking authority; it breaks the actuator ordering of the table (ankle would exceed the knee 0.8e9 and the hip 1.2e9), higher peak joint power and the ankle becomes the strongest joint, which has to be justified in the fiction; no handling downside in the sim, and no survival gain either.
- C, both: the foot is shorter and the ankle stronger; as A, no gain.
## Art cost
- A: new foot mesh and collider (toe cut 2.75 m of 15 m, 18 % shorter silhouette; heel 5.25 m behind the ankle vs 7 m forward, a stubby, heel-heavy foot unless the width goes up from 10 m to ~12 m to keep the 150 m^2 sole), foot_sole data, footprint / dust / contact decals, foot IK targets and the foot rig's toe pivot, mech_frame_balanced.json and the art-vs-frame area check (area within 2 %). Medium cost: one foot asset ({L, R}), its collider, LOD and the contact VFX.
- B: no mesh change needed; the ankle housing / actuator art may want a visibly larger joint (about +60 % torque is +17 % in diameter at constant stress), plus the rig's joint-limit tables and UI/stat sheets. Low cost.
- C: both of the above.
## Recommendation (recommendation only, no body_tuning edit)
Keep the default body (toe lever 9.75 m, ankle limit 1e9 N m). The round-7 rows show the foot length and ankle torque are not binding for a feasible reference at Froude <= 0.09 (and 0.10 below T 2 s), and the Unity tests with matched rows show no benefit from either change. The binding problem is executing the swing (step_time_s, landing place, hip torque saturation at the reference), see step8b.md. If a body change is asked for anyway, B (ankle 1.6e9) is the only one that does not hurt: it costs art almost nothing but needs the actuator-ordering decision.
