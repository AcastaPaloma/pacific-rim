Reading:
1. The cap-fitting thresholds add little error: total rms vs L0 grows by <= 0.01 m on the urban tiles (L1_14_18 1.354 -> 1.362, L1_15_18 0.794 -> 0.795) and 0.003-0.10 m on the seabed tiles (L1_13_18 0.470 -> 0.575 at 1.0 m, the largest step); p99 moves by the threshold itself. The dominant error everywhere is the node subsample of the L0 field (fld columns), which no threshold changes.
2. The hierarchical RTIN bound is not a hard bound on the final mesh: node max error is 1.8-2.3 x the threshold (L1_14_18 0.5 m -> 1.15 m; L1_13_18 1.0 m -> 1.87 m; L3_03_04 1.6 m -> 3.19 m). Budget on the node max, not the threshold.
3. Seabed tiles: field max error is 187-232 m at a few nodes (p99 only 0.19-2.5 m) and the rms of 1.8-2.5 m is that tail (not diagnosed further here). Pixel error of the field alone at the inner ring edge: rms 1.8-1.9 px on L1_12_19 / L1_13_19, 0.67 px L2_06_09, 0.38 px L3_03_04; the cap-fitting threshold changes px rms by <= 0.03 px except L1_13_18 (0.35 -> 0.43 px rms, p99 0.51 -> 0.81 px).
4. Urban L1_14_18 / L1_15_18: rms 1.0 / 0.6 px and p99 4.3 / 2.3 px at 1536 m already at the NOMINAL threshold (error is the node subsample of real hillside roughness + the T-108c footprint fill, not RTIN); cap-fit 0.5 m / 0.25 m changes px rms by +0.007 / +0.001.
5. B1 over the 100 k cap: L1_14_18 144,589 tris at 1 m; fits at 2 m tolerance (97,315 tris, footprint max 3.31 m rms 1.23 m); 1.5 m gives 113,116. L2_07_09 130,157 at 2 m; fits at 6 m (94,193; max 11.1 m, rms 3.9 m); 4 m gives 105,755. Footprint max error scales ~1.7-1.9 x the tolerance.
6. Triangle counts at cap-fit are the baker's unchunked RTIN counts; the C# reader (256-cell chunks, forced shared borders) adds a few hundred to a few thousand triangles (GeoLodReaderTests asserts the reader's count within 3% + 600 triangles of the manifest on the first two tiles per level).
