## What the clip shows (honest)
- Run: `-sliceConfig demo` (feelcheck set + units.mech/physics.body, warm_amber_haze, layerSnap off), physics mech walk_mode 2 with the T-201 fast overlay (default; no `-sliceWalk default`), auto-pilot, follow camera, no HUD.
- Walking: yes, whole clip. The mech advanced from z=1024 to z~1440 (about 5.8 m/s in the 1 s trace).
- Combat: the kaiju is a visible capsule stand-in (not invisible) driven by the placeholder proxy. Scripted duels at 13.5 s, 30 s, 55 s, 80 s place it 90 m ahead; the cannon fires every 2 s; arm swings at 8, 14, 20, 30, ... s (every 10 s). Log: kaiju melee hits on the mech body (hp 0.90 down to 0.00 twice), the kaiju was downed once and a new one "respawned 340 m ahead". In the 5 s contact sheet the kaiju capsule is in frame at t = 15, 20, 35, 40, 60, 65, 70, 75, 85, 90 and 95 s.
- Destruction: 5 impacts (t = 12, 30, 48, 66, 84 s; 16875 MJ each, building state 0 -> 4) and 4 quakes (5, 35, 65, 95 s); 3527 building damage events, 8 buildings removed. Weak spot: the follow camera mostly looks at the mech and the horizon, so the destruction is only readable in a few frames (a building vanishing in frames ~330-420 (t=11-14 s)); there is no dedicated destruction camera cut in this clip (the smoke's cuts need SmokeOut and hide the mech). Treat destruction legibility as not good enough for a trailer; a camera cut or a closer impact target is follow-up work.
- Fall / respawn: NONE. `mech_fell_ever` false, 0 respawns, `fall_events` empty over all 100 s (autopilot steers to the flattest heading, and the terrain on this route has no 4 m steps; the 26 s / 55 s falls seen on forced straight walks did not occur). The TEMPORARY 2.5 s respawn net was on and never fired (not `-sliceNoRespawn`). One run only, one seed; a different heading could fall.
- `record.json` field `mech_com_travel_m` (11582) is NOT a travel distance (it sums the transform of the proxy transform incl. jumps); use the trace (about 410 m).
- Kaiju at t=50 s sits at x=-4255 in the 1 s trace (kaiju respawn/placeholder proxy jump); it is out of view there.

## Frame checks (frame_checks.json)
- Non-blank: pass, 3000/3000 frames (luma std >= 3; mean luma 110-131). (A first pass flagged all frames because the check required >= 16 colours; the palette-limited pixel look has fewer, so the criterion was dropped. Not a threshold relaxation on a style gate: it is this script's own sanity check.)
- Pixel grid (T-148 `blocks_uniform`, 4x4 blocks, best of 16 offsets per frame, 100 sampled frames): min 0.9945, mean 0.9963. Not >= 0.999: about 0.4 % of 4x4 blocks are mixed (seams where the present-stage shift moves rows/columns). Frames are 1920x1080 = 480x270 x 4, no filtering seen, but I did NOT get a strict 100 % grid; with a fixed offset the share is lower (0.65 min), because the present shift varies per frame. M-123 (sidecar-based style check) was not run: the player writes no style_capture sidecars. capture_path lint (L01-L15) does not apply: no capture_path was used.
- Temporal: every frame differs from the previous one (0 identical frames), mean changed-pixel share 5.2 %, max 51 % (camera cut-like jump).
- M-124 fidelity (CIEDE2000, share of pixels <= 2.0, 67 sampled frames): 4:4:4 MASTER min 0.9869 / mean 0.9965 (FAILS the 0.99 per-frame gate on the worst frames; p99.9 worst 4.34); 4:2:0 fallback min 0.840 / mean 0.887 (fails, as expected). CRF 6 is the project setting; I did not lower the CRF or the gate. Lowering CRF (or CRF 0) is the documented way to pass if this clip is to be a trailer master (it would exceed 50 MB).

## Reproduce
Player change (flagged, no effect without `-sliceRecord`): `SliceBootstrap` `-sliceRecord <dir> [-sliceRecordSeconds 90] [-sliceRecordFps 30] [-sliceRecordHud 1]`: smoke auto-pilot + a recurring script (impact every 18 s, quake every 30 s, swing every 10 s, duel every 25 s), `Time.captureFramerate`, one PNG per frame via ScreenCapture at end of frame, no HUD, no hangar cycle, writes `record.json`, quits. Build: `Tools/Build/build.sh --out /tmp/kaiju-build/t203 --no-smoke` (non-development build; needs a pgrep shim when other agents' shell lines contain `-batchmode`). Run: `/tmp/kaiju-build/t203/b1/KaijuGame.app/Contents/MacOS/KaijuGame -sliceConfig demo -sliceRecord <dir> -sliceRecordSeconds 100 -screen-width 1920 -screen-height 1080 -screen-fullscreen 0 -popupwindow` (1920x1080, not 1280x720, so the x4 integer scale of the 480x270 internal frame is exact; wall time ~4.5 min after ~1 min load). Encode: `capture_tools.encode.encode(..., pattern="f%06d.png", fps=30)`.
Screen recording (avfoundation) was not usable (permission prompt hangs), hence the in-player frame dump. Not the batch capture route (it cannot run the mech/combat).
